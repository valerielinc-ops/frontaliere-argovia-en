Full description

Oerlikon is a world-leading technology company, developing innovative solutions in surface technology. At the Wohlen (AG) site, customer-specific coating plants are being developed – from development through construction to successful commissioning. Our customers are active in dynamic growth markets such as aerospace, automotive and energy. Would you like to contribute your know-how to customer-specific plant construction and actively participate in demanding technical solutions? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Constructor (gn)

Their tasks

  • Support in the area of mechanical engineering for all general tasks and projects
  • Preparation of detailed and compilation drawings in accordance with standards and production
  • Implementation of the elaborated designs into standardized documents
  • Establishment of parts lists and other technical documentation such as tables, diagrams, drawings and descriptions for operation, service and production instructions
  • Processing of Amendments (ECR)
  • Responsible for compliance with quality rules and instructions
  • Responsible for the standard and standard parts catalogue (layout parts, designations, etc.)

Your profile

  • Successful, completed apprenticeship/training as a constructor (gn)
  • Experience as a constructor (gn) or polymechanic (gn)
  • User knowledge with mechanical CAD systems (2D/3D), Inventor is an advantage
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Understanding of technical drawings, standards and standards
  • German and English in word and writing
  • Teamwork and good communication skills.
  • Independent working methods and commitment to high quality

We offer

  • Attractive compensation package with good social benefits
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Varied activities in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment

Do you feel addressed? Then we are curious to meet you!

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and high-performance materials. With a unique portfolio that includes surface engineering, high performance materials, coating equipment and components, we improve products by increasing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medicine, luxury, semiconductor and tool technology. The headquarters are located in Pfäffikon, Switzerland. Oerlikon operates in 38 countries, employs around 10,000 people and achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2025.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential – guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
