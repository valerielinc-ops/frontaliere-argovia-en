Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • In cooperation with the educational program management, you are responsible for the further development of the pedagogical concepts, learning and teaching vessels from development to evaluation in the nursing HF educational program.
  • Advise and accompany teachers in the implementation of educational-didactic concepts and the implementation of new forms of learning or promotion.
  • Plan, lead and evaluate projects, for example on new teaching and learning forms or doctoral steps.
  • Stand in exchange with practice firms and take into account the needs of practice in school development.
  • Take part in change management and quality assurance and initiate appropriate measures.

What you bring

  • Diploma as a nurse HF or FH and at least 5 years of professional experience
  • Further education lecturer/in HF as well as proven further education for this function, e.g. project management
  • Ability in self-directed work organization as well as strong communication and performance skills. Lateral leadership experience is desired
  • In-depth knowledge of didactics and current teaching and learning forms in the analogue and digital sector
  • Strength in analytical, conceptual, networked thinking and enjoyment of cross-interface collaboration

The training course Care HF is constantly developing. Therefore, we are looking for a specialist "pedagogical concepts, projects and evaluation". She works in projects and specialist groups closely with the educational management, the teachers and the other specialist departments of the HFGS. It facilitates teaching and learning according to high quality standards and the continuous innovation and development of learning opportunities and the organisation of the educational programme.
