The Opportunity You will be part of a vibrant, up to 15-person host team in Kaiseraugst (close to Basel), working alongside experienced Customer Logistics Managers and an apprentice. We are the driving force behind order fulfillment: tracking and optimizing shipments from initial entry all the way to final delivery. It’s a fast-paced, supportive environment where everyone's contribution matters.

  • Flexible working hours and Home Office (up to 50%)
  • Online/Offline student networking events
  • Developing a deep understanding of supply chain operations with direct market customers at a leading pharmaceutical company
  • Gaining exposure in the wider Global Supply Chain Operations & Logistics organization (140 employees over 4 different locations)
  • Ability to support and contribute shaping the future of the organization by deploying your skills and interest into continuous improvement projects/initiatives

Who You Are

You are currently enrolled at a university, preferably in the field of supply chain management, business administration, life sciences (chemistry, biology, pharmacy), IT / computer science or another degree, or you completed your studies within the last 12 months prior to the start date. Moreover, you are…

  • Interested and excited about working in the SAP/ERP and supply chain systems landscape, including OMP (order entry, delivery creation, and invoicing).
  • Excited about supporting patients worldwide to get the medicine they need.
  • Capable of managing standard analysis, presentation, and process flow software, specifically Google tools/gSuite, Excel, PowerPoint, Lucidchart, and Smartsheet, with the ability to organize and visualize data.
  • Demonstrating the ability to build up a network or international relationships.
  • Possessing a high degree of self-management.
  • Open-minded and curious to learn new things.
  • Equipped with mandatory English language skills, while German language skills are a great plus.
  • Responsible for managing the internship cycle by supporting the hiring process of new interns, onboarding/training, and handing over tasks to the new internship.

Additional Information

  • Please upload your curriculum vitae and your motivation letter specific to this position.
  • Preferred start date: October 2026 or according to agreement, ideally for 12 months, not less than 9 months in Kaiseraugst (CH).
  • This position is suitable for students looking for a full-time internship, however working part-time (at least 32 hours a week) is also possible.
  • Preparing your final thesis during this internship could also be supported.
  • Please note: due to regulations, Non-Swiss/EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/n) must enclose a confirmation from the university with the application documents that a compulsory internship is part of the internship.
