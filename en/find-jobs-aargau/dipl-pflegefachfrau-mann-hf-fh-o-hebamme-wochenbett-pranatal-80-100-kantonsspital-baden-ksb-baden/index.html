Full description

Around pregnancy and postpartum counts your competence – and your humanity. In our Department of Prenatal and Postpartum, you will accompany new and new families with expertise, empathy and commitment. We stand for modern support, individual support and close interdisciplinary cooperation. If you want to take responsibility and support families in a special phase of life, we look forward to your application.

Their tasks

You look after and advise pregnant women, women who have recently given birth and their newborns as well as their relatives with your expertise.

Quality is the A and O in care. You make sure that the individual care planning is implemented.

Our goal is to give families a successful start at home.

We work with Lean Management. This also means that you take responsibility for a zone.

We want to move forward. Get actively involved so that shift operations and interdisciplinary cooperation work even better tomorrow than today.

Your profile

You have a degree as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH (foreign diplomas: SRC recognition)

We are happy to respond to your wishes. In return, we expect you to be flexible and to step in for a colleague or colleague. After all, we are all one team.

We maintain a family environment and look at each other. Especially towards students and learners you are helpful.
