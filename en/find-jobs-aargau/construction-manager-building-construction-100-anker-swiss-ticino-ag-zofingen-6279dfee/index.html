Full description

Construction Manager Building Construction 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a permanent job a construction manager has learned

Your challenge

  • Independent management and coordination of conversion and renovation projects
  • Planning, organisation and monitoring of on-site construction processes
  • Timetable, cost and quality control of projects
  • Tendering and subcontracting
  • Leadership and coordination of all involved craftsmen and specialist planners
  • Contact person for builders and authorities
  • Ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in construction (e.g. building drawing, masonry, carpenter) with further training as construction manager, technician HF or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in construction management, ideally in the area of conversions / renovations
  • Good knowledge of Swiss standards and construction processes
  • Organizational talent, assertiveness and independent, entrepreneurial thinking
  • Communication strength in dealing with customers and partners
  • Secure handling of common IT tools (MS Office, construction administration software)
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence

What we offer you

  • Exciting and varied projects
  • High personal responsibility, short decision paths and perspectives for professional and personal development
  • Experienced, practical team
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary employment conditions
