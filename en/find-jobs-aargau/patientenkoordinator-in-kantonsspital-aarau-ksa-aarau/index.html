Full description

Tasks

  • As a patient coordinator in the field of spinal surgery, you ensure the structured, patient-oriented and interdisciplinary coordination of all administrative, medical and social processes in elective neurosurgical procedures.
  • They are the central contact person for our patients and relatives and ensure the flow of information between all participating professional groups in order to enable an efficient treatment process and optimal entry and exit planning.
  • They coordinate the admission and exit dates, plan the corresponding consultation hours in neurosurgery and organize the special consultation hours (e.g. nutritional advice, physiotherapy, etc.).
  • You are responsible for ensuring the correct project documentation.
  • They maintain our SIRIS database.
  • In addition, the Secretariat for Spine Surgery supports you in administrative activities.

Requirements

  • They have completed training in the health care sector, preferably in nursing, or a medical-administrative qualification with professional experience.
  • They have strong patient- and service-oriented thinking.
  • You have experience in interdisciplinary cooperation or with case management.
  • They have very good organizational and communication skills.
  • They like to work in a structured and independent manner.
  • You are familiar with clinic information systems, preferably with KISIM.

With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
