The Position In the manufacturing unit "Solids Launch & Supply", we take care of the production and packaging of various solid drug forms at the production site in Kaiseraugst. These include film tablets, capsules and granules for the production of oral suspensions. In order for our products to be produced with the highest quality and on time, state-of-the-art facilities are available. A good connection to the metropolis Basel is also given.

The possibility Supported as an intern in the MSAT Solids Launch & Transfer department at the Kaiseraugst production site You the team with exciting and varied tasks. In this internship, you can expect:

  • The independent processing of smaller projects in close cooperation between the various departments of the Manufacturing Unit Solids Launch & Supply
  • Participation in the maintenance of a pharmaceutical quality system in a cGMP environment
  • Support for validation and optimisation activities in the pharmaceutical production of solid drug forms
  • Cooperation in quality risk management processes and product transfers from formulation development to commercial production (upscaling)
  • Creation and revision of GMP documents as well as necessary operating regulations and work instructions (SOPs - Standard Operating Procedures) e.g. in the context of cleaning validation

Who you are

You are preferably enrolled in the field of pharmacy or in general in a scientific or technical discipline or have Completed your studies at a university or university of applied sciences within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a modern working environment as part of an internship of at least 6 months, as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry.

  • You work independently and reliably
  • You are distinguished by a fast comprehension
  • Do you have good IT knowledge in office applications (Google Suite; MS Office)
  • You have very good German and good English skills

The preferred start of the internship is for November 2026, or by arrangement. Please indicate your preferred start date and the desired internship duration in your application. A cover letter is desired.

Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must attach a confirmation from the university that a mandatory internship is part of the education to the application documents.
