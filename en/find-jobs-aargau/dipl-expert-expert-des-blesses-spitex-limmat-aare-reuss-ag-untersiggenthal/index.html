Full description

We are looking for you as Dipl. Wound expert/wound expert Healing requires not only bandages – but a face. You are a wound expert: in passion? Then you are exactly right with us. At Spitex LAR, we don’t just treat wounds. You accompany people professionally, structured and competently through complex healing processes, in close cooperation with all referees and the surrounding hospitals. We are not looking for a “resource”, but a personality who takes responsibility and never loses sight of people.

Why you make the difference with us

  • Focus Carepoint: Part of your work takes place in our modernly furnished wound ambulatorium – without travel times, with optimal conditions for high-quality wound care.
  • Teamwork at expert level: You work closely with two other wound experts and the wound team.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You coordinate therapies with doctors, hospitals and all referees.
  • Quality before time pressure: We give you the time that a professional wound assessment and treatment needs.
  • Knowledge transfer: You train the team in modern wound care and ensure that we always stay up-to-date.

What you have in your luggage

  • Conclusion: Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH with specialization wound expert:in SAfW or comparable.
  • Experience: Sound practice in the treatment of chronic wounds.
  • Mindset: You are a team player, work independently and appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Mobility: driving licence Cat. B.

What we offer you

  • Regulated working hours: Monday to Friday – no evening or weekend services
  • Real work-life balance with scheduled working days.
  • Modern equipment: Digital wound documentation via smartphone so you can focus on care.
  • Extra advantages: Modernly furnished wound ambulatorium for productive days without time to go.
  • Future opportunities: Active participation in your further education and training.
