Pensum: 80 – 100% | Beznau

Do you want to further develop your skills in a dynamic and demanding environment? As a project manager with us, you take responsibility for exciting projects in all phases and actively promote their success. With us you can contribute your ideas and decisively influence the design of the project success. We offer you versatile tasks, flexibility and the opportunity to further develop your expertise in a future-oriented company.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You take on the leadership and responsibility for demanding projects and work closely with your project staff from the specialist departments, the power plant operation and external service providers.
  • You ensure that your projects are implemented on schedule, within the budget and with the desired quality – always taking into account the Axpo Group Guidelines
  • You manage risk management for your projects, identify risks at an early stage, take measures and report on them regularly
  • You support your team and the project staff in all professional and organizational matters and thus contribute to the successful project completion
  • You ensure that all relevant processes are adhered to and act as contact for the supervisory authority ENSI.

Profile and skills

  • A completed engineering degree FH/ETH in electrical engineering or mechanical engineering
  • You have in-depth training in project management and several years of experience as a project employee/engineer or project manager
  • You are drawing Expect yourself through strong assertiveness and entrepreneurial thinking
  • You work independently, show your own initiative and are a real team player

About the team

We attach great importance to the further development of each individual and offer you the opportunity to grow both professionally and personally. In our team, you will find a place where your ideas are heard and your commitment is appreciated.
