Full description

No more desire for gray hospital walks and rigid station structures? We are looking for a health professional EFZ (FaGe) who loves variety! With us, you drive home to the clients, bring quality of life directly into the living room and enjoy maximum personal responsibility. Become part of our dynamic Spitex team! Immediately or by agreement we are looking for 30% - 60% reinforcement as FaGe for the Spitex - your commitment counts! Your Everyday Life on Tour

  • Care in the familiar home: You care for clients in their usual environment and are a real trusted person.
  • Medical technology on site: From blood pressure measurements to wound care – You go professionally.
  • Lived quality: You ensure a needs-based and professional care quality in stable situations and document your assignments digitally.
  • Teamwork: You work closely with all professional groups and let your ideas flow into the team.

  • Arriving: Careful and gradual familiarization with the self-organized working method.
  • Fair expenses and inconveniences: We compensate your car trips and late and weekend services easily and fairly.

  • Modern tools: Digital working with tablet, no paperwork!
  • Real appreciation: A well-established, family-like team that also supports each other interdisciplinary.
  • Development: Possibility of further training in internal and external courses or studying as a nurse HF.

You have it in your luggage.

  • Health professional EFZ as training or equivalent training.
  • At best, exciting additional competencies, as Gross-Spitex we know many possibilities to use them.

  • A warm and binding appearance that beautifies our clients the day.
