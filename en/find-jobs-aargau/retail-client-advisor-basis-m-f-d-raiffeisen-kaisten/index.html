Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Regio Laufenburg, a successful and locally oriented bank with consulting points in Kaisten, Laufenburg and Sulz, we are looking for an open, committed and service-oriented personality as a private customer consultant.

Tasks

  • Competent advice and need-based sale of basic services in the areas of saving, figures, investment and provisions
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Proactive market processing in our business group
  • Identifying and exploiting the customer potential through proactive and continuous support of team-maintained customers
  • Efficient and demand-oriented advice of customers at the Consultants' Office and co-management of cash management
  • Cooperation with target group-specific market processing measures and customer events / performance of representative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training, experience in banking is advantageous
  • Enthusiastic and positive personality
  • Extensive customer and service orientation
  • Regional anchoring in the business circle is advantageous
  • Teamwork, responsibility and initiative
  • Performance-oriented and flexible personality with high independence

What do we offer you?

  • Family, motivated and helpful team
  • Modern banking facilities
  • Promotion of continuing training and development
  • Well-equipped and ergonomic workstations
  • Possibility to help shape the future of our bank with your ideas and commitment

We offer: The cooperative values are our cornerstones: together. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
