Full description

More than a job

In this position, you will work directly for a major international healthcare customer and work in a demanding, highly regulated environment. You ensure that time-critical and sensitive healthcare transports are processed reliably, efficiently and in accordance with the rules.

‎

With your work, you make an important contribution to healthcare and ensure that patients, medical institutions and companies can rely on functioning supply chains.

How you get involved

  • Coordination of international transports in a regulated healthcare environment
  • Monitoring of shipments and proactive action in the event of deviations
  • Ensuring compliance with logistics processes and quality standards
  • Support in optimizing transport and process flows
  • Close collaboration with customers, partners and internal interfaces
  • Support with operational challenges and Escalations
  • Ensuring a smooth flow of information between all parties involved

What sets you apart

  • Completed training in freight forwarding, logistics or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in international freight forwarding or transport logistics
  • Experience in at least one mode of transport (land transport, air or sea freight)
  • Experience in the healthcare, pharmaceutical or MedTech environment is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • High Customer orientation and strong quality awareness
  • Very good written and spoken German and English skills

What's in it for you

  • Market-based remuneration including 13. Monthly salary
  • Extensive training offers, from language courses to individual development programs
  • Exclusive discounts on top brands from fashion, leisure, electronics, living & more
  • Employee recruitment program with attractive bonuses
  • Team and company events

Who we are

Logistics touches every aspect of our lives - from the goods we carry every day consumption to the healthcare we rely on. With us, your work goes beyond logistics: it enables the small and big moments of life for people all over the world.

As a leading global logistics company with the vision to further develop our world, we offer you a safe and stable environment. This is where your work makes a real difference. Whether it's delivering food, developing sustainable transportation solutions or engaging with local communities, your work does more than you can imagine.
