Full description

The rehabilitation clinic in Baden-Dättwil and the rehabilitation clinic Freihof in Baden offer rehabilitation at a high medical level for musculoskeletal, neurological, internistic and rheumatological patients. Thanks to the interdisciplinary cooperation and the excellent expertise of the treatment teams, we adapt our efficient therapy programs individually to the needs of these patients.

Your tasks

  • Care: Medical care of the inpatients in cooperation with the senior physician as well as the senior physician, night and weekend services, are covered by our presence doctors
  • Planning: implementation of the rehabilitation planning of the inpatients, entrance and exit examination, visits
  • Contact person: Medical contact person for medical issues within the interdisciplinary cooperation of the professional groups integrated in the rehabilitation process (e.g. care, therapy, psychologists, etc.) as well as for the referring doctors, family doctors and relatives

Your profile

  • Qualifications: A federal state examination or a foreign diploma recognized by MEBEKO is required
  • Beginning your career: The activity offers newcomers an excellent opportunity to learn acute care medicine, as a large number of patients require intensive internist care.
  • Interests: interest in neurology, neurophysiological diagnosis and treatment, PMR, general internal medicine and rheumatology (of which at least six months ambulance work with predominantly acute pain syndromes and inflammatory system diseases)
  • Personality: Professional and social competence for good interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Working method: Structured and independent work is required, our cadre doctors take over the close professional supervision in the daily reports and three weekly visits
  • Languages: Good knowledge of German, Italian also beneficial

We offer this

  • Continuing education: Continuing education permit category B Physical medicine and rehabilitation (cat B for Phys). Med, recognized in Baden-Dättwil for one year as a C-clinic as well as general internal medicine (AIM) and rheumatology (SIWF applications for the clinic Freihof in Baden pendent). If interested, rotations into C-Clinic Neurology in Baden-Dättwil are possible. We also promote scientific work up to the doctorate.
  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate, also
  • regular training and congress visits
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
