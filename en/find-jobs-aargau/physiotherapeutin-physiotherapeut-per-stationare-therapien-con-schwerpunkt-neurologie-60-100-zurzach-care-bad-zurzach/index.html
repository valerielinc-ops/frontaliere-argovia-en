Full description

The rehabilitation clinic Bad Zurzach offers rehabilitation in all facets at a high medical level. The rehabilitative offer ranges from neurology to musculoskeletal/rheumatology and cardiology to sleep medicine (clinic for sleep medicine). In our team, the diverse treatment options are highly appreciated. The individual care of our patients is important to us and is made possible by a variety of individual and group offers. Modern equipped robotics and MTT as well as water and garden therapy perfectly complement our offer.

Your tasks

  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You carry out findings and treat our inpatients in the field of neurology. You are in a constant exchange with a team of doctors, nurses and other therapeutic professions.
  • Patient-oriented care: You carry out assessments and documentation, which are personally tailored to each patient and support the creation of an individual therapy plan
  • Therapies: You perform individual and group therapies. There are numerous possibilities for this, such as robotics, our modern MTT, the therapy garden and our therapy pools at your disposal.

Your profile

  • Training : Completed training as a physiotherapist or equivalent with at least one SRK PreCheck
  • Personality: You have teamwork skills and enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Interest: You are interested in the Department of Neurology or have already completed further training in this field

We offer this

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern work environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive, appreciative and humorous team that works together to provide the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Training and further education: weekly in-house or interprofessional training, as well as professional support by our specialists. Generous cost sharing in external education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission : Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach
