Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • General contact point for telephone information in German and various foreign languages
  • Care of the customer desk for all foreigner issues
  • Processing and triagulation of the office e-mail inbox
  • Forwarding questions, communications and information to the departments
  • Preparation of file notes for the specialist areas
  • Provision and submission of information and leaflets at the customer counter or for postal mail
  • Processing of return visa applications

What you bring

  • Basic commercial training or comparable
  • Completed further training in the commercial sector (e.g. language diplomas (B2 - C2), specialist cards, etc.)
  • Several years of professional experience in demanding administrative and/or telephone consulting activities
  • Very good knowledge of German and foreign languages (English, French, Italian, other advantageous)
  • Very good service orientation and flexible operational readiness
  • Good IT application knowledge in MS Office range and other applications
  • Enforcement and resilience
  • Confidentiality

Your working environment

In the Office for Migration and Integration, we are responsible for implementing the immigration and asylum legislation and implement the integration policy of the Confederation and the Canton. We issue permits for foreign persons, carry out labour market controls and ensure the implementation of the illegal work legislation. We advise foreigners willing to return and execute the expulsions ordered by the Canton and the Confederation.
