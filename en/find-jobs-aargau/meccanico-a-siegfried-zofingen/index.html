Your Role

To strengthen our team in Zofingen, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as a mechanic (a). In this function, you ensure the safe and trouble-free operation of our machines and plants in the chemical production environment.

You work in a versatile technical environment and make an important contribution to the operational reliability, plant availability and continuous improvement of our production processes through your expertise.

Your Profiles

Their role

  • Maintenance (maintenance, repair) of machinery and equipment in the chemical production environment (e.g. pumps, motors, valves)
  • Care of clean water and recooling plants
  • Cooperation in the optimisation of existing plants
  • Fault analysis and correction of mechanical and mechatronic systems in cooperation with plant managers and production
  • General machining work such as turning, milling and drilling (conventional)
  • Careful documentation of the work carried out
  • Ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements (in particular GMP)

Your profile Specialised knowledge

  • Completed basic mechanical training EFZ (e.g. polymechanic, production mechanic, mechanic)
  • Several years of professional experience as a mechanic in an industrial environment
  • Experience in the maintenance of machinery and equipment is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Readiness for Picettdienst (no shift work)

Personal strengths

  • Strong quality awareness and reliable working methods
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of dismantling and assembling machines
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Teamwork, flexibility and high commitment
  • Service-oriented thinking and action

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers into industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
