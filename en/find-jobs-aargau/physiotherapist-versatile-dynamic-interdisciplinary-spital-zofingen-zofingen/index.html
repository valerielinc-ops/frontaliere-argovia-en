Full description

Their tasks

  • Outpatient physiotherapy: treatment of a wide range of patients, orthopaedics, neurology, internal medicine, sports physiotherapy and therapeutic group training
  • Inpatient care in the acute hospital: Independent physiotherapeutic care of patients in the areas of internal medicine, orthopaedics, neurology and geriatrics
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Close cooperation with doctors, nursing and other therapeutic professions in acute and rehabilitation setting
  • Training and knowledge transfer: possibility to participate in the supervision of physiotherapy students and to actively participate in internal training
  • Team and Development: Active participation in team meetings, internal training and quality development

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Physical therapist (SRK-approved) or Bachelor Physiotherapy
  • Enjoyment of working with patients of all ages or clinical pictures
  • Evidence-based, structured and patient-centred way of working
  • Flexibility, team spirit and the ability to adapt quickly to new situations
  • Safe handling of digital documentation and modern communication
  • Additional training or special interests (e.g. sports, neuro, geriatrics) are welcome, but not a must

What we offer you

  • A versatile location with a broad physiotherapeutic spectrum in a modern acute hospital
  • A family, supportive team that actively lives collaboration and knowledge exchange
  • Flexible working hours and discharge by a dedicated therapy secretariat
  • Modern, well-equipped therapy facilities
  • Attractive internal and external training and development opportunities
  • A working environment that combines professionalism, humanity and future orientation

Personal, Family, Competent In the regional and supra-regional health network, the Zofingen Hospital plays an important role. With more than 750 employees and various partner organizations, we ensure medical care for the population and have also specialized in high-quality old-age medicine. Ms. Monique Huismann, Head of Department Physiotherapy
