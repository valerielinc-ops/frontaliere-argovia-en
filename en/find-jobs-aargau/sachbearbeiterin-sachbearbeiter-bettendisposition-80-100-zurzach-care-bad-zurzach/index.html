Full description

For our location in Bad Zurzach, we are looking for a committed personality who wants to strengthen our great team. Our team consists of 14 motivated people and we look forward to welcoming you to us. After familiarization, we also offer the opportunity for home office to provide a flexible working environment.

Tasks

  • Process briefings: You check and process briefing documents and ensure a smooth administrative reception
  • Collect patient data: You collect all relevant data in the clinic information system (M-KIS, Dynmed, RAP) and keep patient records correctly and seamlessly
  • Cost Language & Agreements: You obtain cost languages, monitor them and coordinate the cost agreements with our patients
  • Bed planning: You take over the responsible planning of the beds across all stationary locations
  • Communication: You maintain contact with internal and external partners such as referees, insurers, patients and the interprofessional team
  • Information Management: You guide interested patients through the house and provide competent information
  • Administration: You support the team with general administrative tasks and contribute to the optimization of our processes

Conditions

  • Training: Completed commercial training (EFZ or commercial diploma)
  • Experience: Some years of professional experience, ideally as a bed dispatcher or in health care
  • IT know-how: Good user knowledge in MS Office, also beneficial in RAP, DynMED and M-KIS
  • Personality: You have a strong organizational talent, a high service orientation as well as flexibility and teamwork
  • Communication: You convince with your clear and empathetic communication and your safe appearance

Your Benefits

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are also rewarded attractively.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your application via our online tool!
