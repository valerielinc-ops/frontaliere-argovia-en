MPA, Arztsekretärin mit Schwerpunkt Orthopädie — Spital Muri

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Clinical Operations Department ensures that everything is organized smoothly in our medical departments from scheduling to reporting from Monday to Friday within regular working hours. For this versatile, responsible and lively task, we are looking for a committed personality as a specialist for the orthopaedic consultation. The preferred working days are Tuesday full day, Wednesday half day and Thursday full day.

Their tasks

  • Ensuring the smooth running of office hours and assistance in other specialist areas
  • Termination of consultation, treatment and diagnostic appointments
  • Preparation and follow-up of office rooms (control as well as filling up of materials and medicines, provision of equipment and cleaning of the same, preparation of consultation-hour-specific lists, forms and brochures)
  • Writing from Diktafon, correcting medical reports and sending, independently processing and setting up workflows
  • Coordination of internal and external requests from patients and referees by e-mail and telephone (reports, prescriptions, appointments)
  • The conscientious maintenance of electronic patient records (KISIM/Business Central)
  • Where appropriate, carrying out specific activities in the area of operation with patient care and assistance during consultation hours

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as MPA, medical secretary
  • A high affinity in dealing with medical devices and PCs
  • Forward-looking organizational talent as well as a reliable, team-capable, solution-oriented and extremely patient- and service-oriented personality
  • With their stylish German and sound terminology knowledge reflect Our professionalism in writing from Diktaphon, as well as in the processing of all correspondence
  • You are a reliable, team-capable, solution-oriented and decidedly patient- and service-oriented personality with organizational talent and pleasure in the profession
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary teamwork
  • Willingness to contribute inputs, learn new things and help shape the further development of consultation hours

Your opportunity It awaits you immediately or by agreement a varied and demanding task in a lively company characterized by growth and modernization with a focus on teamwork. Interdisciplinary cooperation offers space for self-responsible work and internal and external training opportunities.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented if possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 40-50 %
