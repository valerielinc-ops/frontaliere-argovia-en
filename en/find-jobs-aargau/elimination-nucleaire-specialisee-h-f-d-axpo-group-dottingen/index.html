Full description

Pensum: 80–100% I Beznau

Create safe decommissioning and disposal at the Beznau site with us. You take on responsibility in a forward-looking project with a perspective on project management and bring your ideas to an open, diverse team.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You plan, control and monitor the treatment, packaging and conditioning of radioactive waste streams.
  • You are actively involved in the further development of the disposal strategy and the KKB-specific disposal catalogue.
  • You optimize and digitize processes for the treatment, packaging, conditioning and documentation of radioactive waste.
  • You ensure the compliant collection of radiological and material data for the waste data sheets.
  • You create technical and regulatory reports and actively coordinate with specialist departments, external service providers and authorities.
  • You plan and accompany disposal campaigns as well as the procurement of waste packages and external transports.

Profile and Requirements

  • You have completed university studies in a scientific-technical direction.
  • You have additional training or practical knowledge in nuclear physics, nuclear or nuclear engineering, radiation protection, waste management or chemistry – or a similar technical field.
  • You bring several years of experience in the planning and implementation of disposal projects, ideally in the nuclear environment.
  • Ideally, you already have experience in motivating, developing and leading teams through challenging projects.
  • You have experience in project, risk and quality management.
  • You work independently and structured, communicate clearly and convince the team – even if you do not meet all points, we look forward to your application.

About the team

You will become part of a dedicated team at the Beznau site working together on safe decommissioning and disposal. We value openness, mutual support and different perspectives – and celebrate our successes together.
