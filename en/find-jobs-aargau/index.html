G permit and border zone

Aargau borders Germany and France, not Italy: the G permit here is typically issued to German or French residents of the border zone. Italian residents considering a role in Aargau usually apply for a B residence permit instead of cross-border-commuter status, which is reserved for residents of a border municipality in the neighbouring country.
