Full description

Are you interested in sales, retail and figures and are you interested in working analytical and structured in an international environment? You want to take responsibility and actively contribute to corporate success? Then you're right with us. As a Commercial Sales Coordinator at JYSK Switzerland, you are also responsible for sales and profitability in various product areas. You work closely with international colleagues and actively contribute to the further development of our Swiss market. Your workplace is our head office in Zofingen, while your direct leadership is in Germany. You bring engagement and have the following main tasks...

  • Responsibility for defined product areas as well as ensuring sales and profitability
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of campaigns across different channels
  • price design of the assortment and co-design of an optimal assortment structure
  • Analysis of sales figures and key figures and derivation and implementation of appropriate measures
  • Observation of market and competitors to ensure our competitiveness
  • Ensuring information transfer to our branches via internal communication channels
  • takeover of recurring and ad hoc tasks in the daily business, in particular in the context of seasonal activities
  • Participation in meetings and travel activities for voting and market analyses (approximately 15–20 days per year)

To be successful in the role, you can optimally bring the following...

  • Motivation to actively contribute to the success of JYSK Switzerland and take responsibility
  • Completion in an economic science course or commercial training with relevant professional experience
  • Analytical and structured way of thinking and joy in dealing with numbers
  • Self-employed, proactive and solution-oriented operation
  • Good time management and the ability to reliably comply with deadlines
  • Very good English skills in word and writing
  • Secure handling of MS Office, especially Excel
  • Ideally a good commercial understanding and interest in retailing

With us you meet possibilities and...

  • A great corporate culture
  • Scandinavian values and leadership
  • A highly motivated and professional team
  • An international environment with close cooperation across borders
  • An attractive bonus model that rewards strong results
  • Full support and development within JYSK
  • Possibility of Home Office
  • 25 days holiday
  • Uvm.
