Your Role

For our newly created drug product R&D laboratory at Hautpsitz in Zofingen, we are looking for a committed personality with a passion for pharmaceutical development, analytics and practical laboratory work. They work at the interface of formulation development, material profiling, pharmaceutical characterisation and HPLC method development for oral solid dosage forms. You will support innovative drug product solutions from early project phases to the technical basis for decision-making.

Your Profiles

Their role

  • Planning, implementation, documentation and evaluation of laboratory tests for the development and optimization of oral solid dosage forms, in particular tablets and capsules
  • Development and optimisation of formulations and manufacturing processes on a laboratory scale, including mixing, granulation, tableting and coating
  • Implementation of material profiling and pharmaceutical characterizations of active ingredients and adjuvants as well as intermediate and end products
  • Development, optimisation and application of analytical methods, in particular high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), as well as performance of release and physical tests
  • Evaluation, interpretation and presentation of experimental data as a basis for technical and project-related decisions
  • Preparation and maintenance of test plans, laboratory journals, test methods, reports and other development documents
  • Commissioning, qualification, support and maintenance of laboratory equipment and helping to shape appropriate laboratory processes and standards
  • Active participation in the establishment of the new Drug Product R&D laboratory and suggestions for improvement
  • Ensuring compliance with security, environmental protection, data integrity and quality requirements

Your profile Professional knowledge

  • Completed training as a laboratory assistant with a specialization in chemistry, Bachelor in Life Sciences or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of practical professional experience in pharmaceutical development, ideally with fixed oral dosage forms
  • In-depth experience in formulation development, material characterization and practical laboratory work
  • Good knowledge of HPLC method development and other relevant analytical or physical test methods
  • Safe use of Microsoft Office
  • Experience with LIMS, Chromeleon or comparable laboratory and documentation systems
  • Interest in AI-based work is beneficial
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing

Personal strengths

  • Hands-on mentality as well as independent, structured and reliable working methods
  • Enterprising spirit, initiative and pleasure in actively shaping a new laboratory as well as corresponding structures and processes
  • Strong analytical mindset and high quality standards
  • Team-oriented and communicative personality with pleasure in working together in a global R&D environment

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
