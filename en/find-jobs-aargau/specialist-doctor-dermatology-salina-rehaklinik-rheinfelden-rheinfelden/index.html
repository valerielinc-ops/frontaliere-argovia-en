Reha Rheinfelden, Salina Rehaklinik and Park-Hotel am Rhein AG employ around 1,000 people. To achieve the best possible rehabilitation, interdisciplinary teams work together, with each individual’s expertise and experience contributing to success. The Salina Rehaklinik (rehabilitation clinic, specialists and therapies) offers an interdisciplinary concept for the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of the support and musculoskeletal system. The medical competence centre comprises modern diagnostic and therapeutic facilities with a competent team of specialists, therapists and nursing staff. Your team Our outpatient department in the specialist hospital is characterized by a competent interdisciplinary team of specialists and therapists for a diverse range of health care, treatment and care. The ambulatorium has specialists in rheumatology and physical medicine, sports medicine, orthopedics, iron metabolism disorder, osteoporosis, spinal surgery, neurology, interventional pain therapy, dermatology, general medicine, naturopathy, medical opinions, obesity and obesity, anthroposophic medicine as well as acupuncture and medical hypnosis.

As part of a forward-looking succession plan, we are looking for a committed and experienced personality as a reinforcement of our dermatological team. We offer you the opportunity to take on an established dermatological activity with a diverse range of patients and actively develop it further. Look forward to a varied job with a high level of personal responsibility and the opportunity to contribute your professional and personal skills in a professional and appreciative environment.
