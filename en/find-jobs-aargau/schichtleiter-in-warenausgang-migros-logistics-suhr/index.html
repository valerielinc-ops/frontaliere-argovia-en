Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility.

Mansioni

  • Professional management of employees
  • Coordination and support of employees
  • Ensuring timely and qualitatively correct processing of logistics orders
  • Assistance in the complete goods exit process
  • Active participation in the realization of projects and advancement of the continuous improvement process (CIP)
  • Creating operational controlling data
  • Participation in Daily Stand-up Meetings

Requirements

  • 3 to 5 years: logistics goods output
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Logistics
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as logistics specialist EFZ/ (logistics specialist of advantage)
  • Open and communicative personality
  • Resilience and strong enforcement
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good MS-Office knowledge
  • Driving licence as a forklift driver (SUVA recognised)
  • Readiness for 2-shift operation: Early: 05.00-14.00 / Late: 14.00-23.00 incl. Saturdays
  • German (very good knowledge)
