Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics as an HR Administration clerk.

Mansioni

  • Administrative handling of the entry and exit process in close cooperation with the HR Business Managers
  • Acquisition of inputs, transfers and other mutations in SAP (SF-EC)
  • First contact person for questions about time tracking at the location
  • Care of temporary staff
  • Accident and sickness handling services
  • Processing of family allowances (registration, mutations)
  • Management of family issues such as birth, marriage and death
  • Creating (intermediate) certificates
  • Maintaining the corporate structure in the SAP system
  • Participation in the creation/updating of HR processes and projects
  • Switch service

Requirements

  • 3-5 years: Human Resources
  • Completed basic or further training: Kauffrau*Kaufmann, human resources
  • Exact and structured working methods
  • Rapid comprehension, flexibility and a high level of process understanding
  • Service-oriented and positive team player
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (very good knowledge)
