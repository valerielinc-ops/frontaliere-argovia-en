Full description

You want to not only accompany international business, but actively shape it? In this key role, you combine marketing, sales and demanding customer management. You lead your team, move confidently at management level and develop our international chewing gum business together with us.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will be responsible for the marketing and sales activities for our international chewing gum business and develop the business together with internal and external partners.
  • You handle a strategically important international customer, conduct challenging negotiations and represent Delica sovereignly to different decision-makers.
  • You take over the professional and human leadership of a team member and ensure clear goals, priorities and effective cooperation.
  • You analyze sales, volume and profitability, derive measures from them and provide the basis for decision-making and presentations for management.
  • You control challenging commercial issues and ensure a professional implementation of our strategy through close coordination with the relevant areas.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in marketing, sales, key account management or commercial management as well as experience in a demanding international business environment.
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): at bachelor or master level, ideally in business administration, marketing, sales or a comparable discipline.
  • Strong commercial and analytical understanding and security in dealing with different international stakeholders and management levels.
  • You perform convincingly and professionally, communicate clearly and keep an overview even in challenging customer and negotiation situations.
  • You act independently and persistently, respect agreed decision-making paths and combine entrepreneurial thinking with a pronounced team orientation.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary.
