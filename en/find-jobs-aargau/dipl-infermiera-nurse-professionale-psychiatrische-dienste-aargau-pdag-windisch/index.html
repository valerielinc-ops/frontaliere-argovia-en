Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy

In order to strengthen our teams in nursing and social pedagogy, we are looking at our therapy unit of the Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy per 01. January 2027 or by arrangement The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy 5210 Windy Workload: 80 % 80 % Cooperation in 3-shift operations

Over the area

At the Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy (Clinical Director Dr. med.) Angelo Bernardon) are treated children and adolescents up to the age of 18. The integrated treatment offer of the clinic includes an outpatient area with a central ambulatory as well as four ambulatories distributed across the canton, a consultancy and liaison service and an inpatient area with an acute care unit, two psychotherapy units, a day clinic and a clinic school. In the teams, clinical specialists from the fields of medicine, psychology, nursing and social pedagogy work and in the clinic school teachers and curative school teachers. The Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy is a teaching hospital of the University of Zurich and a certified continuing education institution category A.

Tasks

  • You support and promote the social and emotional skills and abilities of patients (age from 7 to ≤ 18 years) in the milieutherapeutic setting
  • You design the daily structure of the patients in cooperation with the team and the other specialist areas
  • Acting in emergency situations You are competent and provide safety for children and young people
  • You are jointly responsible for the implementation of treatment planning

Profile

  • You have successfully completed training in nursing (HF/FH) and already have professional experience, with advantage psychiatric experience
  • You have experience working with children and young people
  • You are flexible, committed, resilient and a responsible person with pleasure in demanding patient care in a lively company
  • You speak or write fluent German and have a secure handling of the common office applications
