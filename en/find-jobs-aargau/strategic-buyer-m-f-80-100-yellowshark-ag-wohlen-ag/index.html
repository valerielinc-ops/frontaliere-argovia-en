Our client is an established industrial company with a broad technical product portfolio and an international focus. As part of the company's continued growth and the strategic realignment of the procurement function, we are looking for an experienced, entrepreneurial-minded individual who will actively shape and further develop procurement.

In this varied and responsible position, you will assume a central role in establishing and expanding modern procurement structures, maintaining and expanding international supplier relationships, and making a significant contribution to the company's long-term competitiveness.

02

Your responsibilities

  • Substantial experience in strategic procurement, ideally acquired in mechanical or plant engineering or in a comparable technical environment
  • Global sourcing expertise with experience in establishing and strategically developing international supplier networks, as well as realigning existing procurement structures
  • Broad technical category expertise, ranging from castings, turned and milled parts to plastic and rubber components, electrical cabinets, control systems, etc.
  • Negotiating skills on the international stage, with a confident presence in price, contract and terms negotiations with suppliers worldwide
  • Analytical and digital skill set with a strong understanding of numbers, excellent Excel skills, experience with SAP or Microsoft Dynamics 365, and fluent German and English
  • Entrepreneurial, hands-on individual who independently takes charge of building up the procurement function, creates sustainable structures, and assumes a central role in the company in the long term
