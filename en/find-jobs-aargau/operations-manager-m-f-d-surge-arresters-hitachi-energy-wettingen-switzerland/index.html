About the position

As Operations Manager surge arresters, you assume overall responsibility for safe, stable and predictable production as well as on-time delivery to our customers. You will lead the area from incoming orders and production planning to silicone injection molding (LSR/HTV) and the assembly of SPU and GIS surge arresters to shipping. You combine a strong production presence with structured, data-based operations management and a clear focus on safety, quality, delivery reliability and continuous improvement.

You report directly to the General Manager of the plant and lead the operations organization surge arresters with production, process and industrial engineering, detailed planning as well as supply & demand planning and order processing.

You can do that.

  • Overall responsibility for the operational performance of the surge arresters with focus on safety, quality, delivery reliability, costs and employees
  • Controlling the entire process from incoming orders to delivery as well as ensuring a stable production flow and realistic delivery dates
  • Head of Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) and coordination of customer requirements, production capacities, material availability and order priorities
  • Leadership and further development of the production department management as well as the teams in order processing and planning with clear goals and responsibilities
  • Ensuring high HSE, product and process quality standards as well as structured solution of deviations and escalations
  • Optimisation of production planning, capacities, stocks, cycle times and bottlenecks based on transparent key figures
  • Ensuring high plant and tool availability as well as close cooperation with maintenance, engineering, supply chain, quality and sales
  • Continuous development of operations through Lean, 5S, Kaizen, SMED as well as SAP- and MES-based processes and digitalization

You bring that with you

  • University degree in engineering, production engineering, industrial engineering, supply chain management or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in an industrial production environment, ideally with a high variety of variants and contract production
  • In-depth leadership experience with several teams and leaders
  • Experience in S&OP, production planning and order processing, ideally with responsibility for delivery reliability and delivery times
  • Very good understanding of production processes, material flow, capacity planning and industrial facilities
  • experience with lean manufacturing and structured problem-solving methods
  • Six Sigma as well as experience in injection molding or electromechanical assembly are advantageous
  • Experience with SAP PP/SD, MES or comparable production systems as well as with an indicator-based control
  • Present, decisive and at the same time cooperative leader with strong customer orientation and enjoyment of cross-divisional cooperation
  • Fluent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English
