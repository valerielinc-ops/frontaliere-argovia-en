Full description

Activities

  • Production of demanding welding components for gas turbines and partly for aerospace according to drawings and technical specifications
  • Manual WIG/TIG welding of nickel-based alloys, chromium steel and Inconel alloys
  • Welding work on robot systems and on circular and longitudinal welding machines
  • Performing orbital and spot welding work
  • Preparation, documentation and updating of working papers and welding instructions
  • Acquisition and feedback of operational data
  • Performing general spooning work such as grinding, straightening and bending
  • Ensuring high quality and timely production

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a plant and apparatus manufacturer or comparable technical training
  • Further development in the field of welding is advantageous
  • Welding tests according to EN ISO 9606-4 are advantageous
  • Sound practical experience in TIG/TIG welding is an advantage
  • Self-employed, careful and responsible work
  • Enjoyment of demanding and technically challenging welding work
  • Reliability, flexibility and resilience
  • Readiness to work in early and late shift operations
  • Good knowledge of German
