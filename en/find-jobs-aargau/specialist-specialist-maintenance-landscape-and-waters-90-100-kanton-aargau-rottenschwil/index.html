Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Implementation of care and maintenance measures on water courses and in nature reserves (e.g. mowing, hedge care, neophyte control)
  • Operation, guidance and maintenance of a modern boom mowing vehicle
  • Maintenance drainage facilities (canals & pumping plants)
  • Maintenance infrastructure nature protection zones (marking, information boards, rest areas)
  • Securing the maintenance of machinery and tools

What you bring

  • Vocational apprenticeship as landscaper, forest manager, farmer or similar training
  • Professional experience as a machinist
  • Basic ecological knowledge
  • Interest and understanding of water and nature conservation measures
  • Driving licence Cat. B/BE requirement

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. Nature and landscape provide habitat for animals and plants as well as recreational space for people. We in the Landscape and Waters department are committed to responsible development of the landscape, ecological enhancement and networking of diverse habitats for biodiversity, sustainable use of river waters and comprehensive flood protection.
