Health Center Fricktal AG | online since: 07.10.2026

Place of work: Villa Robersten Rheinfelden

With us, you can expect a versatile working day in a modern and personal environment. As part of our well-established team, you accompany and supervise our patients around their operations – from preoperative preparation, for example with blood draws, to assistance in minor surgical procedures such as excisions and upper eyelid lifts, to postoperative care and dressing changes, which you can carry out independently or together with the doctors depending on the situation. In addition, the aesthetic area awaits you: After training and familiarization, you will independently carry out aesthetic treatments such as Dermapen4 and Morpheus8 treatments. A warm, professional and courteous approach to our patients is just as important to us as an open exchange and mutual support in the team.

Villa Robersten in the traditional walls of the former Grand Hôtel des Salines Rheinfelden is located directly on the banks of the Rhine and is surrounded by beautiful nature. The hospital of the health center Fricktal AG (GZF) has the latest surgical technology and infrastructure and impresses with first-class medical care, noble ambience and excellent service. The clinic is home to the clinic Aesthea for Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery and the clinic for Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery under the direction of Dr. med. John Doummar – both divisions of the GZF. For external partner clinics and GZF physicians, Villa Robersten is also a preferred place for surgery and treatment. For our Ambulatorium for Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery (Aesthea) and the Clinic for Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery we are looking for 1.
