Full description

A wide range of assembly work requires manual dexterity and technical understanding. You will work closely with a collegial team and will be comprehensively trained. We are looking for a job for our client:

  • Mechanically assembling assemblies and systems
  • Professionally gluing and sealing assemblies
  • Electrically assembling control cabinets
  • Assembling cables for various applications
  • Carrying out assembly work on the basis of technical drawings

  • Safe handling of the EPLAN electrical diagram tool

  • Installation experience would be desirable
