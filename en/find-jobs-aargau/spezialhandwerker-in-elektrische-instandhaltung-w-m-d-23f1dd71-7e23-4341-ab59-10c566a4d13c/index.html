Full description

Pensum: 100%

Shape the energy future of Switzerland with us. In this role, you work on exciting projects in the field of substations and power plants and contribute your know-how to a committed team.

Tasks & Responsibilities

You carry out assembly work on power plants and substations throughout SwitzerlandYou take over the wiring and cabling of protective, control and control systems according to plan documentsYou build cable guidance systems, cable climbers and other mechanical componentsYou manufacture protective, control and self-use cabinets according to technical specificationsYou independently put electrical installations into operation according to NIV and create initial test protocolsYou work closely with employees, supervisors and customers and consistently pay attention to work safety and regulations

Profile and Requirements

You have an apprenticeship as an electrical installer or an apprenticeship recognised in SwitzerlandYou have experience in the field of assembly and service of protective and control systems as well as high and medium-voltage systemsYou have expertise in the electrical execution of secondary systems in substations and power plantsYou work independently, responsibly and gladly in the teamYou are ready to travel all over Switzerland and also spend the night abroad Ideally You already have experience in managing an assembly team on construction sites and good knowledge of MS Office

About the team

Our team works on challenging energy projects throughout Switzerland and supports each other in everyday life. For us, different experiences and perspectives are an important basis for cooperation, quality and innovation.
