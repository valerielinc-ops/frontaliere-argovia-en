Full description

Pensum: 80–100% I bathe

At Axpo, you shape the future of energy with us – with responsibility, vision and a strong team at your side.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You identify and evaluate target-actual deviations/contractual deviations (e.g. delivery delays, material defects) and contract claims.
  • You assess risks from contracts and control these as well as contractual claims (e.g. warranty, liability) from a specified value limit.
  • You act as an interface to project, tactical and strategic purchasing.
  • You create reports and document contract changes, claims and risks.
  • You process third-party claims and enforce self-claims, including defense against supplier demands for cost avoidance.
  • You create uniform, legally compliant contract templates for purchasing, projects and large contracts.

Profile and Requirements

  • Completed studies in engineering, business law, business administration or law
  • Extensive knowledge of contract law as well as a good technical understanding
  • You are strong in negotiations, communicate clearly and keep an overview even in complex situations
  • Ideally, at least 5 years of professional experience as well as a certificate such as "Certified Contract Manager (CCM)" or "International Certificate in Contract Management (ICCM)"
  • German negotiation-safe, English negotiation-safe
  • French is a plus
  • Even if you do not meet all points, we look forward to your application

About the team

As part of purchasing, you work closely with project, tactical and strategic purchasing. We are united by an open exchange, mutual support and the joy of joint success – regardless of origin or career.
