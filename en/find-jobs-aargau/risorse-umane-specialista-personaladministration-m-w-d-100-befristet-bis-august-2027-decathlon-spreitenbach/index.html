Full description

Mission Decathlon Switzerland is growing rapidly: Since the start in Neuchâtel in 2017, we have expanded our network to around 53 sales outlets. And we continue to expand – especially in German-speaking Switzerland. Your main tasks 1. Personnel administration

  • Preparation of employer certificates (e.g. for the unemployment fund) as well as all administrative confirmations for our employees.
  • Applying for and renewing work permits.
  • Handling applications for family allowances and maternity and paternity compensation (EO).
  • Writing interim and employment references in close cooperation with the respective team leaders. 2. Insurance and absence management
  • Recording, processing and controlling variable salary components (hour management, absences, settlement of family allowances and EO daily allowances).
  • Reporting and complete tracking of incapacity for work due to illness or accident in close coordination with HR Payroll Officers.
  • Processing registrations, changes and deregistrations with the AHV compensation office. 3. Operational Support & Payroll Support
  • Active administrative support of the payroll team in the implementation of cross-divisional HR projects.
  • Participation in plausibility checks as well as in monthly wage runs and closing.
  • Processing HR correspondence as well as checking and managing invoices (tax offices, migration authorities, insurance companies, etc.). Profile
  • Training: Completed basic commercial training (EFZ) or equivalent, ideally supplemented by the certificate as a human resources clerk (HRSE).
  • Experience : First proven professional experience in a similar function in the Swiss personnel administration.
  • Expertise : Sound knowledge of Swiss labour law as well as the Swiss social security system.
  • Languages: Fluent in German and English (oral and written). Knowledge of French is a big plus.
  • IT skills: Confident use of common office tools. Experience with ERP or payroll accounting software (e.g. Abacus, ProConcept, Sage) is a big plus.
  • Personality: Precise and structured way of working, absolute discretion in dealing with sensitive data, strong service orientation and the ability to set priorities confidently even in hectic phases.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
