Full description

Ready to actively drive construction and infrastructure projects at Migros Industrie? If you have experience in planning and construction management of new buildings and conversions and are confident in controlling interfaces, we should get to know each other.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Gränichen, Bäckerstrasse 1, 5722 Gränichen

Important notes

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Management and implementation of investment projects in the field of infrastructure and buildings
  • Planning and implementation of our in-house bakeries throughout Switzerland
  • Planning and construction management activities for new buildings/conversions such as calculation, feasibility assessment, tendering, awarding, coordination
  • Conducting sophisticated analyses and meetings with the responsible parties
  • Collaboration in the budget preparation in the area of infrastructure/construction

Props

  • In-depth experience: in the field of infrastructure and buildings
  • Completed studies (university/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of construction, infrastructure or building technology (HVAC)
  • Experience in project management
  • Wide-ranging interest in infrastructure and building technology topics
  • Experience with CAD drawing tools
  • Knowledge in the field of sustainability, energy and energy management
  • Flexible and team-oriented person with a proactive, solution-oriented way of working and a customer-oriented, holistic way of thinking and acting
  • Willingness to travel occasionally within our locations in Switzerland
  • German (fluent)
  • French (fluent)
  • Italian (an advantage)
  • English (an advantage)

Cosa offriamo

  • Employee offers: Cumulus employee points, discounted mobile subscriptions, higher interest rates Migros Bank, club school/fitness fees and more.
  • Occupational pensions: Comprehensive protection in the Migros Pension Fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Flexible working hours with the option of working from home.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for everyone, 6 weeks holiday from 50. Age year, 7 weeks holiday from 60. Age year, purchase of additional vacation days possible.
  • Training and further education: We actively train employees as part of internal programs and, if necessary, support external training with time and money benefits.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks of paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).

Contatto

Application & Contact Carmen Peluso HR Recruiter

Recruitment Process

  • Pre-selection of applications (FFB-Group): We review received applications on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release it to the manager for viewing. The decision on whether to advance or leave the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail.
  • Personal video or telephone interview (FFB Group): If there is a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video interview. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates better within a short period of time in order to invite suitable people for the next round.
  • Discussion with HR & specialist department (FFB Group): The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. The main aim is to learn more about personnel and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit of the company and team.
  • Offer & Contract Issuance (FFB-Group): After our verbal job offer, the most important points of the contract are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post.
