Full description

Are you looking for an exciting and varied challenge? Then you are exactly right with us on station KPP-5.

The KPP-5 station of the Center for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (ZPPS) is a special unit focused on the treatment of patients (aged 18 to 65) with a psychiatric disorder and complex psychosocial challenges, with a focus on recovery and psychosocial needs.

We are looking for you immediately or by agreement as Tasks: • Administrative work around the patient and support of the station management • Processing of e-mail and telephone requests • Taking over of medical-technical tasks • Updating patient data in the clinic information system (ORBIS) and in other software applications • Support for the introduction of new employees according to station-internal specifications • Collection and processing of data for quality control • Main responsibility for ordering and logistical tasks within the station as well as primary contact person for the system-relevant interfaces • Minutes of meetings Requirements • Completed training as a medical assistant or health specialist, preferably with experience in practice administration • Empathic attitude in dealing with people in a crisis situation • Ability to deal with common office applications • Organizational talent with attention to the overall result • Networked thinking and an independent working method • Resilience and reliability • Teamwork and enthusiasm for multi-professional collaboration • High service orientation towards internal and external customers We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. This is why we offer you a lot: Further education is strongly encouraged here. We also invest heavily in team cohesion and offer all our teams access to the software "Moodtalk", which strengthens team communication. In addition, we offer attractive discounts for well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport will receive a handsome eco-bonus at the end of the year. In addition, we offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy holidays.
