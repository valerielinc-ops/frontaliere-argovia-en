Full description

  • Carry out professional maintenance of operational and training materials as well as removal, storage and relocation thereof and provision according to existing regulations and instructions
  • Receive goods and military equipment, check for completeness and quality and correctly record inventory changes in the SAP system
  • Order material, prepare shipments on schedule and carry out packaging and dispatch work independently
  • Carry out warehouse maintenance work and inventories in the area of material management and report deviations according to specifications
  • Assisting members of the army and civilian personnel in taking over and taking back operational and training materials and providing competent support to customers in the assigned area
  • Consistently comply with regulations on occupational safety and environmental protection and contribute to efficient and orderly storage operations
  • Completed vocational training as a logistician or equivalent
  • Have good IT and SAP user knowledge and reliably carry out bookings in the system
  • Show pleasure in working with payers, provide competent support for military and civilian customers and be versatile
  • Category B driving licence and an SUVA recognised forklift R1/R2 required
  • Driving licences of categories C and CE are advantageous
  • Working independently, assuming responsibility and consistently complying with safety regulations
