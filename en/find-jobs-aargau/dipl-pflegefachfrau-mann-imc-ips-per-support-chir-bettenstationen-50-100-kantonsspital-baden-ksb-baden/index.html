Full description

Varied assignments, close exchange in the team and a central support function in late service. Sounds exciting?

You want to contribute your advanced expertise without taking responsibility for your own patient group? Then we have exactly the right task for you. Support our late-duty surgical bed stations where your support is most needed. Become a valuable point of contact for the entire team.

Their tasks

  • On-demand expertise: You support the team punctually in acute situations and work peaks.
  • Eagle Eye: You recognize critical changes lightning fast and act immediately.
  • Team booster: You keep your back free and ensure smooth shift operation
  • Flex late service: Your service hours (between 14:45 and 23:09) are defined together from Monday to Friday. Individual day services ensure quality.
  • Safe Start: In order to start fully, you will be thoroughly trained for at least 80% for 3 months.

Your profile

  • The Foundation: Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH with advanced expertise (IMC course / vocational trainer or NDS intensive care / emergency care)
  • The character: Flexible, decisive, strong in appearance and absolutely stress resistant.
  • The working method: Independent, team-oriented and assertive

Why us? We offer you the freedom to really contribute your expertise and actively shape processes. Here you are heard, appreciated and promoted.
