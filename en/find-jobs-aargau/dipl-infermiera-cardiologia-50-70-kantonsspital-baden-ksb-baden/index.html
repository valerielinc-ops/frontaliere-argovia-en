Full description

In cardiology, you take on as a Dipl. Nurse HF has a responsible role in the care and support of our patients. In close cooperation with doctors, doctors and other specialists, you make an important contribution to high-quality care. With your structured, sensitive and professional way of working, you ensure a smooth treatment process and contribute significantly to the well-being of our patients.

Their tasks

  • They prepare outpatient and inpatient patients
  • They participate in cardiological examinations such as ECG, TEE, stress echocardiographies, Reveal implantations, stress tests and eKVs.
  • They perform venous blood sampling and place peripheral venous catheters.
  • They carefully prepare pacemaker controls.
  • They keep the care documentation carefully and fully record the care services provided.
  • You actively support a smooth and efficient process of cardiological consultation hours

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF (foreign diplomas: SRC recognition).
  • They ideally bring professional experience in cardiology.
  • You convince with your established personality and a pronounced social competence.
  • They also keep an overview in challenging or unforeseen situations and act in a solution-oriented manner.
  • They are characterized by a high service orientation as well as a professional and appreciative treatment of patients and their relatives.
