Full description

Do you not only want to accompany products, but also make them marketable worldwide? At Delica you bring your know-how in food law and actively design declarations and specifications. Place of work: Buchs AG, Birsfelden or Meilen AG.

Mansioni

  • You check recipes for legal compliance in the national and international context
  • You create and review declarations and product and customer specifications
  • You control package texts according to food law requirements
  • You create and manage customer documentation and ensure its timeliness
  • You handle customer and consumer inquiries competently and solution-oriented

Requirements

  • 1 - 2 years: Practice in a similar function at an export-oriented food manufacturer
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): as a food engineer (ETH/FH) or comparable qualification in food law
  • Knowledge of Swiss & EU food law, ideally international markets and SAP
  • Analytical, structured working method combined with high solution orientation
  • Strong service orientation, initiative and enjoyment of a dynamic environment
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)
