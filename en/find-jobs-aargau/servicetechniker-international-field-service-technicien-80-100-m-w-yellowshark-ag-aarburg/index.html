Future employer

For our business partner, an internationally active Swiss industrial company, we are looking for an independent and customer-oriented individual as an International Service Technician. yellowshark AG will support you personally and transparently on your path to a long-term permanent position.

02

Your responsibilities

  • Conducting and leading assembly and commissioning assignments at international customer sites
  • Installation, testing and handover of machines and systems on site
  • Supporting local service partners with maintenance, repair and troubleshooting work
  • Carrying out electrical conversions, extensions and technical modifications to existing systems
  • Training and instructing customers and representatives' personnel in the use of the machines
  • Analyzing and rectifying technical faults directly at the customer's site
  • Close cooperation with internal specialist departments on technical feedback and improvements

Your profile

  • Completed basic technical training as an electrical installer EFZ, automation technician EFZ, automotive mechatronics technician EFZ, polymechanic EFZ or similar
  • Good knowledge of electrical engineering, mechanics and fault analysis
  • Experience in mechanical engineering, assembly or technical service is an advantage
  • Strong customer orientation and a professional demeanour
  • High willingness to travel and enthusiasm for assignments abroad
  • Very good German skills and good English skills for international assignments are essential
