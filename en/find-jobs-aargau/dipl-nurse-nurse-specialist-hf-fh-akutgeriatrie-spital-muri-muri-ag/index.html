Full description

With heart, expertise and empathy - your role in acute care medicine You are looking for a task that makes sense and in which you can fully contribute your skills and empathy? Then become part of our dedicated team in acute care medicine and accompany older patients on their way to greater independence and quality of life.

Their tasks

  • Holistic care: They accompany acutely ill elderly people from entry to discharge, with the aim of restoring their independence step by step
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Together with doctors, therapists (physio, ergo, logo) and the social service you ensure an optimal, holistic treatment
  • Complex care: You apply your expertise in acute care, palliative care and rehabilitative care in a targeted manner and continuously develop it further
  • Co-creation: You actively contribute your ideas to nursing development and interdisciplinary case discussions
  • Support & guidance: You accompany learners and students in everyday life and pass on your knowledge with pleasure

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH (for foreign diploma with SRC recognition)
  • Experience: Professional experience in acute care or ideally in acute care or long-term care
  • Social Competence: Empathetic, resilient and communicative personality with a heart for older people
  • Expertise: High level of expertise in care planning, assessment and documentation
  • Working method: self-employment, sense of responsibility and the ability to prioritize in dynamic situations

Why you are right with us Every day in acute care, you experience how effective care can be. You see how mobility, independence and quality of life of your patients directly improve – and how small advances are of great importance. The moment someone can return home or visibly recover makes your work meaningful and motivating at the same time.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented if possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 60-100 %
