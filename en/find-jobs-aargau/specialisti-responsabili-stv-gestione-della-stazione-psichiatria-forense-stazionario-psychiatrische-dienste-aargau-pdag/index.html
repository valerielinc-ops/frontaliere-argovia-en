Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry (KFP)

For the Center Forensic Psychiatry Stationary (ZFPS) we are looking for you as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. Over the area

The Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry (KFP) consists of the expert opinion centre, the Center for Forensic Psychiatry Stationary (ZFPS) and the Center for Forensic Psychiatry Outpatient (ZFPA). The expert body produces a large number of expert opinions for administrative contracting authorities every year. The ZFPS comprises six wards with a total of 74 beds, on which mentally ill offenders are treated within the framework of court-ordered therapeutic measures (Art. 59 StGB) and crisis interventions are carried out on patients from prisons and remands. The ZFPA runs a forensic ambulatory and offers a prison psychiatric consiliary and liaison service.
