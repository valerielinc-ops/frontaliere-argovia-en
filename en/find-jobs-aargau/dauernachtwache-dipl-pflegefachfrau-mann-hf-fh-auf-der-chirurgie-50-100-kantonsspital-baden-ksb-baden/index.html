Full description

Are you looking for a job where you work exclusively on night work?

At KSB you design care with heart, mind and team spirit. On our surgery, she works hand in hand, taking responsibility and creating a working atmosphere in which you can feel comfortable and develop.

Are you looking for a meaningful task, a strong team and space for self-initiative and want to work exclusively on night work? Then you are exactly right with us.

We look forward to meeting you!

Their tasks

  • Empathic patient care: You look after our patients professionally, attentively and sensitively during the night and are a reliable contact person for their concerns.
  • Quality assurance: You consistently implement the individual care plans and ensure high-quality and patient-oriented care during the night.
  • Proactive responsibility: As part of our Lean Management, you take responsibility for your area and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes.
  • Innovative participation: Your ideas are in demand! Together, we continue to develop shift operations and strengthen interdisciplinary cooperation.

Your profile

  • Education: You bring a degree as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH (for foreign diplomas: SRK recognition).
  • Experience: If you already have experience in acute care, this is your ticket at the top of our list.
  • Safety at night: You like to work independently, be responsible and keep a cool head even in challenging situations.
  • Team spirit: Respectful and supportive cooperation is important to you. Also as a permanent night watch you see yourself as an integral part of the team and contribute to a good cooperation.
  • Desire factor: We take your needs seriously! Tell us what is important to you so that together we can find the best solution for your work-life balance.
