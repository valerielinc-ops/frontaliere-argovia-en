Product Manager Garden — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the operational and economic development of our garden assortment of tools and smart gardening including mowing robots via watering and planting up to care and seeds
  • Control of your product ranges with regard to turnover, contribution margin and budget targets as well as derivation of suitable measures
  • Independent support and negotiation with manufacturers and suppliers as well as evaluation of new partners and competitive sources of supply
  • Continuous monitoring of market and garden trends in order to identify new products, innovations and market opportunities at an early stage and use them for your range
  • Implementation of the pricing strategy across all sales channels and further development of an attractive and competitive product portfolio
  • Co-design of marketing and communication priorities and their implementation together with marketing and sales
  • Close cooperation with purchasing in procurement strategy, availability of goods and control of warehouse upper limits and target ranges

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training (EFZ) preferably in the field of trade or e-commerce including further education or studies in the field of marketing, product management, purchasing or business administration
  • 2–3 years of professional experience in product management or in a comparable activity and ideally knowledge of the commercial or e-commerce environment
  • enthusiasm and a good sense of garden products, trends and innovations
  • Practical product or industry knowledge in the garden sector is an advantage
  • Experience in dealing with manufacturers and suppliers, combined with communication strength and strong negotiating skills
  • An independent and efficient working method as well as initiative, analytical thinking and entrepreneurial action
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, good knowledge of English and pleasure in making a difference together in a team

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
