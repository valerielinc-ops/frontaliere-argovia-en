The Position Roche Kaiseraugst is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network. Over 1900 people are employed here. The site is home to Roche’s largest and most innovative packaging operations worldwide. Through independent cooperation in diverse teams, we pursue our vision: the best possible patient care at low cost for society!

In the Solids DP manufacturing unit, we take care of the production and packaging of various solid drug forms at the production sites in Kaiseraugst and Basel. These include film tablets, capsules and granules for the production of oral suspensions.

The possibility

In the Manufacturing Unit Solids DP, we offer an internship in the Technical Asset Care Group (workshop), which is primarily aimed at students from the fields of pharmaceutical technology, mechanical engineering, life sciences or engineering. During the internship, you will support the Technical Unit at the Kaiseraugst production plant. You will learn how to handle and implement the extensive GMP regulations of the pharmaceutical industry.

In this position, you will

  • Independently work on smaller projects (technical and procedural issues).
  • Establish and revise the standard operating procedures in the area of maintenance, troubleshooting.
  • Support the optimization of different processes.
  • Working with SAP to recreate, adjust and delete maintenance plans and task lists.
  • Clarify questions about the maintenance of various plants.
  • Support the team in changes and measures (corrective actions) as well as in routine activities.
  • Be the contact person for various interfaces and contact persons.

Who you are

The following competencies round off your profile

  • You are currently enrolled at a university or university of applied sciences, or you have completed your degree within 12 months before the start of the internship, in the field of pharmaceutical engineering, mechanical engineering, life sciences, engineering, pharmaceutical management & production or related directions.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English.
  • A high degree of initiative and independent working methods as well as fast comprehension.
  • Interested in constructive cooperation with internal and external stakeholders as well as working in a dynamic team.
  • Good communication skills and presentation skills.
  • Good IT knowledge in office applications.

Additional information

  • The duration of the internship is 6 - 12 months and takes place at the Kaiseraugst location.
  • Please clearly indicate in your motivation letter the desired start date and the duration of the internship. Applications must include a CV and a letter of motivation, both of which are summarized in one document, as well as an up-to-date performance overview.
  • Please state clearly in your motivation letter or CV on which date you completed the university, if this has already happened.
  • Due to regulations, citizens outside the EU/EFTA have to prove with a document from their university that it is a mandatory internship, specify its exact length and present a certificate of enrollment.
  • This internship is not suitable for a bachelor's or master's thesis.
