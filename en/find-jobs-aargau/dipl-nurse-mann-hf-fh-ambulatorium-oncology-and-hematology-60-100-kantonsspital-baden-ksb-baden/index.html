Full description

A position becomes vacant in the Ambulatorium für Oncology/Hematologie. That is why we are looking for an attentive person to look ahead with us immediately or by agreement. We look forward to meeting you Their tasks:

  • You guarantee a competent and specialized care and individual care and advice of our oncological / hematological patients
  • You work independently, administer the prescribed antitumor therapies and conduct the consultations
  • You can support the care on the bed stations with your professional competence
  • Your assessments take place in the oncological ambulatories in the cantonal hospital of Baden as well as at our location in Brugg
  • You are interested in the further development of oncological care and are actively involved
  • In order for the daily routine to work smoothly, you work actively in the interprofessional team Your profile:
  • Diploma as Dipl. HF/FH nurse (foreign diplomas: SRC recognition)
  • Two years of professional experience in acute care
  • Experience in oncology / hematology with appropriate further training is desired or you are ready to complete a specialist training in oncological care
  • Flexible, empathetic and motivated personality
  • An exact, independent and organized working method is a matter of course for you Applications for this post shall be received from staff providers.
