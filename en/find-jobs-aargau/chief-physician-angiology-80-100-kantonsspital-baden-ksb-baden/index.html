Full description

You will take over the management of an excellently networked department throughout Switzerland and shape the future of angiology at KSB. Together with vascular surgery and interventional radiology, you will develop an interdisciplinary vascular center that has grown over 20 years. With the Vascular Access Center, the close cooperation with a large oncology and the fourth largest nephrology in Switzerland as well as the positioning as LE&RN Referral Network Center of Excellence for lymphatic diseases, the KSB has an exceptional network throughout Switzerland and a very broad range of interventions. Together with the current holder, you will shape a structured handover and assume this key role as part of a long-term succession from July 2027.

Their tasks

  • You take over the strategic, medical and organizational management of angiology and strategically develop the department.
  • They ensure high-quality, modern and patient-centered angiological care.
  • They continue and expand the long-standing interdisciplinary cooperation with vascular surgery and interventional radiology.
  • They are regularly active in the catheter laboratory (at least 2 days per week) and actively participate in the planned expansion of capacities and services.
  • They ensure the care of the entire spectrum of arterial, venous and lymphocytic vascular diseases and develop this continuously.
  • They are responsible for specialized procedures such as endovenous therapies, sclerotherapy and phlebectomies.
  • They develop the Vascular Access Center together with oncology and nephrology and strengthen the interdisciplinary interfaces.
  • They work closely with rheumatology in performing capillary microscopy to detect vasculitides.
  • They continue to position the KSB as LE&RN Referral Network Center of Excellence for Lymphatic Diseases.
  • They are involved in teaching, research and further education and promote the professional development of the field.

Your profile

  • You have a specialist title FMH Angiology or an equivalent qualification.
  • They have broad clinical experience across the spectrum of angiology as well as proven expertise in non-invasive and interventional angiology.
  • A habilitation is desired and your commitment to teaching, research and further education is shown accordingly.
  • They have proven leadership and management skills and develop organizations and teams sustainably.
  • You enjoy the interdisciplinary cooperation and would like to strategically develop an already technically excellent department.
  • They think entrepreneurially, act economically and actively develop sustainable cooperation within and outside the hospital.
