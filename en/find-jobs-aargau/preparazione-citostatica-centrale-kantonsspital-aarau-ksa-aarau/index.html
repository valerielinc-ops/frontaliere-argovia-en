Full description

Tasks

  • You will become a member of the Hospital Pharmaceuticals Cytostatic Manufacturing Team. They produce computer-assisted patient-specific chemotherapies under aseptic conditions according to GMP specifications.
  • In addition, preparatory work for production, administrative work in the area of warehouse management, microbiological monitoring of clean rooms and cooperation in the preparation of quality documents are part of your area of responsibility.
  • You also participate in emergency and Saturday missions.

Requirements

  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship as a pharmacist, drugist, laboratory technician or similar (pharmaceutical technology, food technology, health or nurse).
  • Ideally, you already have initial experience in aseptic drug production or in dealing with cytostatics.
  • They have a high degree of responsibility and are reliable and resilient.
  • Joy at work and integration into a team are a matter of course for you.

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
