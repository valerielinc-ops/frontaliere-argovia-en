Full description

You want to lead a team, take on responsibility and at the same time help shape the production of tomorrow? As Team Leader Butterei 100% you get exactly this leeway with us: You lead your team close to everyday life, bring ideas and actively develop processes, plants and processes. You are not just part of the production, but you shape it. If you enjoy technology, enjoy moving people forward and want to make a difference together, this could be your next challenge at Emmi.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility for your area in the butter egg and together with your team ensure a smooth production process.
  • With your eye for the whole, you coordinate projects, keep an eye on quality, hygiene and safety and ensure that everything runs smoothly.
  • You lead and develop your team, motivate your employees and create an environment in which you achieve strong performance together.
  • Your ideas are in demand: You question existing processes and actively drive improvements in the areas of processes, efficiency and technology.
  • In the case of production challenges, you are an important contact person and will find sustainable solutions together with your team.
  • Are you excited about process and control technology? Perfect. With us you can contribute your know-how and actively develop our plants and processes.

You bring that with you

  • A completed training as a dairy or food technologist EFZ forms your professional basis. Ideally, you have supplemented your knowledge with a suitable training at level HF or FS1/2.
  • Experience from a comparable production environment helps you to find your way quickly. First leadership experience is beneficial.
  • Accompanying, motivating and developing people together gives you pleasure.
  • You are happy to take on responsibility and at the same time appreciate the freedom to independently shape your area.
  • With your solution-oriented and thinking nature, you recognize potential for improvement and actively contribute your own ideas.
  • Even in a dynamic production day, you keep an overview and convince with an open, communicative way.
  • SAP skills support you in everyday work, and you can also communicate safely in German.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
