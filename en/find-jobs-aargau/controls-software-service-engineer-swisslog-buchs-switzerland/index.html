Controls Software Service Engineer (Siemens) (m/f/d) Where do people love what they do and are great in it? At Swisslog, of course! We provide performance and efficiency for our customers all over the world and thus give them the decisive advantage in their markets.

  • Contact person for our customers as well as 2nd and 3rd level support (resote or on site)

  • Self-employed work in project teams, creation of customer specifications and plant documentation
  • Further development of standard solutions
  • Performing commissioning and plant tests at home and abroad

Applicant profile You bring with you

  • Completed basic technical training with further training to become technician (HF), engineer (FH) or several years of experience in automation technology
  • A well-founded knowledge of PLC programming in the following programming languages: SCL, AWL and FUP (Safety)
  • Experience with WinCC, Siemens TIA Portal and Siemens Step 7 Classic is desirable
  • Piket-service ready (every 5-6 weeks)
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • French skills are of benefit
  • Travel readiness (20 %) for local operations mainly in the CH/DE area
  • Flair for object-oriented programming
  • Team-oriented personality
