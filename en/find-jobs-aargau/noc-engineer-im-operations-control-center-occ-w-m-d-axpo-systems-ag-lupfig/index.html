Full description

Pensum 80-100 %

In our Operations Control Center (OCC) we bundle NOC, SOC and Service Desk under one roof – the central hub for the 24/7 operation of critical infrastructures – completely “Swiss made”. As a NOC Engineer, you bring your expertise and commitment to successful implementation and implementation to Axpo Systems. We are looking for personalities with a flair for network and IT technologies that are motivated, inquisitive and solution-oriented. You bring curiosity, initiative and joy of learning, are communicative, interested and do not wait passively for specifications, but actively shape.

Tasks and responsibilities

  • You monitor the technical facilities and services of Axpo Systems around the clock (7x24h) and detect faults early on
  • You monitor and analyze security-relevant events, identify vulnerabilities and take appropriate corrective action
  • As part of the incident process, you coordinate with internal and external bodies in the event of a disruption and make clear communication with customers, customers and partners more secure.
  • You document incidents and measures in our ticketing and management systems and keep them up to date
  • You can help with vulnerability management and malware prevention in our OT and IT environments
  • You support the SOC analyst (Tier 1) to protect our critical infrastructure from internal and external threats

Profile and skills

  • You have completed training as a computer scientist or a comparable qualification
  • You have good knowledge of network technology – ideally supplemented by an appropriate certification
  • Experience in networking and monitoring is an advantage
  • You communicate safely in German and English, knowledge of French is an advantage
  • You are team-oriented and enjoy the exchange and cooperation
  • You are characterized by a solution-oriented, resilient and independent working method as well as high personal responsibility
  • You are ready to do shift work

You work at Axpo Systems, an independent subsidiary of the Axpo Group. We offer telecommunications, cyber security and data center solutions for operators of critical infrastructures in Switzerland.
