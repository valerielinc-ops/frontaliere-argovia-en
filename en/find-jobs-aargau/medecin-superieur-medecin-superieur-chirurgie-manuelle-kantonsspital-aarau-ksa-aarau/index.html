Clinic for Plastic Surgery and Hand Surgery

Job: By arrangement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • You take over the work in the operating room as an independent surgeon for hand-surgical emergencies and the entire elective spectrum of hand-surgery. For complex instruction procedures, the clinic management is at your disposal depending on the level of training (replantation, peripheral nervous surgery, microsurgery).
  • They look after outpatient and inpatient patients.
  • Together with an assistant doctor, you take on presence services on weekends and at night.
  • Together with the leading doctors and the senior doctors, you are responsible for the supervision and further education of our assistant doctors.

Requirements

  • You have completed specialist medical training (with MEBEKO recognition) or are about to graduate from the Swiss specialist in hand surgery
  • Passed FESSH exam or shortly before exam completion.
  • You have very good knowledge of German.
  • It distinguishes a high level of teamwork, resilience, social skills, good communication skills and the willingness to take on responsibility.

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
