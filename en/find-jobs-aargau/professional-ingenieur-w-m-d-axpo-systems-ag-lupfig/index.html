Full description

Pensum 80-100 %

As an independent subsidiary of the Axpo Group, Axpo Systems AG is the competence center for telecommunications, cyber security and data center solutions in the Group and third-party customers.

Our customers have a system-relevant order and are in the segment of critical infrastructures. They only know the highest possible standards, because they operate the system-relevant services of Switzerland. This defines the standards of profession, reliability, quality and safety standards and the technical solution competence for us uncompromisingly high – because our performance only enables the crisis-proof execution of these indispensable services.

You are shaping the future of our network infrastructure. With us, you combine practical know-how with an eye for new technologies and actively promote network automation. We look forward to your ideas.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Design and implementation of complex L2/L3 network infrastructures
  • Cooperation in existing and new network concepts and their operation
  • Development and operation of automation solutions
  • Trouble analysis and correction in the Daily Business and 7x24-Pikett
  • Training of internal employees, operations, field support and customers

Profile and Requirements

  • Completed training as computer scientist EFZ, HF, FH or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of practical experience in the network area
  • In-depth knowledge of protocols for Wan and Lan networks
  • Experience in network automation, ideally with Python and Ansible
  • Personal responsibility, solution-oriented working method and willingness to learn new things
  • Organisational capacity, flexibility, service orientation and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
