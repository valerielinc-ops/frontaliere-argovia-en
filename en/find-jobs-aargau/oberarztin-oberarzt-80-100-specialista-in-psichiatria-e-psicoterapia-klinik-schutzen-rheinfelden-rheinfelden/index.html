Senior Physician (80–100%) | Specialist in Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Attentive and competent. We are a private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds in a central location in Rheinfelden and two outpatient clinics in Rheinfelden and Aarau. The clinic is recognised by the FMH as a further training centre for the specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years). Our treatments focus on individual and group psychotherapies. We design the therapeutic environment in an interprofessional team in a resource-oriented manner, taking into account physical and creative therapeutic offers. We attach great importance to somatic and psychosocial aspects.
