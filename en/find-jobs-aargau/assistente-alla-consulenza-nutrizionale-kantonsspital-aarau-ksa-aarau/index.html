Full description

Job opening: From 01.03.2027 or by agreement indefinitely

Tasks

  • They support the nutrition consulting team in consultation hours and contribute to patient-oriented care
  • They perform BIA measurements as part of delegated tasks and document the results according to a uniform standard
  • They take on administrative tasks in the context of nutritional advice and patient care
  • You participate in the planning and coordination of the nutritional advice services

Requirements

  • Completed training as a med. practice assistant (MPA), med. Secretary or a comparable qualification in health care
  • Enjoyment of contact with patients and willingness to be familiar with the implementation and documentation of BIA measurements
  • Careful, independent and reliable working method
  • Safe handling of digital applications and enjoyment of cooperation in the interprofessional team
  • Professional experience in the hospital or in a medical practice is beneficial
