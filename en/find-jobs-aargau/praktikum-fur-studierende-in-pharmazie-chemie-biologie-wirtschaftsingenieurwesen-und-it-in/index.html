The Position Cross-functional collaboration in agile teams, respectful interaction with each other and the open Roche culture will also inspire you to realize your full potential every day. You will be supported by your manager and your colleagues. Together we overcome obstacles and use learning experiences. In your role, you are part of the “QC Digital Backbone” section in Kaiseraugst. The QC Digital Backbone section deals, among other things, with the planning, preparation and introduction of new, global IT systems in the QC, the master data management of QC data and digitalization projects.

The possibility

  • Participation in the local implementation of global Roche projects in QC
  • Support for the collection and analysis of relevant data
  • Support in the coordination of project activities in the QC
  • Support for collecting and updating master data
  • Support in the planning and implementation of trainings & workshops
  • Support in communicating project activities to the organization (e.g. via newsletters, information events or G-Site)
  • Administrative support of the project team

Who you are

  • As a successful intern in the QC Digital Backbone section, you are enrolled at a university or university of applied sciences or have completed your studies within 12 months in the fields of pharmacy, chemistry, biology, industrial engineering or related fields.
  • You are open to new ideas and question conventional thought patterns. You value working in a performance-driven environment driven by mutual respect, discussion and collaboration.
  • German is a must, English is an advantage

Additional information

  • The preferred start date of the internship is now.
  • Duration: 6 months up to 12 months

Application process

  • For the application, we need a valid enrollment confirmation (if applicable), a letter of motivation and a CV.
  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens* must attach a confirmation from the university that a mandatory internship is part of the training to the application documents.
