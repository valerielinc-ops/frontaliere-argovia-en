Full description

More than an organization – become part of our team. Organization is in your blood and you keep the overview even when it gets hectic? Perfect! As a station assistant, you are the first point of contact on our private station. You hold the strings together, coordinate the day-to-day work of the station and, together with a well-established team, ensure that our patients feel in good hands. No day is like the other – that’s what makes your job so exciting. Their tasks: Administrative and organizational support of the station day. Implementation of medical-technical and nursing activities by delegation, such as blood sampling, electrocardiograms (ECG), vital sign checks and support in personal care. Close cooperation with the interprofessional team. Co-responsibility for a professional and smooth process on the station. You work exclusively in early service – also on weekends and public holidays.

  • Your profile: Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ or medical practice assistant EFZ. Joy in dealing with people and working together in a team. Independent, reliable and structured working method. Responsibility and a high service orientation. Motivation to actively shape the station everyday life.
