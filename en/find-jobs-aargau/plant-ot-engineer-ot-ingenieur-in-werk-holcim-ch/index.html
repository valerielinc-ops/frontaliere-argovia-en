Full description

Are you passionate about automation, industrial networks and digital technologies? Then design the production of tomorrow with us! As a Plant OT Engineer, you connect IT and production, continuously develop our systems and ensure that our plants remain safe, reliable and sustainable.

What to expect

  • Ensuring the availability, reliability and safety of our industrial control and OT systems (ICS/TIS)
  • Implementation and further development of company-wide standards for automation, data architecture and digital production systems
  • Configuration, optimization and troubleshooting of industrial networks and communication protocols such as Profinet, Modbus and OPC UA
  • Support and further development of control, MES and Historian systems including system upgrades and lifecycle management
  • Ensuring smooth interfaces between production control (DCS/SPS), quality management (LIMS) and SAP
  • Implementation of innovative digitalization solutions as part of our “Plant of Tomorrow” initiative to increase efficiency and sustainability
  • Ensuring OT cybersecurity in accordance with IEC 62443 and ensuring data quality, integrity and security
  • Coordination of external service providers as well as technical support of system tests, commissioning and plant acceptance
  • Training and support of our employees in dealing with OT systems as well as promoting internal knowledge transfer
  • Close cooperation with production, maintenance, quality management and IT as well as participation in the Pikett service

What you bring

  • Completed studies or further education as a technician: in electrical, automation, electronics or system engineering
  • At least three years of professional experience in automation or system engineering, ideally in the continuous process or cement industry
  • In-depth knowledge of industrial control and automation systems such as DCS, SCADA, PLC, MES and MSR technology
  • Several years of experience with industrial data systems, networks and communication protocols such as Profinet, Ethernet/IP, Modbus and OPC UA
  • Knowledge of OT cybersecurity (IEC 62443), cloud and IIoT technologies, and ideally experience with Python or JavaScript
  • Strong analytical skills and an independent, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength, team spirit and joy in cross-divisional cooperation as well as in the training and support of employees
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good to very good knowledge of English in word and writing

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful training, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.

  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchases, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
