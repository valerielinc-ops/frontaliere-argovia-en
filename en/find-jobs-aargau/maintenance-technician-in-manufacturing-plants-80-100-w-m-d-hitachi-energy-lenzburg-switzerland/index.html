Role overview

At our BiMOS Backend plant in Lenzburg, we manufacture efficient and high-quality power semiconductors for use in motor control systems and inverter systems.

In this way, we make a decisive contribution to making industry, public and individual transport more energy efficient and, on the other hand, our technology enables the production and transport of renewable energies from the production site to the consumer.

Become part of the energy transition and support us in maintaining and improving the availability of our production facilities.
