Full description

For our customer from the Energy Division, we are looking for a:n experienced:n, motivated:n and open-minded project management officer.

Your tasks

  • Supporting the cyber security team in planning, coordinating and tracking tasks and initiatives
  • Structuring activities into clear tasks, responsibilities, milestones and deadlines
  • Maintenance of task lists, planning, status overviews and relevant project documentation
  • Proactive tracking of open tasks and deadlines and early detection of delays and obstacles
  • Coordination of project and governance meetings including preparation, documentation of decisions and follow-up of measures
  • Support for status reports, management documents and other coordination tasks
  • Participation in the continuous improvement of workflows, structures and cooperation within the team

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in PMO, project management, project coordination or in a comparable function
  • Very good organizational, planning and coordination skills
  • Ability to translate complex or poorly structured topics into clear and actionable tasks
  • Experience in the coordination of various stakeholders and parallel ongoing activities
  • Independent and proactive working methods as well as a strong ability to consistently track tasks, deadlines and commitments
  • Very good communication and social skills as well as a safe and professional handling of different personalities and hierarchical levels
  • Pronounced sensitivity combined with the necessary persistence to advance topics and follow them constructively
  • Very good German (min. C1) and good English skills (min. B2)
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft 365, especially Teams, SharePoint, Excel and PowerPoint
  • Experience in the IT environment is an advantage
  • Knowledge of cyber security is an advantage, but not a requirement
