The entity shall

As an intern in the field of Asset Excellence Unit, Assembly & Packaging Devices at the Kaiseraugst production site, you will support the team in a wide range of areas. In this internship, you will:

  • Get to know the packaging processes (delivery, labelling, packaging)
  • Independently work on sub-projects in close cooperation with various interfaces (e.g. mechanics, electrics, packaging development, quality assurance, validation and engineering)
  • Processing of market components, deviations and changes
  • Track and Represent KPIs
  • Support for root cause analyses in deviation management
  • Support for Quality Risk and Inspection Management
  • Implement adjustments and maintenance of GMP documentation
  • Question and improve processes in the GMP-compliant environment.
  • Be part of a dynamic, powerful team with varied tasks.

Who you are

You are matriculated or have Completed your studies at a university or university of applied sciences, preferably in the field of pharmacy, pharmaceutical technology, biotechnology, process engineering within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a modern working environment as part of an internship of at least 6 months, as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry.

In addition, you round off your profile with the following competencies:

  • You are committed, responsible and resilient
  • You have fun working in interdisciplinary teams and communicate openly
  • You have a solution-oriented attitude and bring analytical and organizational skills
  • You communicate safely in German and English

Additional information

  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must accompany the application with a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training.
  • Part-time employment or the writing of a thesis is not possible at this position.

Application process

  • The preferred start of the internship is in February 2027 or by appointment. Please indicate your preferred start date and the desired internship duration in your application.
  • The application period ends on 9 October 2026, the interviews will take place from 20 to 30. October 2026.
