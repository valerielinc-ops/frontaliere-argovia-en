Full description

About the position In this role, you can expect a challenging and varied task, in which you, as a technically-savvy all-rounder with foresight, strengthen our high-performance laboratory. Together with our test engineers, you will carry out tests with circuit breakers. You are the central contact person and ensure that the operation runs smoothly, our test system with short-circuit generator works technically flawlessly and actively acts in the event of failures.

You can do that.

  • Establishment of the operational test facility
  • Preparation and construction of measurements and tests
  • Operation of the short-circuit generator and other control devices and measuring instruments required for the tests
  • Ensure that the installation runs smoothly in operational test operations
  • Maintenance, extension and renewal of electrical and mechanical equipment in a high-performance laboratory
  • Support for maintenance work and necessary installation inspections (auxiliary switch, generator, etc.)
  • Preparation of documentation on the work carried out and revisions

You bring that with you

  • Completed professional training in an electrotechnical profession
  • Sound knowledge in the safe handling of electricity
  • Professional experience in plant and/or power plant maintenance
  • Good German skills, English skills are advantageous
  • Ideally, knowledge in electrical plant technology in test laboratory operation, as well as in control technology, measurement technology, control technology
  • Experience in handling lifting work platforms and hall cranes is advantageous

More about us Our flexible working methods help you optimize your personal and professional performance and create an environment where all employees can develop and grow their skills.
