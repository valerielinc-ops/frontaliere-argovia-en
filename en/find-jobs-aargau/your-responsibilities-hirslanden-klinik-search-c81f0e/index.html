Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

YOUR TASKS

  • You are an important member of our emergency team and therefore responsible for ensuring that our emergency patients are in the best hands.
  • As a multitasking genius, you juggle between triage, first-line care and patient care.
  • With your team spirit, you contribute to the joint success and ensure an efficient and positive interdisciplinary cooperation with our emergency doctors, the emergency service and the bed departments.
  • In addition, you support new employees and students with the introduction and in the diverse everyday work.
  • We appreciate your willingness to serve in all shifts and on weekends. In service planning, we try to consider all your wishes in return.

Your profile

  • You are an expert in emergency care with passion and pleasure in your profession.
  • Whether you have just completed your education or are already very experienced – you are welcome with us!
  • It is important to us that you are motivated, capable of teamwork and reliable and helps to bring our emergency care to the next level.
  • And that the friendly, sensitive treatment of our patients is just as important to you as the good work-life balance.

Our offer

Maybe we have just the right thing for you! With us you will find a working environment that is characterized by appreciation, team spirit and humor. We offer you a varied job, interesting career and development opportunities, a family environment, at least 25 days of vacation, 4 additional paid days off as compensation for the changing time and many other employment highlights.
