The Position Roche Kaiseraugst is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network. Over 1900 people are employed here. In your role, you are part of the “QC Support” department in Kaiseraugst and support the department’s team. The QC Support department deals, among other things, with stability studies of our commercial products and the lifecycle of systems and equipment in the QC as part of the “Future Readiness” initiative.

The possibility In this role, you become part of our dedicated team in quality control and actively shape future-oriented projects. You can expect varied tasks:

  • Actively support local implementation and support of global projects within Quality Control (QC).
  • Collect, maintain and analyze relevant operational key figures as well as master data for continuous process optimization.
  • Coordinate and accompany project activities in close coordination with the quality control interface teams.
  • Schedule interactive training sessions, workshops and information events and run them together with the team.
  • Design transparent project communication via modern collaboration tools and newsletters to relevant stakeholders.
  • Bring your ideas to the administrative and organizational support of agile project teams.

Who you are

We are looking for curious personalities who like to learn new things and who want to make a difference in the healthcare sector together with openness and team spirit:

  • You are currently enrolled in a college or university or have completed your studies (pharmaceuticals, chemistry, biology, industrial engineering or similar) within the last 12 months.
  • You are enthusiastic about quality and project management and ideally bring initial insights or strong interest in quality control processes.
  • You convince through a structured, self-responsible working method and enjoy data-based and analytical questions.
  • You can safely use common productivity and collaboration tools (e.g. MS Office or Google Workspace) and get to know new digital tools quickly.
  • You communicate fluently in German and English and enjoy working together in a diverse, open team.

Additional information

  • Start date: From February
  • Duration: At least 6 months (extension to up to 12 months possible)
  • Required documents: CV, letter of motivation and certificate of enrolment or Certificate or copy of diploma.
  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must attach a confirmation from the university that a mandatory internship is part of the education to the application documents.
