Full description

Do you love organizing processes and ensuring the highest quality standards? At the same time, you take the lead of a motivated team and ensure that everything goes smoothly in the bottling of chocolate. With your commitment and your eye for detail, you help our customers experience the best moments of enjoyment every day. Together we grow beyond ourselves – be there!

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

Motivation letter? No need – complete employment certificates and diplomas already.

Mansioni

  • You ensure a smooth production process and guarantee compliance with processes, guidelines and legal regulations.
  • You lead a production team of about 20 employees and promote their further development through regular discussions.
  • You are responsible for operational and product hygiene and ensure the highest quality standards.
  • You create and adapt production documentation and actively support the division manager.
  • You drive continuous improvements in your area and set new impulses.

Requirements

  • Min. 5 years of professional experience: several years of experience and leadership skills in the production environment, beneficial in the food industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): apprenticeship as plant manager, food technologist, baker/confectioner (m/f/d) or similar, further education HF desired
  • In-depth knowledge of office tools and PCs (SAP knowledge is an advantage)
  • You are a team player with a strong ability to deal with people.
  • Your way of working is independent, structured and precise.
  • You bring a strong occupational safety and quality awareness.
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
