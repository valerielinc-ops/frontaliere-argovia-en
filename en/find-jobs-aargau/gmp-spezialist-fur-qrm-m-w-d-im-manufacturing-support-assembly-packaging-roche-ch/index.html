The Position As a GMP specialist for Quality Risk Management (QRM) at the Manufacturing Support Assembly & Packaging in Kaiseraugst, you are responsible for the planning, coordination and implementation of Quality Risk Management in the Manufacturing Unit. In addition to risk management, trouble-free and stable production is another focus, which is achieved through independent and continuous improvements.

The possibility

  • As a GMP specialist for QRM, you will take over the responsible planning, preparation, processing and moderation of new quality risk analyses as well as their continuous optimization and periodic review in the area of Assembly & Packaging in Kaiseraugst.
  • In your role, you control the entire QRM process within your scope and always test patient safety and compliance with Roche guidelines and cGMP requirements by specifically identifying, investigating and minimizing risks.
  • You develop and maintain local standard operating procedures (SOPs) for quality risk management and ensure their consistent compliance.
  • As an expert (SME) you represent the area in inspections and audits as well as in the local QRM squad.
  • You will work closely with interdisciplinary experts to accurately revise risk analyses related to deviations and changes.
  • Your responsibility includes the independent processing of changes as a change owner and the professional support of change processes as an SME.
  • Through continuous process improvements, you make a decisive contribution to trouble-free and stable production.

Who you are

  • You bring a completed pharmaceutical, scientific or technical degree or a corresponding vocational training with many years of relevant professional experience.
  • Your expertise includes in-depth knowledge of the QRM process as well as proven experience in creating quality risk analyses.
  • A positively thinking, integrity characterizes you, complemented by a trusting, solution-oriented can-do attitude as well as a lively speak-up and error culture.
  • You combine a strong quality mindset with strong organizational skills, strategic, goal-oriented thinking and a clear end-to-end perspective.
  • Experience in change management enables you to successfully implement change processes, to live flexibility and to motivate your environment sustainably.
  • Initial professional experience in pharmaceutical production, development, quality assurance or control is a valuable asset for this position.
  • Very good communication skills in word and writing in both German and English round off your profile ideally.
