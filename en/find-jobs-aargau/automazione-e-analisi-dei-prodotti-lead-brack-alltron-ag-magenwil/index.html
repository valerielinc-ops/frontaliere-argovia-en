  • In-depth knowledge of data-driven business models, preferably in e-commerce or a comparable digital environment
  • Deep understanding of data processes, data quality, scaling and automation
  • Experience in making analytics usable for operational and strategic decisions
  • Practical knowledge in the use of AI or LLM solutions is a big plus
  • Completed studies at bachelor or master level, ideally in business, business informatics, computer science or data & analytics – or an equivalent qualification through relevant professional experience

  • High will to shape, self-initiative and pleasure in creating structures in an area still to be built
