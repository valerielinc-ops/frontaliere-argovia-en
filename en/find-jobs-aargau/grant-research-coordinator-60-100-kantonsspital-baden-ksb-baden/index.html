Full description

Research and innovation are important components of the KSB. In our research department with an integrated Clinical Trial Unit, we supervise clinical trials from phase II onwards from various specialist areas. In this versatile function, you support the research area in particular in financial and administrative matters. You will work in a research environment with partners such as ETH Zurich, Nexus ETH and the Innovation Hub. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • They independently manage the accounting, prepare SNSF financial reports, take over the invoicing and manage third-party funds.
  • They independently take over the administrative and financial support of research projects funded by Horizon Europe and the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). This includes, in particular, budget monitoring and the preparation of interim and final accounts.
  • They support PI in budgeting and prepare financial reporting in accordance with the applicable requirements.
  • They manage scientific and research staff in cooperation with HR and record personnel mutations.
  • They assume responsibility for budget and resource planning as well as regular reporting to the responsible internal and external bodies.
  • They participate in creating, updating and complying with standards, quality management documentation and SOPs.
  • Depending on your previous experience, you also work in clinical trials.

Your profile

  • You have a degree at a technical college or university.
  • They bring in-depth knowledge of accounting and finance.
  • You have experience in the third-party management of research funding institutions such as the SNSF or Horizon Europe.
  • They have very good knowledge of English and IT.
  • They bring knowledge of study and quality management.
  • They are discreet and work independently and structured.
  • They are capable of teamwork, show initiative and bring with them a willingness to learn.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
