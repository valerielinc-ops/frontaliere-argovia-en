Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Make the Aargauer protected areas fit and robust for the future
  • They coordinate the overall orientation for the derivation and implementation of ecologically sufficient buffers around protected areas (biotopes) of national, cantonal and municipal importance in the canton of Aargau
  • Develop together with those responsible for the protected areas and numerous other stakeholders You have a sustainable implementation practice and implement its implementation
  • They initiate studies and projects to derive and implement different types of buffers (nutrients, hydrological, disturbances, etc.)
  • They advise and inform other specialist bodies as well as the public about the Aargauer protected areas and the necessary ecological buffer

What you bring

  • University or university degree in engineering, in the field of environmental engineering, environmental sciences, biology or comparable disciplines (e.g. agronomy)
  • Experience in the practical nature and landscape protection, especially in the cultivated land
  • Experience and knowledge of direct agricultural payments and contracts
  • High negotiating skills and good communication skills
  • Independent, solution-oriented working method in a team

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. Nature and landscape provide habitat for animals and plants as well as recreational space for people. We in the Landscape and Waters department are committed to responsible development of the landscape, ecological enhancement and networking of diverse habitats for biodiversity, sustainable use of river waters and comprehensive flood protection.
