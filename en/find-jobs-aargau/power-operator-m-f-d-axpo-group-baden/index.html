Full description

Pensume: 100% | Baden

Do you want to take responsibility and actively shape international electricity trading? In this versatile function, you work closely with various internal and external partners and ensure that everything interlocks precisely and smoothly in daily electricity trading.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You coordinate the international electricity trade between internal trading departments, external customers and partners and ensure the correct registration to the network operators.
  • You will support our SPOT traders in the correct timetable handling of their daily business in day-ahead and intraday trading.
  • You create programs for our power plant partners and monitor their compliance.
  • You check power plant measurement data and create periodic billing for various Axpo power plants.
  • You are actively involved in the implementation and optimization of our work processes in the department.

Profile and Requirements

  • You have a commercial education or basic technical education with additional commercial education.
  • Energy experience is an advantage.
  • You are willing to work shifts (even on weekends and public holidays) and to provide piquetting services.
  • You have good knowledge of German and English in word and writing. French or Italian are beneficial.
  • You communicate clearly and safely and enjoy working closely with different internal and external contacts.
  • A precise and reliable way of working is a matter of course for you – even if it gets hectic.
  • You are open to change, enjoy learning new things and appreciate an environment in which the team works hand in hand.

Even if you do not meet all points, we look forward to your application.

About the team

Our team thinks and works interdisciplinary and is closely networked nationally and internationally. In everyday work, we coordinate closely with each other and support each other. You can expect a dynamic, team-oriented environment in which open communication is important and you can actively contribute your strengths.
