Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic-psychotherapeutic clinic in Switzerland. The Department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy comprises 100 inpatient treatment stations and a psychotherapeutic day clinic. Through close cooperation with the somatic specialist areas, we treat people with complex mental and physical diseases holistically. Barmelweid employs more than 750 people. It is located at an altitude of around 800 meters on a clearing in the Aargau Jura and is embedded in a unique environment with a nature park.
