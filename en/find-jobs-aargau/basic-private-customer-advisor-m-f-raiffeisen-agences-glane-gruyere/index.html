Full description

For the Raiffeisen Moléson Bank, a very well established bank in the south of the canton of Fribourg, we are looking for a person as a basic private customer advisor (m/f).

Aufgaben

  • Provide advice on basic benefits such as savings, payments, investments and provision
  • Develop solutions tailored to the client's needs, taking into account his/her personal circumstances
  • Offer a personalized welcome to our clients and guide them according to their needs
  • Analyze, exploit and optimize customer potential through active and continuous monitoring of the Agency's client portfolio, in collaboration with advisors from other segments
  • Develop the current customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Guide and support our clients in the use of digital tools
  • Collaborate on market prospecting measures such as external contact and representation campaigns
  • Completing administrative tasks and internal controls related to the function

Anforderungen

  • Bank training completed or equivalent
  • SAQ certification, an asset
  • Ability to energize listening and communication clients
  • Very good ease and skill in interpersonal relationships, sense of welcome and orientation solutions
  • Personality motivated with a very good team spirit and collaboration
  • Home in the riding is an asset
