Full description

Assistant physicians for psychiatry and psychotherapy (60–100%) Careful and competent. We are a private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds in a central location in Rheinfelden and two outpatients in Rheinfelden and Aarau. The clinic is recognized by FMH as a training centre for the specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years). For our stationary area we are looking for assistant physicians for psychiatry and psychotherapy (60–100%) Early applications are welcome. Mansioni - Your main tasks are the psychotherapeutic case management and the medical care of our patients under supervision.

  • Requisiti - The collaboration in an interprofessional team makes you happy, you have very good team and communication skills. - The activity is also for a colleague or a colleague who wants to deal with psychosomatic questions as part of his/her foreign year.
