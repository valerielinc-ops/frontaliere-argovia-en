Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Hubpünt in Seengen, we are looking for a Dipl. Nurse with an additional function in vocational training 80 % Your tasks

  • Professional and individual care and care of residents on the conceptual basis of the model of promoting process care
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of nursing measures in compliance with the criteria of the nursing process and involving the residents concerned and their caregivers
  • Co-creation of an optimal interdisciplinary cooperation with all participants in the care and care process
  • Assuming shift responsibility in the department as well as house responsibility
  • Careful and seamless management of care documentation
  • Precise recording of care and support services and of care and consumables used
  • Implementation of educational concepts and framework curricula in the housing sector
  • Accompaniment, supervision and assessment of learners from the professions FaGe and AGS

You bring that with you

  • Completed professional training as a qualified nurse (HF, FH, AKP, DNII, DNI, PSYKP) or equivalent diploma recognised by the SRC
  • Experience as a vocational trainer and at best vocational training (vocational trainer course, SVEB or similar)
  • Experience in long-term care and dealing with the elderly
  • User knowledge of the software CareCoach (maintenance documentation) is an advantage
  • Empathetic and resilient personality with high commitment
  • Very good written and oral German skills
