Full description

About the role Sika is a global, innovative company for chemical and technical specialties in the construction and industrial sectors. We act according to ethical principles and promote a working atmosphere in which willingness to perform and mutual appreciation can develop. To strengthen our Payroll team, we are looking for a dedicated, customer-oriented team player at the Birr location in the canton of Aargau.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Wages processing for at least two Sika companies in Switzerland
  • Control and partly mutation of master and wage data up to monthly billing, including creation and booking of DTAs
  • Responsible for correct registration and deregistration at social institutions as well as handling and monitoring of accidents and illnesses
  • Preparation and control of social security accounts including their financial statements
  • Reconciliation of accounts

Your skills and experience

  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally further training in payroll and/or HR
  • Professional experience in the payroll sector and understanding of Swiss social insurance
  • Sound knowledge of SAP/HR and MS-Office
  • Accounting skills in reconciliation
  • Reliable, efficient, thoughtful and independent working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages are advantageous

Why you should come to us

  • Uncomplicated managers who trust and a family atmosphere with a friendly tone make working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit.
  • A fair employment package with good social benefits
  • A 40-hour week
  • Free parking in front of the house

Sika is a great employer – especially for employees who rely on trust in cooperation. We welcome your complete application dossier (CV, certificates, diplomas, etc.).

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
