Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy

In order to reinforce our teams in the ambulatories (locations in Aarau, Baden, Rheinfelden and Wohlen) of the clinic for children and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy, we are looking for you as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy 5000 Aarau Workload: 80 - 100 % 80 - 100 %

Over the area

In the clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy (clinical director Dr. med.) Angelo Bernardon) are treated children and adolescents up to the age of 18. The integrated treatment offer of the clinic includes an outpatient area with a central ambulatory as well as four ambulatory facilities distributed across the canton, a consultancy and liaison service and an inpatient area with an acute care unit, two psychotherapy units, a day clinic and a clinic school. In the teams, clinical specialists from the fields of medicine, psychology, nursing and social pedagogy work and in the clinic school teachers and curative school teachers. The Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy is a teaching hospital of the University of Zurich and a certified continuing education institution category A.

Tasks

  • You work in a committed interdisciplinary team of the ambulatory according to institutional objectives
  • They independently take over the case management and carry out investigations, psychotherapies, crisis interventions and counselling for mentally stressed children and adolescents and their families
  • They carry out psychiatric-psychological examinations with the common instruments (psychopathological assessment, test psychology, medical history, third-party information)
  • They prepare diagnoses and assessments according to the valid diagnostic scheme and, based on this, work out a plan of measures according to technically established, guideline-oriented methods and taking into account the individual circumstances of the children / adolescents.
  • You create the necessary treatment documentation and technical reports and bill the services provided

Profile

  • You have a degree in psychology at master's level
  • You have in-depth clinical training in child and adolescent psychology and have completed or advanced psychotherapeutic training
  • They bring great pleasure to the systemic work with children, adolescents and the reference system.
  • They have a high degree of independence, can identify connections and tackle corresponding solutions.
  • You have your own initiative, good communication skills with the necessary pinch of humor as well as a skilful handling of psychological stresses
  • You are safe in dealing with the common Office applications
