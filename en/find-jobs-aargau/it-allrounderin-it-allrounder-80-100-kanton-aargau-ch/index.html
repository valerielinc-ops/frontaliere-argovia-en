Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • As part of the operational IT service, you work as a contact person for IT concerns of the users of the State Chancellery.
  • You make an important contribution to the first- and second-level support of the applications used in the canton.
  • You set up IT workplaces, supervise hardware and peripherals and repair technical problems.
  • You contribute to the operation, maintenance and further development of the applications used and you get involved in IT and digitalization projects and document solutions and processes for the team.
  • You support updates, software releases and tests and contribute to a stable IT operation.

What you bring

  • Completed IT training (e.g. computer scientist, IT supporter, system technician, or equivalent).
  • Experience in IT support or in a comparable all-rounder function as well as enjoyment of direct contact with users.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft 365 and the management of IT workplaces
  • Experience with ticketing or ITSM systems is an advantage.
  • Interest in different applications and the willingness to familiarize yourself with new systems and tasks.
  • An independent, reliable and service-oriented working method as well as enjoyment of working together in a team.

In order to strengthen the IT service of the State Chancellery, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented personality with a broad IT interest. You support our employees in everyday life, support IT workplaces and applications and contribute to the further development of our IT environment.
