Full description

About the role

  • Evaluation, development and support of potential new and existing injection moulding suppliers
  • Analysis of supplier data to identify trends, potential for improvement and risk mitigation strategies
  • Coordination of internal and customer-related audits at suppliers
  • Responsibility for supplier complaint management
  • Close cooperation with purchasing and engineering in the evaluation and qualification of new suppliers
  • Support in the development and optimization of injection molding materials
  • Responsibility for monitoring and continuous improvement of management systems and ensuring quality performance
  • Regular visits to suppliers with a travel activity of approximately 30-50%

Your skills and experience

  • Completed studies in chemistry, plastics engineering, materials sciences or a comparable technical qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in quality management, ideally in the automotive industry, as well as profound knowledge of the VDA 6.3 standard
  • Knowledge of Automotive Core Tools is an advantage
  • Strong communication skills in dealing with internal and external stakeholders
  • Team-oriented personality
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • French or other foreign languages are beneficial
  • Willingness to travel regularly in the international supplier environment
