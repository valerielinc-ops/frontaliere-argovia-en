Full description

Workload: 80–100%

What you will do

  • Own and continuously develop the product strategy, roadmap and backlog for GenAI tools such as AxpoGPT, Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot
  • Drive adoption across Axpo through user communities, product launches and change initiatives, together with communication and education teams
  • Define and track product and adoption KPIs, turning data and user feedback into concrete improvements
  • Engage with employees and business teams across Axpo to understand their needs and identify relevant AI opportunities
  • Manage vendor relationships, licensing, feature requests and tooling costs together with AI engineers and technology providers
  • Create educational content and deliver workshops, presentations and demos, including to senior leadership
  • Collaborate with Legal, Security and Governance to ensure GenAI products comply with policies, regulations and risk controls.

What you bring & who you are

  • Several years of experience in product management or digital product development, ideally in a large or complex organization
  • Experience working with AI, Generative AI or data-driven products – deep technical expertise is not required
  • A track record of driving adoption of digital products or new ways of working, paired with strong stakeholder-management skills
  • Excellent communication and public-speaking skills, with a strong analytical and user-centric mindset
  • Experience managing vendors, licenses or budgets is a strong advantage, as is awareness of AI governance and data privacy
  • Fluent in English and German
  • Spanish is a plus. Even if you don't meet all the requirements, we look forward to your application.

About the team

You'll join Axpo's Generative AI Competence Center, a cross-functional team accelerating the responsible adoption of AI across the Group. You'll collaborate with AI engineers, Product Owners, business teams and senior stakeholders across Axpo.
