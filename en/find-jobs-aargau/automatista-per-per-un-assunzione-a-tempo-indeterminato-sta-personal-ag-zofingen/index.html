Full description

Technical faults at plants are solved quickly and purposefully. The company offers you clear processes and versatile tasks in everyday life. On behalf of our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Resolving production disruptions at plants in a timely manner
  • Immediate immediate action in case of technical failures
  • Maintain existing electrical installations properly
  • Implement installations including schemes and documents
  • Instruct and accompany operators at the facilities

Requirements

  • EFZ as an electrician, electrician, automatic or automatic technician or equivalent qualification
  • Company electrician permit according to NIV13 or willingness to catch up
  • Practice in dealing with PLC controls S7 and Beckhoff
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Professional experience in Switzerland or a recognised EU country
