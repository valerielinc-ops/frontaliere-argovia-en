Full description

Are you a hands-on leader with experience in electrical manufacturing? Then we offer you the opportunity to lead a motivated team in an innovative industrial environment.

Team leader electrical production (gn)

Your tasks

  • Personal and technical management of electrical production with around 20 employees as well as targeted further development of the team and skills
  • Ensuring day-to-day operational business through forward-looking capacity, scheduling and work planning
  • Taking on a key role in the further development of electrical manufacturing by actively shaping changes in organization and processes as well as introducing modern, digitally supported and efficiency-increasing manufacturing concepts
  • Responsibility for occupational safety, quality, delivery reliability and productivity
  • Close cross-departmental collaboration with engineering, project management, supply chain, quality and external partners

Your profile

  • Training as an automation engineer with further training to become a technician, industrial foreman or a comparable qualification as well as corresponding professional experience
  • Several years of management experience in an industrial production or assembly environment
  • Strong design and implementation skills as well as experience in the further development of organizations, processes or value creation models
  • Practical knowledge of lean management, continuous improvement and ideally the design of manufacturing and value creation concepts
  • Good ERP and IT knowledge as well as experience in the digitalization and further development of industrial production processes
  • Clear, authoritative communication, entrepreneurial thinking and good spoken and written German and English

We offer

  • Attractive compensation package with good social benefits
  • A modern production and technology center for thermal coating from 2027 in Hausen (AG)
  • A responsible key role with great scope for creativity in the further development of electrical production
  • Varied work in an internationally oriented high-tech company and close collaboration across departmental boundaries
  • Open corporate culture, a committed team and prospects for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive and future-oriented working environment

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in materials science engineering. With the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating systems, components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, longevity and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to end users, but are mission-critical to the industries that enable modern life: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical devices, luxury goods, tooling, automotive and general industrial. Oerlikon is headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, operates in 38 countries and employs around 10,000 people worldwide. In 2025, Oerlikon achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of job profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and develop their full potential - guided by our core values ​​of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
