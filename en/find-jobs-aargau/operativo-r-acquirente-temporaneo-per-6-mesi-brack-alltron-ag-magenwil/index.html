Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for operational orders and their ongoing monitoring
  • Cooperation in order proposals, forecasts and open orders
  • Support for optimization of prices, purchase costs and order quantities
  • Checking order confirmations, delivery dates and item data
  • Direct exchange with suppliers for questions about orders and deliveries
  • Cooperation in ensuring a reliable flow of goods
  • Maintenance of purchase, supplier and article data in our systems
  • Processing of returns, credits and other purchasing topics
  • Insight into different processes and interfaces around purchasing and distribution
  • Possibility to contribute your own ideas and to optimize existing processes

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training and ideally a commercial or business education
  • First practical experience in purchasing, planning, scheduling or in a comparable commercial environment
  • Interest in procurement, flows of goods and operational purchasing processes
  • Secure German in Word and Writing
  • English skills are beneficial
  • Good MS Office knowledge and pleasure in working with different systems
  • Analytical thinking and a good sense of numbers and relationships
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Openness, learning new things and taking on responsibility
  • Enjoy working with suppliers and internal teams

We offer

  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
