Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that’s what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs over 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the group, and the trend is rising. The company's headquarters are in the canton of Glarus. We produce our fresh chocolates here against a picturesque Alpine backdrop and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division includes over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again.
