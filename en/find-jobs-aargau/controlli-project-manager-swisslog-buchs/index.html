Full description

Controls Project Manager Where do people love what they do and are great at it? At Swisslog, of course! Our teams are at the heart of a world-leading data- and robot-driven company that harnesses the power of technology to shape the future of intralogistics. The result? We deliver performance and efficiency to our customers around the world, giving them the decisive advantage in their markets. Job description Here you do something

You take over the independent project planning and handling of small to medium-sized projects in the area of controls and automation. In doing so, you will accompany our customers from the initial acceptance of requirements through planning and coordination to successful implementation and project completion. For the technical implementation, you work closely with our internal specialists and external partners.

  • Responsibility for the technical and commercial handling of automation and controls projects from the offer phase to the successful handover
  • Analysis of customer requirements as well as development and coordination of technical solution concepts with internal specialists
  • Creation of offers, calculations and planning of resources, dates and costs
  • Control and coordination of all project participants, including customers, suppliers, subcontractors and internal specialist units
  • Organisation and support of tests, acceptances and commissioning as well as ensuring compliance with quality and safety standards
  • Ensure complete project documentation as well as follow-up calculations and continuous optimizations

Applicant profile This is what you bring with you

  • Basic technical training with experience in planning and implementing technical projects, ideally in an industrial environment
  • Good knowledge of control and automation technology as well as a strong technical understanding
  • Organizational, structured and solution-oriented personality with a high level of initiative
  • Experience in coordinating projects, resources and various stakeholders
  • Communication strength and pleasure in customer contact
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Willingness to travel and knowledge of French are beneficial
