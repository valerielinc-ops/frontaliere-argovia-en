Full description

Our Clinic for Sleep Medicine (KSM) in Baden is a specialized institution for the interdisciplinary care of patients with sleep disorders. Our goal: sustainable help for restful sleep and improved quality of life. With a wide range of treatments and our multi-professional team, we offer evidence-based medicine at the highest level.

Your tasks •

  • Sleep diagnostics: You perform computer-assisted sleep tests such as polysomnography, MSLT, MWT, polygraphy, pulse oximetry, actimetry, etc. by themselves and cast them
  • Patient care: Support our outpatient patients with the instruction and use of medical devices and prepare them for the examination night in late service
  • Therapy and Diagnostics: Accompany the discontinuation and monitoring of CPAP therapies in patients with sleep-related breathing disorders

Your profile

  • Training: Completed medical training (diplomatic nurse, FaGe, MPA), certificate for neurophysiological diagnosis (FND) is an advantage
  • Interest: Interest in sleep medicine and interest in the handling of medical technology
  • Knowledge: In-depth PC user knowledge, good knowledge of German, other languages beneficial
  • Personality: teamwork, flexibility and independence
  • Already experience with late and night service
