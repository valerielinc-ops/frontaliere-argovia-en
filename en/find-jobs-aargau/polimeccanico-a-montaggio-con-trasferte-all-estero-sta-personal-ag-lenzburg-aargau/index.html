Full description

The work includes the independent assembly of complex industrial transmissions and hydraulic motors. An experienced team and a clearly structured one-shift operation create a pleasant working environment. Do you want to implement versatile assembly projects with your skills? For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Industrial gearboxes and hydraulic motors mounted independently
  • Assemble, test and commission mechanical and hydraulic assemblies
  • Dismantle, inspect, maintain and professionally revise transmissions
  • Occasional assembly and service operations at home and abroad

Requirements

  • Completed training as a polymechanic or equivalent
  • Experience in the assembly of mechanical assemblies, ideally in mechanical or plant engineering
  • Safe handling of technical drawings, heavy components and lifting equipment
  • Independent, careful working methods and mandatory readiness for missions abroad

Offer
