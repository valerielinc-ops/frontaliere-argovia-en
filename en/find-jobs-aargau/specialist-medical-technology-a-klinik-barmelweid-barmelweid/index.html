Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic clinic in Switzerland. In 285 beds, the clinic takes care of patients in hospital. The employees of Barmelweid Group AG take care of the technical, gastronomic and administrative services of the clinic. They ensure that everything runs smoothly and that patients, visitors and guests feel comfortable. Barmelweid employs more than 700 people.
