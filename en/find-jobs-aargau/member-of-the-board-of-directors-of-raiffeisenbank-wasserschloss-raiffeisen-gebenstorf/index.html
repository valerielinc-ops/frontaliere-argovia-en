Become part of our team!

As the second largest banking group in Switzerland, Raiffeisen is a leader in the retail business with its 208 cooperatively organised, independent Raiffeisen banks. Raiffeisenbank Wasserschloss is a successful and locally oriented bank with 45 employees and around 8,700 cooperatives. As part of the succession arrangement, we are looking for a committed, independent and entrepreneurial personality who, together with the VR committee and in close cooperation with the bank management, will continue to write the success story of our bank. The motion for election to the General Assembly will take place in spring 2027.

What do you expect?

As a member of the Board of Directors, you are jointly responsible for

  • the appropriate and effective supervision of the management of the Bank.
  • the financial situation and development of the Bank.
  • the development and definition of the Raiffeisenbank strategy and the review of its implementation.
  • compliance with laws and regulations and for risk management.

Within the Board of Directors, you are responsible for the financial market and banking sector. Their main tasks include:

  • addressing developments and challenges in the Swiss banking landscape.
  • the assessment of the regional market development and its impact on the bank’s own strategy.
  • monitoring the implementation of the planned market development.

What do you bring?

  • As a member of our bank, you can identify with the cooperative values and management principles of Raiffeisen.
  • You have broad professional experience in specialist, managerial and/or management functions. In particular, you have experience in the financial services sector (including products and organizations) as well as extensive business knowledge.
  • They are able to assess strategic issues in a changing industry holistically and to participate in solution-oriented discussions on the further development of the bank.
  • They have the necessary time (about 15 days a year in total) and the flexibility to be available in unforeseen situations.
  • They are ready to participate in the social life of our region and to maintain an active network with a wide range of stakeholders from business, politics and society.

With a committed member of the Board of Directors, a strong bank management and a competent team, we continue to develop Raiffeisenbank Wasserschloss in a sustainable manner. Christian Bolt Chairman of the Management Board Why Raiffeisen?
