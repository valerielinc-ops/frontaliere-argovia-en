Goods manager: in coop.ch

Coop Genossenschaft — Spreitenbach, Aargau, Svizzera

Tasks

  • Results-oriented and efficient distribution of goods through optimal batch sizes and stock management
  • Planning, terminating and monitoring inventories in coordination with the CCM in the event of change of items, assortment changes, novelties / concept changes

  • Coordination of delivery times to the distribution centers with the responsible persons of logistics and suppliers
