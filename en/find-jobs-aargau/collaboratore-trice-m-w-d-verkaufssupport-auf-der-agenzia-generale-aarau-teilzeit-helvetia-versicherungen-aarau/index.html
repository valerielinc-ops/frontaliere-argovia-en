Customer Consulting – Sales

Place of work: Aarau | Experience level: With professional experience | FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, that means more than just ticking off tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • You actively support the field sales force throughout the entire sales process, thereby contributing to our shared success.
  • As a central contact person, you answer professional and technical questions in the areas of non-life private clients, non-life corporate clients, individual life, accident, health, and occupational pensions (BVG).
  • You look after our private and corporate clients by phone, in writing, and in person with great competence and commitment.
  • With a flair for individual needs, you develop tailored concepts and solutions.
  • Together with the team, you actively help shape market development and sales promotion.
  • You accompany our apprentices in their daily professional life and specifically promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Commercial insurance training and/or several years of experience with an all-lines insurer
  • You already hold the VBV certificate (all-lines) or are willing to obtain it.
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact and a strong service orientation
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for developing individual customer solutions
  • Efficient, solution-oriented collaboration with the field sales force and relevant specialist departments
