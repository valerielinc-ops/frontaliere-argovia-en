Your Role

As a pipe closer in our maintenance, you ensure the safe and reliable operation of our production facilities. You work in a challenging GMP environment and make an important contribution to the production of high-quality pharmaceutical products.

Your Profiles

Your role

  • Manufacture, assembly and repair of piping systems according to isometrics, drawings and specifications
  • Carrying out maintenance, repair and conversion work on production and supply facilities
  • Welding work on pipelines and components (TIG)
  • Assembly and exchange of enamel, glass and PTFE components
  • Independent troubleshooting of piping systems
  • Support for plant shutdowns, audits and projects
  • Ensuring compliance with GMP, safety and quality requirements
  • Documentation of the work carried out in accordance with operational specifications

Your profile Professional knowledge

  • Completed technical vocational training as a tube maker, plant and apparatus builder, pipeline builder, maintenance specialist or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in industrial pipeline construction
  • Experience in the chemical, pharmaceutical or food industry as well as GMP knowledge is advantageous
  • Very good TIG welding knowledge as well as valid welding certificate is advantageous
  • Technical understanding and safe reading of isometrics and technical drawings
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Personal strengths

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of over 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
