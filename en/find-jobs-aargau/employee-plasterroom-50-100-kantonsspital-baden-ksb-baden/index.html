Full description

You like to work practically, have a skillful hand and want a versatile job where no day is quite the same? Then you are exactly right with us!

Become part of our plasterroom team and bring your practical skills, experience and personality to us. We look forward to meeting you!

Their tasks

  • You put on plastic support bandages.
  • You look after and advise our patients.
  • You perform wound treatments and take care of the follow-up after operations.
  • You organize and accompany the orthopedic plaster session.
  • You assist with medical procedures and infiltrations.

Your profile

  • You have an education as a FaGe, nurse HF or MPA.
  • You bring manual skill and enjoy practical activities.
  • Ideally, you have experience with support groups and in the field of orthopaedics.
  • You are capable of teamwork, work independently and reliably.

You can expect a small, warm and experienced team, a varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility, a structured introduction with attractive training opportunities and regular working hours from Monday to Friday.
