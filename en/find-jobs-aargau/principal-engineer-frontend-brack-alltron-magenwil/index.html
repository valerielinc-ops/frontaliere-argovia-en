Principal Engineer – Frontend — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for front-end architecture across multiple teams and domains – from storefront and search to checkout, account and loyalty
  • Further development of our standards for React, TypeScript, Server-Side Rendering, Micro-Frontends and Self-Contained Systems
  • Establishment and establishment of joint component foundations as well as a scalable design system
  • Targeted optimization of web performance and core web vitals as relevant levers for customer experience and conversion
  • Design of modern rendering, streaming, edge, caching and bundle strategies as well as a meaningful front-end observability
  • Elimination of organization-wide technical blockers in local development, tooling, build pipelines and architecture
  • Anchoring security, accessibility and privacy by default across the frontend landscape
  • Defining and further developing our AI tooling strategy including appropriate architecture, automated code reviews and effective use of modern engineering tools
  • Technical coaching of engineers and team leads as well as close cooperation with domain architecture, product management and leadership
  • Representing our technical orientation within the organization, towards budget managers and in the external tech community

Requirements

  • At least ten years of experience in modern software development with a strong focus on the frontend and at least two years in a technical leadership or architecture role
  • Deep practical experience with React and TypeScript in productive, business-critical applications
  • In-depth knowledge of server-side rendering, for example with Next.js, Remix or a comparable setup, as well as with micro-frontends or self-contained systems
  • Proven expertise in web performance, core web vitals, accessibility and developing fast, scalable customer experiences
  • Confident handling of concepts such as DDD, Clean Architecture, Event-Driven Architecture, SOLID, Feature Flags and Dependency Injection as well as Design Systems, State Management and Component-Driven Development
  • Solid understanding of distributed systems, DevOps practices, automated testing, CI/CD and the operation of systems according to the principle "You build it, you run it"
  • Experience in a fast, product-oriented engineering organization with short iterations, regular releases and real end-to-end responsibility
  • Enjoy sharpening requirements together with product management, understanding customer needs and making technical decisions from a user and business perspective
  • Strong coaching and communication skills, servant leadership mindset and the ability to drive complex change with endurance, warmth and humor
  • Very good knowledge of German and/or English as well as continuous curiosity about new technologies, methods and AI-supported software development

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
