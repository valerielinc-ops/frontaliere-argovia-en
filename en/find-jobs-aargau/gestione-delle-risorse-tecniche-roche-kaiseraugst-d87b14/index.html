The Position The Technical Asset Care Manager is responsible for the trouble-free and GMP-compliant operation of the production plants, as well as the stabilization and continuous improvement including the production of adjacent plants/techn. Processes responsible. This requires a good knowledge of operational processes and facilities, a high degree of technical understanding and very good organisational skills for project management.

The possibility

  • Accompanying production, identifying problems and taking action to eliminate them
  • Plans and coordinates interruptions, as well as maintenance activities
  • Ensuring GMP-compliant documentation of technical activities, as well as creating and maintaining internal and cross-divisional operations
  • Work regulations and other GMP-relevant documents in compliance with the current GMP rules.
  • Key figures review and derivation of possibilities for improvement.
  • Optimization of maintenance activities / concepts, as well as implementation and moderation of A3 problem solvings and RCA’s, participation of Gemba Walks.
  • Reduction of unplanned technical downtimes, analysis based on data e.g. OEE & SAP.
  • Development and implementation of training concepts within the area of responsibility.
  • Close cooperation with Reliability Engineering (ARA, ACE, EoL,...) to prepare the basis for investment decisions.
  • Preparation and management of (technical) meetings, huddles incl. Creation & maintenance of the associated performance boards.
  • Willingness to work on a panel when needed

Who you are

  • Completed studies preferably in a technical field or a corresponding professional training with many years of professional experience in the field of pharmaceutical production, development or quality assurance.
  • Several years of professional experience within the affected manufacturing unit, basic knowledge of safety, quality systems and quality assurance concepts, including the application of current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMPs).
  • Good knowledge of the company and its processes as well as very good organizational skills, as well as ability to implement, coach and apply lean and agile working methods.
  • Very good communication skills in word and writing in German and good in English.
