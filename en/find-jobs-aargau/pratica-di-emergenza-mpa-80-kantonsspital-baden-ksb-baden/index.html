Full description

The Emergency Practice is part of the interdisciplinary Emergency Center of the Cantonal Hospital of Baden. Every year, more than 25,000 patients with mild illnesses and injuries visit the emergency practice. As an MPA, you support doctors in the emergency team and family doctors in the districts of Baden and Brugg in caring for these patients. You are an empathetic, team-oriented personality with pleasure in your profession and have a completed education as an MPA? Then you fit perfectly with us! Ideally, you have already completed your training as a vocational trainer or plan to complete it – this is a big plus for us. Become part of our team and support us in patient care, laboratory work and many other varied tasks. You can expect a modern working environment with versatile activities with flexible services in shift operations.

Their tasks

  • You receive the patients and coordinate appointments for follow-up checks and referrals.
  • You perform blood tests and ECG, perform laboratory work in the practice laboratory and administer medications, infusions and vaccinations.
  • You put on casts and rails and wound dressings professionally.
  • You prepare patients for treatments and support the doctors in small surgical procedures.
  • You supervise learners and actively shape the work processes.
  • You also take on various administrative tasks.

Your profile

  • You have completed an education as an MPA and bring several years of professional experience.
  • You already have the vocational training or have planned to complete it.
  • You will benefit from experience in hematological laboratory work.
  • You are an empathetic, team capable and flexible personality.
  • You are ready to take on early, late and weekend services.
  • You have very good knowledge of German.
  • You are adept at using MS-Office.
