Full description

Do you like to combine technical understanding with a good feeling for the needs of the users? As an Application Manager, you accompany applications from requirements analysis through introduction to ongoing operation and are an important interface between the departments and IT. You play an active role in shaping the hospital’s application landscape, supporting employees in everyday life and keeping track of complex issues. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You will analyze requirements together with the various departments of the hospital and develop suitable concepts and solutions.
  • Ensure smooth operation and support the interfaces to the users and suppliers of various hospital applications.
  • Support and handling of incident, change and release management (planning, testing, coordination with suppliers/customers, infrastructure).
  • You accompany the implementation and integration of new applications and technologies and ensure that they are well integrated into the existing IT application landscape.
  • You plan and carry out tests and quality assurance measures, identify problems at an early stage and coordinate their resolution with the relevant specialists.
  • You will train and support users in the use of the applications and answer questions in ongoing operation.
  • You plan and coordinate sub-projects related to innovative applications and ensure that deadlines and budgets are met.
  • You observe technological developments and trends in the healthcare sector, examine new solutions and assess their concrete benefits for the hospital.
  • If necessary, you will be the link between the departments, external partners and IT and ensure comprehensible and solution-oriented communication.
  • You're ready to do piquetting.

Your profile

  • You bring experience in application support and business analysis and know the processes in the healthcare sector as well as their mapping in a hospital application landscape.
  • You have a strong customer and service orientation and enjoy working with different specialist areas.
  • You are familiar with ERP, localization and guidance, transport and order management, facility management and HL7 interface communication.
  • You know terms like ITIL and you can use your technical knowledge in everyday work.
  • You have very good communication skills and can communicate complex technical contexts in an understandable way.
  • You think analytically, work independently and find pragmatic solutions to challenging questions.
  • You have a quick comprehension and keep track of several parallel tasks.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
