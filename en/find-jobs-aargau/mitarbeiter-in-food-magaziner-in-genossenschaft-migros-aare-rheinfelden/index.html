Full description

Are you interested in staple foods and enthusiastic about the variety of supermarket products in the food sector? Are you also a sales talent and convince with your communicative manner in dealing with our customers? Then you are exactly right with us.

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, efficient goods management and ensuring availability are your focus
  • You adhere to the defined processes in the department and ensure a smooth process
  • You load and unload the trucks
  • Assumption of magazine replacement in the event of holidays and other absences
  • You support actively and engaged in all departments

Requirements

  • Ideal professional experience: in the field of warehouse management & food
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • You have a good physical condition
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Sports and fitness offerings: cost-sharing of subscriptions to over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
