Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

From 01. November 2026 we are looking for a temporary reinforcement in the area of data management Tumorboard for 4 months.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for registering patients for the tumor board using the medical software C37 as well as for sending the program
  • You actively participate in the tumor board and take over the logging
  • You create the billing of the tumor board
  • Using tumor software (ODSeasyNet, Adjumed) you document cancer treatments
  • You work closely with documentary doctors and their MPAs
  • You help prepare audits and participate in them

Your profile

  • You have an education in the health care sector or several years of professional experience in this environment
  • You have knowledge of oncological processes and terminology
  • You are adept with digital applications and structured data management
  • Experience with tumor documentation software would be a big plus
  • You describe yourself as a reliable and resilient personality, with a high level of quality awareness and independent working methods
  • You speak fluent German
  • You are available from 01.11.2026

Working hours: You are available/present on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays all day. You will also be present on Thursday morning from 07:00.
