Over the area

The Centre Psychiatry and Psychotherapy ambulant (centre director Dr. med.) Katrin Hanno is part of the Clinic for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (Clinic Director Prof. Dr. med. Marc Walter). With its five locations (Aarau, Baden, Rheinfelden, Wohlen and Zofingen), it offers mental health care for the population in the canton of Aargau. The centre includes four outpatient centres with specialised consultation hours, group therapies and work coaching, as well as four day centres where patients are treated in adulthood. In addition, the center includes the two day clinics in Aarau and Baden as well as the “practice for your mental health” in Zofingen.
