Full description

“You’re burning for psychotherapy and looking for a place where your commitment to people really matters?” Then join our innovative team! Employment level: 60–100% admission by immediate or by agreement

You don't have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. You move that with us

  • You are responsible for the psychotherapeutic interdisciplinary treatments of your own patients in cooperation with specialists in somatic disciplines.
  • You will conduct interdisciplinary treatments with a team of psychiatrists and with the involvement of the social service.
  • You participate in group therapy offers.
  • you participate in further education and training offers of the clinic Barmelweid. You inspire us...
  • with your advanced, at least federally recognized (MEBEKO), psychotherapeutic training, preferably in psychodynamic, cognitive behavioral therapy, systemic or person-centered psychotherapy (at least 2 years)
  • with your mind. 2-year clinical experience in psychiatric care
  • with your enthusiasm and already acquired skills for psychotherapeutic work.
  • with your pleasure to participate in case discussions, clinical lectures and intervisions, or to build and promote such offers.
  • even more if you have already had first experience in the treatment of psychosomatic diseases or are familiar with trauma-specific psychotherapy procedures
  • due to your ability to integrate economic framework conditions into everyday work. We are an innovative and patient-focused institution dedicated primarily to psychotherapeutic treatment and the promotion of mental health. Our two outpatients in Frick and Aarau offer a wide range of psychotherapeutic services and work with a dedicated team of psychologists and psychiatrists. Special consultation hours can be used in the areas of trauma follow-up disorders, psycho-oncology, disorders related to pregnancy and childbirth, eating disorders and pain disorders. There is also a consultancy service with Hirslanden Klinik Aarau. Our social service rounds off our offer. There is a close cooperation between Klinik Barmelweid and its outpatients, so that an outpatient, semi-stationary or inpatient treatment can be carried out as required. In the clinic, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy work closely with internal medicine, making it possible to treat people with multimorbid somatic diseases and psychopathological disorders.
