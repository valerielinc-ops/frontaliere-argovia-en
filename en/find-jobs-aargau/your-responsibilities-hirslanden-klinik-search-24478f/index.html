Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Aarau | Aarau

Reference number: 70761

Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

YOUR TASKS

  • You take over the overall responsibility for procurement and logistics at Klinik Aarau – from procurement to warehouses to distribution and disposal incl. OP logistics, consignments and rental materials
  • You ensure that all materials are available at all times, economically procured and efficiently provided
  • You optimize the entire logistics chain and material flows, especially in connection with the upcoming commissioning of House C as well as further changes on the clinic area and develop processes in a targeted and continuous manner.
  • You actively promote the standardization and digitalization of our processes
  • You are responsible for budget, material costs and inventory and control the costs together with the clinic management
  • Active participation in the further development of the clinic area, especially in your area in the new building Schachenallee as well as other construction projects and ensuring a smooth commissioning
  • You lead your team professionally and personnel and actively develop it further

Your profile

  • Education as Eidg. Dipl. Purchaser/-in / Purchasing Manager/-in or comparable qualification in the field of logistics
  • You have training in leadership
  • Several years of experience in a management position in the field of logistics or procurement / supply chain management (ideally in the healthcare sector)
  • Strong experience in process optimization, project management and transformation
  • Strong business understanding and experience in cost management
  • Very good SAP knowledge
  • Strong negotiating skills
  • Level communication
  • Experience in interdisciplinary cooperation (stakeholder management)
  • Affinity for digitalization, robotics and systems
  • You think networked, structured and solution-oriented
  • You convince with leadership, assertiveness and communication skills

Maybe this is the chance you’ve been waiting for! We offer you a varied management task with attractive creative leeway as well as a dedicated team that supports you during training and in everyday life. In addition, we offer you a wide range of development opportunities, at least 25 days of holidays, and numerous other employment highlights that make your working day even more pleasant.

For additional information, Alexandra Suter, Head of Hospitality & Facility Management and Member of Clinic Management at T, is at your disposal.
