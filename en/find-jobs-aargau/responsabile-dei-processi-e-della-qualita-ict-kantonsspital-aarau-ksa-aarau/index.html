Full description

Job: 01.02.2027 or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

To complement our team, we are looking for a dedicated personality who actively shapes and continuously develops our ICT process and quality management. In this versatile cross-sectional function, you create transparent and binding standards, support the responsible process owners and help to sustainably improve the quality, efficiency and effectiveness of our ICT services.

Their tasks

  • They are responsible for the development, maintenance and further development of the ICT process map as well as uniform process standards based on ITIL.
  • They define process governance and support process owners in the design, implementation and continuous improvement of their processes.
  • They further develop ICT quality management and establish a systematic continuous improvement process.
  • They check the effectiveness and compliance with processes and standards through regular process reviews and audits.
  • They further develop KPI, SLA and quality indicators, create meaningful reporting and derive concrete improvement measures from this.
  • They analyze existing processes, identify optimization and automation potential and accompany their sustainable implementation.
  • You create and maintain process documentation, guidelines and methodological guidelines.
  • You work in cross-divisional projects or take over their management, especially in the areas of service management, process optimization and quality.

Requirements

They enjoy creating structures, simplifying complex processes and implementing sustainable improvements together with others. Quality does not mean additional bureaucracy for you, but clear processes, measurable results and reliable ICT services.

  • They have higher education in computer science, business informatics, process/quality management or have a comparable qualification and relevant practical experience.
  • You have several years of experience in ICT service management as well as in process or quality management.
  • ITIL is not just theory for you: you have experience in designing, introducing, measuring and continuously developing processes.
  • You are familiar with KPI, SLA, process key figures and management reporting.
  • They think analytically and networked and recognize optimization and automation potential in existing processes.
  • They work independently, structured and solution-oriented and pursue agreed improvement measures consistently until implementation.
  • They communicate convincingly at different levels and understand how to win process owners and other stakeholders for common standards and changes.
  • Experience in project management and cross-divisional change initiatives rounds off your profile.
  • They communicate very well in German and have good knowledge of English.
