Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Participation in the processing and decision on appeals against first instance decisions in the field of civil law
  • Drafting of judgments
  • Participation in judgment deliberations
  • Final editorial of judgments

The activity concerns the assessment of complex legal, factual and evidence issues at a high level. With your work, you have a substantive and formal influence on the appearance of the continuously published upper court practice.

What you bring

  • Completed legal studies (lic. iur. / MLaw), preferably attorney's patent
  • Professional experience in justice, lawyer or administration
  • Self-employed, efficient and precise work
  • Good oral and written expression
  • High level of readiness
  • Teamwork, resilience and flexibility

The courts make judgments every day in a wide variety of areas of law.

The focus is on an efficient and citizen-friendly justice system for peaceful coexistence in the Canton of Aargau.
