You don't have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. You move that with us

  • You are responsible for the supervision and implementation of the diagnostic and therapeutic patient procedures of neurological and pneumological patients in sleep medicine and insomnia.
  • You perform sleep medical sleep/wake examinations in inpatient and outpatient patients
  • You work on the evaluation and writing reports of the day and night examinations
  • The setting and control of non-invasive home ventilation is part of your task You inspire us...
  • through your completed vocational training in the medical / nursing area as FAGE, Dipl. Nurse, MPA or neurophysiological diagnostics specialist (FND)
