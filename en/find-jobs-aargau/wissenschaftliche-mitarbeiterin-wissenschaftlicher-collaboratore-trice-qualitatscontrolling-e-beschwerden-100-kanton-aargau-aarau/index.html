Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Implementation and implementation of measures relating to the quality of hospitals and clinics
  • Management of the cantonal quality groups with the list hospitals, monitoring and strengthening the quality development in inpatient hospital care
  • Complaint management and cooperation in supervisory reports and complaints

What you bring

  • Completed university studies in medicine, nursing, business administration in health care or a comparable education
  • Several years of professional experience in quality management, preferably in hospital, clinic or health care
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system as well as ideally experience in quality development and supervision in the stationary sector
  • Strong conceptual, organisational and communication skills as well as experience in the management and moderation of committees and specialist groups
  • High social competence, stylish written expressiveness and a sovereign handling of challenging complaint management issues

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Health Department focuses on health protection. We ensure needs-based, affordable and high-quality healthcare, promote cooperation and digital networking between healthcare providers and strengthen health-conscious behaviour among the population.
