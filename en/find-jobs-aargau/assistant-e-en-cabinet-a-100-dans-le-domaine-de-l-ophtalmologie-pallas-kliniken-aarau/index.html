Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland. We continue to innovatively develop our range of services within these specialist areas. Be it through new treatment methods, equipment, additional locations and cooperations or perhaps also through the soon cooperation with you?

Responsibility

Are you a motivated and open-minded personality? Then we would be happy if you become part of our team in Aarau.

Their main tasks

  • You are responsible for patient care during the office hours and their special examinations
  • Visual field measurements (octopus)
  • Investigations before cataract operations (biometrics, topography)
  • Measurements for retinal disease and optic nerve (OCT) investigations
  • Preliminary clarifications such as car refractometer and glasses control
  • Miscellaneous administrative activities

Requirements

  • Vocational apprenticeship as medical practice assistant, dental assistant or optometrist or diploma as medical secretary
  • Experience/expertise in ophthalmology is beneficial
  • Very reliable, independent, service-oriented and team-oriented way of working
  • They are well-groomed and impeccable.

Benefits

  • Perks on eye and aesthetics services and products
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in a dynamic and motivated team
  • Careful and thorough training
  • Regulated working hours and five weeks of holidays per year, which can be obtained individually by agreement
  • Large range of internal and external training courses
  • Our practice is located directly in front of the train station in Aarau
