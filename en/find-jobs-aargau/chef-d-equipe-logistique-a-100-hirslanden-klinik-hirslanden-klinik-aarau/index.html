Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Aarau | Aarau

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74672

Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

YOUR TASKS

  • Personnel and professional management as well as further development of a logistics team with 12 to 16 employees
  • Preparation of work and deployment plans and implementation of employee interviews
  • Ensuring hospital-internal material supply and availability of goods
  • Responsibility for centralised and decentralised warehouse management including inventories
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations
  • Assumption of project tasks in the new construction project “Schachenallee”
  • Working closely with the procurement team to ensure efficient procurement and logistics processes
  • Maintenance and control of master and disposition data in SAP
  • Creation and evaluation of KPIs as well as implementation of improvement measures in logistics

Your profile

  • Completed training as a logistician with further training in logistics or a comparable qualification
  • At least five years of professional experience in logistics, some of which are in a management position
  • Experience in procurement is beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of SAP and MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook)
  • Initial experience in project management as well as in the analysis and optimization of logistics processes
  • Ideally, knowledge of healthcare
  • Networked thinking and an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Willingness to actively shape change and continuously develop and improve processes

Working hours are generally from Monday - Friday, 7:00 - 16:00.

Does that sound like you? Then we may have just the right thing for you – because we have a lot to offer.A working environment that is characterized by real appreciation, strong team spirit and a good dose of humor awaits you. In addition, there is a varied job, attractive career and development opportunities, a family together, at least 25 holiday days, 4 additional paid days off as compensation for the changing time – and many other employment highlights.

For additional information, Stefano Vital, Head of Procurement and Logistics, is available at stefano.vital@hirslanden.ch.
