Business Development Manager Physical AI / Robotics — Brack.Alltron AG

Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for operational processing and strategic development of the Robotics product range in line with our category strategy and growth targets
  • Identification of new market opportunities and technology trends around robotics, machine learning and physical AI as well as scouting, onboarding and successful market launch of new manufacturers
  • Responsibility for the defined revenue, contribution margin and budget targets as well as the entrepreneurial management of the business area
  • Leading manufacturer and supplier negotiations and jointly developing a competitive and attractive product portfolio for our various distribution channels
  • Together with marketing and sales Development and implementation of go-to-market activities, communication priorities and marketing measures
  • As a central interface and coordination point between manufacturers, sales, marketing and customers, transfer new technologies into concrete business opportunities
  • Supporting sales in the acquisition and development of new and existing customers, especially for larger projects, tenders and solution-oriented customer inquiries
  • Implementation of the pricing strategy via the various sales channels as well as close cooperation with purchasing, sales and logistics for large orders and strategically relevant projects
  • Organisation and implementation of internal and external training and events

Requirements

  • You have a commercial or technical basic education and ideally a higher education or training. a degree in Robotic, Product Management, Business Administration or equivalent
  • You bring 5–7 years of professional experience in product management, business development or a comparable go-to-market function
  • You know the Swiss ICT channel and ideally already have an active network with manufacturers, partners and/or customers
  • Topics such as physical AI, machine learning, robotics and data-based solutions inspire you and you have appropriate technical knowledge
  • You combine analytical and networked thinking with entrepreneurial action and recognize market and business potential early on
  • You convince through high initiative, an independent and efficient working method as well as strong negotiating and communication skills
  • Contact with manufacturers, partners and customers is yours and you understand how to attract different stakeholders for new topics and business opportunities
  • You are a solution-oriented personality and a team player who likes to work across departments
  • You are sure to communicate in German, have very good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
