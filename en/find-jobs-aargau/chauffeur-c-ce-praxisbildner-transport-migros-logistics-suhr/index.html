Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility in the function as a practice instructor.

Mansioni

  • Practical trainer* of the apprentices to the road transport specialist EFZ (daily operation)
  • Delivery of shops and branches in accordance with the requirements of day-to-day operations
  • Gentle treatment of the products to be transported (food)
  • Transport temperature monitoring
  • Return of empty goods and products
  • Occasional support for double tours

Requirements

  • 2-3 years: practical trainer* in transport
  • Initial or continuing education completed: Transport
  • Completed training as road transport specialist with EFZ
  • Category C/CE driving licence
  • Training permit for road transport professionals according to VZVArt. 20
  • Multiannual, accident-free driving practice and willingness to work flexible hours
  • Joy of daily contact with customers and learners
  • Kind and well-groomed appearance, good manners
  • German (very good knowledge)
