Full description

For our team in the Cantonal Hospital Baden we are looking for a reinforcement as

Tasks

  • Independent psychological diagnosis, therapy and support of oncological patients and their relatives in the inpatient and outpatient setting
  • Case documentation and reporting
  • Participation in medical and psychological reports and tumor boards
  • Cooperation in the multi-professional and interdisciplinary team

Requirements

  • University or University of Applied Sciences degree in Psychology (Master/License)
  • Completed psychotherapy
  • Interest in or completed further education psycho-oncology (SGPO/WPO) beneficial
  • Reliability and responsibility
  • Good social skills, independent and efficient working style and high resilience
  • Enjoyment of multi-professional and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
