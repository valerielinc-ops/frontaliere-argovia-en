Interventional psychiatry

In the Department of Interventional Psychiatry, electroconvulsion therapies (EKT) are performed in short anesthesia. The preparation and follow-up of the ECT patients is carried out in the recovery room. We also offer repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) and esketamine treatments. We are looking for a committed person to work in the recovery room immediately or by agreement. Re-entrants are welcome. The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. Over the area

Interventional psychiatry offers treatment alternatives or supplements to psychotherapeutic and drug therapies at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy. Interventional psychiatry refers to the treatment with electrical or electromagnetic methods, it can also include special pharmacological interventions. These methods are a treatment alternative or supplement to psychotherapeutic and drug therapies. At the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at PDAG, we carry out treatments using electroconvulsion therapy (EKT), repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) and treatments with esketamine.
