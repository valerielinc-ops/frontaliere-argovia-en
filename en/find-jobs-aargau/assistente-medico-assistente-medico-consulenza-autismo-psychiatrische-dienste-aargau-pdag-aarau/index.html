Full description

For the autism consultation of the clinic for children and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy in Aarau we are looking from 01. January 2027 or by arrangement as

Tasks

  • Under qualified guidance, you will learn to independently clarify and treat patients with suspected autism spectrum disorders (ASS).
  • They learn about common psychological testing procedures and carry out psychopharmacological attitudes, counselling and psychotherapies under supervisory supervision and specialist final responsibility.
  • They make diagnoses and assessments according to the valid diagnostic scheme and, based on this, work out an action plan
  • You are participating in emergency hospital services
  • You create the necessary treatment documentation and technical reports and bill the services provided completely

Requirements

  • You have a Federal Medical Diploma or an equivalent degree in medicine recognised by MEBEKO
  • You ideally have previous experience in child and adolescent psychiatry, psychiatry or pediatrics
  • They have clinical competence, good relationship skills, analytical thinking skills and good written skills
  • They show resilience and initiative in clinical everyday life and have good manners and humor
