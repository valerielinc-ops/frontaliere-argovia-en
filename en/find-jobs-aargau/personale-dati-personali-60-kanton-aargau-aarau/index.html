Your working environment

In the Office for Migration and Integration, we are responsible for implementing the immigration and asylum legislation and implement the integration policy of the Confederation and the Canton. We issue permits for foreign persons, carry out labour market controls and ensure the implementation of the illegal work legislation. We advise foreigners willing to return and execute the expulsions ordered by the Canton and the Confederation.
