Full description

We offer a comprehensive neurological rehabilitation offer at the rehabilitation clinic Bad Zurzach at a high level. The aim of the interprofessional teams, which work closely together, is to provide patients with a better quality of life and independence on their way back to everyday life. This requires experience and expertise as well as commitment and empathy. We are looking forward to a neuropsychologist who would like to participate with us. Temporary employment from now until the end of June 2027.

Your tasks • Conduct of neuropsychological examinations in stationary setting • Implementation of neuropsychological therapy • Advice to relatives • If you are interested in participating in the Zurza headache programme ZKP • Participation in interdisciplinary reports and internal training

You bring with you • Graduate degree in psychology and at least commenced postgraduate training in neuropsychology • Experience and interest in rehabilitation setting and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation • Constructive, creative, pragmatic and solution-oriented approach • Teamwork, at the same time independence, flexibility and resilience • Excellent oral and written knowledge of German, further language skills beneficial

We offer you • Recognized training institution of the University of Zurich for practical postgraduate training in neuropsychology • Clinical supervision and intervision • Versatile, challenging and self-responsible work in a future-oriented company • A motivated and committed interprofessional team that values a pleasant and open working environment • Miscellaneous education and training with a generous share of costs • Above-average participation in pension fund contributions incl. Assumption of administrative costs and attractive options of the pension plan • Free admission to the wellness spa FORTYSEVEN. Various other advantages of the group
