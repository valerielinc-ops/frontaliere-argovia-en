Full description

Do you want to combine your sales talent with your coordination skills? Are you interested in stock and goods management and also like to communicate with our customers? As a magazine specialist you are responsible for the right products to land in the right place.

Mansioni

Requisiti

  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure of selling, you make shopping a positive experience
  • you have a good physical condition
  • PC skills and a high digital fitness distinguish you
  • English (good knowledge)
