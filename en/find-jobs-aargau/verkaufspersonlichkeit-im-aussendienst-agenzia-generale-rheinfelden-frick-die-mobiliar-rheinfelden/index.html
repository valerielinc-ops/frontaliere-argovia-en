What awaits you

For our team of the general agency Rheinfelden/Frick we are looking for a sales person in the field service.

This is what they do with us

The focus here is on competent support and the active expansion of your private and corporate customer portfolio.

This is what you bring with you

  • Anchored in the Fricktal or in the region
  • Experience in the insurance, banking or sales sector
  • Enjoyment of customer contact
  • Independent, responsible and customer-oriented working methods

Working with us

In the general agency Rheinfelden/Frick you can expect a well-established and committed team with 36 employees, including three apprentices. We live close to customers and, thanks to our own claims department in Rheinfelden, we are there for our customers directly on site.

A modern working environment with attractive working conditions and versatile training and development opportunities.

For this function, according to the Insurance Supervision Act, you need the insurance intermediary/in VBV, profile non-life and life. You're not bringing this one yet? No problem – we support you with targeted internal training and accompany you on the way to the required degree.

Your appointment will be by agreement.

You can find out more about us as an employer at mobiliar.ch/rheinfelden.
