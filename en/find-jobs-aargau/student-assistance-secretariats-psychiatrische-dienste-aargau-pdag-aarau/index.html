Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy

For our secretariats of the Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy in Aarau and Windisch we are looking for you as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Over the area

In the clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy (clinical director Dr. med.) Angelo Bernardon) are treated children and adolescents up to the age of 18. The integrated treatment offer of the clinic includes an outpatient area with a central ambulatory as well as four ambulatories distributed across the canton, a consultancy and liaison service and an inpatient area with an acute care unit, two psychotherapy units, a day clinic and a clinic school. In the teams, clinical specialists from the fields of medicine, psychology, nursing and social pedagogy work and in the clinic school teachers and curative school teachers. The Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy is a teaching hospital of the University of Zurich and a certified continuing education institution category A.
