Full description

They ensure that invoicing in day-to-day business runs reliably and invoices are prepared on time. They process rejections, clarify tariff issues and provide patients and internal and external bodies with competent support. In the absence of the team leader, you assume leadership responsibility. The position is available as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • They ensure day-to-day operation and telephone accessibility in the area of billing.
  • They invoice on time and process rejections, cancellations and interim, supplementary and final invoices.
  • They control the facturalists and ensure correct classification.
  • They clarify problem cases in billing and provide competent information to patients as well as internal and external bodies.
  • You fully document the processes in your area of responsibility and adhere to the agreed goals on time.
  • They create correspondence for patients and external bodies and do further administrative work.
  • They submit proposals for optimising processes and working with internal bodies.
  • They work in the team and are actively involved in projects.

Your profile

  • They have completed commercial or equivalent training.
  • They bring several years of professional experience in billing, preferably in the hospital environment or with a health insurance company.
  • Good knowledge of medical tariffs and the Swiss health care system is an advantage.
  • They work in a structured, efficient and responsible manner.
  • They are service-oriented and value contact with patients as well as internal and external partners.
  • They are ready to assume leadership responsibility in the absence of the team leader.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
