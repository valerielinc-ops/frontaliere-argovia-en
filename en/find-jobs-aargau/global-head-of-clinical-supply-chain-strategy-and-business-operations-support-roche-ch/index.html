The Opportunity

  • Strategic Leadership & Network Integration: Lead end-to-end PTDS business strategy, aligning functional initiatives with PTD and R&D Vision 2030, while overseeing annual strategy reviews, SQDEC goal setting, and global business project portfolio management (bPPM).
  • Digital & AI Transformation: Act as the enterprise business integrator driving core digital, ERP, and AI roadmaps, including high-visibility programs like ASPIRE (SAP S/4HANA & ICSM migration), CLARA deployment, OMP, and Analytics & Reporting (A&R).
  • Operational Excellence & Capability Building: Direct line-manage the PTD OE Team Lead and OE Hub to champion Lean Six Sigma methodologies, Kaizen events, and root cause analyses across PTDx projects to cultivate Lean capabilities globally.
  • Global Resource & Financial Planning: Lead cross-functional T0–T3 budget rounds, managing 1-year operational resourcing and 3–5-year strategic financial horizons, including clinical trial study-related costs (PVC planning), in partnership with Global Demand & Supply Planning and Product Portfolio Teams.
  • Business Continuity & Risk Management: Establish and govern robust risk management frameworks, emergency incident response procedures (LEM), and Business Continuity Management (BCM) strategies to ensure resilient clinical supply chain operations globally.
  • End-to-End Process Management: Maintain business process ownership across Plan-to-Deliver operations, ensuring regulatory compliance, high-quality master data, and global process standardization across complex supply networks.
  • Environmental Sustainability: Oversee sustainability and carbon reduction initiatives across the global clinical supply chain, line-managing the PTD Sustainability Lead and driving CO2 sensitivity analyses and reporting.
  • Executive People & Matrix Leadership: Empower and direct line-manage/matrix-lead a diverse, multicultural team across primary global hubs (Kaiseraugst/Basel, South San Francisco, Mississauga) to foster high engagement, talent flow, and organizational development.

Who you are

  • Educational Background: Hold a Bachelor’s degree in Life Sciences, Supply Chain Management, Engineering, or Business Administration; a Master’s degree, MBA, or advanced degree is strongly preferred.
  • Industry Experience: Bring 15+ years of progressive professional experience in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or healthcare industry, with deep domain expertise in global clinical trial supply operations and regulatory environments.
  • Leadership & Matrix Management: Demonstrate 5+ years of direct line management and executive leadership experience, successfully guiding large, cross-functional, multi-site teams across complex matrix organizations.
  • Transformation & Enterprise Systems: Proven track record leading major digital transformations, Agile/SAFe frameworks, and enterprise ERP implementations (e.g., SAP S/4HANA, OMP, or legacy SAP).
  • Strategic & Financial Acumen: Possess demonstrated expertise in long-range strategic planning, global resourcing plans, study-cost management (PVC), project portfolio controls (bPPM), and functional budget governance.
  • Process Excellence & Professional Certifications: Hold recognized certifications such as PMP or Lean/Six Sigma Black Belt (or equivalent), with a proven history of embedding Operational Excellence (OE) and Continuous Improvement methodologies.
  • Global Readiness & Languages: Fluent in English (German language skills are an advantage), with the flexibility and willingness to travel internationally up to 10%.
