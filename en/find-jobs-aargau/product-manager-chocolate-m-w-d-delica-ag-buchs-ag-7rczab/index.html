Full description

Chocolate is more to you than enjoyment? Then design with us what inspires tomorrow on the shelf. As Product Manager Chocolate, you will continue to develop our Easter range, launch new product ideas and combine consumer needs, market trends and Swiss quality with successful product management.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You develop and optimize our Easter range in the field of chocolate and derive new product ideas from consumer needs, market trends and analyses.
  • You control product and innovation projects from the idea to the successful introduction and coordinate internal and external participants.
  • You analyze the market, product range, sales, budget and profitability and derive concrete measures for the further development of the portfolio.
  • You work closely with research & development, production, marketing, sales and planning and bring different requirements together in a targeted manner.
  • You support our main customer Migros at category level and support sales with concepts, presentations and convincing decision-making principles.

Requirements

  • 2–3 years of experience: in product, marketing or project management, preferably in the food or consumer goods environment.
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): or a comparable further education in business administration, marketing, food technology/sciences or a related field.
  • You convince through your own initiative, a sense of responsibility and a solution-oriented way and manage to bring together different participants for a common goal.
  • You work analytically, structured and independently, think creatively and develop new ideas for products and assortments with pleasure.
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office and SAP skills.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)
  • French (advantageous)
