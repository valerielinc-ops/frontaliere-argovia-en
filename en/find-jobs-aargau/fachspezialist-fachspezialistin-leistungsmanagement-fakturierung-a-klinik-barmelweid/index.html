You move that with us

  • With your good MS-Office knowledge, SAP IS-H is an advantage
  • With your independent, dutiful and resilient nature
  • With your ability to work in a team and your forward-looking and solution-oriented way of working About us Barmelweid offers its more than 750 employees an attractive, interdisciplinary working environment in a family atmosphere.
