Spitex Upper Sea Valley – We need reinforcement!

The Spitex Oberes Seetal is the public Spitex of the municipalities Fahrwangen, Meisterschwanden, Sarmenstorf and Bettwil. With around 30 employees, we are there for our clients 365 days a year. Our goal is to help people to live in their own home in a self-determined and safe way for as long as possible. We are looking for a committed and versatile personality who likes to work independently, enjoys dealing with people and at the same time contributes as a team member. Employee/housekeeping and nursing assistant/in 40-50 % Their tasks

  • They support our clients in cleaning work, textile care and purchasing.
  • They independently carry out the planned care and support tasks within the scope of their competences.
  • They recognize the need for support and contribute to a pleasant everyday life.
  • They enjoy PC work and assignment planning.
  • They work closely with our interdisciplinary teams.

This is what they bring with them.

  • experience in cleaning private households; A basic course for home helpers in Spitex is an advantage.
  • Completed training as a nursing assistant (module 1 + 2).
  • Independent, reliable and responsible working methods.
  • Safe handling of digital media and enjoyment of administrative tasks.
  • Cat B driving licence and own vehicle.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (minor level C1).
  • Guidance of electronic client documentation and recording of services.
  • empathy, respectful behavior and joy in dealing with people.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Careful and personal introduction.
  • An open, appreciative and motivated team.
  • Varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility and direct contact with our clients.
  • Appreciative working environment and attractive employment conditions.
  • Various attractive employee advantages – you can find out more on our homepage.

We look forward to hearing from you! Send us your application – we are looking forward to meeting you personally!
