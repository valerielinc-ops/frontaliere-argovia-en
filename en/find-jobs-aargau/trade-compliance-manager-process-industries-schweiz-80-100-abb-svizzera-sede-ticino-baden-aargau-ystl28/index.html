At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Business Manager - E2E

__

In this role, you own the Trade Compliance framework for the Local Business Line (LBL) and are accountable for establishing, implementing, and continuously improving the policies, processes, controls, and governance required to ensure compliant execution of our global business activities. You ensure that all international business transactions, project deliveries, imports, exports, cross-border shipments, and related trade activities are conducted in accordance with ABB's Global Trade Program and applicable customs, export control, sanctions, and origin regulations. As the LBL's Trade Compliance authority, you define and maintain the operational trade compliance framework. You establish clear processes, responsibilities, controls, and escalation mechanisms and ensure effective implementation across all relevant functions. You drive the integration of trade compliance requirements into daily operations and act as the main point for complex compliance matters, regulatory changes, and risk mitigation. You are further responsible for developing, documenting, and maintaining procedures and training programs related to export controls, sanctions, customs, and origin management.

Working model is: #LI-Hybrid


You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Develop, improve and implement processes of LBL’s customs procedures, origin management, sanction lists screening, dual-use classification and export controls.

  • Functionally lead trade compliance resources in the LBL.

  • Lead and oversee Trade Compliance within the Local Business Line (LBL), including the implementation and continuous improvement of ABB’s Trade Compliance Program, internal directives, internal control system, local trade reviews, self-audits, and corrective actions.

  • Provide expert guidance and training on customs, origin, export control, and sanctions compliance, supporting the LBL, coordinating trade compliance resources and support functions, and maintaining strong alignment with key stakeholders such as CTCO, TCS, PCE, declarants, and logistics teams.

  • Drive Trade Compliance in day-to-day operations and complex projects, including risk assessments, internal checks, master data and supplier-related compliance requirements, and regular updates to stakeholders on implementation progress and relevant regulatory changes.

  • Advise and drive all trade compliance matters in LBL’s complex projects to different countries


What we are looking for:

  • Degree as a Foreign Trade Specialist / Manager or Export Control / Customs Compliance Officer with at least 8 years of experience in a relevant field (Senior Trade Compliance or Legal/Compliance background)

  • Excellent experience in free trade and preferential origins as well as in trade compliance matters

  • Solid knowledge in Swiss (GKV), EU and US (EAR) dual-use and sanctions regulations

  • Experience in an industry with large project businesses in various countries and ability as a people leader and solution-oriented team player

  • SAP and SAP GTS E4H experiences

  • Fluent in English, German is a plus
