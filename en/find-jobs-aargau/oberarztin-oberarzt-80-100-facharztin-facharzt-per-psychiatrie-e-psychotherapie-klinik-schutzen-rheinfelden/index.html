Full description

Attentive and competent. We are a private clinic for psychosomatic medicine, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds in a central location in Rheinfelden, as well as two outpatient clinics in Rheinfelden and Aarau. The clinic is recognized by the FMH as a postgraduate training institution for the specialist qualification in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years).

Our outpatient clinic in Aarau is located directly at the train station and has a large catchment area.

  • We offer adults outpatient psychiatric treatment and outpatient psychotherapy using various methodological approaches.

Position

For our outpatient clinic in Aarau, we are looking for, with immediate effect or by agreement, a

Senior Physician (80–100%) | Specialist in Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (Aarau Outpatient Clinic)

Responsibilities

  • Your varied responsibilities include psychotherapeutic case management and medical care of our patients, participation in the further development of the treatment services, and supervision of resident physicians and psychologists.
  • You play a significant role in shaping collaboration within an interprofessional team and have excellent teamwork and communication skills.

What we offer

  • We offer you an interesting, multidisciplinary work environment, centrally located, pleasant premises, excellent conditions for psychotherapeutic treatments and very attractive employment conditions based on a 43-hour working week with 6 weeks of vacation, a wide range of continuing education opportunities, as well as a vibrant workplace culture characterized by open exchange and varied events for employees.

Contact

For further information, our chief physician and clinic director, Dr. med. Katharina Gessler katharina.gessler@klinikschuetzen.ch , 061 836 26 62, will be happy to assist you.
