Full description

The actual location of this job is in Stein, AG, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

Every day, our teams across the world work together to turn scientific breakthroughs into life-changing medicines that reach up to 100 million patients a year. Lonza is a place where people can make a unique impact, grow their careers and belong.

As Associate Director, Tech Transfer & Lifecycle Lead you take ownership of critical customer programs and play a pivotal role in bringing innovative products to market, leading technology transfers, commercial launches and lifecycle management activities within a state-of-the-art Drug Product fill & finish manufacturing facilities. You'll provide expert technical leadership, work in cross-functional teams and shape the processes, validation strategies and manufacturing standards that ensure successful product launches, sustained process performance and operational excellence. Check out Your Career in Drug Product Services at Lonza!

This is a fully site‑based role. Working together in person supports close, real‑time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will do

  • Lead high-value and high-complexity Drug Product technology transfer and product lifecycle programs, driving the end-to-end journey from clinical development through process validation (PPQ), regulatory submission, commercialization, CPV/OPV and lifecycle management.
  • Act as the senior technical authority for complex customer programs, defining commercialization and validation strategies, providing technical governance, and serving as the escalation point for critical manufacturing, process and compliance challenges.
  • Lead the transfer, scale-up and implementation of Drug Product manufacturing processes into GMP operations, ensuring manufacturing readiness, successful commercial launch and sustainable routine production performance.
  • Develop and oversee Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 Process Validation strategies, including risk assessments, PPQ execution, statistical evaluations, trend analysis, process monitoring and post-approval change assessments.
  • Partner closely with customers and cross-functional stakeholders across Manufacturing, MSAT, QA, QC, Regulatory Affairs and Supply Chain to deliver program milestones, support regulatory submissions, and ensure inspection readiness for PAI and GMP audits.
  • Mentor Technology Transfer Leads and MSAT experts while driving continuous improvement and harmonization of SOPs, validation standards, CPV frameworks and lifecycle management processes across a growing global manufacturing network.

What we are looking for

  • Extensive experience in biopharma, pharmaceutical or CDMO environments, with deep expertise in Drug Product technology transfer, process validation and lifecycle management within aseptic fill & finish manufacturing.
  • Strong technical knowledge of aseptic processing, including biologics, with experience in technologies such as lyophilization, prefilled syringes, sterilization processes and primary packaging.
  • In-depth understanding of GMP, CMC and global regulatory requirements, with proven ability to support commercialization, regulatory submissions, validation activities and post-approval changes.
  • Demonstrated success in leading complex technology transfer and commercial launch programs, influencing cross-functional stakeholders and driving strategic decisions in fast-paced manufacturing environments.
  • Expertise in Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 Process Validation, including PPQ, CPV/OPV, process performance monitoring, statistical data analysis, trend evaluation and continuous process improvement.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder management and leadership skills, with the ability to mentor technical teams, navigate complex customer interactions and deliver impactful results across global and cross-site networks.
