02.09.2026 Würenlos Neuenhof Spreitenbach

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Würenlos serves customers between Baden and Zurich, in the Limmat Valley and the Furt Valley. Every day, our employees with empathy and professionalism find tailor-made solutions for different financial issues.

What awaits you?

  • You will accompany our customers with their financing requests regarding living and advise them holistically in the areas of assets, provision and security
  • You develop tailor-made solutions that are tailored to the individual needs and life situations of our customers
  • You actively and sustainably manage your customer base and recognize new potential
  • You gain new customers and further expand existing relationships
  • You support sales and marketing activities and actively contribute to their success
  • You represent our bank outside and maintain a strong local network

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking education
  • You have trained in banking or finance or would like to further develop in this area
  • You have absolutely necessary practical experience in financing consulting and ideally experience in corporate banking
  • You enjoy consulting, recognize customer needs and act sales-oriented
  • You work independently, reliably and accurately and appreciate cooperation in the team
  • You are characterized by commitment, flexibility and a high service orientation

Our values of sustainability, quality, passion, trust, respect, appreciation and clarity form the basis for our cooperation – in the team as well as with our customers. Trust, appreciation and passion are more than just words for us. We focus on partnership-based cooperation, promote initiative and support each other. We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote the different perspectives, experiences and competencies in our teams. Because this diversity makes us special and contributes decisively to our success.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.

We offer flexible working models

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.

We enable the purchase of additional holiday weeks

Our employees can purchase up to two additional holiday weeks per year in addition to the regular holiday days.
