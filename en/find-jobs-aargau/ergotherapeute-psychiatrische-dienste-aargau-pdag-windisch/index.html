Over the area

The Center for Psychiatry and Inpatient Psychotherapy is part of the Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at PDAG (Clinic Director Prof. Dr. med. Marc Walter). It is responsible for the modern acute psychiatric treatment of people between 18 and 64 years of age, which includes biological, psychotherapeutic and socio-medical applications and is based on open-door and no-restraint concepts. The center is dual by Dr. med. Maxim Zavorotnyy and Petra Meyer, Head of Care, and includes five stations. There is a close network for interventional psychiatry. For doctors, the centre is a SIWF-approved training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy category A inpatient (3 years) and for interventional psychiatry (rTMS and EKT).

Tasks

  • They perform individual and group therapies for people with mental health problems aged 18 to 64 in the acute and subacute inpatient psychiatric setting.
  • You bear the ergotherapeutic responsibility and are the first contact person for two stations with the above-mentioned focuses
  • They implement the ergotherapeutic treatment (focus on social psychiatry and psychosis treatment) in an evidence-based and needs-oriented manner. You maintain a technically sound documentation
  • They contribute proactively to the conceptual development of ergotherapeutic treatment at the Concentration Station
  • They are part of the intra- and interprofessional team and actively participate in the implementation of the therapeutic goals within the framework of the overall treatment
  • They contribute professionally, including in interprofessional reports, team meetings and supervision

Profile

  • You have completed studies or training in occupational therapy (BSc, FH, HF)
  • They are distinguished by professional experience in the same or comparable environment and ideally have clinical experience in the field of psychiatry
  • You will preferably have experience in carrying out therapeutic interventions in psychosis treatment and/or social psychiatry. Or they have a special interest in the topic and are open to further education.
  • In-depth knowledge of ergotherapeutic models and their assessments is desired
  • They think and act in a networked and systemic way, work innovatively, independently and team-oriented
