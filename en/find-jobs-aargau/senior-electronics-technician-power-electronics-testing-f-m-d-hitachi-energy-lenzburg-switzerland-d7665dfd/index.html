Full description

The opportunity

The Gate Unit Group develops, qualifies, supports production, and manages the lifecycle of gate units for IGCT devices. We translate semiconductor, converter, and system requirements into robust and reliable products, covering hardware design, embedded firmware, manufacturing, and testing.

To strengthen our team in Lenzburg, we are looking for a Senior Electronics Technician – Power Electronics & Testing who enjoys combining hands-on technical work with engineering problem-solving and wants to contribute to innovative power electronics products throughout the development lifecycle.

This position offers an excellent opportunity to develop technical expertise in power electronics and semiconductor applications while working in a collaborative and innovative engineering environment. You will gain exposure to the full product lifecycle, from development and testing through production support, and contribute to technologies that help shape the future of efficient and sustainable energy systems.

How you'll make an impact

  • Support the development of gate units by collaborating with development engineers on analog and digital circuit design, schematic capture, and PCB layout activities
  • Maintain laboratory equipment, test infrastructure, and inventory, ensuring calibration, availability, and a safe, well-organized work environment
  • Contribute to the implementation, testing, and validation of electronic hardware solutions
  • Coordinate with suppliers and EMS partners regarding component sourcing, procurement activities, order management, and shipment handling
  • Design, build, and maintain test fixtures, automated test systems, and data acquisition solutions to enable efficient and reliable product validation
  • Perform hands-on debugging, repair, and rework of electronic assemblies to ensure product quality and reliability
  • Contribute ideas, identify opportunities for improvement, and support the continuous enhancement of products, processes, and testing capabilities

Your background

  • Completed apprenticeship, technician qualification, or bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • 3-5 years of experience in electronics development, testing, manufacturing, or validation; alternatively, recent graduates with a bachelor's degree and a strong technical foundation combined with a passion for hands-on engineering are also encouraged to apply
  • Understanding of analog and digital electronics and electronic measurement techniques
  • Interest in both hardware and software development
  • Practical, hands-on, and solution-oriented approach to technical challenges
  • Experience with Altium Designer and/or KiCad is an advantage
  • Python programming skills and knowledge of SAP, are a plus
  • Fluent German language skills (written and spoken) and the ability to communicate effectively in English in a professional environment are required
