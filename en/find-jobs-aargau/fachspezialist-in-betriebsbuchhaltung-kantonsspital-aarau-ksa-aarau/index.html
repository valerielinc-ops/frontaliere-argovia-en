Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the preparation, analysis and quality assurance of business data as well as for the timely execution of the monthly accounts according to REKOLE®.
  • Co-responsibility for the preparation of annual data collections and tariff bases (ITAR K, SpiGes, BFS) using HCe TIP.
  • Ensuring high data quality through the further development of financial control instruments, in particular cost centre, cost carrier and profit centre calculation, as well as through the implementation of plausibility checks.
  • Close cooperation with Business Controlling as a competent contact person for business issues according to REKOLE®.
  • Participation in the optimization, standardization and digitization of processes, evaluations and reporting instruments.

Requirements

  • Completed business studies (FH/university) with a focus on controlling or accounting, expert in accounting and controlling or an equivalent qualification.
  • Several years of professional experience in company accounting or controlling, ideally in the Swiss health care system.
  • Very good SAP knowledge with a focus on CO as well as excellent Excel knowledge
  • Experience with HCe TIP is beneficial.
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills, high numerical affinity and enjoyment of complex business contexts.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented personality with a high level of initiative and a strong team orientation.
