With heart, expertise and pleasure in training – shape the care of tomorrow with us! To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and responsible personality as Specialist Health EFZ with additional responsibility in vocational training. Your tasks

  • Professional and individual care and support of our residents / clients.
  • Ensuring high-quality and needs-oriented care.
