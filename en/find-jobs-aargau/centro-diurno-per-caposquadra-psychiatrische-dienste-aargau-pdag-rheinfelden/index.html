Full description

For our day center in Rheinfelden, we are looking for a committed and creative leader immediately or by appointment as tasks:

  • You will manage the day center professionally, organizationally and economically and will be actively involved in everyday life
  • You will lead a small nursing team (3 people) and work closely with interdisciplinary specialists
  • You will work with the patients to create a varied daily routine with creative group offers and activities
  • You will recognize crisis situations early on and act professionally and solution-oriented
  • You will network with relatives, referring physicians, employers and external specialist agencies Requirements:
  • Qualified HF/FH nursing specialist with experience in psychiatry, ideally in a social psychiatric environment and creative flair
  • Leadership experience or willingness to take on leadership responsibility
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration and creative group offerings
  • Initiative, social skills, flexibility and resilience We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. That's exactly why we offer you a lot: we strongly encourage further training. We also invest a lot in team cohesion and offer all of our teams access to the “Moodtalk” software, which strengthens team communication. We also offer attractive discounts on well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport receive an impressive eco-bonus at the end of the year. We also offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy a holiday.
