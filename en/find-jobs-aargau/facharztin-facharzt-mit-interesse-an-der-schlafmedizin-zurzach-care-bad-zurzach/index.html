Full description

Our Clinic for Sleep Medicine (KSM) in Bad Zurzach (from 1 September 2026 in Baden) is a specialized institution for the prevention, diagnosis and therapy of sleep disorders of all kinds. Patients aged six and over are diagnosed individually and then treated on an outpatient basis. The aim is to lead people to a healthy and restful sleep as quickly and sustainably as possible.

Their tasks

  • Diagnosis: implementation and supervision of sleep medical diagnostics (e.g. polysomnography, polygraphy) as well as creation of individual treatment concepts
  • Medical care: Intensive and often years of support for our patients so that they can live healthier and with a higher quality of life again

Your profile

  • Qualifications: Swiss Specialist Degree in Pneumology, Internal Medicine, Psychiatry, Neurology or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition
  • Interest: You bring interest in sleep medicine. Prior experience or qualifications are desired, but can also be acquired at the location.
  • Personality: A creative, team-capable, implementation-strong and communicative personality with a structured, independent working method as well as the competence to actively develop interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation.

This is what we offer you.

  • Co-creation: Participate in a modern working environment and actively participate in a future-oriented company
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive, appreciative and small team that works together for the best possible patient care and lives flat hierarchies
  • Work-Life Balance: Regulated working hours and openness for part-time work, job sharing or other suitable forms of work
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Scientific activity: We support active participation in research projects
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Take advantage of discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
