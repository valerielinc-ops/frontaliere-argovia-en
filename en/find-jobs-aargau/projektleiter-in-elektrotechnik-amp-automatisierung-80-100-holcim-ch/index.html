You want to make a difference? Then you are right at Holcim. In our team you can contribute your ideas and actively shape them. We are looking for a motivated and powerful personality for exciting tasks in our project department at the cement plant Siggenthal.

Your field of activity

• You are responsible for planning, controlling and implementing the electrical and automation part of investment projects in cooperation with internal specialist departments and external partners. • You are responsible for the entire electrotechnical and automation scope – from medium voltage to MCCs and sensors to automation with SIMATIC STEP 7 and the connection to higher-level expert systems. • You take responsibility for the cost, deadline and quality management of your projects and ensure efficient implementation • In close coordination with the procurement team, you compare offers, are part of the contract negotiations with suppliers, clients and subcontractors and make informed decisions • Ensure compliance with all contractor safety management processes during assembly and commissioning • As an internal contact, you advise specialist departments on all questions of project management

Your profile
