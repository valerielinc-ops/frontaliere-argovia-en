Full description

The rehabilitation clinic in Baden-Dättwil offers rehabilitation at a high medical level for musculoskeletal and neurological patients. Thanks to the interdisciplinary cooperation and the excellent expertise of the treatment teams, we adapt our efficient therapy programs individually to the needs of these patients.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: Take over the diagnosis, treatment and clarification of inpatient (supervised) and outpatient patients with musculoskeletal and neurological diseases in individual and/or group setting
  • Counselling and guidance as well as auxiliary care: You take over the instruction and guidance for ADL training of patients and their carers
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration: Work independently in an interdisciplinary team with interfaces to the medical profession, nursing and various therapeutic disciplines such as physiotherapy and speech therapy

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as an occupational therapist or equivalent training with at least one positive SRK PreCheck
  • Personality: You are an enthusiastic and communicative personality with a sense of humor. You convince through a sense of responsibility and high-quality work, can set priorities effectively and do not lose sight of the needs of our patients.

We offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
