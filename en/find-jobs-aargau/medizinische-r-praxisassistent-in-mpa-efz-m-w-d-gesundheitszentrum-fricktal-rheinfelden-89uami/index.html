Health Center Fricktal AG | online since : 11.08.2026

Location: IAVO Frick

You value the personal contact with the patients: inside and an individual care is important to you? Laboratory, triage, office assistant, infusions - the diversity of the profession of an MPA gives you pleasure? Do you stay calm in hectic situations? Would you like to become part of a growing network of modern practices and a member of the entire GZF team? Then you are exactly right with us! We look forward to your reinforcement.

  • You look after our patients before, during and after the examination with high level of expertise and empathy
  • You coordinate, plan and assist the office hours and contribute your strong organizational strength.
  • You will independently carry out blood tests, laboratory analyses, infusion therapies and other tests such as ECG, 24-hour blood pressure measurement and lung function tests
  • You perform X-ray images while ensuring careful, safe and high quality imaging
  • You take on administrative tasks, create billing according to TARDOC and work routinely with the practice software Axenita
  • You answer telephone inquiries competently and service-oriented – always with a friendly attitude and a high degree of customer orientation
  • You have completed training as a medical assistant: in EFZ or an equivalent qualification
  • You bring several years of professional experience in everyday practice and stay calm and sovereign even in unexpected situations
  • You communicate clearly, empathetically and professionally thanks to your very good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • You are perceived as an open, warm personality who likes to work in a team and also enjoys independent work.
  • You feel comfortable in a lively practical environment, react flexibly to changing requirements and actively contribute to a positive, collegial working environment
