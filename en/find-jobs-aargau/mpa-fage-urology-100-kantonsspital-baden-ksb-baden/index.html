Full description

In the urological ambulatory, you accompany patients through diagnostic and therapeutic interventions – from preparation to follow-up care. You support the medical team in everyday life, keep an overview of the office hours and contribute your technical skills to small procedures. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Together with the medical team, they accompany the urological interventions under varied conditions – whether with or without X-rays or anesthesia and ensure a smooth process.
  • They take samples, prepare them professionally and reliably forward them for analysis.
  • You design the varied everyday life with your organizational talent, prepare consultations, take responsibility in day-to-day business and thus create a smooth process.
  • In cooperation with the medical team, they triage all emergencies and consultancies and integrate everything into the daily routine in a structured manner.
  • They support the medical team during the consultation and carry out minor medical measures independently.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a medical assistant or health specialist.
  • They prefer a regulated activity from Monday to Friday.
  • They bring a quick comprehension, technical flair and manual skill.
  • Experience in dealing with endoscopes is an advantage – or you are ready to get involved.
  • They are flexible, communicative and respond empathetically to different people and situations.
  • They value a family team and like to work interdisciplinary.
