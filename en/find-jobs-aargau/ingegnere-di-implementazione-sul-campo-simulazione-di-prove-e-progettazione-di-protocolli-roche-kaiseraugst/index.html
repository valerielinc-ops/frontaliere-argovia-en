The Position As a Digital Solution Lead - Trial Simulation & Protocol Design (aka Forward Deployment Engineer) you will operate at the intersection of Clinical Science, Product, Data Science, AI, and Engineering to translate complex scientific and clinical challenges into innovative digital solutions. You will partner directly with clinical scientists, medical writers, biostatisticians, and clinical study teams to understand real-world challenges, reimagine workflows, and develop AI-powered technology solutions that transform clinical development. Combining deep scientific expertise with strong technical capabilities and a forward deployment engineering mindset, you will take ownership from problem discovery and solution design through rapid prototyping, technical validation, integration, and deployment. You will work closely with engineering teams to turn innovative concepts into scalable, production-ready solutions. You will also serve as a strategic thought leader, identifying opportunities to leverage Generative AI, intelligent agents, and automation to fundamentally transform clinical research and study design.

The Opportunity

Forward Deployment & Solution Engineering

  • Partner directly with clinical and scientific teams to understand complex workflows, identify unmet needs, and translate challenges into technology solutions.
  • Work closely with end users to discover problems, challenge existing processes, and identify opportunities for digital transformation.
  • Rapidly prototype, experiment, and validate solutions, incorporating user feedback into iterative development.
  • Bridge scientific, business, and technical teams, translating complex requirements into actionable technical designs and deployable solutions.
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to take solutions from concept through development, integration, deployment, and production readiness.
  • Identify opportunities to scale successful solutions into reusable enterprise capabilities.

Clinical Science & Domain Expertise

  • Apply deep understanding of clinical science, clinical research, and clinical trial design to solve complex scientific and operational challenges.
  • Understand clinical protocols, study objectives, endpoints, eligibility criteria, patient populations, treatment strategies, and study assessments.
  • Partner with clinical scientists, biostatisticians, and medical writers to translate scientific and statistical concepts into structured digital capabilities.
  • Understand clinical development processes, data standards, and regulatory considerations.
  • Challenge traditional approaches to clinical study design and identify opportunities to improve quality, efficiency, and decision-making through technology.

AI-Native Solution Development & Innovation

  • Identify and develop opportunities to embed Generative AI, Agentic AI, machine learning, and intelligent automation into clinical research workflows.
  • Design and prototype AI-powered solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and agentic workflows.
  • Explore intelligent agents capable of orchestrating complex workflows, generating structured content, validating information, and supporting scientific decision-making.
  • Apply appropriate AI models, frameworks, and architectures to solve domain-specific problems.
  • Evaluate AI solutions for accuracy, reliability, explainability, traceability, and responsible use in regulated environments.
  • Move promising AI innovations from experimentation and proof-of-concept into production-ready applications.

Technical Architecture & Engineering Collaboration

  • Work alongside software architects, AI engineers, data engineers, and full-stack developers to translate business and scientific needs into technical solutions.
  • Demonstrate hands-on technical capabilities to independently explore problems, develop prototypes, and validate technical feasibility.
  • Bring strong technical fluency across Python, SQL, APIs, data models, and modern application architectures.
  • Understand cloud technologies, microservices, system integration, data pipelines, and enterprise interoperability.
  • Drive integration and collaboration across multiple applications, platforms, and technology ecosystems.
  • Troubleshoot complex functional, data, and technical challenges and work across teams to resolve them.
  • Ensure solutions are scalable, secure, maintainable, and aligned with enterprise technology standards.

Strategic Leadership & Stakeholder Management

  • Serve as a trusted technology and domain partner to senior scientific, business, product, and engineering stakeholders.
  • Shape solution strategies and technology roadmaps based on evolving scientific needs and emerging technology trends.
  • Lead cross-functional teams in a matrix environment, bringing together scientific, product, data, and engineering expertise.
  • Balance immediate user needs with long-term enterprise scalability and platform strategy.
  • Influence decisions through clear communication of complex scientific and technical concepts.
  • Foster a culture of experimentation, innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Who You Are

  • PhD in Clinical Science, Clinical Research, Biostatistics, Biomedical Sciences, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, or a related scientific discipline.
  • Strong understanding of clinical development, clinical research, and clinical trial design.
  • Ability to engage credibly with clinical scientists, researchers, biostatisticians, and medical writers.
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or life sciences industry is strongly preferred.
  • Familiarity with clinical data standards, regulatory frameworks, and structured clinical data is an advantage.
  • Proven experience translating complex scientific or business problems into technology-enabled solutions.
  • Demonstrated ability to work directly with end users, understand workflows, and rapidly prototype and validate solutions.
  • Hands-on programming and technical experience, preferably with Python, SQL, APIs, and data engineering.
  • Understanding of modern software architectures, cloud technologies, application integration, and enterprise systems.
  • Experience working closely with engineering teams to deliver end-to-end digital solutions.
  • Ability to operate effectively across problem discovery, solution architecture, development, deployment, and adoption.
  • Demonstrated experience applying Generative AI, LLMs, and AI/ML techniques to real-world problems.
  • Strong understanding of agentic AI, multi-agent architectures, RAG, prompt engineering, and AI workflow orchestration.
  • Experience building AI prototypes and proof-of-concepts using Python and relevant AI frameworks.
  • Understanding of AI evaluation, model validation, explainability, human-in-the-loop approaches, and responsible AI.
  • Ability to identify opportunities where AI can fundamentally reimagine workflows and decision-making..
