Full description

In the KSB hospital pharmacy team, you produce medicines for the internal clinics and other institutions in the Canton of Aargau. In the central cytostatic preparation, you work with modern devices in the clean room and produce parenteral anti-tumor therapies precisely and safely. Your careful working method contributes directly to ensuring that patients receive their individually prescribed therapy reliably. The position is available as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You produce parenteral anti-tumor therapies in the clean room with the CATO software and comply with the specified quality and safety requirements.
  • You manage the medicines used in the warehouse management program and take over accounting, inventory and control of the expiration dates.
  • You take on administrative and organizational tasks, such as billing patients or microbiological monitoring.
  • If necessary, you support your colleagues in sterile and non-sterile production.
  • You work very carefully and document your work steps reliably.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a pharmaceutical assistant, drugist, medical assistant, nurse or a comparable qualification.
  • Ideally, you will have several years of professional experience in the aseptic production of medicines.
  • You have an advantage over experience with the CATO software.
  • You work very accurately, cleanly and responsibly and reliably adhere to defined processes and guidelines.
  • You stay focused and reliable even in high workload.
  • You enjoy working in a team and are flexible in different tasks.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
