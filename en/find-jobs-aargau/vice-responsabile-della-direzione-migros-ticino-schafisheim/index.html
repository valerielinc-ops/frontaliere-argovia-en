Full description

In our up-and-coming VOI Migros partner branch in Schafisheim, we are looking for a deputy to the management. They are the driving force in our team, shaping customer relationships and optimizing processes. With your commitment, you make shopping for our customers an unforgettable experience.

Luogo di lavoro: VOI, VOI Schafisheim, Seetalstrasse 113, 5503 Schafisheim

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, ensuring availability and goods management are important to you
  • In the absence of the management, you are responsible for the professional and personnel responsibility of the branch
  • The responsibility for order and cleanliness in the work area lies with you
  • They are actively involved in all areas of expertise

Requirements

  • A few years of professional experience: in the retail trade, preferably in the supermarket
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade specialization food and beverages
  • As a customer-focused personality with pleasure in sales, make shopping a positive experience
  • They bring reliability, flexibility and high operational readiness
  • Your self-employment and forward-looking and precise working methods distinguish you
  • They bring readiness for flexible work assignments (without Sundays / holidays) and assignments on Saturday
  • Ideally, you already have leadership experience
  • in German (negotiation certain)
