Full description

For reinforcement we are looking for you as from 1 October 2026 or by agreement

Tasks

  • Management of the website and intranet as well as responsibility for its strategic and technical development
  • Professional responsibility for the internal communication platform "Beekeeper"
  • Project management for web and intranet projects
  • Management of the web agency and coordination of agency, internal IT and other suppliers
  • Supervision of our web and intranet editorial team (support, training), creation of instructions
  • Writing and optimizing website texts
  • Managing our Google company profile and publishing social media posts
  • Creating newsletters
  • Monitoring, controlling and reporting of online communication measures

Requirements

  • Successfully completed education or training in the field of digital or online marketing
  • Experience in a similar position desirable
  • Sound knowledge in the areas of UX, AI, SEO, content marketing, accessibility and online data protection
  • Knowledge of CMS (ideally TYPO3)
  • Knowledge of Adobe Acrobat Pro and/or InDesign as well as Google Search Console and GA4, plus Matomo
  • Familiarity with the communication platform "Beekeeper" is an advantage
  • Good experience in newsletter tools
  • Strong organisational, communication and team skills as well as the pleasure of using them in daily work
  • Very good written and oral language
