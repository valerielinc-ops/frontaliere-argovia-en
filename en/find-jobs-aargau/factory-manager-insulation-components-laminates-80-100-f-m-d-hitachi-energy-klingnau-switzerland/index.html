Full description

The opportunity

Our factory in Klingnau, Switzerland is available to our customers as a competence center for high-precision composite processing at world level. The location thrives on a flexible and motivated team with a flat hierarchy and short decision-making paths. Through the constant expansion of our competencies in the field of composite processing as well as through the continuous improvement of our business processes, we offer our customers innovative, reliable, individual and safe solutions for their concerns in the power generation/distribution, railway, space, service and industry segments. We provide these at reasonable prices, on time and in the desired quality. We are securing our competitiveness by automating and optimizing our production lines in order to be able to respond even faster and more flexibly to global market and customer requirements.

Would you like to become part of our agile team and be able to directly shape and experience the entire value chain from customer enquiries to production and delivery? Then we look forward to receiving your application!

How you'll make an impact

  • Managing overall manufacturing operations, including staff
  • Leading and champion a zero-accident/ safety first culture, ensuring full compliance with all HSE regulations and fostering a safe and environmentally responsible work environment (license to operate)
  • Ensuring achievement of operational targets, including operating cash flow, project delivery (on-time and within budget), Customer on-time delivery (COTD), and cost reduction
  • Monitoring and controlling the factory's financial budget and ensuring financial targets are met without compromising quality or safety standards
  • Exploring and integrating new technologies and innovations to enhance production capabilities and meet evolving industry demands. Encourage a forward-thinking mindset within the team to stay ahead of technological advancements
  • Establishing robust quality assurance programs, ensuring adherence to industry standards and company specifications. Drive quality improvement through target setting, monitoring and benchmarking to continuously improve processes
  • Driving the implementation of continuous improvement methodologies to increase productivity and reduce costs to improve safety, quality, delivery and cost
  • Maintaining strong relationships with key customers by ensuring timely delivery of high-quality products. Address customer feedback promptly to continuously improve customer satisfaction
  • Living Hitachi Energy's core values of safety and integrity, which means taking responsibility for your own actions while caring for your colleagues and the business

Your background

  • A bachelor's degree in engineering, Manufacturing, Business Administration, or a related field
  • 10 years of experience in manufacturing management
  • Strong understanding of manufacturing processes, quality control, and safety standards
  • Proven ability to lead and inspire teams, drive engagement, and achieve high performance
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to manage complex situations and resolve issues
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor and develop team members, implementing training and development programs that enhance skills and promote career growth
  • Proficiency in both spoken & written German and English language is required
