Full description

For our hospital we are looking for a committed personality as team leader patient admission & reception. In this versatile function, you combine leadership with active participation in day-to-day business. You lead a team of around 25 employees both professionally and organizationally. They work actively in the team and ensure that our processes for patient reception and reception function reliably and patient-oriented.

Their tasks

  • Operational management and management of the team (24-hour shift operation) in cooperation with the group management
  • Active participation in day-to-day business and assumption of services, especially in the event of bottlenecks and unforeseen situations
  • Ensuring data quality and standardised processes
  • Planning of personnel assignments and responsibility for annual and operational planning including overtime and compensation management
  • Ensuring quality of service on the treatment pathway
  • Professional and organisational development of the team
  • Interdisciplinary participation in projects
  • Close cooperation with the Head of Clinical Support, Finance and other internal and external contacts

Your profile

  • A commercial basic education or medical basic education supplemented by an administrative further education
  • Social security training
  • Good knowledge of the procedures and processes in an acute hospital as well as experience in patient reception and process management
  • Ideally, you will have leadership training at the level of team leadership and/or experience in training learners
  • A pronounced service and customer orientation in dealing with patients, relatives and internal and external contacts
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office, PEP, Business Central and KISIM
  • Very good knowledge of German with stylish oral and written language
  • Foreign language skills are an advantage

Your opportunity As of January 1, 2027 or by appointment, you can expect a varied and demanding key role at an important interface in the hospital with a focus on teamwork. The interdisciplinary cooperation offers space for self-responsible work and internal and external training opportunities. Patient admission and reception are often the first contact to our house – They play a decisive role in shaping this contact.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented if possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 80-100 %
