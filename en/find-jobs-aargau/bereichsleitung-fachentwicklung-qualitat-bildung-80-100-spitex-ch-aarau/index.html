Full description

Spitex Region Aarau: Health in the best hands! Working together for a better quality of life The Spitex Region Aarau is one of the leading providers of outpatient care in the region and provides its services on behalf of the municipalities. Our dedicated and qualified employees are the heart of our organization. They make a valuable contribution every day – for the health, independence and quality of life of people in all phases of life.

We search by 1 October or by agreement Head of Department Development, Quality & Education (80-100%) We are in an exciting development phase. For the further development of our organization, we are creating the new function Divisional Management Specialist Development, Quality & Education. In this key role, you actively shape the professional future of the Spitex Region Aarau and contribute to ensuring that our care remains innovative, effective and high-quality tomorrow. As a member of the management team, you participate in the strategic decisions of the organization. Your task They are responsible for the professional, qualitative and conceptual development of the Spitex Region Aarau. Where:

  • develop our care, services and care concepts in an evidence-based and future-oriented way
  • Responsible for quality management and the further development of standards, concepts and guidelines
  • initiate and lead organization-wide development and change projects
  • promote innovation and accompany the introduction of new supply models
  • ensure the transfer of knowledge as well as the strategic orientation of education, training and further education
  • Support and advise leaders and teams on complex technical issues
  • manage specialist groups and responsible persons, both professionally and partly in terms of personnel
  • represent the Spitex Region Aarau in expert committees and maintain important networks in the healthcare sector
  • As a member of the Executive Board, you share responsibility for the strategic development of the organization

Your profile They combine a high level of nursing expertise with strategic thinking and enjoyment of organizational development. They shall have at their disposal:

  • Completed training as nurse FH or HF
  • further education at university level in nursing science, specialist development, advanced practice nursing or a comparable field
  • Several years of experience in specialist development as well as in managing projects and change processes
  • In-depth experience in ambulatory healthcare, ideally in the Spitex
  • In-depth knowledge of quality management and evidence-based nursing development
  • Leadership experience and a modern understanding of leadership in the sense of Servant Leadership
  • High level of communication, consulting and networking competence
  • Enjoy developing people, teams and organizations

Experience in vocational training is an advantage. This is what awaits you

  • a newly created key position with a large design scope
  • Cooperation at management level
  • the opportunity to actively shape the future professional orientation of one of the larger Spitex organizations in the Canton of Aargau
  • Short decision-making paths and an appreciative corporate culture
  • Modern working conditions and advanced employment conditions
  • Flexible working models and the opportunity to buy a holiday
  • Attractive training and development opportunities
  • a dedicated team that sees change as an opportunity

Interested? We look forward to your application.
