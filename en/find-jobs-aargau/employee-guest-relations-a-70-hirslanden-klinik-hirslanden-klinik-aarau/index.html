Reference number: 75-190

Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for top medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "first-class medicine with personal treatment as we wish for our loved ones".

YOUR TASKS

  • You care for our private patients in non-medical matters, organize and fulfill the wishes and needs of private patients
  • You receive and leave our private patients and are responsible for ensuring patient satisfaction and for supporting the departments in complaints
  • You have a good working environment and an optimal cooperation with all departments
  • Administrative activities and management and active work with CRM complete your task profile

YOUR PROFILE

  • You have completed a training in the field of hotel industry or some years of professional experience in the field of guest relations
  • You are a communicative, service-oriented and contact-friendly personality
  • You are skilled in the use of modern IT infrastructure and have an independent, reliable and customer-oriented working method
  • You like to work in a team and can be used flexibly – your 70% workload is spread over 5 working days
  • We presupposed a knowledge of German in word and writing, each further language is advantageous
