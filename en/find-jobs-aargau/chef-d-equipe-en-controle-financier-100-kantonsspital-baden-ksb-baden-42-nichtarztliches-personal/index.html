Full description

They play a central role in the financial and performance-related management of the Canton Hospital Baden. With your team, you create transparency about financial, performance and personnel data and support the clinical areas in informed decisions. You combine analytical thinking with a good understanding of the requirements of a hospital and develop controlling together with your team. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • They coordinate the budget process and support the clinical departments in budgeting.
  • You create financial, performance and investment planning as well as forecasts and scenario analyses.
  • They develop controlling instruments and reporting solutions and conduct controlling discussions with the clinical departments.
  • They use data analytics and AI applications to specifically comment on management reports and evaluate financial and performance data.
  • You work on digitization and BI projects and drive the automation of controlling processes.
  • They create and test economic calculations and monitor the economics of medical services.
  • They analyze outpatient and stationary performance data as well as tariffs and revenues.
  • They support management, HR and line managers in the economic management of personnel costs and supervise the business case.

Your profile

  • You have a degree in business administration, finance, controlling, business informatics or a comparable education.
  • They understand data models and data architectures and have experience with BI and analytics solutions.
  • They have several years of professional experience in controlling, ideally in the health care or hospital environment.
  • You are familiar with BI solutions such as Power BI, Qlik or Databricks and are interested in new technologies and AI applications.
  • They think analytically and conceptually, work accurately and have a high number affinity.
  • You communicate clearly and advise your contact persons competently.
  • They are binding, implement goals consistently and enjoy working with others.
  • You are a trustworthy, well-established personality and have very good knowledge of German in words and writing.
