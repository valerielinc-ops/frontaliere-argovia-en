Product Manager:in Microsoft Surface und Monitore — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • You assume operational responsibility for the Microsoft Surface and LCD range and continuously develop it along our category strategy
  • You always have an eye on sales, contribution margin and other relevant key figures and steer your portfolio specifically towards the defined budget goals
  • In close cooperation with Microsoft and our suppliers, you conduct negotiations and secure attractive conditions
  • You use market and trend analyses to identify new potential, products and sources of supply at an early stage.
  • With regard to product range and article management, you control the process from the article opening and master data maintenance to the implementation of the pricing strategy across all sales channels.
  • Together with purchasing, you implement the procurement strategy and keep an eye on inventory, target ranges and automated ordering processes.
  • Manufacturer invoices, bonuses, credit notes and claims you process reliably and ensure the necessary reporting
  • Together with marketing and sales, you will develop effective marketing and communication priorities for your range
  • As a central interface, you connect Microsoft and other suppliers with sales, marketing, purchasing and accounting
  • Regulatory and legal requirements for your product range are reliably ensured

Requirements

  • 2–3 years of professional experience in product management or in a comparable product and commercial function
  • Good knowledge of the IT market as well as enthusiasm for products, technology and commercial connections
  • Experience in dealing with key figures such as sales, contribution margin, margin or pricing and ideally in the management of a range or product portfolio
  • Negotiation skills and communication skills in exchange with manufacturers, suppliers and internal stakeholders
  • A good sense of market trends and the needs of our customers
  • Independent and efficient working methods combined with initiative and a high sense of responsibility
  • Analytical and networked thinking as well as an entrepreneurial approach
  • Solution-oriented personality who keeps an eye on priorities even in a dynamic environment
  • Good MS-Office knowledge and safe German in word and writing
  • Very good knowledge of English for cooperation with international partners

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
