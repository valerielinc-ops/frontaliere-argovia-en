Full description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Division Global Head of Service Excellence

__

The Global Division Service Automation & Care Manager is responsible for delivering lifecycle value across the PI installed base through Automation modernization initiatives and Care Agreements.

The role owns the visibility and management of the installed base lifecycle, working closely with Automation Managers, Product Managers, Service Managers, and Business Lines.

The position contributes to growth strategies in close alignment with Process Industries Automation, Service Managers, and Product Managers.

Location Flexibility:

We welcome applications from qualified candidates across all locations globally. Hiring decisions are based on skills, experience, and business needs rather than the locations displayed.

Key • Responsibilities:

1. Installed Base & Lifecycle Ownership

  • Increase visibility and understanding of the Automation and PI installed base across Business Lines.
  • Define lifecycle and segmentation approaches to identify growth opportunities, risks, and modernization needs. Identify and accelerate automation modernization opportunities across the installed base.
  • Promote best practices in lifecycle management and installed base development across the organization and facilitate cross-functional alignment and standardization to support sustainable growth.
  • Partner with Product Management, Service, and Business Line teams to develop scalable lifecycle offerings.

2. ASM & Care Portfolio Leadership

  • Oversee the evolution and growth of the ASM and Care portfolio, ensuring clear positioning and customer value.
  • Grow ASM adoption and expansion in partnership with Product Management, Service teams, and Business Lines.
  • Establish governance and visibility for contracts and renewals, and reshape the portfolio value proposition and performance.

3. Program Leadership

  • Lead the Care & ASM workstream within the DCS Recovery Program.
  • Define priorities, KPIs, governance, and stakeholder alignment to deliver measurable business impact.
  • Support initiatives that increase Care penetration, ASM adoption, and protection of the installed base.

4. Service Portfolio & Business Line Enablement

  • Build a consistent service portfolio structure and value proposition across Process Industries.
  • Support initiatives that improve service delivery, portfolio adoption, and operational excellence.
  • Support the transition toward value-based customer engagements and scalable service solutions.

5. Performance Management & KPIs

  • Define and monitor key performance indicators that support lifecycle growth and service excellence.
  • Track performance across Care, ASM, modernization, renewals, and pipeline development.
  • Use data-driven insights to identify improvement opportunities and take corrective actions.

Required Capabilities & Personal Attributes:

To thrive in this role, you bring significant experience within Industrial Automation, gained over many years working with automation systems, lifecycle services, modernization programs, or service portfolio management. You have a solid understanding of how to create long-term value from an installed base and know how to translate strategy into measurable business results.

You are a commercially minded leader who combines strategic thinking with a practical approach to execution. Navigating complex, global matrix organizations comes naturally to you, and you are comfortable aligning with stakeholders across different functions, levels, and geographies, even when you do not have direct authority.

You are valued for building good relationships, creating alignment, and helping implement change through collaboration. Your customer-centric mindset enables you to shift conversations from activities and products to outcomes and value. You are equally comfortable discussing portfolio strategy with senior leaders and working with operational teams to turn plans into action.

Being effective in this position requires an organized and structured approach, solid ownership, and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously. You are motivated by continuous improvement, driven by cross-functional collaboration, and focused on delivering sustainable growth through innovation, service excellence, and operational discipline.
