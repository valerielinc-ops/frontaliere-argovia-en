Full description

Pensum: 100 %

Become part of Group IT at Axpo Services AG in Baden and help shape the cybersecurity of a dynamic, international energy company. We value teamwork, curiosity and the courage to tackle and implement things together.

This is what awaits you

  • Monitor networks, systems and applications for security breaches and intrusions using security tools and platforms.
  • You will assist in detecting, analyzing and resolving security incidents – including downstream investigations and reporting.
  • You pre-analyse security messages and assess their potential impact.
  • You can help identify, track and fix infrastructure vulnerabilities.
  • You can help deploy, configure and maintain security tools such as firewalls, SIEM, IDS/IPS, EDR, threat intelligence and the Azure security environment.
  • You carefully document security incidents, procedures and processes, and work with IT and other teams to implement security measures.

This is what you bring and this is how you are

  • You have a bachelor’s degree in computer science, cyber security or a related field – or equivalent practical experience.
  • You have experience in security operations, ideally in an enterprise environment. You will also bring basic knowledge of cybersecurity, networks, firewalls and security protocols.
  • You’ll be familiar with SIEM tools like Azure Sentinel or Splunk, as well as endpoint protection solutions like Microsoft Defender for Endpoint.
  • You communicate well, enjoy working in a team and are motivated to learn new things and face new challenges.
  • Ideally, you have relevant certifications, such as CompTIA Security+, Certified Ethical Hacker or Microsoft Certified: Security Operations Analyst. Experience with Azure security tools such as Defender for Cloud or software insights, such as in Python or Bash, are also beneficial.
  • You speak English and German
  • Knowledge of Spanish is a plus.

Even if you do not meet all requirements, we look forward to your application.

About the team

Group IT is part of the COO area. Our colleagues work at various locations in Switzerland and abroad. We work closely together, support each other and bring in different backgrounds and perspectives to strengthen our security situation together.
