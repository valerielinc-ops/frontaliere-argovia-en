Build the new Junior Center with us

The Junior Center is an internal service center of learners for Axpo subject areas. Here, learners process real orders from the company – from photo and video productions to digital content and visualizations – and gain valuable practical experience. As the responsible person, you build up this offer together with us, further develop the service portfolio and ensure that internal clients are professionally supported. At the same time, you create an environment for learners in which they can take responsibility, grow on real projects and further develop their skills.You coordinate the assignments, accompany the learners professionally and methodically and ensure a high-quality and timely implementation. If you like to organize, develop people and build something with creative media projects, we look forward to your application. Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You build up the Junior Center and continue to develop the service portfolio.
  • You accept internal assignments and prioritize them together with specialist areas and learners.
  • You plan assignments and accompanying learners both technically and methodically during implementation.
  • You ensure professional and timely implementation – including quality control and debriefing.
  • You advise internal clients on format, scope and scheduling.
  • You maintain the exchange with vocational and practical trainees as well as other specialist areas.
  • You collect feedback and use it to further develop the offer.

Profile and Requirements

  • You have completed a vocational training as a mediamatist or in a comparable field and are interested in media, digital content and visualization.
  • You bring experience in accompanying learners as well as in coordinating assignments or projects.
  • You like to organize, think quality-consciously and bring different stakeholders together.
  • You communicate strongly and lead learners clearly, appreciatively and bindingly.
  • Ideally, you will bring secure Microsoft 365 skills as well as good German and English.

Even if you do not meet all points, we look forward to your application.

About the team

Our vocational training team in HR accompanies over 470 learners throughout Switzerland. We work across locations, support each other and like to try new things – open to all perspectives and backgrounds.
