Agent in receipt of goods (m/f/d) — Planzer

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You coordinate and monitor the booking visits of our ramps and ensure a smooth process
  • Assigning the ramps to the delivering trucks is also part of your responsibility
  • You take over the handling of our internal and external chauffeurs and are the direct contact person for suppliers, clients and internal offices
  • In regular exchange with internal and external interfaces, you clarify open questions independently
  • Customs notifications you carry out independently and reliably
  • The professional storage of the goods as well as the goods and pallet inspection are part of your everyday work
  • You process delivery notes and create corresponding error logs for deviations
  • When unloading containers, you actively participate and operate various industrial trucks

Why do you succeed?

  • Ideally, you have completed an apprenticeship as a logistician or already have experience in goods receipt
  • When dealing with the common EDP applications you are safe on the road - knowledge in AS400 is an advantage
  • A valid, SUVA-approved forklift pass belongs to your profile
  • Your independent, exact and forward working method distinguishes you, even if it gets hectic
  • You are sure to communicate in German and ideally bring English or other language skills
  • Flexibility, physical strength and a pronounced hands-on mentality are a matter of course for you
  • As a reliable team player, you like to participate and feel comfortable in a dynamic logistics environment

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Sports offer over lunch: padel, football or unhockey? With us you can rent free seats over lunch and let off steam

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
