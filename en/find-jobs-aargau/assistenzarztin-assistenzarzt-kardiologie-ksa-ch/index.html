Full description

Job: Spring 2027 or by appointment, indefinitely

Tasks

With us, you will be structured and trained in the entire cardiology. Secured scheduled rotations:

  • Ambulatorium with practical spectrum: echocardiography, Holter-EKG, ergometry incl. spiroergometry;
  • Echolabor for stationary patients and PET Imaging,
  • outpatient cardiac rehabilitation,
  • Heart catheter laboratory.

With good qualification and longer employment, further special rotations for 3 to 6 months:

  • Rhythmology including EPU laboratory and extended pacemaker consultation,
  • deep rotation in the cardiac catheter laboratory with insight into structural interventions and complex PCIs,
  • Advanced Imaging incl. Insight in the heart MRI and deeper imaging of echocardiography incl. TEE and stress echocardiography
  • intensive care unit,
  • If applicable, Insight into a practice.

They take care of inpatient and outpatient patients under the supervision of our cadre doctors and actively participate in reports, visits and our extensive training program in accordance with the requirements of the SIWF.

Requirements

As a committed, communicative personality, you attach importance to a collegial and constructive cooperation. You have a Swiss medical diploma or a diploma recognised by MEBEKO and are seeking the title of Cardiology. Clinical experience in General Internal Medicine of at least 2 years and 6 months of cardiology is desired. We offer you a high-quality further education with many training hours, modern working time models and a friendly, collegial environment. The workload is 100%, part-time (80%) is possible.

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
