Full description

About the position As a maintenance operator, you are part of the eMobility area and work in various production areas such as clean room, testing and grey room. You take on tasks in the area of maintenance and at the same time support the production operation by operating and monitoring fully automated production plants. With your flexibility within the learned areas as well as your solution-oriented and careful working method, you contribute to a safe, efficient and high-quality production process.

You can do that.

  • Processing of production batches according to the valid working instructions under clean room conditions in the areas of eMobility, clean room, testing and grey room
  • Setting up, operating and monitoring the fully automated production facilities as well as operating the applicable control systems such as MES, SAP and BO Report
  • Flexible job rotation between the areas and activities learned and willingness to learn further work areas
  • Implementation of Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) on complex systems in mechanical production and testing according to work instructions as well as daily machine controls including SPC
  • Monitoring and ensuring stockpiling of production media and maintenance of consumables for production machinery
  • Organisation and implementation of cleaning work at workplaces, cleanroom infrastructure, plants, materials and modules in accordance with the applicable work instructions
  • cooperation in ensuring quality, order and safety as well as in the continuous improvement process
  • Additional punctual support for maintenance technicians and engineers
  • Support in the training of new maintenance operators as well as assuming additional tasks according to the direction of shift management

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training EFZ or EBA in a technical professional field
  • Knowledge of German at least level C1
  • Solution-oriented approach as well as good problem-solving ability and the ability to work in a structured and stressed manner in case of process difficulties and in compliance with deadlines
  • Hands-on mentality as well as good communication skills and social competence with the ability to communicate effectively at different hierarchical and competence levels
  • Basic knowledge of standard software such as MS Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Openness for a 2-shift system as well as readiness for continuous personal development and participation in training

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to develop professionally and personally.
