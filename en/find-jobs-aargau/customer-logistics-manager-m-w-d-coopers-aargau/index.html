Full description

For our client, we are looking for an experienced Customer Logistics Manager (m/f/d) to join the Global Supply Chain Operations Organization in Kaiseraugst. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring the timely and reliable supply of semi-finished and finished products to third-party customer markets worldwide, maintaining high service levels, right-first-time order fulfillment and full compliance with quality and documentation requirements.

Working closely with global and local stakeholders, as well as cross-functional planning and logistics teams, you will coordinate upstream replenishment activities, maintain accurate master data and ensure appropriate supply chain setups. In addition, you will drive continuous improvements to the cost-to-serve model, leverage data analytics and technology to optimize supply chain performance, and identify opportunities to increase efficiency and productivity while ensuring customer needs and global supply objectives are met.

Start date: asap/latest 18.01.2027

Planed Durration: 12 Months (extension possible)

Workload: 100%

Contract Type: Contracting

Location: Kaiseraugst

Remote: Yes, max 40%

Your tasks

  • You ensure global market supply to third party customers on time in full at the right quality and cost
  • You partner with different interfaces for upstream replenishment coordination from central packaging
  • You continuously improve the cost-to-serve-model (incl. KPI metrics).
  • You leverage data for decision making by using e.g. analytics, simulation tools and customer insights
  • You drive efficiency through identifying value-adding and productivity-enhancing opportunities through technology and data
  • You collaborate with different functions in the markets to understand the changes which may impact supply to the country and align with planning functions to adjust the Replenishment Plan
  • You ensure appropriate supply chain set-up (incl. e.g. master data)

Your Profile

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business, or a related field (or equivalent specialized training).
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience in Supply Chain Management and Operations within the pharmaceutical industry.
  • SAP, Microsoft Office, and familiar with AI-driven supply chain tools/analytics.
  • Professional fluency in English (written and spoken); German is a plus
