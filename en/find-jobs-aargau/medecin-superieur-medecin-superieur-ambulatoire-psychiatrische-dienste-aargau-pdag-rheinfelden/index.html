Full description

For our ambulatorium in the Fricktal in Rheinfelden we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by arrangement a committed personality as

Tasks

  • You lead and coordinate the professional groups under your supervision in the interdisciplinary treatment team
  • They accompany assistant physicians, assistant psychologists in further education and supervision
  • You create and supervise medical reports for referees, insurance companies, legal representatives and other clients
  • You actively lead team reports and training
  • You take over cadre services in the ambulatory and participate in the service system of the PDAG (about 6 weekend days per year at 100% expense)
  • They clarify adult patients with mental disorders, treat them and undertake crisis interventions and triagulations.
  • They discuss the location with relatives, advisers, employers, residential caregivers and other caregivers
  • You will take on clarifications and treatments in our new competence center for ADHD and autism

Requirements

  • You bring a Swiss-recognized specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or are about to graduate
  • You speak German as your mother tongue or have a language certificate at level C1
  • Ideally, you already have knowledge of personnel management
  • They act independently, make safe decisions and show a wide range of professional interests
  • They like to move and routinely work in interdisciplinary teams
  • You act in a structured, flexible and solution-oriented manner
