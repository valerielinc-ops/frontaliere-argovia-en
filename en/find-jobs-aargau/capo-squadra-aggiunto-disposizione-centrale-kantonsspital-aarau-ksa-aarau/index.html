Full description

Job opening: From now on or by agreement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • Ensuring a forward-looking, compliant bed, surgical intervention and laying disposition
  • Support and deputy of team management and assumption of leadership responsibility in their absence
  • Coordination of day-to-day operations
  • Quality management support
  • Administrative and organisational tasks

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as a medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant (MPA), health specialist / health specialist (FaGe) or dipl. Nursing professional HF as a basic requirement
  • Tertiary degree (HF/FH or equivalent) in health care or related field, leadership training or willingness to complete it
  • Several years of experience in the acute hospital and understanding of complex clinical and administrative interfaces
  • Leadership experience as well as experience in the guidance and support of employees is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of clinical processes, patient flow, capacity control and experience with IT/planning systems
  • High communication, coordination and social competence, analytical thinking, assertiveness and solution-oriented action

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
