The Position We are a dynamic, production-accompanying compliance team in Kaiseraugst! In this role as GMP Compliance Specialist (internal: Asset Excellence Manager), you ensure the continuous GMP compliance and stability of our processes. As a central interface, you work closely with production, quality assurance (QA) and other support functions to ensure the highest standards in a highly regulated environment.
