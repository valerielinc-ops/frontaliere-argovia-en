Full description

Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

Security requires people who take responsibility. In this multifaceted key role, you actively shape the clinic’s safety organization and ensure that occupational safety, health protection, fire protection and physical safety are effectively implemented in everyday hospital life. You work closely with managers, clinic management and internal and external partners and make an important contribution to a safe environment for employees and patients. Do you want to contribute your experience, take responsibility and actively shape the safety of our clinic? We look forward to your application. This post does not take into account recruitment dossiers.

Tasks

  • As a safety officer, you are the central contact person for physical safety, occupational safety and health protection, fire protection and patient safety (without medical aspects).
  • Managers, clinic management and specialist areas support and advise you on security issues and ensure the implementation of necessary measures
  • The further development of the security, emergency and event organization according to legal requirements and industry solution H+ as well as the maintenance of the security manual are part of your area of responsibility.
  • You analyze, document and assess hazards and risks as part of Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) and derive suitable measures from them
  • You plan and coordinate training, instructions and awareness-raising measures and thus promote a sustainable safety culture
  • Safety concepts, fire protection requirements, escape and rescue routes as well as safety systems you ensure and develop further
  • As a contact person, you coordinate cooperation with authorities, external partners, entrepreneurs and the commander of the company fire brigade.
  • The topics of locking and access systems, reporting, security measures and participation in budgeting are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • With your expertise, you will be involved in occupational health management and in other specialist committees

Profile

  • You have a basic technical or commercial training as well as a completed further training as a safety specialist EKAS
  • Ideally, you will bring further training or certification in the field of fire protection (VKF)
  • Several years of experience as a security officer, preferably in the SME environment and ideally in the healthcare sector, distinguishes you
  • Experience in project management as well as knowledge in object protection, locking and alarm systems are advantageous
  • Your high social skills, communication strength and assertiveness help you convince different stakeholders
  • Complex technical topics are conveyed in an understandable way and appear safely and professionally even in demanding situations
  • Networked thinking, an independent working method and a solution-oriented approach to challenges are among your strengths
  • Even in crisis situations, you keep an overview and act calmly
  • Very good MS Office skills, very good German skills in word and writing as well as good oral French skills round off your profile
  • Occasional trips to Sion and assignments outside the regular working hours in emergency situations are a matter of course for you

Pensum: 60-80 %
