The Position As part of Pharma Technical Development (PTD), we are realizing our pharmaceutical ambitions: bringing groundbreaking therapies from clinical phase I to market readiness to deliver reliable, day-to-day care to every patient. The group works closely with external and internal interfaces, the compliance team and quality assurance. It is crucial for ensuring smooth operation and reliable supply of clinical trials with primary and secondary packaging.

The possibility Are you ready to make a critical contribution to pharmaceutical development and gain deep insight into the world of clinical packaging? Are you looking for a challenging and at the same time highly relevant internship where you can actively participate in the design and optimization of processes? Then we have just the right thing for you. We offer a unique internship opportunity in our field of clinical packaging at the Kaiseraugst site. Here you work at the interface of technology, quality and patient safety. As an intern in GMP document management, you play a central role in structuring, harmonizing and revising our existing GMP documents. This is an essential component for the quality, compliance and efficiency of our clinical packaging processes. Your mission: bring structure to the GMP document landscape. Your main tasks include:

  • Analysis and evaluation of existing GMP documents (SOPs, technical documents, master validation plans, validation plans, user manuals, etc.) for completeness, consistency and timeliness.
  • Structuring and harmonizing the document landscape in order to minimize redundancies and create a clear, uniform systematic.
  • Revision and recasting of documents in close cooperation with experts to ensure that they comply with current regulatory requirements and internal standards.
  • Active participation in the implementation of document management systems and processes.
  • Support in the preparation and implementation of audits and inspections from a documentation perspective.
  • This internship offers you a rare opportunity to get to know a wide range of pharmaceutical packaging. Not only will you gain in-depth insight into the clinical supply chain and the specific requirements for clinical study materials, but also the strict GMP guidelines that shape our industry.
  • You will work in a dynamic team dedicated to delivering vital medicines for clinical trials worldwide. You will learn from experienced engineers, quality managers and operations experts and directly contribute to ensuring that our processes run smoothly and compliant.

Who you are

You are a student in the field of pharmaceutical engineering, engineering (e.g. mechanical engineering, process engineering), natural sciences or a comparable degree program, or you have already successfully completed your studies and are looking for professional experience. You also have:

  • a strong interest in quality assurance, regulatory requirements (GMP) and document management.
  • Safe handling of MS Office (especially Word, Excel, PowerPoint) is a matter of course for you.
  • You are fluent in German and English (verbal and written)
  • You work extremely structured, precise and detail-oriented.
  • You have very good analytical skills and the ability to formulate complex issues clearly and comprehensibly.
  • You are motivated, self-responsible and proactive.
  • You are looking for a pure internship to gain valuable professional experience.

Additional information

  • The internship is for a period of 12 months.
  • The earliest start date is December 1, 2026.
  • The supervision of a bachelor or master thesis is not possible in this position.
  • We look forward to your meaningful application with CV, motivation letter and testimonials.
  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must attach to the application a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training.
