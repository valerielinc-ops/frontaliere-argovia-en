Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • When loading and unloading our delivery vehicles, you take part and ensure that everything lands safely in the right place.
  • You carefully control rolling carts and GDP pallets while keeping an overview
  • You record incoming goods reliably and then sort them by region and tour
  • You provide the packages ready for shipment and thus contribute to a smooth process
  • Fork and drawbar trucks as well as fast-moving trucks you operate safely and efficiently

Why do you succeed?

  • Ideally, you already have experience in goods handling or logistics
  • You have a recognized forklift.
  • Even in physically demanding activities, you actively participate
  • A reliable, independent and careful way of working distinguishes you
  • Flexibility, teamwork and commitment are among your strengths
  • Good German skills in word and writing round off your profile
  • Your assignment takes place from 5.30 p.m. to about 11.00 p.m.

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times – modern and safe work equipment is available to you
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
