Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Receiving, capturing, prioritizing and processing incidents and service requests by telephone, e-mail and ticket system as first level support for our users in the department
  • Analyzing, fixing and documenting standard disruptions and coordinating escalations to 2nd level support or the relevant specialist teams
  • Ensuring a high ticket quality through complete categorization, classification, prioritization and clean routing for efficient control of the ticket flow in the sense of lean dispatching
  • Proactively monitor resolution times and intervene in the event of delays or critical escalation
  • Collaboration in operational IT operations (user management, IMAC/D), maintenance of asset data (inventory, licenses and CMDB) and technical support of training rooms
  • Accompany and promote our learners on their way to becoming an IT professional

What you bring

  • Professional experience in a ServiceDesk, First Level Support or equivalent position
  • In-depth technical knowledge in the workplace and Microsoft environment (ideally M365), user and authorization management and experience with ITIL standards
  • Rapid comprehension and enjoyment of structured analysis, prioritization and coordination of tickets
  • Pronounced customer and service orientation coupled with a stylish, addressable communication in German (word and writing)
  • Independent, reliable and well-organized working methods as well as a high quality awareness, even in a dynamic operating environment
