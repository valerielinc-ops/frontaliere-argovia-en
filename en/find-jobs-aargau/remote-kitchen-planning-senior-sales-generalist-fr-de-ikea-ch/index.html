Full description

Who you are

  • You have experience in selling and, ideally, selling kitchens or using kitchen planning tools.
  • You communicate with ease and confidence in French and German. Any other language is an asset.
  • You are able to organize your work independently, be creative and collaborate with different departments.
  • You have a good knowledge of our product range and use it to offer customers solutions tailored to their needs.

What you'll do every day

  • Accompany guests throughout their project, from the planning of their kitchen to its installation, to provide them with a smooth and enjoyable experience.
  • Understand the needs of customers and proactively propose appropriate solutions to enhance customer satisfaction and boost sales.
  • Conduct sales interviews and provide personalized and caring advice to build customer confidence and lasting relationships.
  • Design kitchens using digital tools, taking into account the individual needs and wishes of customers during virtual appointments.
  • Offer complete support before and during purchase through screen sharing, telephone and e-mail.
  • Use remote selling techniques to contribute to the growth of online business and ensure its sustainable profitability.

You think this could work for you?

As we have already indicated, we value the well-being of our collaborators.

So we want to say 'thank you'!

  • 28 days of vacation (based on 100 % shift)
  • 8 weeks paid parental leave for fathers; 20 weeks paid parental leave for mothers
  • Very attractive social benefits
  • 15% discount on your purchases - in each IKEA store in Switzerland.
  • Would you rather come to work on public transport? We cover between 85% and 100% of the price of an annual subscription.
  • Free parking
  • Unlimited access to the LinkedIn learning platform
  • Favourable and balanced meals in our canteen (4.50 CHF for meat dishes and 3.50 CHF for vegetarian dishes).
  • Premiums paid to all employees

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
