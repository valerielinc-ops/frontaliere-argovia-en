Full description

Klinik Im Hasel AG is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for the treatment of addiction and trauma disorders. As a health insurance-approved institution and part of the cantonal hospital list, we offer high-quality, evidence-based and human-oriented treatment for our patients. With around 170 dedicated employees at three locations – our inpatient clinic in Gontenschwil, the ambulatorium and the day clinic in Lenzburg and the trauma-specific ambulatorium in Baden – we combine professional competence with an appreciative and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Employees: in cleaning and hotel industry (f,m,d) 80 – 100%

For our location in Gontenschwil, we are looking for a reliable reinforcement for our cleaning team and hotel industry.

Your tasks

  • Deputy Head of the Hotel Industry team in their absence
  • Daily maintenance cleaning in the areas assigned by the maintenance management
  • Basic cleaning of the patient rooms after their exit and preparation of the rooms before entering
  • Cleaning of wet cells (shower/WC) and filling of hygiene material
  • Emptying of waste bins and ashtrays
  • Representation of deputies in the field of maintenance cleaning

Your profile

  • Experience in maintenance cleaning desired
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • High relation to cleanliness and hygiene
  • Know-how regarding the use of cleaning agents and cleaning aids
  • Own passenger cars
  • Readiness for unforeseen operations
  • Accustomed and sovereign in dealing with people from different cultures and social strata
  • We want a basic attitude that is characterized by appreciation and respect in dealing with our patients: inside.

Our offer

  • Interesting and challenging activities
  • Working hours Monday to Friday according to work schedule between 6.00 and 18.00
  • Dedicated and established team
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Progressive working conditions and social benefits
  • Well-developed occupational health management
