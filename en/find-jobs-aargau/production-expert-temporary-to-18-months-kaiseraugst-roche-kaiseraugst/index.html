The Position The freeze-drying line (GEF) is responsible for producing sterile drugs in the form of lyophilized vials.

The possibility

  • Operation of equipment for freeze-dried vials
  • Production process monitoring
  • Aseptic work in clean room conditions
  • Microbiological monitoring
  • GMP-compliant documentation
  • Cleaning and maintenance of machinery, equipment and premises
  • Cooperation in the continuous improvement of our process

Who you are

  • You have completed technical vocational training, ideally as a pharmacist or chemist, and ideally have experience in sterile production and working in clean rooms
  • They have experience in the GMP-regulated environment as well as a good technical understanding and sense of responsibility in dealing with plants
  • They are flexible, ready to work and enjoy working in a team – even in a dynamic, layer-based production environment
  • You are confident in the common MS Office applications and have a good knowledge of German
  • They like to think beyond conventions, are open to new ideas and actively shape changes
  • They value a performance-oriented work environment characterized by mutual respect, collaboration and constructive exchange.

The job is limited to 18 months The team works in a 6-4 layer model
