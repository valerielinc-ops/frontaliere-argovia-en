Full description

For our customer from the energy sector, we are looking for a:n experienced:n and motivated:n Salesforce Platform Manager for the Aarau location.

Your tasks

  • Managing Salesforce platform development from testing and approval of professional requirements to acceptance, production and transfer of operations
  • Managing and prioritizing the development backlog together with the business process owners and the service owner as well as ensuring the definition of ready and definition of done
  • Responsibility for resource, quarterly and release planning taking into account Salesforce release cycles and dependencies on environments
  • Testing and approval of solution designs incl. Fit gap analysis, data model, integrations, security, permissions and data protection
  • Conducting design reviews and ensuring architecture governance, the “native-first” principle and the applicable quality gates
  • Technical control of external service providers in a multi-provider environment incl. Monitoring progress, quality, deadlines and delivery results as well as escalation
  • Participation in technical assessments in the framework of mini-tenders
  • Coordination of kick-offs, status, milestone and handover meetings as well as preparation of user-friendly reporting on status, risks and measures
  • Planning and coordination of releases, deployments, data migrations, rollbacks, cutover and go-live in coordination with DevOps, business and system managers
  • Preparation and implementation of acceptance incl. Sign-offs and Go/No-Go decision bases
  • Responsibility for hypercare phases, stabilization and operational transfer incl. Operational documentation and knowledge transfer
  • Control of problem management with root-cause analyses and sustainable correction of causes of errors
  • Maintenance and further development of Team Charter, RACI, process documentation, roadmap and architecture repository as well as transfer of Lessons Learned into the workings of the Platform Team

Your profile

  • Completed education or training in computer science, business informatics or a comparable discipline (HF, FH or university)
  • Several years of experience in managing Salesforce projects, in particular in further development, release and deployment management
  • Proven experience in the technical management of external service providers in a multi-provider environment
  • In-depth knowledge of the Salesforce platform (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, Data Model, Authorization Concepts, Release Cycles)
  • Good knowledge of Salesforce DevOps, Salesforce DX, Git/GitHub, CI/CD pipelines, sandbox strategies, test coverage and code quality
  • Experience in requirements and backlog management as well as in agile procedures incl. Sprint and release planning
  • Experience in solution design and architecture governance for integrated platforms as well as a good understanding of interfaces, security and data protection
  • Experience in release, deployment, acceptance, cutover, hypercare and operational transfer processes
  • Strong control and communication capability as well as a secure appearance towards business, architecture, operations and external partners
  • Analytical, structured and result-oriented working methods as well as the ability to make dependencies, risks and conflicts of objectives transparent and to prepare decisions
  • Salesforce certifications such as Certified Administrator, Platform App Builder, Sales Cloud Consultant / Agentforce Sales Consultant, Service Cloud Consultant / Agentforce Service Consultant or equivalent
  • Negotiable German (min. C1) and good English skills (min. B2)
  • Readiness for regular presence at the Aarau site
