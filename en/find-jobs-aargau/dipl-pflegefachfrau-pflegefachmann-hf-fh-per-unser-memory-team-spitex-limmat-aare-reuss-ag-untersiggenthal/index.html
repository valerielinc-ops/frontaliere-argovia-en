Full description

Spitex LAR AG is not a 0-8-15 care service. With a catchment area of over 55,000 inhabitants in six municipalities, we offer future-oriented, hospital-external care – modern, flexible and always focused on the highest quality. What are we missing? You! Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH for our memory team Are you looking for a meaningful task with a lot of heart and personal responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us! For our specialized dementia team, we are looking for a committed qualified nurse who can provide competent and empathetic support to people with dementia in their usual environment.

Your mission

  • You look after and care for people with dementia at home – individually, respectfully and professionally
  • You take over the case management and work closely with relatives, family doctors and other specialists
  • You recognize changes early and design suitable support offers with
  • You give You are happy to continue your knowledge – e.g. to colleagues or learners in a team
  • You bring your expertise and help to develop our dementia-specific offers

Your rucksack

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH (or equivalent SRC recognition)
  • CAS Person-centered care of people with dementia or training as a dementia coach is beneficial
  • Joy and experience in dealing with people with dementia – or great interest in deepening yourself in this area
  • Independent, reliable working method and a good feeling for people
  • You are mobile (Driving Licence Cat. B), like to travel and appreciate the variety in Spitex work

Our promise

  • A dedicated interdisciplinary team that works according to the current state of knowledge
  • High degree of independence in day planning
  • Regular training and support in your further development
  • Attractive employment conditions and modern work equipment
