Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You start with us as the driver of the cat. B/BE and become step by step on your future activity as chauffeur Kat. C/CE prepared
  • We enable you to train as chauffeur Kat. C/CE in theory and practice - and at full wage
  • After successfully passing the test, you will take over cargo transports throughout Switzerland in accordance with the planning of the deployment. Current overnight stays are also included.
  • The training for the CZV approval examination is also part of your training program
  • You deliver the goods on time and professionally to our customers and ensure a friendly and reliable service
  • Loading and unloading? No problem for you - you take care of the return name and sorting of the containers and make sure that everything is safely stowed away.
  • You ensure that the goods are optimally secured and take care of the correct handling of the scanner and the transport documents
  • Your vehicle is your most important business card - so you take care of its care and maintenance

Why do you succeed?

  • You have the driver's license Kat. B or BE already in your pocket
  • You are enthusiastic about technology and vehicles and bring joy to trucks, road traffic and logistics
  • A forward-looking, clean and independent way of working distinguishes you
  • You are reliable, flexible and willing to give full throttle even in stressful moments due to your resilience
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Quality bonus: Quality is written in our capital and rewarded
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Generous expense regulations: With our expense regulations you are well taken care of

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
