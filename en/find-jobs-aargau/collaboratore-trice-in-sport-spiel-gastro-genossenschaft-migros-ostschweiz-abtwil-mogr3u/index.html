Full description

The leisure facilities of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland allow many people variety, recreation and sporting activities. With movement and relaxation offers, they provide a balance to everyday life and time with quality that the body and mind remember.

Mansioni

  • You will advise our guests and ensure that they experience the best sports, games and bowling offers
  • You are responsible for both the sale and reservation of products as well as the operation of the checkout and the correct billing
  • Support for group events – from small team events to big celebrations
  • You take care of cleaning the sporting goods and work areas
  • You serve our guests in the restaurant and throughout the sports area – always with a friendly attitude
  • You conjure little treats with skill and joy for our hungry guests

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in gastronomy or leisure is an advantage
  • Completed basic or further education: beneficial in gastronomy or leisure
  • You have a friendly, service-oriented appearance and work independently and reliably
  • Evening and weekend assignments are no obstacle for you – you are flexible and ready when it matters
  • Experience in gastronomy or leisure is an advantage – but also lateral entrants with enthusiasm for sport are welcome!
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: discount on subscriptions to leisure and fitness facilities
