Full description

Mission About Decathlon Switzerland Since 1976, Decathlon has pursued a clear mission: " Moving People through the Wonders of Sport" . Our global family includes over 105,000 team members, 2,000 stores in 70 countries, and 85 private labels. Our Swiss adventure began in 2016. Since then, we have grown strongly: today, we have almost 1,000 employees in over 30 branches, supported by 5 central areas (logistics, e-commerce, finance, HR, etc.). To strengthen our HR team in Spreitenbach, we are looking for a specialist in Payroll & HR administration as part of a succession. The HR team consists of a total of 20 people, 6 of whom are specifically responsible for administration and payroll. Your mission You took over the independent handling of payroll accounting and HR administration for around 300 internal employees. For this role, we are looking for a very structured and dynamic personality who combines deep payroll expertise with hands-on HR administration. Your tasks 1. HR administration

  • Employee Lifecycle Management: Management of the entire personnel administration from A to Z – incl. Drafting of employment contracts, contract amendments, processing of terminations and issuing of employer certificates.
  • Authorities & reporting: Timely and correct handling of processes relating to withholding tax, family allowances, pension fund (BVG), AHV/EO/APG as well as illness and accident reports. 2. Payroll & Compliance
  • Payroll processing: Independent implementation of the monthly payroll run for approx. 300 employees using Workday and Abacus.
  • Data maintenance: recording and processing of all wage-relevant changes (entries, wage adjustments, withdrawals, variable wage components).
  • Compliance: Ensuring full compliance with Swiss legal requirements, social insurance (AHV, BVG, UVG, etc.) and tax authorities. 3. HR projects & special tasks
  • Process & system optimisation: Active participation in key projects in the HR area (e.g. further development of Workday, e-planning or introduction of digital payroll).
  • Fleet management: Support and management of the company car fleet. Profile
  • Expertise: You have in-depth knowledge of Swiss payroll accounting and personnel administration – acquired through appropriate training or relevant professional experience.
  • Affinity for numbers & IT: You have an affinity for numbers, are experienced with IT tools and ideally already have experience with Workday and Abacus.
  • Working method: You work in an extraordinarily structured, precise, reliable and discreet manner. In addition, you are characterized by a high degree of independence and initiative.
  • Languages: Fluent German is mandatory. Very good English skills (at least level B1) round off your profile.
  • Sports enthusiasm: Of course, you actively do sports yourself and share our passion for it! Our recruitment process We believe there's more to you than just your resume. That is why we attach great importance to your potential and cultural fit. Our process is transparent and structured as follows:
  • AssessFirst Potential Assessment: We start with an online personality and potential test. This step will help us better understand your personal drivers and behavioral patterns to ensure the position is an optimal fit for your strengths.
  • 1. Interview: With Jana (Talent Acquisition Specialist)
  • 2. Interview: With Marine (Payroll Leader) and Tamara (Payroll Specialist)
  • 3. Interview: With Lionel (HR Director) Timeline:
  • Decision: Mid-November at the latest
  • Starting a job: Ideally at the beginning of December Are you looking for a new challenge in a dynamic, sporty environment with a great team? Then don't hesitate – join us!
