Full description

The Bachem Group is investing in a new planned location in Sisslerfeld in northwestern Switzerland. This step is part of the long-term strategy to expand production capacities by the end of the decade. The new production site will play a central role in achieving these goals. The planning phase of the project offers you unique opportunities to actively shape the organization of the future.

As part of the planned site development, the System Expert Materials Management Sisslerfeld Site Development (a) 100% ensures that material management processes are mapped efficiently, GMP-compliant and sustainable in the planned systems. The role significantly contributes to the development and successful commissioning of digital solutions for storage, production supply, sampling, dispensing, tank farm management and disposal processes.

Their tasks

  • Active participation in the development of the new location and in the design of the future materials management organization
  • Representation of the requirements of the operator organisation in the design, implementation and further development of the system landscape
  • Development and optimization of end-to-end processes taking into account organizational, operational and systemic interfaces
  • Translation of professional requirements into sustainable digital solutions in cooperation with engineering, IT and external partners
  • Participation in the conception and further development of ERP, WMS, MES and other relevant systems and business processes
  • Planning, implementation and support of tests, qualification and commissioning activities from the perspective of the future operator organisation
  • Examination and professional approval of project-relevant documents as well as ensuring compliance with safety, environmental and quality requirements
  • Support building a powerful, efficient and digitally supported materials management organization

Your profile

  • Higher education in logistics, supply chain management, business informatics, industrial engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of experience in the conception, introduction or further development of business processes and digital solutions
  • Strong understanding of end-to-end processes and their organizational and systemic mapping
  • Very good knowledge of integrated system landscapes, especially in the environment of ERP, WMS and MES; Experience with SAP or SAP-related logistics processes and SAP EWM is advantageous
  • Experience in the project environment as well as in cooperation with interdisciplinary teams and different stakeholders
  • Experience in a regulated production environment, ideally in the pharmaceutical, chemical or similar industry, is beneficial
  • Analytical and structured personality with strong end-to-end thinking and the ability to transform complex relationships into workable solutions
  • High level of initiative, a sense of responsibility and strong communication as well as the pleasure of helping to shape new structures and solutions in a dynamic project environment

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
