Full description

Company presentation

At McDonald’s, every visit becomes a sunny experience. Our team loves to make guests smile and create a welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels comfortable. If you like to be in contact with people and are looking for a job that is easy, dynamic and full of opportunities, you are at the right place with us!

Job description

As a Guest Experience Leader, you are the radiant heart of our restaurant. You greet our guests with a smile, introduce them to our latest offers and support them with the order. With your positive energy, you motivate the team and ensure that every guest leaves happy. You have an eye for the small details and an open ear for the wishes and feedback of our guests, which you share with the management to make each visit even better.

Main tasks

  • Welcome guests and create a cheerful atmosphere
  • Presentation of new offers and support for orders
  • Passing on guest feedback to team and management
  • Support the team with enthusiasm and team spirit
  • Ensuring a perfect restaurant experience for every guest

Requirements

  • Sunny, open being
  • Passion for making people happy
  • Team spirit and reliability
  • Attention to detail and willingness to listen
  • Motivation to learn and develop – no experience necessary!

Benefits

  • Flexible working hours for a good work-life balance
  • Dynamic, friendly and supportive team
  • Training and further education opportunities – many managers start as team members!
  • Remuneration and working conditions in accordance with the provisions of the collective employment contract (L-GAV)
  • 50% discount on our products in our restaurants in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
  • Sunrise subscriptions at a reduced price
  • Access to exclusive discounts via the platform "Corporate Benefits"
  • Additional holidays offered for over 50s
  • Free access to the largest online training library
