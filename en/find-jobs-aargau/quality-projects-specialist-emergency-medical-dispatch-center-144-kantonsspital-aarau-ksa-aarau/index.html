Full description

Job: 1 January 2027 or by arrangement, indefinitely

Tasks

Quality management

  • They further expand our quality management and ensure that it is implemented in the everyday life of dispatchers.
  • They evaluate key figures such as acceptance and alarm times or the medical query and derive concrete improvements from this.
  • Planning and implementation of quality checks and internal audits
  • Preparation of insights and recommendations for leadership
  • Participation in the development and maintenance of quality standards and requirements
  • They create a climate where mistakes and near-events are discussed openly so we can evolve.

Project management

  • Planning, coordination and implementation of projects – from the idea to the evaluation
  • Cooperation with internal and external partners
  • Preparation of decision-making bases, project documents and presentations
  • Monitoring of project objectives, deadlines and results
  • Ensuring sustainable implementation and evaluation of completed projects

Further development of the SNZ 144

  • Identification of development and innovation potential
  • Accompanying change and development projects
  • Support for leadership in organisational and technical issues
  • Observation of developments in rescue, emergency response and quality management
  • Introducing new ideas and modern solutions

Requirements

  • Completed higher education in quality management, project management, health care, rescue or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience in quality and/or project management
  • Experience in managing or coordinating projects
  • Experience in Swiss rescue services, health care or in an emergency call centre is an advantage
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Joy of initiating and implementing changes
  • Communication strength and secure presence at different hierarchical levels
  • Ability to understand complex issues
  • High personal responsibility and organisational skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good IT user knowledge and secure handling of Microsoft 365
  • Affinity for digitalization and new technologies
