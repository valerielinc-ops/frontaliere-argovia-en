Full description

Take on leadership, help shape and make a difference together! You bring experience in the plaster room, love the combination of craft, organization and leadership and are looking for the next career step? As a team leader, you take on the professional and organizational responsibility at KSB, shape a dynamic day-to-day work and actively shape the future of our plaster room. We look forward to your impulses!

Their tasks

  • Leading & coordinating: You lead the plasterroom team professionally and organizationally and ensure smooth daily routines.
  • Treatment & care: You create support bandages made of plastic, perform wound treatments and postoperative follow-up care and accompany our patients empathetically.
  • Office hours organisation: They organise and accompany the orthopaedic plaster hour.
  • Networking: They promote constructive, interprofessional cooperation across different disciplines.

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional HF or MPA EFZ.
  • Expertise: In-depth practical experience in the plaster room as well as routine in creating support bandages.
  • Leadership: Initial leadership experience or strong motivation to assume leadership responsibility and develop employees in a targeted manner.
  • Working method & personality: You work independently, structured as well as responsibly and convince as a communicative organizational talent with a strong team spirit.

This is what awaits you

  • Team culture: A small, motivated and well-established team.
  • Design leeway: A varied activity with a high degree of personal responsibility.
  • Perspective: A structured introduction and a wide range of further education opportunities.
  • Work-life balance: Regular working hours from Monday to Friday.

Ready for a new challenge? Then we look forward to meeting you and bringing your experience, skill and personality to our team!

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
