Full description

Returns, defective items, installation services, warranty extensions and product insurance: The After Sales Partner Management team ensures that there are clean processes behind all this. As a Senior After Sales Development Manager, you will help shape processes and EU sustainability initiatives.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG main camp Dintikon, parallel road, 5606 Dintikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Analyse internal processes based on data and further develop them with a focus on cost efficiency and customer satisfaction
  • Driving force behind sustainability initiatives for returns and defective items
  • Ensure compliance with legal standards and continuously ensure that new regulations are sensibly implemented (e.g. laws under the European Green Deal)
  • With methodological and professional competence, develop solutions with a strategic effect and convince through experimentation and innovation
  • Reliable and collaborative contact point for internal and external stakeholders who support you with questions and problems relating to after sales

Requirements

  • 2-3 years: Professional experience at the interface of process optimization, data analysis or operation, ideally in e-commerce or in a dynamic, international environment
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): In connection with the advertised position
  • Very strong analytical skills and a strong affinity for numbers and data, thanks to which you quickly understand relationships and processes
  • You are used to working independently, but the exchange in the team is also a matter of course for you
  • Above-average communication skills, high assertiveness and determination in difficult situations
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (negotiable)
