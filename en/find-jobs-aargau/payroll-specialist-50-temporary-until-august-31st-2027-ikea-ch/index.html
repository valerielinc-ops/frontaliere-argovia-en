Full description

Who you are

This is a great opportunity for those who want to deepen their knowledge in Payroll and to further develop existing HR knowledge.

  • Relevant education or further training in Payroll, HR or Social Insurance.
  • Knowledge of Swiss payroll, social security, taxation and employment-related regulations combined with hands-on payroll experience.
  • Good analytical skills and a flair for numbers
  • Ability to strike a balance between keeping a good overview and paying good attention to detail
  • Ability to prioritize and organize own work
  • Good service mindset
  • Quick learner who is easily able to adapt to changing demands
  • Ability to communicate confidently and clearly with various audiences both verbally and in writing
  • Role model for IKEA Culture & Values
  • Self-reliant and motivated with proven ability to work as part of a team as well as independently
  • Ability to ensure integrity of HR systems, guidelines, labour, tax and social security law
  • Ability to recognize connectivity to accounting
  • knowledge in SAP Success Factors would be a plus
  • Fluency in German and preferably in English as well. Any other national languages are a significant plus.

Your responsibilities

  • Record and administrate personnel master data in payroll system, timekeeping system and CDS
  • Record and check variable payroll data
  • Start payroll process and check payroll data to secure correct data for payment/accounting
  • Secure a correct registration of co-workers for the different social insurances/tax authorities
  • Secure registration/announcement of co-workers to different daily allowance insurances and monitor payments of daily allowances and prepare and forward credit notes for accounting
  • Establish monthly statements to tax authorities (withholding tax) and monitor incoming invoices and forward invoices to accounting
  • Monitor expiration of child allowances
  • Establish year-end statements for social insurances and tax authorities and assistance for reconciliation
  • Establish of diverse personnel and salary data reports
  • Complete various forms for public authorities (social insurances, execution offices)
  • Contact to external or internal partners and support line managers, co-workers and local HR in social insurance and HR system related matters
  • Establish personnel ID cards (Badges) and secure correct building accesses for co-workers in the units
