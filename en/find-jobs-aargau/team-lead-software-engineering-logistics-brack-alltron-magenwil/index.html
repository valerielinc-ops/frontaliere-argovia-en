Full description

Tasks

With us you not only design software, but also bring your personality, actively develop the department and shape the e-commerce of tomorrow. As a hands-on team lead, you will become part of a sympathetic, cross-functional product team in the field of logistics with product manager and four engineers. You share responsibility and develop scalable solutions with real impact for our customers: inside and central processes in the company.

At the heart of the logistics domain is a large, highly individualized and monolithic ERP that has grown over more than a decade and manages our warehouse every day. You help scale our operations in this system, make the codebase better with every step and lead the step-by-step modularization in Java services.

  • You expect a lot of creative leeway and the chance to shape the culture in the team and to achieve impact even across team boundaries
  • You lead, coach and develop your team with a focus on personal responsibility, quality and continuous learning
  • You work hands-on, from the first idea to architecture and solution design to productive operation, in our ERP and in new Java services. You design modular architectures and interfaces with approaches such as domain-driven design and self-contained systems
  • You bring curiosity, team spirit and willingness to learn and shape not only the implementation, but also architecture, quality and the question of what should be built at all.
  • You work in variations of Scrum or Kanban. Engineers are also rolling up their sleeves in refinements and are actively involved in architecture and product issues.
  • At the same time, there is enough legacy, complexity and grown structures to really make a difference with resilience and a clear view and make systems step by step better.
  • Real effect: Each of the more than 5 million packages that leave our warehouse annually runs through your team’s code, and you’ll help shape where our WMS evolves.

Requirements

We match when you

  • Many years of experience in software development and several years of management experience with personnel responsibility in cross-functional product teams, ideally in logistics (e.g. WMS) or e-commerce
  • Have in-depth knowledge of Java or a comparable language (e.g. C#, Kotlin), have experience with SQL and data modeling and regularly releast with CI/CD after "You build it, you own it"
  • uses AI-supported software development (e.g. coding agents such as Claude Code) and wants to further develop in a team
  • Are familiar with modular software architecture, interface design and asynchronous data processing and can decide what remains, what is isolated and what is erased in legacy code
  • Experience with ERP migrations or have worked on ERP systems
  • Understand you as a Servant Leader, develop employees in a targeted manner and have effectively established concepts such as Psychological Safety, OKRs and an open feedback culture in practice
  • Ideally, you have a good knowledge of German

We probably don’t fit together so well if you

  • Prefer frameworks such as SAFe or projects that start with a specification
  • has worked mainly in highly regulated areas
  • Without experience in a fast-paced, product-oriented environment with distributed systems
  • Feel more comfortable with role models where infrastructure, backend, frontend, testing and product thinking are clearly separated
  • Completely formulated requirements, instead of sharpening them together with product management and really understanding customers
  • See further education above all as a responsibility of the employer and do not continue to learn for their own interest, be it "on the job", outside at an evening event or simply reading a book
  • Are more frustrated by imperfect systems than motivated - Spoilers: We are not there yet
  • Work and good mood strictly separated from each other

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
