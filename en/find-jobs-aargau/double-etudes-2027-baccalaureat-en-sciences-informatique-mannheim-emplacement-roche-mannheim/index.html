The Position Looking for a diverse and future-oriented dual study course? Then join us in reinventing the future of healthcare. At Roche, we are developing innovative diagnostics and medicines to enable people to live longer and better lives. Join this mission and make a decisive contribution to patients worldwide from the very beginning.

Tasks

  • You rotate through a wide range of departments such as Global IT, Production or Finance and build a strong network.
  • You program software, databases and web applications with Python and Java.
  • You analyze complex business processes and translate them into tailor-made IT solutions.
  • You take responsibility in IT project management, consulting and the operation of our core systems (e.g. ERP and CRM) from the very beginning.

Your practical training will be held at Roche Diagnostics in Mannheim. The lectures will be held at the Baden-Württemberg Dual University in Lörrach. We will grant a rent subsidy for the accommodation in Lörrach. You will also receive a travel refund.

Your advantages with us We rely on the model of Personalized Training : You actively shape your training content in close consultation with your instructors. We offer you this additionally:

  • Finance: Attractive remuneration as well as holiday and Christmas money.
  • Work-Life-Balance : 30 days holidays, company sports and comprehensive health care.
  • Development: intensive exam preparation, university entrance qualification (optional) and various further education.
  • Extras : International work environment, foreign assignments and discounted canteen catering.

Prerequisites

  • General university qualification
  • Specialized university qualification
  • Fachhochschulereife and proof of the passed suitability test of the Dual University Baden-Württemberg

Duration 3 years. One month pre-primary, company-specific internship
