Full description

Do you have a sense of basic food and enjoyment of the variety of supermarket products? Do you have leadership experience, sales talent and communicate safely with our customers? Then you are exactly right with us! As a market management deputy, you support the branch management and are the central contact person for your team.

Mansioni

  • Leadership of Fresh Areas in the Supermarket
  • Responsibility for the leadership and development of department managers, employees and for the promotion of young talent
  • Ensuring the defined processes in the relevant areas
  • Achieving revenue and cost targets
  • Assignment planning and employee interviews
  • Deputy and supporting market management

Requirements

  • Professional and managerial experience: in the field of food
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • Responsible personality with pleasure in selling
  • Leadership experience
  • PC knowledge / digital fitness
  • Further education as a retail specialist*
  • in German (negotiation certain)

  • Mobile Subscriptions: Various Perks on Mobile Subscriptions
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items

  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
