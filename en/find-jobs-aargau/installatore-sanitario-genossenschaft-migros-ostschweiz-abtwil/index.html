Full description

The recreational facilities of Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz provide many people with variety, relaxation, and sports activities. They offer movement and relaxation opportunities for a balance to everyday life and for quality time that the body and mind remember.

Responsibilities

  • Maintenance, monitoring, and ensuring the operation of technical systems
  • Implementing a simple warranty management
  • Assisting in budgeting
  • Reporting and general correspondence
  • Recording and monitoring energy consumption
  • Identifying, proposing, and implementing energy-saving measures

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the field of maintenance and management, in the area of HVAC or similar functions
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): as an electrician or automation technician
  • Good computer skills, especially in the areas of reporting, general correspondence, and control systems
  • Independent, structured, and responsible working style
  • High team spirit as well as an open and flexible personality
  • Willingness to work flexible hours, weekend shifts, on-call duty, and occasional night work
  • German (very good knowledge)

What We Offer

  • Professional pension: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Insurance: Favorable conditions with various insurance companies
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on vacation offers and events
