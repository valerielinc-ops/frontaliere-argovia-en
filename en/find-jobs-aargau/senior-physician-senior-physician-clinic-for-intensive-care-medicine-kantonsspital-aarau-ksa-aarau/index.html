Role overview

Tasks

  • Self-responsible, practical treatment of patients according to evidence-based intensive care guidelines. Our clinic covers the entire spectrum of interdisciplinary intensive care medicine – with the exception of transplantation medicine, combustion medicine and heart surgery
  • Regular participation in bedside teaching as well as in interdisciplinary and interprofessional training
  • Mentoring and supervision of rotary doctors and specialist assistant doctors
  • Cooperation in the further development of the clinic through active participation in the creation and optimization of treatment concepts
  • Active participation in and supervision of simulation trainings
  • Possibility to deepen your knowledge in echocardiography (including echocrotation in cardiology) and sonography including acquisition of the SGUM Certificate of Ability (FA) POCUS
  • Opportunity to participate in research projects

Requirements

  • Federal Medical Diploma or MEBEKO Recognition
  • Federal Specialist Title Intensive Medicine present or submitted, or confirmation of equivalent training by the SGI
