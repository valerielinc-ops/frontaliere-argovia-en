Full description

You are familiar with server and storage systems and ensure that our backend infrastructure runs reliably. In the field of ICT and digitalization at the Canton Hospital Baden, you will continue to operate and develop our systems. You analyze complex disruptions, implement infrastructure projects and automate processes. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You will continue to operate, monitor and develop our server and storage platforms.
  • You ensure the availability, performance and security of our backend systems.
  • You plan and realize infrastructure projects and document the results.
  • You are responsible for the lifecycle management of server, storage and virtualization systems.
  • You will analyze and repair complex disruptions in 2nd and 3rd level support.
  • You bring your expertise to architectural and design decisions for new technologies.
  • You automate operational processes with scripts and infrastructure tools.
  • You support the further development of backup, disaster recovery and business continuity concepts.
  • You take care of Pikettdienst and help ensure that our systems work reliably even outside regular working hours.
  • You work with internal departments as well as external partners and manufacturers.

Your profile

  • You have completed training in computer science or a comparable qualification with a focus on systems engineering or data center operations.
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the operation of physical and virtual server systems, especially VMware ESXi, as well as storage systems.
  • You analyze problems structured and find efficient solutions.
  • You also keep track of complex disruptions and critical situations.
  • You are flexible and ready to take on tasks in adjacent specialist areas.
  • You live in German-speaking Switzerland or have your center of life in the region. If necessary, you are also easily accessible for on-site operations.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
