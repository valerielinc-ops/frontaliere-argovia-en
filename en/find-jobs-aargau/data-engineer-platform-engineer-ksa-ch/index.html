Tasks

We are looking for people who want to move big things in the team. Social skills, willingness to learn and an active contribution to team culture count as much as our strong technical skills. We live a modern management style, promote self-responsible work and focus on an open culture of error. We do not see employee development and further education as a bonus – but as part of your journey with us.

  • Development, operation and continuous optimization of scalable data pipelines for BI use cases
  • Integration, transformation and processing of structured data from clinical and administrative source systems
  • Ensuring data quality, availability, traceability and governance throughout the pipeline
  • Collaboration with BI, Analytics and departments to implement requirements
  • Automation of ETL/ELT processes and continuous development of data product standards

Requirements

  • Completed studies (Informatics, Business Informatics, etc.) or comparable experience
  • In-depth knowledge of data modeling as well as ETL/ELT tools, Python and SQL
  • Experience with Lakehouse Data Platforms with Cloud Native Technology
  • Understanding BI requirements and analytical issues as well as interest in data quality
  • Team-oriented, structured working method and high quality orientation
