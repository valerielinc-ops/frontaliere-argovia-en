Full description

With more than 350 employees at 17 locations, Pallas Kliniken is one of the leading providers of ophthalmology and aesthetic medicine in Switzerland. We stand for the highest medical quality, personal care and a strong cooperation. Our dedicated specialist staff plays a central role in the individual care of our patients. Through close interdisciplinary cooperation, we guarantee holistic support at the highest level – characterized by expertise, empathy and innovation.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated, independent and entrepreneurial personality as a senior physician / senior physician anesthesia 40-100 %

Responsibility

As a senior physician / senior physician anesthesia in our group, you prove your professional, medical skills in your field at our locations Aarau, Bern, Olten and Zurich. They provide excellent services at the highest level. They ensure that our patients are optimally cared for before, during and after an operation. You expand your expertise proactively and continuously, together in a team or alone, in internal or external training.

Requirements

  • Specialist title Anesthesiology
  • 3-year professional experience at a recognised continuing education institution in Switzerland (requirement to obtain a BAB)
  • Successful, goal-oriented and entrepreneurial thinking and acting, with a high understanding of quality and services
  • High emotional intelligence and resilience in dealing with patients, employees and other internal and external partners
  • Excellent observer and communicator in dialogue with patients and team members
  • Ability to quickly identify problems and independently develop solutions
  • Extensive consulting competence in German with easy to understand and addressee-appropriate language in word and writing

Benefits

You benefit for your work from our modern infrastructure, in which you can treat your patients professionally. We support you in your work optimally and in the best possible way. In addition to our modern infrastructure, we also offer you internal and external training opportunities. Excellent earning opportunities are a matter of course for us.

Are you looking for a versatile and exciting task in a family group of companies? We look forward to your application.

For technical questions, Prof. Dr. med. Christoph Tappeiner is available at christoph.tappeiner @pallas-kliniken.ch.

For additional information, see Mr. Diego Cicco, at tel. +41 58 335 31 39 or via email diego.cicco@pallas-kliniken.ch.
