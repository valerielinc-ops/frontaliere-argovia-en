Pensum: 80–100%, bathing

We are looking for a committed and dynamic personality who will drive the digital transformation in the energy industry together with us. In this exciting position, you act as a catalyst for innovative ideas: you recognize new technologies, evaluate their benefits for our daily work and lead and accompany pilot projects for the best ideas.

Tasks & ResponsibilitiesYou scout technologies and trends and quantify their benefits for the high-voltage network.You develop use cases with the specialist departments, formulate the added value and specify the requirements.You support the implementation of the digitization strategy of the Division Grid and develop your own project ideas.You lead digitization and innovation projects, from the commissioning of software development (in the case of new applications) to commissioning.You supervise robot and automation projects in the field together with the operation.You work together with internal teams and external partners (e.g. universities, start-ups).

Profile & RequirementsCompleted degree in engineering or physics or equivalent qualification. At least 3 years of professional experience in managing projects, ideally in the field of digitization and innovation.Knowledge in the field of energy and interest in new technological developments. Strong analytical skills and problem-solving skills.Strong organizational skill and ability to lead multiple projects at once.Excellent communication skills and team orientation. Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

About the team The responsibility for Digitalization & Innovation of the Networks Division, together with the technical and financial planning and asset analysis, lies in the Strategic Asset Management of the Distribution Division (networks). We work and think interdisciplinary and offer you a challenging and versatile job in a dynamic environment. If you want to be at the forefront of the digital transformation in the energy industry, want to contribute your own ideas and love to implement innovative projects, then we look forward to your application.
