About the role We supply, assemble and guarantee the commissioning of metering systems in the field of concrete production. This includes all aspects such as installation, repair and service. Dosing systems include tanks, pumps, pipelines, wiring, valve controls, balances and similar components. In the course of team strengthening, we are looking for a service technician for the region of Western Switzerland, ideally in the Fribourg/Berne area.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Supply, assembly and commissioning of our plants in concrete plants and mobile concrete plants
  • Performing service work, troubleshooting and repairs of equipment
  • Control of the exact dosage for concrete additives
  • Implementation of internal assembly and inspection work in the own workshop

Your skills and experience

  • Completed technical vocational training (e.g. agricultural machinery mechanic, sanitary installer, polymechanic, small equipment mechanic, etc.)
  • Independent, precise and reliable working methods
  • Good knowledge of German, knowledge of French advantageous
  • User experience in MS Office
  • Category B ticket and regular willingness to travel to Western Switzerland

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
