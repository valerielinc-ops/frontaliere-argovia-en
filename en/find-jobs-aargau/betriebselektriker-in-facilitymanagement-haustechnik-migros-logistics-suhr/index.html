Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility in the function as a company electrician.

Mansioni

  • Ensuring uninterrupted operation of the building installations, facilities and infrastructure
  • Design and implementation of electrical security checks
  • Definition and implementation of energy and operational optimization measures
  • Independent conduct of inspections and maintenance
  • Resolving incidents and carrying out initial interventions
  • Collection and preparation of offers
  • Independent management of sub-projects
  • Coordination of external service providers and craftsmen

Requirements

  • 5-6 years: Electrical / Building Technology / HLKKSE
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Electrical installation
  • Completed basic training as electrical installer EFZ / assembly electrician EFZ or comparable training
  • Several years of professional experience in dealing with HLKKSE systems
  • Service and solution-oriented thinking
  • Independent and team-oriented action
  • Good IT knowledge of the Microsoft Office range
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • German (very good knowledge)
