29.09.2026 Villmergen

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Villmergen is a locally anchored and well-positioned bank with a balance sheet total of around 1.5 billion. 40 motivated employees work with a lot of commitment and passion for our cooperative members and our customers every day. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed, communication-strong and professional personality as head of services and member of the bank management.

What awaits you?

  • You are responsible for the technical and personnel management of the organizational unit «Services» (4 employees), which comprises the following areas: regulatory system, risk control, master data, payment transactions, accounting, compliance, security, computer science and infrastructure
  • In cooperation with our Board of Directors and the Bank Management, you shape and develop the future of our Bank and bring your ideas and perspectives to the table.
  • You assume responsibility for managing and monitoring compliance risks (especially VSB/GW, investment business, data protection, cross-border) as well as preparing bank accounts and the annual budget
  • Together with your team, you ensure the smooth and efficient handling of daily business and contribute to the maintenance of the high quality standards of our bank.

What do you bring?

  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in the back office, operations or service environment in banking
  • Practical experience in the field of compliance and regulations in the financial sector
  • Completed higher banking education and/or higher business/legal education (Uni/FH or equivalent)
  • Very good conceptual and analytical skills as well as experience in the implementation of process optimizations and digital solutions
  • High personal responsibility, team and service orientation as well as the ability to set priorities and maintain an overview in a broad area of responsibility

Are you looking for a versatile management task in which you take on responsibility and actively promote the further development of our bank? As Head of Services and Member of Bank Management, you take on a central role in our bank and contribute significantly to our success with your leadership skills, entrepreneurial thinking and service orientation. Do you feel addressed and would like to shape the future of Raiffeisenbank Villmergen together with us? I look forward to your application. Christian Stutz Chairman of the Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?
