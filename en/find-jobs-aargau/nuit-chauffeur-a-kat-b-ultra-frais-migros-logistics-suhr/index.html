Full description

We do logistics for Migros. For our new division Ultrafresh, we are looking for active support to strengthen our transport team at the location in Suhr, either immediately or by appointment.

Mansioni

  • Delivery of shops and branches in accordance with the requirements of the arrangement, all Switzerland

  • Enjoyment of contact with customers and good knowledge of German
