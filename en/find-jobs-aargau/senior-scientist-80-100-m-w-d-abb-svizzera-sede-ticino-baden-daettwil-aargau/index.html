Your role and responsibilities

Our team's mission is to drive innovation at the intersection of power electronic converters and electromagnetics, translating cutting-edge research into next-generation ABB products. We focus on electromagnetic modeling of power converter systems and components, conducted and radiat-ed emissions mitigation, passive and active filtering strategies, and shielding. By joining us, you will shape the future of energy systems — generating pioneering ideas at the frontier of academic research and industrial innovation, while developing transformative technologies that advance ABB's product portfolio.

Are you passionate about enabling a sustainable future through electromagnetics research in pow-er converter systems? We want to hear from you! A dynamic, international team is excited to wel-come you as our newest member. Together, we will push boundaries and make a lasting impact on the energy transition.

The work model for the role is: #LI-Hybrid

You will be mainly accountable for

- Pioneer innovative concepts for ensuring electromagnetic compatibility and interference compliance in high-power converters

- Develop EMC models to predict and analyze electromagnetic compatibility and optimize electromagnetic design of circuits and systems

- EMC measurements of conducted and radiated emissions of converters and HF characterization of components

- Patent innovative solutions and demonstrate technology leadership through e.g., high-impact publications

- Collaborate closely with ABB research centers, business units, and universities to transition research into real-world applications

  • Qualifications for the role

- PhD in electrical engineering, physics or related discipline with specialization in power electronics or electromagnetics

- Proven research excellence demonstrated through high-impact publications and patents

Deep technical expertise in

- EMC/EMI theory and solid theoretical background in electromagnetic modelling and field simulations

- Power electronic converter topologies, filters, magnetics, and semiconductor switching devices

- Simulation tools such as CST, COMSOL, ANSYS, SPICE circuit simulator (LTSpice, SIMetrix), Simulink

- Hands-on laboratory skills in HF-electromagnetic measurements (conducted and/or radiated emissions), with a proactive, self-driven approach to designing, building, and testing EMC/EMI-compliant power converters. Versatile skill set with the ability to bridge theory and practice across multiple technical domains.

- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced industrial research environment and deliver results

- Excellent communication skills in English, both written and verbal
