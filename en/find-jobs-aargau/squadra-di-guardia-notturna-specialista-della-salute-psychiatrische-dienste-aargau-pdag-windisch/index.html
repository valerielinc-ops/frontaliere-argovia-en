Full description

As of August 1, 2026 or by appointment, we are looking for you to join the night watch team at the Center for Integrated Emergency Psychiatry and Crisis Intervention:

  • You will complement the nursing team with psychiatric, medical and nursing expertise in the treatment of acutely mentally ill people
  • You will quickly build a trusting relationship with the patients and demonstrate a proactive attitude to ensure high-quality service
  • Together with the entire team, you will accompany admissions and transfers at night
  • You work in an interesting and varied area and actively contribute to the further development of processes and offers with your good ideas. Requirements:
  • Health professional, preferably with professional experience in (acute) psychiatry
  • You are an enthusiastic professional with a high level of technical, social and self-competence
  • You have a resource- and solution-oriented understanding of care
  • You are flexible, committed and a responsible personality who enjoy demanding patient care all in one Lively operations
  • You speak and write German fluently and are confident in using common office applications. We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. That's exactly why we offer you a lot: we strongly encourage further training. We also invest a lot in team cohesion and offer all of our teams access to the “Moodtalk” software, which strengthens team communication. We also offer attractive discounts on well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport receive an impressive eco-bonus at the end of the year. We also offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy a holiday.
