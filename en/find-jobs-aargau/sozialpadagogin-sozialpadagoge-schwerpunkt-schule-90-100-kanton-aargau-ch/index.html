Exciting tasks await

  • Individual support and learning support for young people in small groups in the fields of German, mathematics, general education and sport in close cooperation with our teachers

  • Accompanying young people in sporting and movement-oriented activities
  • Support of the group in the breakfast or Lunch Care

What you bring

  • Degree in social pedagogy with additional pedagogical qualification (e.g. integrative promotion, adult education)

  • Interest in working with behavioral male adolescents

Your working environment

The Jugendheim Aarburg is an institution for the execution of civil and juvenile criminal law measures. It includes male adolescents and young adults who are at risk in their development. With pedagogical and therapeutic measures, the employees support the clients in integrating themselves into the social order. In addition, specific educational and professional opportunities provide a basis for young people and young adults to prepare for a self-responsible, tort-free life.

For the closed residential group, we are looking for a specialist who will provide the special training of max. 8 young people ensured. The workload of 90 - 100% can be done on request in four days (Monday to Thursday / regular working hours, no shift service).
