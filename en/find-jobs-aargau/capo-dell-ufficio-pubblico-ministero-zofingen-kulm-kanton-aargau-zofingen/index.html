Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Lead strategically, operationally and professionally the public prosecutor's office Zofingen-Kulm (about 25 employees)
  • Together with other prosecutors, ensure efficient and uniform processing of criminal proceedings until the final judgment in the districts of Zofingen and Kulm
  • In addition to your management function, you also work actively as a prosecutor and perform pikettdienst
  • Provide within the framework of the requirements of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office for internal and external communication
  • Participate in cantonal and intercantonal bodies and ensure optimal cooperation with the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office and the other public prosecutors' offices, the cantonal and regional police and the courts

What you bring

  • Completed legal studies and a lawyer's patent
  • Qualified, long-standing legal professional experience in law enforcement
  • Strong leadership skills and practical leadership experience
  • Pronounced assertiveness and convincing communication skills

The election to the senior prosecutor is made by the Grand Council at the request of the Government Council.

Your working environment

In the public prosecutor's office in Aargau, we are responsible for criminal proceedings against adults in the canton of Aargau. We lead the preliminary proceedings and prosecute crimes and offenders in close cooperation with the police. We issue criminal orders, bring charges and represent the state criminal claim in court.
