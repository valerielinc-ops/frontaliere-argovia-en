Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG, Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Mansioni

  • Technical and human resources management of small quantities logistics
  • Responsibility for operational customer support in the area of small-quantity logistics
  • Development and further development of the Business Services portfolio
  • Development of concepts, business cases and decision bases
  • Adopting customer and business requirements as well as developing economical and scalable service solutions in cooperation with the responsible specialist areas
  • Coordination of implementation of new services between contracting entities and departments and ensuring business evaluation
  • Strategic procurement and further development of machine stretch film and other consumables
  • Development and monitoring of key figures and derivation of appropriate measures
  • Close cooperation with department leaders in the design and implementation of logistics services

Requirements

  • Several years: management and project experience in a logistical or service-oriented environment
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences): Business administration, logistics, supply chain management or comparable discipline
  • Experience in the preparation of business cases, economic calculations and process models
  • Strong analytical skills as well as entrepreneurial thinking and acting
  • Convincing personality with high communication, networking and implementation competence
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Flexible place of work / home office: We promote flexibility, agility and efficiency with our home office options
  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Food: In our staff restaurants, employees can cater at preferential prices
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
