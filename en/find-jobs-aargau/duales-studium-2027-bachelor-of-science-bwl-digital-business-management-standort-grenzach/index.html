The Position

Are you looking for a diverse and future-oriented dual study program? Then join us in reinventing the future of healthcare. At Roche, we are developing innovative diagnostics and medicines to enable people to live longer and better lives. Join this mission and make a crucial contribution to patients worldwide from the very beginning.

What tasks await you?

  • Digital Compliance and Security: You learn how to manage IT systems safely and implement the strict regulatory requirements of the pharmaceutical industry legally safely. - Business Analytics with Big Data: You are using large amounts of data and developing mathematical models to optimize our business processes on an equal footing. - Innovation through key technologies: You are busy with pioneering technologies such as AI to develop new digital business models and drive innovative approaches.
  • Strategic Finance and Controlling: You support company planning through precise analyses in finance and accounting, and you are the basis for important management decisions. - Market research and marketing: You analyze developments in the health market for our marketing strategies in order to sharpen them and make processes more efficient.

Your practical training will be held at Roche Pharma in Grenzach. The lectures will be held at the Baden-Württemberg Dual University in Lörrach.

Your advantages with us

  • We rely on the model of Personalized Training : You actively shape your training content in close consultation with your instructors: inside.
  • We also offer you:
  • Finance : Attractive remuneration as well as holiday and Christmas money.
  • Work-Life Balance : 30 days holidays, company sports and comprehensive health care.
  • Development : Intensive exam preparation, university entrance qualification (optional) and varied further education.
  • Extras : International work environment, foreign missions and discounted canteen catering.

What condition do you have to meet?

  • General university qualification
  • Subject-related university qualification
  • Subject-level university qualification and proof of the passed suitability test of the Dual University Baden-Württemberg

How long does your dual degree course take? 3 years. Advance one month, company-specific internship
