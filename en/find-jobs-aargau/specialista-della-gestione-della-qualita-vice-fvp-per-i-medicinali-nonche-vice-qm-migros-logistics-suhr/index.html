Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow. Start with us into the future of logistics as a specialist in quality management, Stv. FvP Arzneimittel and Stv. QM 100%.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (Suhr), Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Mansioni

  • You take on joint responsibility for quality management, including the fulfilment of agreed or desired quality standards (e.g. GDP, IFS Logistics, etc.).
  • In addition, you share responsibility for ensuring food safety in the MVB along our processes and quality requirements.
  • You ensure the management of the HACCP & Food Defense documentation, keep it up-to-date and check its implementation in the company.
  • You define quality goals with the specialist areas and assess and document the state of compliance.
  • Your area of responsibility includes the preparation and support of the implementation and documentation of internal audits (by auditor team).
  • As deputy of FvP from MVB You ensure compliance with the requirements in the handling of medicines in the MVB at the Suhr location (according to the contract and instructions).
  • In addition, you are responsible for the implementation of quality management at the Ecublens (MLR) locations and at the operations headquarters in Western Switzerland and work on site for about 1 to 2 days a week.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the areas of quality management and logistics.
  • Completed degree programme (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Completed vocational examination (EFZ or equivalent) and further completed higher specialist training (uni or FH) in the field of logistics, process and/or quality management.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of quality standards and Q systems (e.g. IFS Logistics/GDP).
  • A high level of customer orientation and pronounced strength in process design and its implementation are part of your profile.
  • In addition, bring Your in-depth expertise and practical experience with HACCP systems as well as the legal requirements in the handling of food (including medicines Cat E) in logistics.
  • You convince with organizational talent, negotiating skills, conflict ability, team spirit and creativity.
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (very good knowledge)

  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Development opportunities: We offer individual support programs and task hours. For most professions, you can complete a multi-week stage in another company.
  • Introduction: During the introductory days, you will receive a systematic and comprehensive introduction to the company and the M-Group.
