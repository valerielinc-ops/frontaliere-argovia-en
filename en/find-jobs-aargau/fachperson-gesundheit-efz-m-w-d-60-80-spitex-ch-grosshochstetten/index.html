Looking for a new challenge? Bring your expertise to us! In our SPITEX centre in Grosshochetten we look after people in 18 communities with heart and know-how. If you, as a health professional, are looking for more than just a job - namely a meaningful task in an innovative, supportive team - then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for an immediately or by agreement Health professional EFZ (m/f/d) 40% – 80% With passion and professionalism, strengthen our team and make a real difference to our clients

Their tasks

  • Holistic care: They care for and care for people in their home environment with a lot of empathy and patience
  • Medicine & documentation: You support with nursing and medical tasks, you also independently carry out the nursing documentation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health professional EFZ (FaGe)
  • You like to work independently, responsibly and flexibly
  • You enrich the team with your positioned and committed nature
  • You are in possession of a valid category B driving licence
