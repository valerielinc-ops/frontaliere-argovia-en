Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Analysis of existing concepts, logistics solutions and interfaces to the SNZ 144 as well as partner organizations for the identification and prioritization of supply gaps
  • Development of a scalable target concept with clear roles, management structures and practical processes for medical emergency preparedness in the event of major events
  • Digitalization and automation of manual processes using Microsoft 365 as well as specialist solutions including definition of requirements, data protection and fallback levels
  • Demand identification, monitoring and sustainable management planning for material, storage and logistics procurement
  • Coordination of all stakeholders through specialised groups and workshops, preparation of departmental reports and participation in intercantonal bodies

What you bring

  • Master’s degree from a technical college or university in a relevant subject area or degree from a secondary technical school with corresponding additional training (DAS, MAS), preferably in business administration, public management, health care, rescue, civil protection, logistics or business informatics
  • Several years of experience in project and process management, in organizational development or in the development of complex concepts
  • Practical experience in public administration as well as in safety-critical areas such as health and rescue or public protection
  • Know-how in procurement projects, material management, logistics or technical system implementations
  • High level of coordination and communication, negotiating skills and a confident attitude towards experts, management bodies and external partners

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Health Department focuses on health protection. We ensure needs-based, affordable and high-quality healthcare, promote cooperation and digital networking between healthcare providers and strengthen health-conscious behaviour among the population.
