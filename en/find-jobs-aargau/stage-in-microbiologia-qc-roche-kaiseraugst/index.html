The Position At our location in Kaiseraugst we offer an internship in QC Mikrobiologie from 2027.

The Kaiseraugst production site is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network. More than 1500 people are employed here and the site is home to Roche’s largest and most innovative packaging company in the world, as well as sterile bottling, antibiotics and solida. QC Mikrobiologie manages the entire Kaiseraugst site as well as Basel. The department consists of 4 laboratory units and the E2E experts who have the conceptual oversight and supervise laboratory-independent projects.

The possibility

As an intern in the field of QC microbiology at the Kaiseraugst production site, you support the team in a wide range of aspects.

Part of this internship can be

  • Gain insights into the versatile project work of the E2E experts of QC Microbiology via 4 different units and 2 different sites (Basel and Kaiseraugst);
  • Support and cooperation in ongoing projects. Independent processing of own projects including creation of documents and data evaluation;
  • Example of projects: updating and creating layouts with visualization of sampling points for microbiological environmental monitoring, creating and maintaining an e-library, writing rationales, creating and presenting concepts/study plans;
  • document creation and editing;
  • Accompanying/coordination and Scribe of professional and organizational routine meetings (local and global);
  • Grade: laboratory activities are excluded from the tasks. Some tasks can also be done remotely.

Who you are

You are matriculated or have Completed your master’s degree at a university or university of applied sciences, preferably in the field of natural sciences (biology/microbiology/molecular biology/biotechnology/pharmaceuticals) within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a modern work environment as part of a 12-month internship, as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry.

In addition, you round off your profile with the following competencies:

  • Independent and structured working methods;
  • High level of initiative and rapid comprehension;
  • enjoying constructive cooperation and communication with internal and external interfaces;
  • Very good knowledge of English and German;
  • Good MS Office skills
  • Google Suite knowledge is beneficial, interest in project management tools, such as Monday.

Additional information

  • The preferred start of the internship is in January 2027 or by appointment. Please enter your preferred start date in your application.
  • Part-time employment or the writing of a thesis is not possible at this position.
  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must accompany the application with a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training.
