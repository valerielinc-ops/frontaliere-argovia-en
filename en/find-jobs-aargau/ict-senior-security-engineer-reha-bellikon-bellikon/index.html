Full description

Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

As a Senior Security Engineer, you assume operational responsibility for central, permanently recurring security tasks and build up critical knowledge and action competence within Suva Clinics. You can expect a newly created, responsible specialist function with a direct influence on the cyber resilience of Suva clinics. You move between day-to-day operations, strategic initiatives and cooperation with specialized external partners. At the same time, you will gain creative leeway in building up internal security expertise and in the further development of our ISMS. This post does not take into account recruitment dossiers.

Tasks

  • Security Monitoring & Troubleshooting in the areas of XDR, SIEM, NAC and NDR, including maintenance and troubleshooting
  • Basic Digital Forensics & Incident Response including coordination with the SOC
  • Further development of vulnerability management as well as tracking and implementation of security measures together with local teams
  • Maintenance and further development of the ISMS
  • Ensure compliance with ISO 27001 and ICT minimum standards, including maturity analysis, controls and gap assessments
  • Co-creation and further development of security strategy and architecture across network, cloud (M365/Azure) and endpoint
  • Conducting security configuration checks and continuously developing our security solutions, including MS Defender, SentinelOne, CrowdStrike, Microsoft Azure and M365
  • Implementation of risk analyses, ongoing risk tracking and step-by-step reporting to internal and external stakeholders

Profile

  • Completed training in computer science (HF/FH) with a focus on IT/Cyber-Security or comparable training or relevant certifications (e.g. CISSP, AZ-500, SC-100)
  • Several years of professional experience in an operational cyber security function
  • In-depth practical knowledge in the following areas:
  • SIEM/XDR/NDR/NAC
  • Vulnerability Management
  • IAM/PAM
  • AD/DNS/DHCP/CS
  • Security architecture
  • Good knowledge of security standards and frameworks, in particular ISO 27001 and ICT minimum standard
  • Experience in cooperation with external security service providers (SOC, MSSP) is an advantage
  • Analytical, structured and independent working methods as well as a high level of initiative and responsibility
  • Strong communication skills and very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is advantageous, willingness to provide piquetting services
