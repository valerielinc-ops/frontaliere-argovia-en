Internal Job Title: Quality Control Specialist I

This role is based in Stein, Switzerland. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support for this role. Please only apply if this location is accessible for you.

Join our Quality Control team and play a critical role in ensuring the quality, safety, and compliance of medicines that help improve and extend people's lives. In this role, you will provide services to analytical laboratories. You will lead equipment maintenance and qualification activities while providing scientific expertise and collaborate with cross-functional teams in a GMP-regulated environment. This is an exciting opportunity for laboratory professionals who enjoy combining technical excellence, problem solving, and continuous improvement while making a meaningful impact on patients worldwide.

Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Perform lab equipment maintenance and qualification activities
  • Review and documentation of maintenance and qualification activities.
  • Responsible for external service technician regarding on-time execution with required quality
  • Maintain laboratory equipment and ensure its readiness for use.
  • Participate in investigations, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Contribute to KPI tracking and quality-related documentation.
  • Collaborate with Quality, Manufacturing, and cross-functional teams to ensure compliance and operational excellence.
  • Follow HSE and quality requirements to maintain a safe and compliant working environment.

Essential Requirements

  • Education as Analyst, degree or vocational training in Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, or a related scientific field.
  • First experience in a laboratory environment, preferably within a regulated industry.
  • Basic understanding of GMP/GxP practices or willingness to learn.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills.
  • Fluent German and basic English skills.

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience in Quality Control or analytical testing.
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical, biotech, or life sciences industry.
