Full description

The rehabilitation clinic in Baden-Dättwil offers rehabilitation at a high medical level for musculoskeletal and neurological patients. Thanks to the interdisciplinary cooperation and the excellent expertise of the treatment teams, we adapt our efficient therapy programs individually to the needs of these patients.

Your tasks

  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You treat our inpatient patients in the field of orthopaedics, geriatrics, cardiology, internal medicine. You are in a constant exchange with a team of doctors, nurses and other therapeutic professions.
  • Patient-oriented care: You have an ICF-oriented work spectrum, from med. Training therapy until reintegration into the private or professional environment
  • Therapies: You perform individual and group therapies

Your profile

  • Training : Completed training or study as a movement therapist, sports scientist or equivalent training
  • Personality: You have teamwork skills and enjoy interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration
  • Interest: You are interested in MSK or have already completed further training in this area

We offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your online application!
