The Position At the Kaiseraugst site, around 1,800 employees with innovative spirit and the highest quality standards ensure the continuous care of patients worldwide. As part of Pharma Technical Operations (PT), the site is a central pillar of Roche’s production of sterile medicines. The MSAT Sterile Team (Manufacturing Science and Technology) is actively involved in shaping technological progress, validation and optimisation of aseptic processes. It combines scientific expertise with GMP compliance to ensure safe, efficient and sustainable manufacturing processes for sterile drugs.

The possibility

  • Strategic responsibility: You take responsibility for the cleaning validation strategy, create GMP-compliant SOPs, plans and risk assessments, and act as a subject matter expert (SME) for PQS documents.
  • Operational excellence: You organize cleaning validation sample trains in the company end-to-end (planning, execution, sample transport and operating materials).
  • Data analysis & studies: You carry out evaluations, in-line data analyses, recovery studies, monitoring and spray shadow tests and actively contribute your knowledge to adjacent specialist areas.
  • Troubleshooting & Collaboration: You analyze causes of process deviations, assess impact analyses, support tech transfers and play a leading role in global, cross-functional teams.
  • Interface coordination & representation: You coordinate tasks in the team and at interfaces, participate in planning and control meetings and represent the area during inspections and audits.
  • Team development & coaching: You promote the professional and personal development of your colleagues and actively shape team development.

Who you are

  • Expertise & experience: In-depth experience in cleaning validation (solid drug forms) and in-depth understanding of current GMP guidelines.
  • Analytical Thinking & Troubleshooting: Strong technical understanding for independent root cause analysis and data interpretation.
  • Communication & Leadership: Strong communication strength (in German and English) as well as enjoyment of coaching and sharing knowledge in a team.
  • Agile mindset: A structured, precise way of working combined with openness to change and solution-oriented ideas.
  • IT affinity: Good understanding of common IT systems and digital tools in the production and validation environment.
