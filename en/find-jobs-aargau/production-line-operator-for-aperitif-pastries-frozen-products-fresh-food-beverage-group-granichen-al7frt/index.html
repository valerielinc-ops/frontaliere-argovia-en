Full description

We ensure that favorite products are created – in Switzerland and for Switzerland. As a plant manager, you are at the heart of our production. With your technical know-how, you ensure that our plants run smoothly. Are you ready for a new challenge?

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Gränichen, Bäckerstrasse 1, 5722 Gränichen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Professional management of installations and troubleshooting
  • Material preparation according to the recipe
  • Operation and installation of modern production facilities
  • Carry out quality controls
  • Support in band work and packaging

Requirements

  • 3 - 5 years: plant manager or food technologist
  • Completed initial or further training: plant manager or food technologist
  • Willingness to work in shift mode (early/late) between 4 a.m. and 11 p.m. and flexibility for weekend assignments (seasonal)
  • Experience in industry, preferably in the food industry
  • High hygiene standards and a sense of responsibility
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave (instead of 14 weeks), 4 weeks paternity leave (instead of 2 weeks).
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
  • Club School Migros: Refund of 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Sports and fitness offers: refund of CHF 200 per fitness annual subscription.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees as part of internal programmes and, if necessary, support external training with time and money.
  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are per you!
  • Cumulus points: 3 times points on food, 6 times points on all other products.
  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications (FFB Group): We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email.
  • Talks with HR & department (FFB-Group): The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team.
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
