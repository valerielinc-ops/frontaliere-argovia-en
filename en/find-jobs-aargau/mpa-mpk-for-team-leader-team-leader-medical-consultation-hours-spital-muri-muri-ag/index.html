Full description

Our clinical operation combines the office hours and secretariats of the medical office hours. Work takes place from Monday to Friday within regular working hours. For the medical department with a versatile, interesting and lively area of responsibility, we are looking for a committed team leadership immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Management and management of medical consultation hours
  • Planning and organisation of office hours
  • Project participation in interdisciplinary processes
  • General administrative activities
  • Mutual representation of the team leaders of the clinical establishment in absences
  • Assistance in the team: appointment, writing medical reports from Diktaphon, coordination of internal and external requests from patients and referees by e-mail and telephone (reports, prescriptions, appointments) as well as careful maintenance of electronic patient records (KISIM/Business Central)

Your profile

  • Basic medical education (MPA, MPK or similar) possibly with commercial further education
  • Some years of professional experience in this area are beneficial
  • Leadership experience
  • Experience in optimizing and adapting processes
  • High social skills, communication skills and team skills
  • Very good PC knowledge, ideally knowledge in dealing with Navision & KISIM
  • Safe mastery of medical terminology
  • Style-confident oral and written expression

Your opportunity It offers you a varied and challenging task in a vibrant company characterised by growth and modernisation with a focus on teamwork. The interdisciplinary cooperation offers space for self-responsible work and internal and external training opportunities.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented if possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 80-100 %
