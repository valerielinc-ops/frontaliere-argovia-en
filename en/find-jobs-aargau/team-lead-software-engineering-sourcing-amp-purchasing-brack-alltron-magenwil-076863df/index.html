Full description

Tasks

  • You lead, coach and develop an engineering team with a focus on personal responsibility, quality and continuous learning
  • You work hands-on in development – from the first idea to the go-live
  • You will work closely with product management, stakeholders and adjacent teams to implement modern and future-proof software solutions in the field of sourcing and purchasing
  • You use modern architectural concepts such as domain-driven design (DDD), self-contained systems (SCS), data mesh and develop interface designs and modular components in your team.
  • You ensure high code quality – through code reviews, pair programming and structured testing processes
  • You optimize our development and operational environments (CI/CD, DevOps practices) and ensure smooth deployments Requirements
  • You have several years of experience (minimum 5 years) in software development, of which at least 1 year in a leadership role
  • Ideally in e-commerce or similar
  • You’ll feel comfortable in short, agile sprints and drive automation to quickly deliver stable, working software.
  • You bring in-depth knowledge of Java and SQL / data modeling, as well as experience with CI / CD and DevOps - cloud experience is an advantage
  • You have strong communication skills and leadership mentality – empathetic, clear and motivating
  • Create a trusting team environment, support an open culture of error and learning, and use tools to strengthen motivation and collaboration
  • You are familiar with modular software architecture, interface design and asynchronous data processing
  • You have completed a degree in (business) computer science or an equivalent qualification We offer:
  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
