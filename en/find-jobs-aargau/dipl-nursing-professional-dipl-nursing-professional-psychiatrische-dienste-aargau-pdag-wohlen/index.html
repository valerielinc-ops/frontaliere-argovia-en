Full description

In order to strengthen our team in nursing and social pedagogy, we are looking for therapy at our clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy at 1. January 2027 or by arrangement

Tasks

  • You support and promote the social and emotional skills and abilities of patients (age from 7 to ≤ 18 years) in the milieutherapeutic setting
  • During the night service, you will be responsible for the night’s sleep, safety and well-being of our patients.
  • You design the daily structure of the patients in cooperation with the team and the other specialist areas
  • Acting in emergency situations You are competent and provide safety for children and young people
  • You are jointly responsible for the implementation of treatment planning

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Medical professional HF/FH and already have professional experience, benefiting from psychiatric experience and/or experience with children and adolescents
  • You are flexible, committed, resilient and a responsible person with pleasure in demanding patient care in a lively company
  • You speak and write fluent German and have a secure handling of the common office applications
