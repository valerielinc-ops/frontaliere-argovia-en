Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, ML Suhr, Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Physical receipt of goods (with/without SSCC in compliance with work instructions)
  • Verification of incoming goods according to defined criteria
  • Determination of deviations (SQS)
  • Unloading truck / railway car with 5 tons of truck
  • General cleaning work
  • Select delivery alerts, collect master data, special cases
  • Correct handling of unloading aids / conveyor technology
  • Correct start-up/decommissioning of the tools (forklifts, free-riding trucks)

Requirements

  • 3 to 5 years: Logistics incoming goods
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Logistics
  • Completed training as a logistician EFZ incl. Work experience truck driving (5 t truck of advantage)
  • SUVA recognised forklift certificate
  • Good German skills in word and writing as well as good PC skills
  • Accurate, independent working method
  • Readiness to work in shift operation: (5.00 to 2.15 pm / 2.00 to 11.00 pm) also Saturday assignments possible
  • German (good knowledge)
