Full description

The Bachem Group is investing in a new site in Sisslerfeld in Northwestern Switzerland. This step is part of our long-term strategy to expand production capacity. The new production site will play a central role in achieving these goals. For this large-scale CAPEX investment project, we are seeking an experienced Senior Project Manager Automation

Your tasks

  • Act as Senior Project Manager and Automation Subject Matter Expert (SME) for Downstream Processing (DSP) Automation within a major CAPEX program (> CHF 100 million).
  • Lead the automation workstream for downstream manufacturing processes, including:
  • Chromatography
  • Filtration (UF/DF, TFF)
  • Purification and related process unit operations
  • Translate process requirements into automation concepts, batch logic and control strategies
  • Review and optimize automation designs and system architectures in collaboration with external partners
  • Integrate systems into the overall automation landscape (DCS, PLC, MES, Historian)
  • Ensure compliance with GMP, GAMP5, data integrity and validation requirements
  • Coordinate and lead external engineering firms, system integrators and contractors
  • Collaborate closely with Engineering, TechOps, IT/OT, and Quality
  • Support the project from concept phase through commissioning, qualification, and handover
  • Contribute to standardization and development of automation concepts for the new site
  • Ensure delivery in terms of quality, budget, and timeline

Your profile

  • Degree in automation, chemical engineering, biotechnology or a related field
  • At least 8–10 years of experience in automation within pharmaceutical or biotech environments
  • Strong expertise in process automation
  • Experience with DCS system (Siemens PCS7, PCSneo), PLC (e.g. Siemens TIA Portal), SCADA (WinCC) and batch control
  • Solid understanding of GMP, GAMP5 and CQV processes (IQ/OQ/PQ)
  • Proven experience in CAPEX projects and interdisciplinary teams
  • Experience with MES, Historian and data/PAT integration
  • Independent, structured working style with high responsibility
  • High resilience and flexibility
  • Very good knowledge of English
  • German is a plus

Our offer

  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Company‑run daycare center on the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
