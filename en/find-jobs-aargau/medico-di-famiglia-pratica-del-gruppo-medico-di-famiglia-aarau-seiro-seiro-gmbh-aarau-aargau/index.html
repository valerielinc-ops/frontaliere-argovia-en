In a group practice in the region of Aarau, Canton Aargau, your patients are cared for by a family doctor over the entire breadth of general internal medicine, from prevention to diagnosis and long-term care of chronic diseases. We are looking for you immediately or by agreement as a specialist for general internal medicine in the amount of 60 – 100%.   • Active participation in the further development of medical quality and processes
