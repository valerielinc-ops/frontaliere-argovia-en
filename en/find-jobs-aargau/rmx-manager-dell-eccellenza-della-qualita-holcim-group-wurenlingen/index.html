Full description

Do you not only want to secure concrete quality, but also actively develop it with digitalization, innovation and technical know-how? Then take on a central role in our quality management and shape the future of our concrete production in Switzerland.

What to expect

  • Key User FIRE-Q: Responsibility for digitalization, further development and international coordination of the QC software with the aim of being the contact point for the FIRE-Q software in Switzerland for the German-speaking concrete technologists as well as the Italian language area
  • Mix design optimization: Analysis and optimization of recipes with focus on quality, efficiency and profitability
  • QC interfaces: Professional responsibility for the implementation of new QC systems and interfaces
  • QC management in Ticino: responsibility for employees, accredited laboratory and regional QC activities
  • Special and research projects: Independent management of strategic innovation and research projects
  • Participation in cross-divisional projects as well as international coordination on quality and digitalization topics

What you bring

  • Training as a concrete technologist:in, materials scientist:in, concrete or civil engineer:in
  • 2–5 years of professional experience in concrete technology and a sound understanding of standards and quality requirements
  • Excellent IT user knowledge and a high affinity for new technologies and digitalization
  • Precise, structured and independent working with a high sense of responsibility
  • Communication strength, initiative and a team- and customer-oriented working method
  • Confident knowledge of German as well as tried-and-tested knowledge of English and Italian
  • Category B driving licence

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful familiarization, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.

  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
