Full description

You want not only to ensure concrete quality, but also to actively advance it through digitalization, innovation and technical expertise? Then take on a key role in our quality management and help shape the future of our concrete production in Switzerland.

What you can expect

  • Key User FIRE-Q: Responsibility for the digitalization, further development and international coordination of the QC software, with the aim of being the point of contact for the FIRE-Q software in Switzerland for German-speaking concrete technologists as well as the Italian-speaking region
  • Mix design optimization : Analysis and optimization of formulations with a focus on quality, efficiency and profitability
  • QC interfaces: Technical responsibility for the implementation of new QC systems and interfaces
  • QC management in Ticino: Responsibility for employees, accredited laboratory and regional QC activities
  • Special and research projects: Independent management of strategic innovation and research projects
  • Participation in cross-functional projects as well as international coordination on quality and digitalization topics

What you bring

  • Qualification as a concrete technologist, materials scientist, concrete or civil engineer
  • 2–5 years of professional experience in concrete technology and a sound understanding of standards and quality requirements
  • Excellent IT user skills and a strong affinity for new technologies and digitalization
  • A precise, structured and independent approach to work with a high sense of responsibility
  • Strong communication skills, initiative and a team- and customer-oriented approach to work
  • Business-fluent German as well as proven practical English and Italian skills; category B driving licence

What we offer

  • Onboarding & Getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and forward-looking work environment. We support you from the very beginning with thorough, practical and structured onboarding so that you can get off to a successful start.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. At our company, you as an individual with your unique strengths and potential are at the center. Those who want to develop are supported and can achieve a great deal.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: At our company, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that sticks together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trust-based leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours, a 41-hour workweek, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days of vacation, the option to purchase vacation days, and extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive Compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee Benefits: From Reka-Checks and shares at preferential prices to the Swibeco benefits platform - we make sure that you feel completely comfortable and benefit from attractive additional benefits
