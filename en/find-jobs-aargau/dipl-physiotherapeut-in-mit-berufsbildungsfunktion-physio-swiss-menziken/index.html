In addition to classical physiotherapy, we also cover various special fields, such as neurological therapies, pelvic floor therapy, shock wave and electrotherapy, child physiotherapy, sports physiotherapy or emmeth therapy. Individual treatments at the highest level are our first priority for our qualified employees. Our wide range of offers for rehabilitation and prevention is complemented by an in-house therapy bath. As an apprenticeship company, we offer several internship places as part of our studies as a physiotherapist FH. To complement our team at the new Falkenstein Health Center location, we are looking for you – immediately or by appointment! dipl. Physical therapist with vocational training function, 80 - 100%

What we offer you

  • A house that stands for humanity, respectful coexistence and a dynamic environment.
  • A varied activity in which you can work independently and creatively.
  • Top employment conditions because we value good work.
  • Further education opportunities, so that you always stay on the pulse.
  • Flexible working hours.

Your tasks

  • Self-responsible treatment and care of our patients on medical prescription.
  • Implementation of high-quality, evidence-based therapies, including use of our modern therapy bath.
  • Active participation in service-oriented, communicative and empathetic care.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with long-term care, the internal family practice and the doctors of the ambulatorium.
  • Practical support and coaching of our physiotherapy students at BFH and ZHAW.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as a dipl. Physical therapist/FH or equivalent training with SRC recognition.
  • Additional qualification in the field of vocational training or the willingness to acquire it.
  • You are a committed, open-minded personality with high social skills and team skills.
  • You understand how to accompany patients and our residents empathetically, motivatingly and professionally.
  • High quality and service orientation characterize your working method.
  • You bring innovative spirit, act forward-looking and are interested in constantly developing your expertise.
