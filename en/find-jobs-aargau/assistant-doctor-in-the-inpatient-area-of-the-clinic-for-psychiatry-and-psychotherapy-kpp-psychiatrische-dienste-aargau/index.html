Full description

To strengthen our team we look for agreement you as

Tasks

  • Under medical supervision, you are responsible for the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of adults at one of our stations. They treat people with clinical pictures from the entire psychiatric spectrum
  • Participation in on-call services as part of the PDAG service concept, including about 6 weekend days per year (at 100% workload)
  • As part of an interdisciplinary team, they work closely with other centres within the clinic and partner institutions.
  • They produce reports to the cooperation partners (doctors, psychologists, insurance companies, offices and authorities)

Requirements

  • You have a federal medical diploma or an equivalent degree in human medicine recognized by MEBEKO
  • They ideally bring professional experience in psychiatry/psychotherapy or somatics
  • You are an efficient, well-organized, flexible and resilient team player and are happy to take on responsibility
  • Your German skills are very good, level C1
