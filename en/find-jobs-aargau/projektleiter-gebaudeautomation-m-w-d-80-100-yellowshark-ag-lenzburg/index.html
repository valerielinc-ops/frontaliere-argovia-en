01 Future employer

For one of our clients, we are looking for a dedicated and technically skilled professional in the field of building automation. In this position, you will play a key role in planning and implementing sophisticated automation solutions and accompany projects from the concept phase through to successful completion.

You can expect a varied range of responsibilities involving modern projects, considerable autonomy and the opportunity to apply and further develop your expertise in a targeted manner.

02 Your task

  • You are responsible for independently planning and managing projects in the field of building automation.
  • From the initial idea through implementation, you develop technical concepts and suitable solutions.
  • You prepare tender and submission documents and support their processing.
  • As the project lead, you ensure that quality, costs and deadlines are adhered to.
  • You coordinate collaboration with internal and external project stakeholders.
  • Communication with customers, specialist planners, authorities and other contacts is also part of your varied range of responsibilities.
  • You actively help ensure the successful completion of projects and bring your own ideas to the further development of the solutions.

  • Completed technical vocational training, preferably in building services engineering, HVAC or a comparable field
  • Further technical training, for example as an HF technician in building services engineering or building automation
  • Sound practical experience in the planning, design or implementation of building automation systems
  • Good knowledge of the relevant technical standards and guidelines, as well as a solid understanding of HVAC, sanitary and electrical systems and their control
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable role
