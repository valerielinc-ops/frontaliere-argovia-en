Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Negotiation preparation (creation of protocols, comparisons, calculation tables, etc.)
  • Minutes and participation in court hearings, hearings, inspections, etc.
  • Independent editing of judgments (dispositive, with reasoning) in all proceedings of criminal, civil, child and adult protection law
  • Clarification of legal issues
  • Granting of legal information in the tenancy law
  • Independent case handling in legal opening proceedings

What you bring

  • Completed master’s degree in law at a Swiss university or shortly before graduation
  • Interest and ability to deal with complex legal issues in a solution-oriented manner
  • Precise, forward-looking, reliable and independent working methods
  • Rapid comprehension and good editorial skills with very good knowledge of German
  • Legal, administrative or legal practice beneficial

The courts make judgments every day in a wide variety of areas of law.

The focus is on an efficient and citizen-friendly justice system for peaceful coexistence in the Canton of Aargau.
