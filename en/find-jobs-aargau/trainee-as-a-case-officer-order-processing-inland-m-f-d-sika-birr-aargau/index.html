About the role Would you like to gain practical experience in the commercial sector and prove your organizational talent? Then become part of our team in Birr. You will support our team in day-to-day business and get to know the manifold processes of order processing.

  • Active support of order processing from order to delivery and billing
  • Processing of customer orders including returns, credit and direct debits
  • Monitoring of delivery dates, open orders and residues
  • Maintenance of customer and order data in the relevant systems
  • Support for customer inquiries, complaints and the organization of transports
  • Close cooperation with customers, sales consultants and internal interfaces
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks, evaluations and process optimization

Your skills and experience

  • Completed or ongoing education (KV, secondary school, commercial school) or ongoing or completed studies
  • Interest in commercial, logistical and customer-oriented processes
  • Very good PC knowledge (Word & Excel)
  • First experience with SAP, Salesforce or similar ERP/CRM systems is advantageous, but no requirement
  • Joy of communication and customer contact, German in word and writing (French of advantage)
  • High self-motivation, willingness to learn and a reliable and structured working method
  • Careful working methods and strong organisational talent
  • Interested in taking on responsibility and becoming familiar with new topics

Why you should come to us

  • A motivated, experienced team with a lot of know-how and commitment
  • A 40-hour week
  • The opportunity to actively participate in the further development and digitalization of customer service
  • For a large company perhaps surprisingly flat hierarchies with manageable decision-making paths
  • Free parking on site

We look forward to your complete application dossier (CV, diplomas, certificates, etc.).

Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
