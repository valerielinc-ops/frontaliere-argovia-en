Pensum: 80–100%, bathing

We are looking for a committed and dynamic personality who will drive the digital transformation in the energy industry together with us. In this exciting position, you will lay the foundations for data-driven decisions and AI-assisted automation.

Tasks & Responsibilities

You are instrumental in implementing the division’s data strategy and you shape how we handle data. You are responsible for Grid data on the company-wide data platform of Axpo.You organize the “Data Community Grid” and support the division to develop into a data-driven organization. You become the first point of contact for data questions in the division.You define and monitor data quality rules and initiate corrections with data controllers.You identify data-related use cases where the use of the data platform adds value. Specifically, you analyze and visualize asset, SCADA and project data.You implement smaller data projects yourself and work closely with internal (e.g. IT, software development) and external partners (e.g. universities, start-ups). Where necessary, you can also support the project planning and installation of IoT sensors on our “hands-on” assets. Profile & RequirementsCompleted degree in engineering or comparable qualification. An electrical engineering degree is an advantage.At least 5 years of professional experience in the field of data/analytics and experience in the management of projects and in stakeholder management.Very good programming knowledge (e.g. Python, PySpark, SQL, PowerBI) and experience in dealing with data platforms (e.g. Databricks).Experience with IoT, system architectures and visualization of live data is an advantage.Experience in physical asset management and in the operation of power grids is an advantage.Knowledge in the energy sector and interest in new technological developments.Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

About the teamThe responsibility for Digitalization & Innovation of the Networks Division, together with the technical and financial planning and asset analysis, lies in the Strategic Asset Management of the Distribution Division (networks). We work and think interdisciplinary and offer you a challenging and versatile job in a dynamic environment. If you want to be at the forefront of the digital transformation in the energy industry, want to contribute your own ideas and love to implement innovative projects, then we look forward to your application. #Grid
