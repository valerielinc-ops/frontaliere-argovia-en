Full description

Cleanliness, hygiene and order are more than just tasks for you? Then design a well-kept and safe working environment with us. At Delica, you make an important contribution to the quality, sustainability and well-being of our employees every day. Together we ensure the highest standards and Swiss quality.

Mansioni

  • You carry out cleaning work independently according to the cleaning plan and given intervals.
  • You clean floors, lounges, offices, sanitary facilities and other assigned areas professionally.
  • You operate cleaning machines safely and ensure their careful use.
  • You consistently comply with hygiene, safety and legal regulations.
  • You report damages, defects or special events to the responsible authority in a timely manner.

Requirements

  • At least 2 years of professional experience: in professional cleaning.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a cleaning specialist EFZ or cleaning practitioner EBA of advantage.
  • You are physically resilient, flexible and enjoy working in a team.
  • You work reliably, on time, independently and with high quality orientation.
  • Delight in the careful handling of cleaning products and work equipment.
  • in German (negotiation confident)
