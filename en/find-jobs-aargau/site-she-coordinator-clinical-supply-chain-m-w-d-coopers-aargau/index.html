Full description

For an internationally active organization in the field of clinical supply chain, we are looking for an experienced personality who is responsible for and further develops the topics of safety, health & environment (SHE) throughout the location. The position combines strategic responsibility with operational implementation and offers the opportunity to actively shape safety, health and environmental issues in a GMP-regulated environment. You will work closely with executives, specialist departments and various stakeholders and contribute significantly to the safe implementation of clinical supply chain processes.

Hard facts

  • Pensum: 60%
  • Location: Kaiseraugst
  • Home Office: By arrangement, mostly on site
  • Work model: Contracting

Their tasks

  • Overall responsibility for all SHE activities within the Clinical Supply Chain Organisation at the Kaiseraugst site
  • Co-shaping the strategic orientation and goals in the area of safety, health & environment together with the leadership team and relevant stakeholders
  • Coordination and implementation of cross-site SHE initiatives and activities
  • Implementation and moderation of risk assessments and ensuring sustainable SHE risk management
  • Promoting standardisation, harmonisation and best practices within the organisation
  • Responsibility for building and operational security as building officer:r for safety and environmental protection
  • Development, maintenance and monitoring of the existing SHE programme
  • Promoting awareness and knowledge on SHE-relevant topics within the organisation
  • Main contact:in for occupational safety, health and environmental protection
  • Responsibility for topics related to controlled substances and chemical management
  • Advising and supporting teams and stakeholders on SHE-relevant issues

Your profile

Must-have

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry or a comparable regulated environment
  • University degree in pharmaceutical engineering, natural sciences or a comparable field of expertise or technical training with corresponding professional experience
  • In-depth experience in pharmaceutical production, packaging, quality assurance, compliance or supply chain
  • Very good understanding of GMP requirements as well as regulatory and quality-related requirements
  • Proven experience in Safety, Health & Environment (SHE/EHS/HSE)
  • Experience in carrying out risk assessments and managing safety and environmental issues
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
  • Experience in managing and coordinating cross-functional projects or initiatives
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English in words and writing

Nice-to-have

  • Leadership experience in a matrix organization
  • Experience in agile organizational structures
  • Knowledge of clinical supply chains as well as pharmaceutical manufacturing and packaging processes
  • Further education or certification in Lean Management, Six Sigma, Operational Excellence, GMP or Supply Chain Management
