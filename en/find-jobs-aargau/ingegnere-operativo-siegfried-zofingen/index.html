Full description

"Expect to grow" , personally and professionally: At Siegfried you can expect exciting challenges and numerous opportunities to prove your talents. As a trusted and respected partner to the pharmaceutical industry, we maintain the highest standards of safety, quality and sustainability – while fostering a culture that empowers you to succeed. Become part of a fast-growing, global company where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a collaborative environment. Here you’ll find a workplace that values both collective success and your individual contributions – and offers you long-term opportunities to grow and make a difference.

Your Role

As a company engineer, you are the central technical contact person for our production facilities and ensure smooth operation together with internal and external partners. You work on challenging technical projects and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our facilities.

Your Profiles

Their role

  • Technical contact person for the assigned production facilities
  • Coordinating plant failure activities together with production, maintenance, workshops and external service providers
  • Developing and implementing corrective and preventive maintenance measures
  • Close cooperation with maintenance and other interfaces
  • Support internal contracting authorities/entities in defining and establishing technical requirements
  • Manage and implement technical projects taking into account current project management standards and GEP requirements
  • Monitoring project costs, deadlines and project accounts
  • Creating and maintaining plant documentation according to uniform standards

Your profile Professional knowledge

  • Completed basic technical training, e.g. as a constructor, polymechanic or comparable, supplemented by further training as a machine engineer HF, machine engineer FH or a comparable qualification
  • Process engineering knowledge is desirable
  • Industry knowledge in chemical, pharmaceutical or related industries is beneficial
  • Knowledge of chemical plant construction or experience with industrial production plants is desirable

Personal strengths

  • Team-oriented, communicative, open and authentic personality
  • Quiet, structured and reliable working method – even in hectic situations
  • Networked and solution-oriented thinking
  • High level of initiative, responsibility and assertiveness

  • Staff restaurant / cafeteria: catering on preferential terms
  • Centrally located: Train station near location with very good connections to public transport
  • Access to exclusive corporate benefits and benefits for our employees

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of over 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
