Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Legal supervision proceedings against persons and institutions in the health sector in cooperation with the cantonal doctor, cantonal dentist and cantonal pharmacist
  • Examination of health and social security applications in the field of ambulatory and inpatient health professions
  • Answering questions on the modalities of the entire occupation of health professionals
  • Support in legal matters relating to health law topics such as therapeutic law, medical history and data protection

What you bring

  • Completed law degree and several years of experience, ideally in the field of public administration
  • In-depth knowledge of public law and administrative procedures as well as interest in regulatory issues in health care
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Established and interested personality who retains his resilience even in hectic situations

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Health Department focuses on the protection of health. We ensure needs-based, affordable and high-quality healthcare, promote cooperation and digital networking between healthcare providers and strengthen health-conscious behaviour among the population.
