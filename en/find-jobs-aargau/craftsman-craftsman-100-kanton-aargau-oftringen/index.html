Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Design and care for green oases: You take care of the professional care and the cutting of trees, shrubs and green areas, so that everything blossoms in full glory
  • Road construction specialist: You implement construction and surface work on the cantonal roads precisely and thus contribute to a smooth infrastructure
  • Ready for winter use: During the winter service, you tackle the pikett planning and ensure that the roads remain safe and passable
  • Security comes first: By signaling construction sites correctly, you ensure that all work runs smoothly and safely.

What you bring

  • Completed craftsmanship training as forest keeper, specialist for maintenance, horticulturist, road builder, mason or equivalent
  • Category B driving licence mandatory
  • High personal responsibility with flair for craft work
  • For the winter and pikett service a car and residence in the region is a requirement (maximum 30 minutes)
  • Ideally, ticket C1 E, or the willingness to obtain it

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. From green maintenance to winter service, from the redesign of local transits to innovative traffic management and the planning of new, safe and environmentally compatible transport facilities – in the civil engineering department we ensure the construction, maintenance and operation of the over 1'150 km long cantonal road network as well as the expansion of the more than 950 km velorout network.
