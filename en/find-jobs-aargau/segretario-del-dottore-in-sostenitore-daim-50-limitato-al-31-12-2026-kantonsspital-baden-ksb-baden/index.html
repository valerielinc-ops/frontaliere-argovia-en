Full description

In the Department of Anesthesia and Intensive Medicine (DAIM) of the Cantonal Hospital of Baden, an exciting place in the IDIS is released by immediately or by agreement. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a reliable and dedicated person who takes over the administrative and organizational support of the doctors team in the intensive care unit and contributes to the success of the team with a proactive working style. Your tasks:

  • You offer administrative and organizational support for the intensive care team.
  • You take the personnel management, including entry and exit as well as rotations.
  • You are responsible for data management and controlling statistics.
  • You are the contact person for patients, doctors and internal and external partners – both in writing and by phone.
  • You conduct the business correspondence, organize sessions or events, record protocols and write medical reports.
  • You support across departments and represent the team within the support center DAIM. Your profile:
  • You have a completed training as a doctor's secretary or hospital secretary and ideally experience in a clinic as well as several years in healthcare.
  • You are familiar with medical terminology and have knowledge in insurance.
  • You are a predictive, proactive, communicative personality with a “Can Do” mentality.
  • You can safely master the German language in word and writing.
  • You have general IT skills and very good user knowledge in the MS Office standard programs.
  • You work exactly, efficiently and independently.
  • You are service-oriented, flexible and ready to work in new areas of work. Their advantages: The KSB offers more than an exciting workplace with good working conditions.
