Full description

Do you not only want to watch, but actively shape the decarbonization of industry? Then you are exactly right with us. During your 6-month internship, you will dive deep into our core strategies around decarbonisation and the circular economy and work on sustainable solutions for tomorrow’s industry.

For our cement plant Siggenthal, we are looking for a curious and committed personality who will work with us on new paths for sustainable building materials and resource-saving processes.

Your mission

• You are responsible for projects with a high level of networking across various areas of the cement plant • Optimize the use of natural raw materials and promote the use of recycled materials • You are actively working to reduce the CO2 balance and the energy demand of our products – while maintaining high quality.

Your field of activity

  • Raw materials: You classify natural raw materials, products and recycled materials and define optimal mixtures for the highest possible proportion of recycled materials.
  • Analytical methods: You understand, evaluate and compare existing analysis methods for mineral substances.
  • Process technology: You analyze and optimize process steps such as grinding, mixing, homogenizing and conveying raw materials.
  • Control technology: You optimize existing control mechanisms and develop an ongoing investment project to improve analysis and control technology.

Your profile

  • You are in a bachelor's or master's degree or have already completed it - ideally in materials science, process engineering or chemistry
  • You bring a hands-on mentality and enjoy both planning projects and conducting tests directly in the company or laboratory
  • You work in a structured, self-initiated and happy team and are interested in process technology
  • You have good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

What we offer you (besides the foundation for your career) • Mentoring at eye level: Our Plant Manager is your sparring partner - open, honest, direct. • Impact: You work on the biggest challenge in our industry: Building the future without emissions. • Safety & freedom of action: The strength of a global market leader at the back, the freedom of a local plant under the nose.

Ready to enter your career?

We are not looking for perfect CVs, we are looking for real personalities.

If you feel like not only managing the foundation of Switzerland, but reinventing it, then get in touch.

Let’s not talk about jobs. Let’s talk about your future at work. We look forward to your application!
