Full description

Your tasks

  • Leadership and strategic directionYou lead the Documentation department with clear goals on quality, content, and budget. You pursue the goal consistently and actively align your department with the future requirements of the KKL.
  • Responsibility for technical documentationYou are responsible for the setup, structure, organisation and ongoing updating of the technical documentation of the KKL, ensure audit reliability, traceability and regulatory compliance and ensure optimal usability throughout the power plant.
  • Digitalization, automation and process streamliningYou drive digitalization in the field of technical documentation – from digital document management to digital archiving to efficient search, storage and workflow structures – and realize automation and simplification potential.
  • Shape change and strengthen collaborationYou actively shape change processes, bring your own ideas to further develop your area of responsibility, strengthen the security culture and work closely with IT and other interface areas to implement sustainable, sustainable solutions.

This is what you bring with you

  • BackgroundDegree at a technical university in a technical or business environment. Additional experience in the technical environment is essential, ideally in plant or power plant construction or in a comparable, highly regulated environment. We take over the initiation of the procedure for a positive risk decision (according to VAPK) as part of the employment process for you.
  • Document Management and DigitalizationSound knowledge in the organization of technical documentation, ideally in digital archiving as well as their integration into processes, experience with document management systems, ideally in nuclear technology.
  • Leadership and change competence Leadership experience or several years of experience in a technically leading role with a clear suitability for a leadership function, pleasure in the development of employees and in anchoring a performance-oriented and digital mindset in the team.
  • Work style and implementation powerStructured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with a high sense of quality and responsibility as well as the ability to make complex requirements understandable and translate them into practical solutions.
  • Language and IT competenceVery good user knowledge of the MS-Office tools, high affinity for digital tools and systems, very good German language and writing skills as well as ideally good English language skills.
