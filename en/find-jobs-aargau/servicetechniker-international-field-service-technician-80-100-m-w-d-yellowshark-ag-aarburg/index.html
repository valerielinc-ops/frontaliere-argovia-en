For our business partner, an internationally active Swiss industrial company, we are looking for an independent and customer-oriented personality as a service technician International. Yellowsharek AG accompanies you personally and transparently on the way to a long-term permanent position.

02 Your task

  • Implementation and management of assembly and commissioning operations at international customers
  • Installation, inspection and handover of machines and systems on site
  • Support of local service partners in maintenance, repair and malfunction operations
  • Design of electrical conversions, extensions and technical adjustments to existing plants
  • Training and instruction of customers and representative staff in handling the machines
  • Analysis and repair of technical faults directly with the customer
  • Close cooperation with internal departments in technical feedback and improvements
