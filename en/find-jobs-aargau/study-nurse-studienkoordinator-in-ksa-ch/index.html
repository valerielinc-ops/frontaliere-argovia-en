Full description

Tasks

We conduct clinical studies in radio-oncology, oncology and imaging. As one of the few centres in Europe, we are a member of the North American study network NRG-Oncology. Other specialties of our center are the leading position in Switzerland in the combination of radiotherapy and deep hyperthermia as well as clinical research in the field of stereotaxy / radiosurgery. We carry out studies initiated by national or international study organizations, university clinics or even by us. As a member of our study team consisting of study coordinators and doctors, you have the following tasks.

  • Planning and implementation of research projects and clinical studies (monocentric, national and international) in accordance with the Human Research Act (HFG), the Data Protection Act and the international guidelines, such as the ICH E6 GCP in our department.
  • Share responsibility for achieving a high-quality valid data quality
  • Implementation of quality management in clinical studies initiated by us (monitoring, data clearing)
  • Co-responsibility in the implementation of reporting obligations externally to authorities and ethics committees, sponsors and research centres
  • Collaboration in the REDCap Office, secure and efficient operation of our REDCap platform and advice to researchers at the KSA on electronic data collection.

Requirements

  • Completed education in health care or scientific bachelor
  • High level of teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Precise working methods and organisational capacity
  • High affinity for digital applications

Advantages are

  • Professional experience in clinical research
  • CAS/DAS Clinical Research
  • Knowledge of data protection and the secure processing of sensitive (in particular personal and health-related) data
  • Experience in developing and managing REDCap projects
