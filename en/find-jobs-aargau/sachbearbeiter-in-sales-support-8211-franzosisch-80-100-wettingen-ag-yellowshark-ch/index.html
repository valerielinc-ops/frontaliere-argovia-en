For several renowned clients in the region, we are looking for dedicated, language-skilled professionals with solid experience in order processing and professional customer correspondence in German and French.

Our clients are established companies from various technical and industrial sectors that place great importance on quality, service orientation and smooth customer support. In this varied role, you will act as the link between customers, sales and internal departments and make a significant contribution to efficient and professional order processing.

If you enjoy communicating with customers and feel comfortable in a dynamic, international environment, we look forward to receiving your application documents.

02 Your responsibilities

  • Independent recording, checking and processing of customer orders in the ERP system
  • Professional handling of complaints in close cooperation with the After-Sales team to develop sustainable customer solutions
  • Careful maintenance and updating of customer master data in the CRM/ERP system to ensure high data quality
