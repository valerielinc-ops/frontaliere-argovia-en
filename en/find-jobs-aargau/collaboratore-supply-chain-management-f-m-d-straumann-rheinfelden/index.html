Objective of the function

The employee Supply Chain Management organizes, controls and monitors the entire material and goods flow from the supplier to the warehouse to the production and the customer. It ensures that material, information and shipments are structured, on time and economically through the company 'Fairsen'. As an internal service provider, it proactively supports all specialists in production, so that they can optimally work and that internal goods flows are continuously optimized.
