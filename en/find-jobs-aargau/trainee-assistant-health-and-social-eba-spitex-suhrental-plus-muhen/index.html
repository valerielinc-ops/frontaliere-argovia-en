Versatile, innovative and professional – Spitex Suhrental Plus

With 130 employees, we support our clients in somatic and psychosocial care, specialised palliative care and housekeeping, so that a self-determined life at home is possible. With foresight, we train health professionals at secondary and tertiary level.

A profession with meaning? Not a day like the other? No problem!

You want to support, care for and accompany people – and learn new things every day? Then you are exactly right with us!

Apply now with us for an apprenticeship from August 2027 as

Trainee Assistant Health and Social EBA

Your education with us

  • You will learn everything about the help and support of people of all ages in health and personal care – directly at our clients at home.
  • You will work closely with experienced professionals and gain practical experience in various situations.
  • You will become an important part of our team and learn to take responsibility, work independently and react empathetically to people.

We offer you

  • An exciting, varied and practical training
  • A warm, collegial team
  • Support in your personal and professional development
  • Insights into diverse care areas
  • An education with a future and many perspectives

Your profile

  • Completed school (Real / Sek / Bez)
  • Being interested in other people
  • Good manners and sense of responsibility
  • Sensitive and independent personality
  • Good IT and German skills
