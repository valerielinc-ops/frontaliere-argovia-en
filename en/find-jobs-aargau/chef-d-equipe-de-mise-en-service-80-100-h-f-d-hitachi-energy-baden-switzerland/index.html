The opportunity

Step into a leadership role where your expertise shapes the future of energy solutions. As Commissioning Team Leader at Hitachi Energy, you'll lead a skilled team of engineers to bring complex projects to life—ensuring systems are delivered safely on time, and at peak performance. This is your chance to make a tangible impact on critical infrastructure, drive innovation, and set new standards for operational excellence in a dynamic, global environment.

How you'll make an impact

  • Supervise and guide a team of Commissioning Engineers, providing technical expertise, coaching, and performance management
  • Develop, review, and approve detailed commissioning plans, procedures, and schedules aligned with project requirements and safety standards
  • Oversee all commissioning phases, including pre-commissioning checks, functional testing, and performance verification
  • Work closely with project management, engineering, tender/sales teams, and clients to ensure seamless execution
  • Identify and resolve complex technical issues during commissioning, implementing corrective actions and optimizing system performance
  • Allocate personnel, equipment, and materials effectively to meet deadlines and budget targets
  • Uphold safety protocols, quality standards, and environmental regulations throughout the process

Your background

  • Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering (or equivalent)
  • Minimum 6 years of experience in commissioning, start-up, or operations in industries such as power generation, oil & gas, or heavy industrial projects
  • At least 3 years in a leadership role managing commissioning teams
  • Proven ability to develop and implement commissioning strategies and procedures
  • Strong troubleshooting skills for complex technical challenges
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
