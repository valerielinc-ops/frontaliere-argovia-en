Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

Bellikon Rehabilitation Clinic has a uniquely high degree of specialization in accident rehabilitation. In her fields of activity - neurological, orthopaedic and work-oriented rehabilitation as well as sports medicine - she has a special know-how.

Tasks

  • Swiss diploma or recognised equivalent (recognition by the Federal Office of Health or Medical Occupational Commission MEBEKO), of advantage with completed specialist training
