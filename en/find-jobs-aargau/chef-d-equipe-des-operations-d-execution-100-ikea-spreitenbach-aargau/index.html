Who you are

  • Previous experience in logistics or a related field, with at least two years in a leadership or coordinator role.
  • Strong leadership skills, with the ability to inspire and motivate a team to achieve goals and objectives.
  • Good knowledge of retail and/or fulfilment operations processes, tools and working methods.
  • Knowledge of budgets, costs, and KPI's and how to use them for business planning and management.
  • Ability to deal with the unknown in a constantly changing omnichannel environment.
  • Ability to communicate in a clear and targeted way in German and English with different people in various situations.
  • forklift license is an advantage (at least willingness to learn)

Your responsibilities

We are looking for a Fulfilment Operations Team Leader to lead our amazing In-store Fulfilment Operations (Logistic) Team. As a Fulfillment Operations Team Leader at IKEA, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the smooth and efficient operation of our fulfillment processes. You will lead a team responsible for managing orders, coordinating shipments, and optimizing warehouse operations to meet customer demands and exceed expectations. This role requires a strong leader with exceptional organizational skills and a keen eye for detail.

As Fulfilment Operations Team Leader your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  • Working with your team to secure the highest goods availability and operational readiness.
  • Lead and motivate your team to achieve daily operational targets and performance goals.
  • Assume the responsibility for identifying, recruiting, retaining, and developing the diverse talents within your team.
  • Secure effective, qualitative, and efficient daily Fulfillment (Logistic) operations.
  • Ensure and maintain a clean, organized and safe working environment.
  • Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics to track performance, identify areas for improvement, and drive operational excellence.
