Full description

Spitex Fricktal AG is a modern and innovative non-profit Spitex. On behalf of 17 municipalities we offer our services in a catchment area of approx. 50,000 inhabitants of the upper and lower Frick Valley. Our approximately 100 employees and 20 learners and students serve more than 600 customers in around 140,000 assignments per year. In addition to general somatic care, Spitex Fricktal AG also offers specialist care in the areas of psychiatry and specialised palliative care.
