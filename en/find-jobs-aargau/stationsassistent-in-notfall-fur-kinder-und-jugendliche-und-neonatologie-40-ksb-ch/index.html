Full description

Empathetic, dynamic and well organized? Become the backbone of our team! For our pediatric emergency department and neonatology, we are looking for a committed reinforcement immediately or by agreement. In this versatile interface function, you keep your back on the nursing team and make a significant contribution to the optimal care of our smallest patients.

Their tasks

  • Organization & Administration: You ensure a smooth station flow and take on organizational, administrative and logistical secretarial tasks.
  • Medical technology: You reliably take care of equipment maintenance and material readiness.
  • Delegate care: As directed, you support the care team in medical tasks (e.g. blood sampling, vital sign checks).
  • Flexible use: Your working hours are between 07:30 and 14:00, irregularly spread over 7 days.

Your profile

  • Qualification: Completed training as a medical assistant (MPA EFZ).
  • Personality: High resilience, team spirit and flexibility in a dynamic acute environment.
  • Working method: networked thinking, quick comprehension and a pronounced service orientation.

What to expect

  • A meaningful and varied task in a motivated, interdisciplinary team.
  • Plannable half-day shifts in the early stage.
  • Contemporary employment conditions and scope for development.
