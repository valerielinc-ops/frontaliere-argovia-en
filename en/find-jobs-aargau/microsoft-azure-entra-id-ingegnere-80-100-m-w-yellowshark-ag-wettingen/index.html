01 Your future employer

We are looking for an experienced Workplace Engineer for an established IT company in the Aargau region. The position offers the opportunity to provide comprehensive support to regional clients while applying broad technical expertise. The company values a solid IT education and offers attractive compensation for qualified professionals.

02 Your responsibilities

  • Support and further develop modern workplace environments with Windows 10/11, Microsoft Intune and Microsoft 365
  • Manage and configure end devices, software distribution and client management with Microsoft Endpoint Manager / Configuration Manager (SCCM)
  • Ensure the stable and secure operation of clients, mobile devices and peripherals, including troubleshooting as part of 2nd-level support
  • Implement IT security policies, patch management and device configurations in line with modern standards
  • Help automate and optimize workplace processes using PowerShell, Azure Automations and modern cloud technologies

03 Benefits

  • Contributions towards external training and professional development
  • Flat hierarchies
  • Flexible working hours
  • Guaranteed hybrid or work-from-home arrangements
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace
  • Public transport discounts

04 Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as an IT specialist in platform development (EFZ) or an equivalent IT qualification
  • In-depth knowledge of Windows client operating systems, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Intune and Azure Active Directory / Entra ID
  • Experience in client management, software deployment, patch management, and analyzing and resolving technical issues
  • Good knowledge of PowerShell and an interest in automation, cloud solutions and modern workplace technologies
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented approach to work, with a strong customer and service orientation

05 What you should know

  • Flexible working-hours model
  • Good social benefits
  • Varied IT projects for regional clients in Central Switzerland
  • Work in an established and growing technology environment
  • Short lines of communication and personal client support
