Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Planzer Package

Planzer package stands for reliable and fast parcel delivery in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied activity in a future-oriented company that relies on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the parcel world!

Tasks

  • In the selected shift model – team Late (from 16:00 to 23:00) or team early (from 23:30 to 07:00) – you ensure that everything runs smoothly every day.
  • Vehicles and lattices are loaded and unloaded efficiently, so that our logistics processes work smoothly at all times.
  • Packages are precisely prepared according to clear process specifications – both manually and with the support of our modern sorting system.
  • With a trained view, you place shipments correctly on the system, carefully control labels and make sure that each package reliably reaches its destination. - Responsiblely secure and control cargo in the vehicle and reliably handle faulty or damaged shipments. - Modern equipment such as scanners, conveyor systems or forklifts are used safely and become part of a dedicated team that delivers maximum performance together in the Planzer family day after day.

Profile

  • Physical fitness, resilience and enjoyment of active work are the hallmarks of your work – even packages up to 30 kg are also used with energy and endurance.
  • In a dynamic working environment you keep track of the situation at a high speed and make full use of it in every shift.
  • First experiences in parcel logistics are of benefit, but the most important are motivation, commitment and a quick understanding.
  • Reliability, responsibility and a perfect reputation make you a valuable reinforcement for our team.
  • With a secure German language skills in word and writing and true team orientation, you convince in daily use. - Flexible work assignments fit perfectly to your lifestyle, and commitment is a matter of course for you. - You are looking for more than just a job: You want to be part of a strong Planzer family, where cohesion, respect and real grips count.
