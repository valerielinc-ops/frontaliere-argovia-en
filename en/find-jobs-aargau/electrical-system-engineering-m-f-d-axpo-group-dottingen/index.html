Full description

Pensum: 80–100% I Beznau

For our engineering team at the Beznau nuclear power plant, we are looking for reinforcements.

With your know-how in electrical/system technology, installation technology and technical fire protection, you can keep our systems safe and reliable - today and in the future.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Design, design and implementation of the renewal of fire protection equipment (fire alarm and extinguishing centres, HVAC systems)
  • Creation and updating of fire protection documents (e.g. fire case control matrices, fire section plans, test regulations for integral tests)
  • Support for the implementation of fire protection measures until commissioning
  • Preparation of specifications and technical purchasing rules and government documents
  • Project management, coordination of interdisciplinary teams, close cooperation with the company and external partners

Profile and Requirements

  • Studies (HF/TS, FH, ETH) in Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Building Engineering, Civil Engineering, Security & Safety Engineering or comparable
  • Fire protection specialist VKF or willingness to train in this direction
  • Good technical and planning understanding
  • Structured, independent and quality-conscious working method
  • High sense of responsibility in the safety-relevant plant environment
  • Very good German skills in words and writing, English skills are advantageous
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and SAP benefits
  • Analytical, pragmatic and team-oriented personality

About the team

We are a dedicated and interdisciplinary team that supports each other and develops reliable and innovative solutions together. Quality and safety are our top priority.
