Your Role

In this dynamic sales position, you will contribute to market development with panel manufacturers in French-speaking Switzerland. You will identify market opportunities, build strong customer relationships and successfully position our solutions in the field of power distribution. In close cooperation with internal teams, business partners and stakeholders, you will contribute directly to the sustainable growth of the company.

Working Model: Hybrid

This position is part of the electrification and smart power department in Baden. The workplace is Morges.

  • Your responsibilities
  • You will achieve your business goals by identifying new market opportunities, building a sustainable sales pipeline in Salesforce, and developing targeted sales strategies
  • You will attract and develop new customers by demonstrating the benefits of ABB solutions and demonstrating exemplary professionalism in the sales process, communication and follow-up process.
  • You maintain long-term partnerships with your customers through early identification of needs, expert advice and excellent service throughout the entire sales cycle
  • You will support customer projects from the discovery of the opportunity to its successful completion, developing customized technical and commercial solutions in collaboration with internal teams and partners
  • You will coordinate all relevant stakeholders and ensure professional sales processes through clear communication on delivery times, general terms and conditions and contract clauses
  • Requirements
  • Several years of successful experience in technical sales or project sales, ideally in the areas of electrical distributors, power distribution or building management, with convincing results
  • Technical experience and relevant education or training in the field of electrical engineering and electrical distribution
  • Ability to manage complex sales processes and communicate effectively with decision-makers at all levels
  • The proven ability to build long-term relationships with customers through a partnership-based approach
  • Expertise in strategic consulting on business models, market positioning and differentiation arguments
  • A person who can work in a team, has professional communication skills and a cross-departmental mindset
  • Language skills: French (native), fluency in other languages is an advantage. Valid category B driver's license and valid Swiss work permit
