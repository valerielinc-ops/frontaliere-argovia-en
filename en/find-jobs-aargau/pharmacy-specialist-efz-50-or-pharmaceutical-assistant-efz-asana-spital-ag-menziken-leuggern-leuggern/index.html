Full description

Asana Spital Denuggern is the innovative health centre in Zurzibiet. In a family atmosphere, we guarantee the high-quality basic medical care of the region. We are a family-friendly employer with progressive employment conditions for around 450 employees. With us, you do not work in a classic public pharmacy, but are an important part of medical care. With your work in the background, you contribute every day to ensuring that our patients are treated reliably. We are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement

Departmental Description

The hospital pharmacy and the central warehouse are responsible for ensuring that our doctors and nursing staff always have the necessary medicines, dressing materials and other utensils available for everyday hospital life. The warehouse contains around 9000 different packs (from walking stick, copy paper to plaster and knee splints) and an internal list of medicines contains the 500 most important medicines in stock.
