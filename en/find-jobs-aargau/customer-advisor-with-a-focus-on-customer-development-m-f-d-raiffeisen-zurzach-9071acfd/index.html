25.10.2026 Zurzach

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Aare-Rhine, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Bad Zurzach, Leibstadt and Leuggern, we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as a customer consultant with a focus on customer development (m/f/d). We are DIE Bank im Zurzibiet for customers and employees.

What awaits you?

  • Identifying and exploiting customer potential through proactive support and targeted addressing of the customer portfolio
  • Establishing, further developing and intensifying customer relationships into a main banking relationship
  • Holistic advice and support for customers in the areas of savings, figures, investing and pensions with a focus on investment and pension business
  • Systematic and proactive processing of sales opportunities as well as arranging and receiving consulting appointments
  • Identification of needs and involvement of the competent consultants in further customer needs
  • Participation in target group-specific market development measures and campaigns

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking or finance training
  • Enjoy a proactive approach to customers and prospects and build new contacts
  • Several years of experience in client consulting, ideally in the investment and pension business
  • Strong sense of customer needs and the ability to systematically identify and use potential
  • High initiative, perseverance and final strength
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented working method
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action

You recognize opportunities, actively approach people and develop customer relationships. With your sense of customer needs and potential, you contribute to expanding existing customer relationships and deepening the banking relationship in the long term.
