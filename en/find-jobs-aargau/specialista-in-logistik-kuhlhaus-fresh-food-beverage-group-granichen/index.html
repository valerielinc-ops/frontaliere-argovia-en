Full description

Enough of the summer heat? Then we have the right job for you! We are looking for a dedicated logistics provider for our cold store. With your use in shift and weekend inserts you make an important contribution to a functioning logistics. Interested? We look forward to your application.

Mansioni

  • You book goods inputs and outputs and ensure the careful storage of products
  • You are responsible for picking in a working environment of 4-6 degrees
  • You ensure a smooth operation in the storage management
  • You serve the high regal warehouse and the central palletizing system

Requisiti

  • Apprentices are also welcome: Logistics
  • Technical and mechanical flair
  • readiness for shift operations and weekend work
  • Logical thinking and imagination
  • good use of digital tools
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Handy subscription: Discounted mobile subscriptions at Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and, if necessary, for three other people you know.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick pay with full net wage.
  • Cum MAXS points: 3-fold points on food, 6-fold points on all other products.
  • Employee offers: Employee points Cumulus, Discounted mobile subscriptions, Higher interest rates Migros Bank, contributions Club school/fitness and more.
  • Holiday: 5 weeks holidays for all, 6 weeks holidays from 50th year of age, 7 weeks holidays from 60th year of age, additional days are possible.
  • Club school Migros: 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Sports and fitness offers: refund of CHF 200 per fitness annual subscription.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees in the context of internal programs and support external training with time and cash benefits if required.
  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali
  • we are by you!
