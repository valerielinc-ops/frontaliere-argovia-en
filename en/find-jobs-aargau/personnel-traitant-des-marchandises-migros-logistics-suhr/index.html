Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG, Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Mansioni

  • Careful and impeccable planning as well as efficient management of freshness ranges, including timely completion of all administrative tasks
  • Ensuring and optimizing business regulations (readiness for delivery, warehouse range and depreciator)
  • Calculation and distribution of promotional, seasonal and holiday quantities
  • Enforcement of applicable delivery requirements with suppliers
  • Ensure proper and efficient cooperation with internal bodies, contracting entities and suppliers
  • Timely resolution and handling of differences in the context of the audit
  • Collaboration in shop support at marginal times as well as occasional Saturday services in the rotation process. Saturday coverage is provided by the entire team and usually corresponds to approx. 5 to 7 missions per year.
  • Target-oriented and efficient planning as well as cooperation with internal and external partners

Requirements

  • 2 years: disposition, ideally in the field of fresh assortments
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial or equivalent
  • Strong business understanding and affinity for figures and analyses
  • Secure handling of MS Office and good SAP knowledge
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength and negotiating skills in dealing with internal and external partners
  • Flexibility and readiness for occasional outings at marginal times as well as Saturday services in the team rotation model
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible workplace / home office: With our home office options, we promote flexibility, agility and efficiency
  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Food: In our staff restaurants, employees can cater at preferential prices
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Cumulus Points: Employees of MVB AG can collect additional Cumulus Points for purchases in Migros stores and specialty markets
