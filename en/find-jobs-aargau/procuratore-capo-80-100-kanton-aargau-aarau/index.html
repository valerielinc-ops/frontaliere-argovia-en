Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Participation in the body of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in the further development and coordination of the entire public prosecutor's office Aargau
  • Active participation in the implementation and further development of the digitalisation of criminal prosecution
  • Approval of non-administration, suspension and recruitment orders and control of penal orders with language competence
  • Appeals against penal judgments of the district courts and the higher court and representation of the public prosecutor's office Aargau in federal court
  • Conduct of jurisdiction proceedings incl. Representation of the Public Prosecutor’s Office before the Federal Criminal Court

What you bring

  • Completed legal studies and a lawyer's patent
  • qualified many years of legal professional experience, preferably in law enforcement
  • profound knowledge of criminal law and criminal procedure law, criminology and forensic technology
  • proven leadership competence and many years of experience in personnel management
  • a high affinity for IT topics and motivation for participation in corresponding projects

Elections shall be made by the Grand Council at the request of the Government Council.

Your working environment

We are responsible for criminal proceedings against adults in the canton of Aargau. We lead the preliminary proceedings and prosecute crimes and offenders in close cooperation with the police. We issue criminal orders, bring charges and represent the state criminal claim in court.
