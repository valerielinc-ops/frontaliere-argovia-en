About the position

For our production site in Wettingen, we are looking for an experienced leader in our field of surge arresters. In this role, you are responsible for safe, stable and timely production in silicone injection molding (LSR/HTV) as well as in the assembly of SPU and GIS surge arresters.

You combine practical leadership directly in production with structured production planning and control. Together with your team, you ensure that customer orders are produced in the right quality, order and on the agreed date. At the same time, you will continuously develop processes, plants and working methods.

You report to the Operations Manager surge arresters and lead a team of process and industrial engineering, detailed planning as well as shift lines and production teams.

You can do that.

  • Responsibility for production planning and control in injection molding and assembly with focus on customer requirements, delivery dates, capacities and material availability
  • Ensuring safe, stable and timely production and responsibility for quality, productivity and costs
  • Leadership and development of production and technical teams with clear priorities and structured leadership routines
  • Active presence in production through Gemba walks as well as support for critical issues, quality deviations and escalations
  • Control of capacities, resources, tool changes and production sequences as well as early detection of bottlenecks and risks
  • Ensuring HSE, quality and process standards as well as carrying out structured cause analyses such as 5-Why, Ishikawa and 8D
  • Further development of processes, plants and tools in close cooperation with maintenance, toolmaking, quality and supply chain
  • Continuous improvement of production performance through Lean, 5S, SMED, key figures and SAP- and MES-based processes

You bring that with you

  • Technical training, for example as an engineer or technician, or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in an industrial production environment, ideally in injection molding, assembly or in comparable production processes
  • Experience in leading and developing production and/or technical teams
  • Good understanding of production planning, capacity control and industrial production processes
  • Experience with SAP, MES or comparable production systems is an advantage
  • Knowledge of lean manufacturing and structured problem-solving methods such as 5-Why, Ishikawa or 8D
  • A present, solution-oriented leader who sets priorities, makes decisions and keeps an overview even in demanding situations
  • Enjoy cross-divisional collaboration and implementing change together with your team
  • Flowing knowledge of German and good English
