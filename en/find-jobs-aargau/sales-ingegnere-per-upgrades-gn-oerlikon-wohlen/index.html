Sales Engineer for Upgrades (gn)

To support our after sales team, we are looking for a passionate engineer (gn) for our growing upgrade and retrofit business.

Their tasks

  • Global responsibility for the expansion and maintenance of the retrofit and upgrade portfolio, in close cooperation with product management
  • Global technical sales of retrofits and upgrades, in close cooperation with the regional sales and service teams
  • Independent creation of offers and execution of upgrades and retrofits, including the support of the service team performing the work
  • Support for life-cycle issues, in close cooperation with engineering, product management and purchasing
  • Technical customer support and field service worldwide
  • Further develop and promote the quality, speed and efficiency of upgrade and retrofit processes worldwide
  • Supporting the development and maintenance of our installed base
  • Development and support in defining and implementing the after-sales strategy to ensure customer satisfaction and achieve sustainable and profitable growth with upgrades and retrofits

Your profile

  • Technical education or studies and at least 5 years of experience in technical service and/or electrical engineering, customer service and upgrade/retrofit business
  • Enjoyment of technical challenges and a result-oriented working method, combined with a strong customer and service orientation
  • Experience in thermal spraying or its industrial application
  • Open and strong personality with a positive and supportive mindset and very good communication skills in German and English

We offer

  • Attractive compensation package with good social benefits
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Varied activities in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment

Do you feel addressed? Then we are curious to meet you!

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in material science engineering. With the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating equipment, components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, durability and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to end users, but critical to the business of the industries that make modern life possible: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical technology, luxury goods, tool industry, automotive industry and general industry. Oerlikon is headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, operates in 38 countries and employs around 10,000 people worldwide. In 2025, Oerlikon generated sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential – guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
