What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. The Emil Frey Baden in Oberrohrdorf offers a touch of British Cars, as an exclusive service partner for Jaguar and Land Rover. Their tasks

  • You are interested in sales and want to develop further in car sales – First sales experience is beneficial, but not mandatory
  • Your customer- and service-oriented personality as well as your communicative nature distinguish you
