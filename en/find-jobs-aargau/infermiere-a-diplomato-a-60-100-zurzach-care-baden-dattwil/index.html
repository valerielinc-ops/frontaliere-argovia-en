Full description

In Baden – Dattwill we are looking for you as a nurse and a key role in the rehabilitation process. You are not only executive, but also co-decisive: for the quality, progress and quality of life of our patients.

Our clinic specializes in the rehabilitation of musculoskeletal and neurological patients. On our station with 38 beds, you work closely with doctors, therapists and other specialists – at eye level and with mutual respect.

Centrally located in Baden - Dattwil, directly at the Cantonal Hospital, very easily accessible by public transport

This is what awaits you

Care with focus on people

  • You accompany the patient: inside during the entire rehabilitation phase
  • You carry out professional basic and treatment care
  • You recognize resources, promote self-employment and progress

Interdisciplinary and responsible

  • Active participation in the interdisciplinary team
  • Share responsibility for care quality and process design
  • Day guidance for learners

Diversity and Development

  • Varied daily care with professional requirements
  • Medical technology – such as infusions, PICC, VAC, diagnostics, ECG
  • Space for personal initiative and personal development

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist or comparable training with at least one positive SRK-PreCheck
  • Experience: You have experience in nursing and ideally bring experience or interest in MSK/Neurology
  • Personality: You are an empathetic and team-oriented personality who carries out her profession with joy and commitment.

This is what we offer you

  • Co-creation: Care with time, quality and meaning, actively contribute.
  • Inspiring and collaborative working atmosphere: You can expect a great, committed team that actively supports you in your role and values collaboration.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the Wellness - Therme FORTYSEVEN

Care is a strong role for us – take it on.

We look forward to your application via our online tool!
