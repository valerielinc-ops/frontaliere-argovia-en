Full description

Belonging, meaningfulness and competence With a well-established team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

You want to continue to be a nursing specialist, then you are right with us. We are happy to make the best way for you together.

  • 2- or 3-year-old training
  • Cooperation with HFGS, Careum or XUND
  • With or without commitment, which affects your salary
  • Start your training 4 times a year with us possible

Tasks

  • Professional responsibility for the entire care process and for the implementation of organisational and medical-technical tasks
  • Design of professional communication and relationship with patients and their relatives
  • Cooperation in the intra- and interprof-essional team
  • Self-employed design of a careful and current knowledge management
  • Co-responsibility for logistical and administrative processes
  • Rotation Neuro/Pato/Incineration Station and Early Reha

Profile

  • Completed vocational education and training EFZ, degree in technical secondary school or maturing
  • Completed apprenticeship as a specialist woman/man Health EFZ entitles you to a shortened study of 2 years
  • Aptitude internship in the Rehaklinik Bellikon (3 days)
  • Learning readiness and enthusiasm
  • Efficient, analytical, systematic, evidence-based and reflected work
  • High communication and team skills
  • Interdisciplinary thinking and acting
  • A sense of responsibility
  • Physical and psychological resilience

Penalty: 100%

Employment: temporary

Admission: 2027-03-15
