Wanted: Good soul for our care pool (late service)

Don't you want to go? As an AGS or nursing assistant SRK, you support different teams exactly where you work most – and bring a breath of fresh air to the stations with your positive nature.

Apply now and experience diversity!

Their tasks

  • Diverse care & support: Self-responsible implementation of competence-oriented care measures and everyday support of patients – always in consultation with the care specialist.
  • Attentive attention: You recognize changes in patient status at an early stage and inform the responsible nurse immediately.
  • Fixed frame: You work in late service (14:45h – 23:09h) and also take over seat guard functions if necessary.
  • Perspective: For the time being, the job is limited to 6 to 12 months – with the option of a subsequent permanent job.

Your profile

  • Qualification: Completed training as a health and social assistant (AGS) or nursing assistant SRK.
  • Personality: You are a positioned, motivated “good soul” that brings variety to our teams with positive energy and joy.
  • Flexibility: You work independently and quickly find your way around new teams and situations.
  • Language skills: Fluent German in word and good knowledge in writing.
