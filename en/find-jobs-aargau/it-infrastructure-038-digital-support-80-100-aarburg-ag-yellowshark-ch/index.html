01 Future employer

Every company is only as good as its employees – this is the credo of our established partner, which has established itself as a technology group in Switzerland. On its behalf, we are looking for a skilled IT Supporter (m/f) who brings a high degree of personal responsibility and strives for personal development.

02 Your task

  • You are responsible for first- and second-level support, primarily for Microsoft products as well as company-specific software, hardware and general communication tools
  • You are responsible for first- and second-level support in the area of digital advertising panels
  • You independently process user reports via IT tickets, forward them to the relevant specialist departments and coordinate/monitor the progress of the problem
  • You participate in various projects in the area of digital media products as well as the internal IT infrastructure

03.07.19

04 Your profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an IT specialist (ideally specializing in systems engineering / platform development) or have several years of professional experience in a comparable role and further training, e.g. as an ICT Supporter SIZ
  • You have very good knowledge of MS Office products and Microsoft operating systems and good networking knowledge (TCP/IP)
  • You are proficient in working with Active Directory and demonstrate a strong service orientation and communication skills
  • You are well organized and work independently and accurately
  • You speak and write German confidently and have good English skills (knowledge of French is an advantage)

05 Useful information

  • A positive and motivated team
