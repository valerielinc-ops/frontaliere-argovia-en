01 Future employer

Every company is only as good as its employees – that is the motto of our established partner, which has established itself as a technology group in Switzerland. On its behalf, we are looking for an experienced IT Supporter (m/f) who brings a high degree of personal responsibility and strives for personal development.

02 Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for first- and second-level support, primarily for Microsoft products as well as company-specific software, hardware and general communication tools
  • You are responsible for first- and second-level support for digital advertising panels
  • You independently process user reports via IT tickets, forward them to the relevant specialist departments and coordinate/monitor the progress of the issue
  • You participate in various projects involving digital media products and internal IT infrastructure

03.07.19

04 Your profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an IT specialist (ideally specializing in systems engineering / platform development) or have several years of professional experience in a comparable role and further training, e.g. as an ICT Supporter SIZ
  • You have very good knowledge of MS Office products and Microsoft operating systems, as well as good networking knowledge (TCP/IP)
  • You are proficient in using Active Directory and have a strong service orientation and communication skills
  • You are well organized, work independently and accurately
  • You speak and write German confidently and have good English skills (French language skills are an advantage)
