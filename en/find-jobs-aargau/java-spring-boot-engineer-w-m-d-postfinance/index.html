Role overview

Do you want to develop modern backend applications and further develop yourself professionally? With us, you will get the opportunity to help shape demanding systems, use modern technologies and develop in the medium term towards a senior developer. Our team is called "Online Transaction Services" in the Solution Team Online Banking and we are responsible for the banking transaction data of our customers. With the latest technologies, we process the data in real time and make it available within milliseconds via our microservices. With us, you create smart services for our customers and move PostFinance sustainably.

You contribute significantly to the further development and maintenance of our Java-based applications, as well as the implementation of Event-Driven Microservices with Spring Boot

You help shape the design and architecture through active participation in technical discussions and decision-making

You promote continuous improvement and knowledge exchange in the team

You support the team with valuable feedback, which increases the code quality and contributes to further development

Your knowledge strengthens the team and increases the quality and effectiveness of our software solutions

You develop and maintain our applications according to Scrum

Higher degree in computer science (uni or FH) or corresponding professional experience in software development

Experience in developing Java applications, spring boot and microservices architecture

Understanding DevOps and experience implementing CI/CD pipelines
