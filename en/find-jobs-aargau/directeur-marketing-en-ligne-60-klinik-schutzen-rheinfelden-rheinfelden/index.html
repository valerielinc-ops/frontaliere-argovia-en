Full description

Schützen Rheinfelden AG includes KLINIK SCHÜTZEN RHEINFELDEN, a leading private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland and SCHÜTZEN HOTELS RHEINFELDEN with the hotels Schützen Rheinfelden and Eden im Park. Our clinic-in-hotel concept combines excellent medical competence with high quality of stay. We are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement one or more Online Marketing Manager 60% Do you think digitally, have a sense for relevant content and want to actively develop our online presence? You know how websites, social media and search engines work together, keep an eye on new developments like GEO and bring topics together with various experts to the point? Then we look forward to you.

This is what awaits you •

  • You continuously supervise and develop our websites and provide current, relevant and target-group-oriented content.
  • You are responsible for our social media channels, develop them further and plan and publish suitable content together with the marketing team.
  • You manage our intranet and ensure that information is up-to-date and easily accessible.
  • You control our activities in the areas of SEO, GEO and online ads
  • You observe digital trends and new technologies and bring suitable ideas into our marketing.
  • You analyze the performance of our digital measures and derive optimizations from them.
  • You work closely with the entire marketing team as well as internal and external partners.

You bring that with you •

  • Education or further education in the field of online marketing, communication, marketing or digital media
  • Professional experience in online marketing and supporting digital channels (preferably in an agency)
  • Experience with website and content management systems
  • Good knowledge of social media, SEO and online advertising
  • Interest in GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and current developments around AI and digital search
  • A good sense of content, language and different target groups
  • •Analytical understanding and enjoyment of further developing digital activities based on metrics
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Joy of teamwork and implementing topics together with graphics and other specialist areas
  • We offer our more than 400 employees
  • An attractive working environment in a family atmosphere with du-culture and many employee events
  • Employee breakfast and discounted food in our hotel restaurants
  • •6 weeks of holidays and the possibility of unpaid holidays
  • A versatile offer for health promotion
