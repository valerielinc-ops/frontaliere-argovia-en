Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a Project Manager Equipment (gn) for the “Project Delivery” area at Wohlen, AG and from the beginning of 2027 in Hausen, AG.

Project Manager (gn)

With your professional open nature, you can communicate across departments and interact in the network of customers, partners and suppliers. They undertake the following activities:

  • Successful management and implementation of international customer projects in the field of Aero, Automotive, IGT (Industrial Gas Turbine) and Medical
  • Carry overall responsibility for achieving the project objectives in terms of quality, costs and deadlines
  • Function as a competent and customer-oriented contact point for all aspects of the customer with regard to project handling from incoming orders to handover to the service organization and support of the technicians (gn) during installation and acceptance
  • Support of system sales with technical advice, plant concepts and review of calculations and offers in cooperation with the specialist departments
  • Coordination of internal resources and suppliers/third-party providers for the smooth handling of international customer projects
  • Management of evaluations of new components for customer projects with the specialist departments
  • Compilation of regular status reports to department managers (gn) / controlling as well as direct forwarding of information in the event of special events within the business unit or to the customer
  • Monitoring the project progress using SAP (ERP system) and forming the provisions (forecasts) of ongoing projects
  • Creating a detailed project schedule to monitor progress and budget
  • Evaluation of the project outcome with appropriate tools and techniques
  • Risk management to minimize project risks and maintain comprehensive project documentation

Your profile

  • Completed university education (mechanical engineering, process engineering or energy engineering), level TS / mechanical engineer FH
  • In-depth experience in handling international projects as project manager (gn)
  • Business training is an advantage
  • Technical degree IPMA Level C
  • Experience in planning, organizational and commercial matters
  • Very good German and English (in word and writing), French is advantageous

We offer

  • Varied work in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment
  • An attractive compensation package with good social benefits

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in material science engineering. With the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating equipment, components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, durability and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to end users, but critical to the business of the industries that make modern life possible: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical technology, luxury goods, tool industry, automotive industry and general industry. Oerlikon is headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, operates in 38 countries and employs around 10,000 people worldwide. In 2025, Oerlikon generated sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential, guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
