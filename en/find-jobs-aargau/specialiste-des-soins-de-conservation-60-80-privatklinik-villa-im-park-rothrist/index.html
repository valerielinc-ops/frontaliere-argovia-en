Business description

In the private clinic Villa im Park, whose character is characterized by the stately, historic property, around 40 observing physicians perform more than 1,800 procedures per year. As a Listespital, the private clinic Villa im Park in the Canton of Aargau has a public service mandate and treats patients from all insurance classes.
