Full description

In our polyvalent central laboratory with the fields of hematology, clinical chemistry, clotting, urine/point diagnostics and immunohematology You are a central contribution to the diagnostic care of our hospital. You bring your expertise to a broad spectrum of analysis. A varied environment with modern infrastructure and a dedicated team awaits you.

Their tasks

  • You take on challenging laboratory work in a polyvalent 24/7 operation.
  • You analyze and validate laboratory results within the framework of medical-analytical findings, mostly independently.
  • You work on a regular basis in all subject areas and thus gain versatile experience.
  • You participate in shift, weekend and night work.

Your profile

  • You have completed vocational training as BMA HF or an equivalent education (for a foreign diploma, mandatory with SRK recognition).
  • Ideally, you will bring professional experience in a polyvalent hospital or private laboratory.
  • You are a committed, independent and team-capable personality and look forward to working in an innovative laboratory.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
