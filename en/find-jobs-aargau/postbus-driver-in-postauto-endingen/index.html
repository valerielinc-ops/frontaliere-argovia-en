Original description

Would you like to sit at the front left and join us as a PostBus driver? Do you bring a qualification for bus driving? Then you are exactly right with us, because with you at the wheel our passengers arrive on time and safely to their destination. You can do that. - On our varied regional lines you bring our passengers to their destination from Monday to Sunday. - You are the host: in our post buses and advise our customers on tariff and timetable issues. - The daily contact with your passengers enriches your everyday life with exciting encounters and you create positive experiences for our customers. - As a driver: you move much more than just your vehicle on the road. In direct customer contact, you experience new and varied situations every day. You bring that with you - You have driving practice in passenger transport and enjoy the variety that irregular working hours (including Sundays and public holidays) bring with them. - For this job, you need a category D driver’s license and a valid driver qualification certificate. - Reliability and enjoyment of daily customer contact are among your strengths. - You have a good knowledge of German and are well versed in using digital devices.
