Full description

Production – (80-100%) LED installation technician*in To strengthen our team in the workshop + development department, we are looking for a committed personality with a technical flair immediately or by agreement. This is what awaits you In our LED workshop with integrated locksmith and construction, we develop and produce high-quality LED light boxes, luminous lettering and individual special solutions – from the first idea to commissioning. You can expect a varied job in a small, well-established team, in which you can actively participate in exciting projects with a lot of personal responsibility and creative leeway. You make aluminum frames, cut back walls and rivet them. Then you equip the constructions with LED stripes, solder the components by hand and ensure professional wiring. You implement serial and parallel circuits and carry out basic electrical calculations – from the determination of the overall performance via the dimensioning of the power supplies to the calculation of the appropriate cable cross section. You will master the reading of technical drawings and electrical diagrams, install power supplies, put systems into operation and perform electrical device checks. In addition, you are an important technical point of contact for our project managers and support them in the planning and offer phase, technical feasibility, electrical

  • to assess the requirements and installation possibilities of illuminated signs. You document your work independently and ensure compliance with our quality standards. In addition, you will support our assembly teams in assembly and installation operations throughout Switzerland. On already assembled illuminated signs, you carry out repair and service work, analyze technical faults and provide with your know-how for professional troubleshooting directly on site. You also work with lifts and skyworkers.

Your profile You have a basic technical or craftsmanship training – for example in the mechanical, electrical or event technology sector. Precise and structured work distinguishes you. A clean, orderly and responsible way of working is a matter of course for you and contributes significantly to the quality and safety of our products. Your profile is rounded off by secure knowledge of German and a category B driver’s license. This is what we offer you We offer you a workplace in a modern new building with professional infrastructure, short decision-making processes and a collegial environment with open communication. You will receive a careful introduction to our systems and processes and have the opportunity to develop further. Flexible working hours, at least five weeks holidays and free parking round off our offer. Further information For additional information Matthias Ostermünchner is at your disposal.
