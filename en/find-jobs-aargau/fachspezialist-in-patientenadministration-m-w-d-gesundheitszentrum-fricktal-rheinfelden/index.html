Do you like to move at the interface between doctors, patients and payers? Issues such as cost securing, tariffs and billing inspire you, and you appreciate a varied activity with responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us. Our 10-member team is also happy for your reinforcement.

  • You take on a wide range of administrative tasks in the area of patient administration
  • You have a commercial education or a comparable education in administration
  • You have already gained professional experience in a relevant field of activity
  • You have good knowledge of the German social security system
  • You have tariff knowledge in health care
  • You are safe, friendly and professional towards patients and insurance companies
