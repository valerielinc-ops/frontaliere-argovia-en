After a long professional break, you want to start controlling again and bring your know-how into the business? Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You support monthly reporting including income statement, balance sheet and cash flow as well as KPI analysis for the Group.
  • You analyze value flows and profitability across different business divisions and identify optimization potential.
  • You contribute to financial planning, budgeting and in the form of a pool of forward-looking and forward-looking business plans.
  • You first use ad hoc analyses and cost-effectiveness calculations as a basis for strategic decisions.
  • You continue to develop reporting and controlling processes and support projects in the field of digitalization and process improvement.
  • You actively contribute your experience and work closely with various stakeholders within the division.
