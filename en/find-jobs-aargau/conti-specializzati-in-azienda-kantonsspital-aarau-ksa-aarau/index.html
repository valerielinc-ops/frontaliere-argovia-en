Role overview

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the preparation, analysis and quality assurance of business data as well as for the timely execution of the monthly accounts according to REKOLE®.
  • Co-responsibility for the preparation of annual data collections and tariff bases (ITAR K, SpiGes, BFS) using HCe TIP.
  • Ensuring high data quality through the further development of financial control instruments, in particular cost centre, cost carrier and profit centre calculation, as well as through the implementation of plausibility checks.

  • Very good SAP knowledge with a focus on CO as well as excellent Excel knowledge
