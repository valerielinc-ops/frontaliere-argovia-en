Full description

In our clinic in Baden-Dättwil, we offer surveillance rehabilitation and neurological rehabilitation at a high medical level.

Are you looking for a workplace where professionalism, team spirit and a good mood go hand in hand? Then you are exactly right with us!

We are a modern rehab clinic with a young, dynamic team. We attach great importance to cooperation – we support each other, face changes openly and develop together.

New ideas are always welcome. We value people who think, take responsibility and want to make a difference. The humor is also not neglected: We laugh a lot, celebrate joint successes and ensure that the work is fun.

What makes the job special

  • Patient-oriented therapy: You treat our patients over several weeks and accompany them on their way back to an independent life. You build a trusting relationship and experience firsthand how they progress and achieve their goals.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Therapy, care and medical service work hand in hand. Your expertise flows actively into the exchange and contributes significantly to the successful rehabilitation
  • Multiple medical histories: Each patient brings an individual medical history. This diversity makes your day-to-day work varied and exciting as you learn about different diagnoses and treatment approaches.
  • Physical therapy: You are responsible for the treatment of inpatient and outpatient neurological/musculoskeletal patients. If necessary, you will take part in interprofessional reports/visits and document your treatments according to ICF (findings, progress documentation and exit report)

This is what you bring with you

  • You have experience and enjoy working with neurological and musculoskeletal patients
  • You are a trained physiotherapist with SRK recognition
  • You have a further education in neurology, optimal Bobath
  • You have high empathy.

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at Wellnes-Therme FORTYSEVEN
