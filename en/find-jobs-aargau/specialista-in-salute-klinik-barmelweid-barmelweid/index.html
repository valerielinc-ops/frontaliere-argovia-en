Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic clinic in Switzerland. In 285 beds, the clinic provides inpatient care for patients. The clinic specializes in geriatric, internistic, cardiovascular and pulmonary rehabilitation, long covid, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy, psychocardiology and sleep medicine. At Barmelweid, the focus is on lean thinking and methods: all efforts are geared towards patients. This includes standardized safety rounds, the patient board and the leveling of the load on the stations by means of flows. The care in the department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy is completed with the method of reference care, the care in the department of Internal Medicine with the care managers. As a rule, patients stay on the Barmelweid for 2 to 6 weeks. This gives our nurses time to engage with the patients in depth. A lively company with interprofessional work awaits you, which retrieves all the skills of a nurse or a nurse. Barmelweid employs more than 700 people. It is located at an altitude of around 800 meters on a clearing in the Aargau Jura and is embedded in a unique environment with a nature park.
