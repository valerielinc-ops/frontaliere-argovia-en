Full description

Spitex RKS - Where stories begin and are written Young or old, from Switzerland or from far away: people in challenging phases of life or illness entrust us with the most important moments of their everyday life. Every visit writes a new story – and we are in the middle of it. We need you to write these stories together. Employees/housekeeping Why this job is different With us, you do not just drive from effort to effort. You are expected by people, families and stories. No day is the same – and that is exactly what makes the work of Spitex RKS so special. Who you are for us A personality that recognizes: Sometimes it just needs a clean, well-kept room. Sometimes the eye for detail and independent tackling. And sometimes a friendly “How are you really doing today?” does more than any therapy. What we promise you No perfect everyday life - housekeeping is not perfect. However, we are there for each other when it gets hard. We value your expertise and focus on trust, closeness and honest decisions.

  • Real teamwork – We stick together.
  • Being heard – Leaders really listen to you.
  • Freedom – trust instead of control, personal responsibility instead of micromanagement.
  • Appreciation – Your work will be seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility – working hours that take your private life into account.
  • Learning and growing – opportunities to further develop and shape your ideas.

Your role

  • Think and communicate in a team-oriented way.
  • Acting independently and attaching importance to quality.
  • Continuously learn and shape ideas.
  • Openness to feedback and commitment to common goals.
  • Willingness to take responsibility.

Ready for Spitex RKS? Do not hesitate – send us your application documents. We look forward to meeting you: personal@spitex-rks.ch
