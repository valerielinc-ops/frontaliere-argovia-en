About the role Do you know the daily challenges in purchasing, production planning, logistics or master data maintenance from your own experience? In order to strengthen our Projects & Processes team, we are looking for a motivated personality with SAP experience and interest in the further development of our supply chain processes at our Birr location (AG).

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Analysis, optimization and further development of SAP-supported business processes within the supply chain
  • Cooperation in local SAP projects and process improvements
  • Support for the specialist areas of purchasing, production planning, logistics and master data management
  • Coordination of requirements between business and IT
  • Preparation and maintenance of process documentation and training documents
  • Support and support of SAP Key Users
  • Participation in the introduction and further development of SAP solutions
  • Support of our logistics centre as part of the SAP ERM implementation

  • Commercial or technical basic training with further education in the field of supply chain, logistics, business analysis, business informatics or business administration.
  • Initial experience in projects or process optimizations, for example as a key user, sub-project member or specialist responsible.

  • Purchase
  • Production planning
  • Logistics / warehouse
  • Master data management

  • Practical experience with SAP MM, PP, WM or EWM is advantageous
  • Enjoying process optimization and digital solutions
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
