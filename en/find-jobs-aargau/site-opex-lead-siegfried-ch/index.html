Your Role

At Siegfried we are currently looking for a Site OPEX Lead to join our team in our site based in Zofingen.

Reporting to the Site Head of Siegfried Zofingen and functionally reporting to the Global Head of Operations Management and Operational Excellence, the Site OPEX Lead will lead and deploy the site Operational Excellence strategy and management system to sustainably improve performance, ensuring alignment with safety, quality, and regulatory requirements as well as global OPEX standards.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Strategy & Deployment:

· Define and deploy the site OPEX strategy aligned with site and global priorities · Lead the local, cross-functional translation of strategic objectives into structured improvement programs and roadmaps.

  1. Project Portfolio & Governance:

· Manage the full lifecycle of the OPEX project portfolio (idea to sustainability) · Ensure structured prioritization, governance, and tracking of initiatives, including global projects and initiatives

  1. Performance Management & KPIs:

· Define and monitor relevant KPIs linked to value drivers (safety, quality, delivery, cost) · Drive KPI-based steering and performance review routines

  1. Capability Building & Coaching:

· Train and coach employees and leaders in Lean, Six Sigma, and problem-solving methods · Lead cross-functional and sustainable site capability-building and ownership of improvement practices

  1. Culture & Cross-functional Enablement:

· Foster a continuous improvement mindset across all functions · Guarantee a functional tier meeting/reporting system that allows cross functional interfaces optimization · Ensure an ideation process · Enable cross-functional collaboration and alignment on improvement priorities

Main Tasks

· Define and align the site improvement program with the leadership team · Identify, assess, and prioritize improvement opportunities · Lead strategic cross-functional improvement projects · Deploy standard improvement methods and governance routines · Ensure sustainability and ownership of implemented improvements · Provide fact-based analysis and recommendations to site leadership

Your Profile

What are we looking for?

  • Degree in engineering, science, or related field

  • Strong Lean/Six Sigma expertise with certification would be a plus (Black Belt preferred)
  • Strong project and program management skills
  • Analytical thinking and data-driven decision-making capability
  • Strong leadership, coaching, and change management skills
  • Ability to influence in a matrix organization
  • Fluent in German and English
  • Willingness to travel once a quarter

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network of 16 sites across three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly skilled professionals, we take the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial scale and manufacture safe drugs for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations today that can carry out the development and manufacturing of APIs and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
