Full description

Working behind the scenes to ensure our store runs smoothly? Welcome to the fresh winning team. Without logistics, nothing works here. It is the bottleneck, efficiency booster, and heartbeat all in one. Here we need absolute professionals like you. Denner. This is our store.

Responsibilities

  • Plan store orders using the route planning program
  • Record and coordinate daily orders, as well as prepare delivery documents
  • Ensure a timely and smooth flow of information to our stores and drivers
  • Respond to and handle customer and supplier inquiries regarding transportation
  • Support operational coordination in case of route planning changes
  • Perform general administrative tasks

Requirements

  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a comparable role
  • Completed basic or further training in the transport sector
  • Proven geographical knowledge of German-speaking Switzerland and Ticino
  • Willingness to work shifts between 03:30 and approximately 19:00 (Mon-Sat)
  • Team player with good communication skills
  • Good knowledge of MS Office (Excel) and preferably SAP S4/HANA module TM
  • German (fluent)
  • Italian (advantageous)
