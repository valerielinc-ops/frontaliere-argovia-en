Full description

Who you are

  • At least three years of experience in an SEO role, with demonstrable success in improving organic search performance and delivering business impact.
  • Strong knowledge of SEO content strategy, keyword research, onsite and offsite optimisation, and the technical aspects of search optimisation.
  • Experience with SEO and analytics tools such as Google Search Console, Google Analytics, BrightEdge, SEMRush[JA1.1], or comparable platforms, and the ability to turn data into clear priorities and recommendations.
  • Familiarity with local SEO, including optimising Google Business Profiles and improving visibility across Google Maps and other location-based search results.[JA2.1]
  • Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to explain SEO opportunities clearly and collaborate effectively with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Curiosity and a proactive learning mindset: you stay informed about the fast-changing search landscape, understand how AI Search and Social Search are developing, and can translate emerging trends into practical opportunities.
  • Understanding of how SEO and SEA work together within SEM and Integrated Search. Preferably, you also bring hands-on SEA experience, including campaign optimisation, budgeting, bidding and targeting, to help drive this agenda forward.
  • Excellent written and spoken English is essential. Knowledge of a Swiss national language is beneficial.
  • A customer-focused mindset, attention to detail and motivation to continuously improve, learn and adapt to change.

Your responsibilities

You're an integral part of our Country Integrated Media team at IKEA Switzerland, reporting to the Integrated Media Manager.

As our SEO Specialist, your primary focus is to grow organic visibility, attract high-quality traffic to IKEA.com and optimise search performance across various digital channels. You turn SEO insights into practical improvements and collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to move our search agenda forward.

Key responsibilities

  • Execute and continuously improve SEO activities to support organic growth and business objectives.
  • Research keywords, customer search intent and search opportunities to prioritise content and optimisation activities.
  • Review and optimise content across digital touchpoints, translating onsite and offsite SEO insights into actionable recommendations.
  • Identify technical SEO improvement opportunities and work with internal and external stakeholders to develop and implement solutions.
  • Monitor and report on SEO performance, including visibility, rankings, organic traffic and conversion, using insights to prioritise improvements.
  • Stay on top of a fast-changing search environment, including AI Search and evolving customer search behaviour, and translate relevant developments into opportunities for testing and continuous improvement.
  • Help move the SEM and Integrated Search agenda forward by connecting SEO and SEA, sharing keyword and performance insights, aligning organic and paid search tactics, and contributing to the development of Social Search with internal and external stakeholders.

SEO is the core focus of this role. Experience in SEA is an additional valuable strength for advancing the SEM and Integrated Search agenda.
