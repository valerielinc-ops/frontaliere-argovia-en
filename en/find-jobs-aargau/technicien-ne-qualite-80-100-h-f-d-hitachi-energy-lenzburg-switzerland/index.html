Full description

About the position As a quality technician, you ensure the quality of our manufactured products and make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of our production processes. You carry out intermediate and outgoing inspections, evaluate quality deviations and initiate appropriate measures in cooperation with the relevant interfaces. In addition, you support with quality projects, internal training and audits. With your careful and solution-oriented way of working, you help to meet the high quality requirements of our customers.

You can do that.

  • Performance of intermediate and exit checks on manufactured products and testing and evaluation of results against the applicable quality requirements
  • Testing and evaluation of non-conformity reports (NKM, NMR) and taking appropriate measures in cooperation with the relevant interfaces
  • Support in the evaluation and processing of quality reports as well as lot-on-hold reports
  • Independent assessment of tolerances and manufacturing defects and decision on the introduction of appropriate measures
  • Planning and implementation of internal quality training as well as active participation in the further development of quality assurance, CAR and 4Q activities and projects to improve yield
  • Support for CAR projects related to customer complaints as well as participation in internal and external audits and the implementation of improvement measures
  • Introduction and documentation of continuous improvements (CIP) as well as management of the internal shift 6S activities in cooperation with the OpEx team and shift management
  • Ensure customer quality requirements, carry out shift transfer and report on the quality situation in line briefings as well as compliance with occupational safety, health and environmental regulations; in case of personnel bottlenecks and according to the instructions of shift management, you also provide operational support in the production process

You bring that with you

  • Basic knowledge of quality testing and production processes acquired through professional experience; vocational or technical training is advantageous
  • Experience in testing and evaluating products and in detecting and assessing quality deviations
  • Structured, careful and solution-oriented working methods and the ability to derive appropriate measures in the event of deviations
  • Communication ability and the ability to communicate quality information in an understandable way and to work with different interfaces
  • Willingness to take responsibility for quality, continuous improvement and compliance with safety and environmental regulations
  • Awareness of the confidential handling of sensitive quality and customer information

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to actively participate in future-oriented solutions.
