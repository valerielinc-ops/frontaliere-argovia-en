Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility in the function as an area chauffeur*in.

Mansioni

  • shunting activities of various vehicle combinations (motor cars, trailers, cityliners)
  • Provision of trucks for loading and unloading
  • Securing the cargo
  • Correct setting and monitoring of transport temperatures
  • Compliance with the requirements of the disposition
  • Gentle treatment of the products to be transported (food)

Requirements

  • min. 5-6 years: Transportation
  • Category C/CE driving licence
  • Multiannual, accident-free driving practice and willingness to work flexible hours
  • Friendly and well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)
  • No education/training required
  • German (good knowledge)
