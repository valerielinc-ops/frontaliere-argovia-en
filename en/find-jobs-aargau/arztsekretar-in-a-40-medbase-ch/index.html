Welcome to Medbase Abtwil

Our modern, versatile medical center is located right next to the Säntispark in Abtwil, just a few kilometres from St. Gallen. Here we look after patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. Around 50 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine, sports medicine or performance diagnostics as well as orthopaedics, physiotherapy or traditional Chinese medicine TCM work together interprofessionally. Our Medical Center is certified by Swiss Olympic in the field of sports medicine. We maintain a close cooperation with the Säntispark, whose modern infrastructure we use for certain forms of therapy. At Medbase Abtwil, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is basically possible in all functions.

You can move that

As a medical secretary at Medbase, you take care of the smooth running of the practice administration. Your organizational talent and discretion help ensure that the practice works optimally and that the team can focus on the patients.

  • Support for doctors in administrative tasks
  • Coordination of everyday practice and appointment management
  • Processing of patient requests by telephone and e-mail
  • Management and maintenance of patient data

What you bring

  • Training as MPA or FaGe, ideally further training as medical secretary
  • Good IT knowledge (MS Office, Outlook)
  • Flexibility for shift work and structured working methods
  • Responsibility and discretion
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum B1 level), knowledge of English is advantageous

About us

The Medbase Group operates more than 150 medical, pharmaceutical and dental facilities in Switzerland. In this way, she has greatly expanded her position as a leading provider of outpatient, integrated medicine in recent years. Around 3,200 employees look after patients holistically – from prevention to acute medicine and rehabilitation. More than 480 family and specialist doctors, 340 therapists, 190 pharmacists, around 360 dentists and specialists in orthodontics, implants and dental hygiene work together in a coordinated and interprofessional manner in the group. For companies, Medbase Corporate Health offers specific consulting and holistic projects in company health management. In its 12 Sports Medical Centers, Medbase supervises and treats athletes in leisure and elite sports. Medbase also provides junior researchers in the healthcare sector and trains around 340 young women and men.
