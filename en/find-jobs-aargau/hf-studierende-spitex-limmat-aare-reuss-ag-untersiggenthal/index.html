Full description

Spitex LAR AG is not a 0-8-15 care service. With a catchment area of over 55,000 inhabitants in six municipalities, we offer future-oriented, hospital-external care – modern, flexible and always focused on the highest quality. What are we missing? You! HF students

Your mission

During your studies, you learn to support people during their course of illness and their recovery process in a goal-oriented manner and with professional care as well as to accompany and advise relatives. Planning, controlling and evaluating the care process as well as carefully managing the care documentation. During the training, you will be introduced to the competencies of the various work processes according to the framework curriculum Pflege HF and gradually take on more responsibility and work increasingly independently. Your rucksack You enjoy your profession, enjoy being on the road and enjoy working in a team as well as independently. If you have also passed the aptitude test for admission to the Higher School, then you are exactly right with us.
