We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Capture and mutation of our range of tables & chairs on our SAP and DIVA systems
  • Preparation of sales documents and assortment grid
  • Preparation and maintenance of data to strengthen the online shop
  • Ongoing contact with our suppliers and branches
  • Administrative handling of our advertising inputs
  • Support of the purchaser in the assortment planning
  • Commercial or comparable education or training
  • Good PC user knowledge in Microsoft Office (especially Excel)
  • First commercial experience
  • Independent organisational talent
  • Understanding business relationships
  • PC User Knowledge in SAP R/3
  • Experience in the furnishing industry
  • PC User Knowledge in DIVA
  • Foreign languages (English/French)
