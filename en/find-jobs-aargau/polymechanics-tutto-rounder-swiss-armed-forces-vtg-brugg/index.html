Full description

In a nutshell

In this role, you will work on a variety of systems in the field of engineering and bridge equipment, with a specific focus in your day-to-day work on polyester and plastics work (GRP). You will combine craftsmanship with mechanical tasks and will be primarily responsible for professional maintenance. If you enjoy practical work, have an affinity for plastics and polyester processing, and take responsibility for quality, you can look forward to an exciting and varied working environment.

  • Start date: April 1, 2027
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20510
  • Work location: Auhofweg 17, 5200 Brugg

You can contribute by

  • Carrying out versatile maintenance work on bridge systems and other engineering equipment
  • Producing workpieces, custom-made items, and mechanical fixtures, and preparing technical documentation
  • Processing and maintaining military operational and training equipment made of plastic, metal, and wood
  • Advise, accompany and monitor customers (troops and third parties) during handovers and returns with regard to driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and vehicle inventory
  • Document completed work, ensure quality controls and contribute to compliance with safety regulations

What makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as a polymechanic EFZ, agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ, construction machinery mechanic EFZ or equivalent mechanical training
  • Professional experience in mechanical manufacturing and maintenance, ideally in the field of machines, systems or military material
  • Good knowledge of plastics processing (especially polyester and GRP work), as well as experience in metal and wood processing and with conventional machines
  • Independent, structured and quality-conscious way of working, as well as manual dexterity and technical understanding
  • Ability to work in a team, reliability and flexibility, as well as willingness to work polyvalently in various specialist areas
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language, as well as enjoyment of dealing with customers (troops and third parties)

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in action Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Health in the workplace We support and advise our employees in the area of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Armed Forces and, with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia personnel, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors: The LBA offers a broad spectrum of workplaces throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.
