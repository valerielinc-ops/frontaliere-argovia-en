Full description

In your role as project manager/in IT and digitization projects, you actively shape the digital development of our foundation group. As part of our Operational Excellence team, you are an important interface between departments, IT, external partners and management.

You lead and accompany versatile projects around digital solutions, processes, tools and system landscapes. You bring structure into requirements, recognize connections and ensure that ideas create viable and practical solutions. In order to be successful in this role, you bring experience in project management, a good understanding of IT and process contexts and enjoy working with different stakeholders. Your workplace is in Bad Zurzach, home office possibilities are available.

Your tasks

  • Management and cooperation in IT and digitization projects within the entire foundation group
  • Evaluation, selection and introduction of new applications and digital solutions
  • Acceptance, structuring and prioritization of requirements together with relevant specialist areas
  • Analysis of existing processes, system landscapes, interfaces and data flows
  • Translation of professional requirements into clear IT and solution concepts
  • Coordination of internal stakeholders as well as external service providers and software partners
  • Preparation of economic considerations, basis for decision-making, project plans and status reports
  • Ensuring transparent project management in terms of deadlines, quality, risks and resources

What we want from you

  • A university or university degree in business informatics, industrial engineering, computer science, business administration with an IT focus or a comparable education
  • Ideally, you will bring 5 to 10 years of relevant professional experience in IT, digitization or organizational projects
  • You have experience in project management and have already managed interdisciplinary projects with specialist areas, IT and external partners
  • You have a good understanding of complex system landscapes, interfaces, data flows and integration logics
  • You have strong knowledge of the Microsoft environment, especially Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and ideally Power Platform.
  • Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central is an advantage
  • You understand IT projects not only technically, but also as process and change projects
  • You work structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented
  • You communicate securely with departments, IT, external partners and decision-makers
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English

What we offer

  • Work in a modern work environment and actively participate
  • Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Generous cost-sharing of education and training
  • Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your online application!
