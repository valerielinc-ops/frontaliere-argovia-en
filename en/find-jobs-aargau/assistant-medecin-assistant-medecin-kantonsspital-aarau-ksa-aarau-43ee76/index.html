Full description

Neurology

Job opening: by agreement indefinitely

Tasks

They look after patients with neurological diseases in the outpatient consultation, the bed ward, on the emergency and on the Stroke unit under specialist supervision. They gain experience in the entire spectrum of the subject and are part of a team of doctors. They actively attend the interdisciplinary and internal training courses. After a training phase, you participate in the weekend services.

Requirements

  • Swiss state examination in human medicine or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland (MEBEKO recognition)
  • Your training goal is specialist neurology or you have, as part of another specialist training, interest in neurology.
  • Prior knowledge of internal medicine is desired.
  • Very good language skills in German (verbal and written) are required.

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
