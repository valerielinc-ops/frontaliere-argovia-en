Full description

As a specialist in general internal medicine, you take over the continuous basic medical care of residents in retirement and nursing homes directly on site in the Aargau region. In a flexibly agreed time frame between 20 and 100%, you carry out regular visits, make independent medical decisions and are accompanied by an experienced MPA in tandem. Permanent employment is possible immediately or by appointment and is particularly suitable for doctors who want to combine long-term patient care with plannable and individually designable work models. What awaits you in this challenging task

  • Specialist care for patients in retirement and nursing homes
  • Conducting visits directly on site in the facilities
  • Close cooperation with nurses and interdisciplinary teams
  • Support in everyday life through an experienced MPA in the tandem model
  • Independent organisation and organisation of your everyday work
  • Continuous support of patients over a longer period of time

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in general internal medicine
  • Alternatively degree as a practicing doctor or practicing physician
  • Enjoying the care of geriatric patients
  • Interest in independent and structured working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Empathetic and calm treatment of patients
  • Self-employment and responsibility
  • Joy of interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Flexibility and organisational capacity
  • Interest in long-term patient support

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Flexible working models between 20 and 100%, from single daily assignments to full-time work
  • 43-hour week on a full-time workload as well as the opportunity to do some of the administrative work in the home office
  • 25 to 30 days of holiday per calendar year depending on age
  • Working time model Leisure Plus with the possibility to receive additional holidays against a corresponding wage reduction
  • Pension fund benefits beyond the minimum legal requirements
  • Income security in the event of illness or accident of up to 730 days
