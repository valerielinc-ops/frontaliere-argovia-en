Product Lead Pricing — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for product vision, strategy, goals and an outcome-oriented roadmap in the area of pricing
  • End-to-end responsibility from product discovery to delivery and rollout to adoption and continuous development
  • Full product and backlog responsibility for a cross-functional Scrum team with software engineering, BI and data science
  • Close cooperation in tandem with Team Lead Engineering: You are responsible for the why and what, while Team Lead manages the how
  • Continuous discovery with user feedback, data, hypotheses, prototypes and experiments to identify the most effective solutions
  • Prioritize opportunities and initiatives based on business value, effort and risk, and develop well-founded business cases
  • Further development of our pricing platform around pricing recommendations, repricing, dynamic pricing, price releases and B2B/B2C pricing calculation
  • Control of product success via relevant key figures such as gross margin, price index, degree of automation, time-to-price and price error rate
  • Active stakeholder management and close cooperation with category management, commercial, finance, data and IT
  • Identification of new potentials around automation, machine learning and AI for smarter and scalable pricing processes

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in product management, of which several years with real strategic product responsibility as product manager, senior product manager or product lead
  • Demonstrate practice in the development of product visions, strategies and outcome-oriented roadmaps
  • In-depth experience in product discovery, for example with user interviews, problem validation, prototyping, hypotheses and experiments
  • Secure Scrum practice as a product manager with own backlog responsibility and active design of Scrum events
  • Experience in the technical management of cross-functional product or development teams
  • Ability to assess priorities and trade-offs on a data-based basis and control decisions about product metrics, business cases and OKRs
  • Technical understanding of system landscapes, interfaces, data models and data-driven or algorithmic solutions
  • Strong stakeholder management as well as strong communication, moderation and persuasion skills
  • Analytical, conceptual and entrepreneurial thinking as well as the pleasure of translating complex relationships into concrete product decisions
  • Experience with advanced AI, ML and LLM technologies
  • Knowledge of pricing, revenue management or other data-intensive business domains is a clear plus

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
