Full description

Workload: 80–100% Fixed-term 1 year

Want to actively help shape Axpo's strategy and create real value through your analytical mindset? Then we look forward to hearing from you – fixed-term for 1 year, starting January 1, 2027 in Baden.

What you will do

  • Support the preparation of the annual strategy dialogue and strategy reporting for the Executive Board and Board of Directors
  • Help shape the continuous development of strategy reporting, question existing processes, and drive automation and digitalization
  • Support the development and implementation of strategy communication to strengthen the visibility and understanding of Axpo's strategy
  • Contribute to creating content on Axpo's strategy – from reports and presentations to digital formats
  • Foster internal exchange through regular updates, contributing to knowledge sharing and strategic alignment

What you bring & who you are

  • You hold a Master's degree in engineering, economics, or business administration
  • You bring two to three years of relevant professional experience – ideally in strategy management, management consulting, or the energy sector
  • You have strong analytical and conceptual thinking combined with a solution-oriented approach
  • You use AI tools such as our internal AxpoGPT to work efficiently and with a future-oriented mindset
  • You speak English at a native or business-fluent level
  • German is a plus

Even if you don't tick every box, we still look forward to receiving your application.

About the team

You will join the Corporate Strategy team, which plays a key role in shaping Axpo's strategic direction. We value open, respectful collaboration where different perspectives are welcome and great things are achieved together..
