Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Support as part of a team of six lawyers They provide the legal service in the processing of appeal proceedings from the entire field of the law on foreigners and in first instance proceedings concerning the postponement of the execution of the criminal remittance of the province.
  • You take on administrative tasks in the legal service, make legal clarifications and create draft orders and decisions.
  • In the Workers Section, you participate in the processing of sanctions procedures as part of the implementation of accompanying measures for the free movement of persons.
  • They make clarifications for the Working Section and draft legal hearings and reminders, as well as draft orders and criminal complaints.

What you bring

  • Completed legal studies (lic. iur. or MLaw)
  • Interest in migration law issues
  • Very good verbal and written expressive ability in German
  • Flexibility and commitment
  • Discretion

Your working environment

In the Office for Migration and Integration, we are responsible for the implementation of immigration and asylum legislation and implement the integration policy of the Confederation and the Canton. We issue permits for foreign persons, carry out labour market controls and ensure the implementation of the illegal work legislation. We advise foreigners willing to return and execute the expulsions ordered by the Canton and the Confederation.
