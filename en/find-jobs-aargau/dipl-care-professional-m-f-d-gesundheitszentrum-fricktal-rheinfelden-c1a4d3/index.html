Health Centre Fricktal AG | online since : 13.11.2026

Place of work: GZF Laufenburg

In the Seora – Living in old age at the two locations in Laufenburg and Rheinfelden, the needs as well as the self-determination and co-determination of our residents are at the center. In your Seora team, colleagues from different professional groups – carers, employees of the activation and resident service – work together respectfully and on an equal footing. Together, you ensure that our residents are cared for personally and warmly in a family atmosphere – always taking into account their individual needs and life story. Thanks to the immediate proximity to the acute hospital, qualified medical assistance and medical care is provided at both locations around the clock and the interdisciplinary cooperation with case management and physiotherapy is lived daily. Seora is part of the GZF.

  • You care for and care for our residents individually and respond to their needs and wishes
  • You take responsibility for the area assigned to you
  • You carry out the care documentation independently and responsibly
  • You help and support our learners in their tasks
  • You have an education as a Dipl. nurse and bring advantage of several years of professional experience
  • You enjoy dealing with older and demented people
  • You like to work independently and feel comfortable in the team
  • Readiness for shift work and weekend service, but no night watch
