Full description

Bring your technical expertise to us where production runs, as:

Service technician for production facilities (in-house) (gn)

Their tasks

  • Independent performance of maintenance (maintenance, inspection, repair and calibration) of Metco Coating Service production facilities (corundum blasting, coating and grinding facilities)
  • Error diagnosis and correction of plant shutdown malfunctions
  • Coordination of maintenance appointments with production and external suppliers as well as with the internal installation team
  • Cooperation in the construction and commissioning of new production facilities
  • Management of spare parts and wear parts warehouse
  • Acquisition of measuring equipment and coordination of calibration of measuring equipment
  • Maintenance of maintenance plans in SAP and documentation of performed maintenance in ORBIS/SAP
  • Cooperation in the implementation of improvement measures

Your profile

  • Successfully completed vocational apprenticeship as a mechatronics engineer, automatic engineer or electrical engineer (gn)
  • Knowledge of automation technology (ABB robots, Siemens controllers)
  • Experience in industrial plant maintenance
  • Ideally, already begun further training as a maintenance specialist (gn) with a Federal Law. Specialist card
  • Resilience, flexibility, solution-oriented and independent working methods
  • Willingness to work independently into new plants and processes and to carry out more complex tasks (e.g. calibration of plants) independently in the longer term
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage

We offer

  • Attractive compensation package with good social benefits
  • Direct impact on the further development of our global digital after-sales solutions
  • Collaboration with international teams and customers
  • Modern working methods, high personal responsibility and scope for design
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Varied activities in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and high-performance materials. With a unique portfolio that includes surface engineering, high performance materials, coating equipment and components, we improve products by increasing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medicine, luxury, semiconductor and tool technology. The headquarters are located in Pfäffikon, Switzerland. Oerlikon operates in 38 countries, employs around 9,500 people and generated sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2024.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential – guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
