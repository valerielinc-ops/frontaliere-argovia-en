Your Role

As an asset reliability engineer, you ensure that the performance, availability and reliability of our assets are sustainably improved. Structured analysis, effective maintenance strategies and technical improvement measures They make a significant contribution to ensuring reliable and economical production in the long term.

Your Profiles

Their role

  • Analyze failures, maintenance costs, asset effectiveness and risks, and identify critical vulnerabilities and recurrent sources of disruption
  • Develop and optimize maintenance strategies based on reliability data, root cause analyses and plant criticalities to ensure an optimal relationship between preventive, predictive and corrective maintenance
  • Leading and supporting reliability initiatives using cause, error and influence analyses as well as other reliability methods
  • Monitor the implementation and effectiveness of improvement measures based on relevant reliability indicators (e.g. MTBF - Mean time between failures, MTTR - Mean repair time)
  • Ensuring data quality in the CMMS and deriving recommendations for action for maintenance, planning and investment projects
  • Further develop the reliability culture by promoting best practices, condition monitoring strategies and standardized ways of working

Your profile Professional knowledge

  • Several years of professional experience in reliability engineering, maintenance, asset management or engineering in an industrial environment
  • In-depth knowledge of reliability methods such as RCA, FMEA, Risk Based Maintenance (RBM), Reliability Centered Maintenance (RCM) and Condition Monitoring
  • Experience in dealing with CMMS systems (ideally SAP PM) as well as in the analysis of maintenance and operational data
  • Knowledge of KPI systems such as MTBF, MTTR, OEE and availability analysis

Personal strengths

  • Strong analytical skills, high problem-solving skills and a very good technical understanding
  • Ability to moderate interdisciplinary teams and drive improvement initiatives
  • Strong communication and persuasion skills
  • Independent, proactive and results-oriented working method
