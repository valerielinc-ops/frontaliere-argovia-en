Full description

About the position As team leader/in electrical assembly at the GI Valves factory in Seon or Turgi leads a team of about 15 employees. You take on a central role in daily production control. You coordinate operational activities within the team and ensure that assembly, testing and logistics processes are efficient, safe and quality-appropriate. You are the direct contact person for the employees and work closely with production management and other interfaces. Your commitment contributes significantly to achieving our production goals and continuously improving. In addition to coordinative activities, you are actively involved in the assembly process (about 50%).

You can do that.

  • Management of the team both technically and operationally as well as in terms of personnel
  • Organisation and monitoring of production processes (assembly, testing, logistics)
  • Compliance with safety, quality and environmental requirements
  • Assistance in the training of new employees and carrying out safety training
  • Cooperation with interfaces such as planning, quality and logistics as well as documentation of production performance
  • Implementation and enforcement of the overall organisational objectives

You bring that with you

  • Technical vocational training (e.g. electrical engineering, electrical installation, mechatronics)
  • Further education as a technician HF is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in the assembly of electrical components
  • Experience as a team leader and good communication skills
  • German in word and writing, English skills of advantage
  • SAP knowledge and experience with digital production systems (MES) are a plus

More about us Our flexible working methods help you optimize your personal and professional performance and create an environment where all employees can develop and grow their skills.
