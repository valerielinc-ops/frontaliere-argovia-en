The Position Roche Kaiseraugst is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network. More than 1900 people are employed here. The site is home to Roche’s largest and most innovative packaging operations worldwide. Through independent cooperation in diverse teams, we pursue our vision: the best possible patient care at low cost for society!

In the Manufacturing Unit Assembly Packaging, at the production sites in Kaiseraugst and Basel, we take care of the production and packaging of various pharmaceutical forms (syringes, auto-injectors, vials). At Ocular Assembly, we have focused on packaging syringes for the treatment of eye diseases that lead to vision impairment, in particular wet age-dependent macular degeneration (nAMD), diabetic macular edema (DMÖ) and post-vein occlusion macular edema (RVV).

The opportunity

We offer internships that are primarily aimed at students from the fields of pharmacy, natural sciences, life sciences or engineering. During the internship you support the production or packaging company. You will learn how to handle and implement the extensive GMP regulations of the pharmaceutical industry.

Your tasks in this position

  • Preparation and revision of operating rules and work instructions (Standard Operating Procedure) for production.
  • Support for a wide range of activities and smaller projects (e.g. process optimizations, document adjustments).
  • Assistance and post-processing of walks in the production area (GEMBA walks).
  • Cooperation and clarification in planned and unplanned events in the production processes.
  • Support the team with changes, deviations and corrective actions.
  • Assistance with inspections and insight into GMP requirements for pharmaceutical companies
  • Contact person and support of daily meetings.
