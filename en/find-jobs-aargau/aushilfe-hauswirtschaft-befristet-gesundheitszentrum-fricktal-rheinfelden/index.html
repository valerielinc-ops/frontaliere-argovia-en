Health Center Fricktal AG | online since : 22.09.2026

Place of work: GZF Rheinfelden

Do you have a good sense of cleanliness and order? Are you having fun with varied tasks? Then you are exactly right with us - our housekeeping team is an important internal service provider responsible for the areas of value preservation, cleaning and disposal throughout the hospital. Our team of around 20 people is looking forward to your reinforcement!

  • You are responsible for the maintenance and intermediate cleaning of the patients: interior rooms, offices and examination rooms as well as medical facilities
  • For basic cleaning, you clean the floor surfaces with the scrubber or dust off high-altitude shelf with the help of a safety ladder
  • You are flexible and ready for various shift services in the time window of:
  • Mo - Fr: 06.00 - 12.00
  • Sa or So: 07.00 – 14.00
  • Experience in cleaning service in a hospital operation, insulation room is an advantage
  • Compellingly good knowledge of German (at least B1)
  • Flexibility regarding service planning (early service including weekend)
