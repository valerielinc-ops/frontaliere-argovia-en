Full description

The opportunity

Join our Semiconductor Packaging and Reliability Engineering team at the Central European Hitachi Energy Research Center in Baden-Dättwil. You'll contribute to a project focused on the reliability of HVDC stations, a key technology for long-distance, sustainable energy transmission. In this internship, you'll work closely with a multidisciplinary team, gaining hands-on experience in statistical modeling and reliability engineering. We offer a supportive environment where you can grow, learn, and make a real impact. The duration of the internship is 6 months.

How you'll make an impact

  • Assist in researching and developing the reliability profile of HVDC station on the component level
  • Assist in developing and applying novel statistical and mathematical models for reliability analysis
  • Developing and implementing stochastic reliability models for components in HVDC stations
  • Documenting and communicating the solutions developed to ensure knowledge transfer within the research team, academic cooperation partners and relevant business areas

Your background

  • Currently pursuing a M.Sc. degree in electrical engineering, power engineering, physics, applied mathematics, mechanical engineering or a related discipline (official enrolment is essential)
  • Understanding fundamental concepts in statistics and probability
  • Familiarity with reliability engineering and related concepts
  • Programing skills for data analysis and machine learning are a plus
  • Fluency in written and spoken English
  • Good communication and cooperation skills, creativity and the ability to work independently

More about us

We are a technology-driven company with a strong focus on innovation, continuous improvement, and sustainable manufacturing. We offer flexible working conditions, dedicated mentorship, and a supportive, inclusive environment where you can grow personally and professionally – empowering you to thrive while balancing life and work.
