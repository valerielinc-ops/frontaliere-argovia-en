Full description

The opportunity As a Shift Maintenance Technician, you will play a critical role in ensuring the availability, efficiency, and continuous improvement of semiconductor production systems. This role focuses on the Corrective and Preventive maintenance and optimization of Diffusion & Cleaning equipment while working closely with cross-functional teams to enhance safety, productivity, and system reliability.

How you will make an impact

  • Ensure maximum uptime of semiconductor production systems through proactive maintenance and troubleshooting of Diffusion equipment
  • Participate continuous process improvements in production systems, implementing workplace safety and efficiency practices aligned with 5S methodology
  • Collaborate effectively with maintenance, process, and production teams to achieve project goals and optimize production lines
  • Participate in system upgrade projects and initiatives to modernize production tools and processes
  • Maintain comprehensive documentation of daily work activities, maintenance tasks, and system performance
  • Support the troubleshooting and resolution of technical issues within the team
  • Train and mentor colleagues on Diffusion systems and best maintenance practices

Your background

  • Completed technical vocational training (e.g., Automation Technician, Electronics Technician, or similar), additional technical or methodological training in vacuum systems or related areas is a strong advantage
  • Hands-on experience in maintaining semiconductor production systems or similar industrial equipment
  • Solid understanding of electronics, mechanics, and pneumatics; experience with Diffusion Semiconductor systems is a plus (e.g., ASM, Centrotherm furnaces)
  • Experience with metrology systems for process control (e.g., Surfscan 6200, Semilab WT-2000) is highly desirable
  • Proficiency in English and German if possible, with excellent communication and documentation skills
  • Strong team player with excellent problem-solving and organizational skills, willing to work in shifts to ensure uninterrupted production operations
