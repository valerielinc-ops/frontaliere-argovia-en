Full description

About the position As a polymechanic, you are part of a prototype workshop and systems engineering in the high-performance laboratory in Baden. In an interdisciplinary environment, you work on different tasks and bring your technical skills to bear in a dynamic and varied working environment. You take over the production of components for test equipment and plant components and support the maintenance of the operating facilities.

You can do that.

  • Manufacture of test equipment and plant components based on sketches and production drawings
  • Creation of CAD drawings and 3D models
  • Operation and programming of conventional and CNC-controlled lathes and milling machines
  • Performing mechanical machining such as turning, milling, drilling and reworking of components
  • Sawing, shearing and bending work
  • Carrying out welding and soldering work and willingness to further develop these capabilities
  • Collaboration in the prototype workshop and close cooperation with development, systems engineering and maintenance
  • Ensuring maintenance of operational facilities including documentation

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a polymechanic or equivalent technical training
  • Experience in manufacturing components according to sketches and technical drawings
  • In-depth knowledge in the operation and programming of conventional and CNC-controlled machines
  • Experience in creating CAD drawings and models; CAM skills are beneficial
  • craftsmanship and experience in mechanical processing
  • Knowledge of welding and soldering are a plus
  • Teamwork, initiative and flexibility as well as enjoyment of working in an interdisciplinary and dynamic environment

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to actively participate in future-oriented solutions.
