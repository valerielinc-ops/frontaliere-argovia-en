Full description

Tasks

With your positive personality, you not only shape an engineering culture and organization, but also actively shape the e-commerce of tomorrow. You lead different committed, cross-functional product teams and together with them you are responsible for scalable solutions with real impact for our customers: inside and central processes in the company.

  • You expect a lot of creative leeway and the opportunity to shape an engineering organization sustainably, from working methods to technology and architecture decisions to the further development of teams and executives.
  • You coach, develop and empower the team leads of your area with humanity, clarity and sensitivity, so that they lead effective teams and develop roles, responsibility and decision-making spaces in a targeted manner.
  • Together with the Head of Product, you will design product-oriented and agile development processes, keep the focus on business goals and establish a data-informed working method
  • You work agile with short iterations and in suitable versions of Scrum or Kanban
  • Yes, we have enough legacy too. This is one part of the exciting challenge. Together with the Domain Architect, you navigate through technical trade-offs, make pragmatic decisions, actively shape the future with a lot of implementation skills and keep the can-do spirit alive in the team.
  • You are part of the technology leadership team and together with your peers shape the strategic direction and technical vision of the entire engineering organization

Requirements

You strongly identify with a culture where

  • AI is exploited to the maximum
  • Servant leadership, benevolent feedback and the targeted further development of people and organization belong together
  • Product works closely with engineering, wants to understand problems in depth, lives true agility, relies on MVPs, short iterations and fast learning and makes decisions consistently from a user and business point of view
  • technically full-stack thinking, pair programming, whiteboard sessions, code reviews, automated testing and CI/CD with several productive releases per day are normal. Teams assume real end-to-end responsibility in the sense of "You build it, you own it"

We are a good match if you

  • are a pioneer in AI engineering, bring the organization strongly to greater efficiency in AI engineering and at the same time ensure that we do not produce an AI slop - whether through automated AI reviews in the pipeline, adapted agile processes or better tooling
  • Experience in domains such as purchasing, product data, pricing, logistics or ERP or have a general knowledge of business applications and e-commerce
  • not only know the described culture, but live, coach and anchor in the organization and contribute to a strong team culture with appreciative communication, warmth and a good dose of humor
  • a great coach and role model for team leads and organization
  • Complex topics are addressed with perseverance and resilience and are also perceived as a motivating lighthouse in challenging situations
  • Concepts such as DDD, Event-Driven Architecture, Clean Architecture, SOLID, Feature Flags or Dependency Injection are understood as tools that you establish in a structured organization. At the same time, you have a solid understanding of distributed systems and DevOps practices
  • Data-based leadership: Deployment frequency, lead time and incident recovery are signals for you, not goals. You see OKRs as a tool to make a targeted impact
  • at least 10 years of experience in modern software development, of which at least 2 years in a leadership role
  • Speaking German and English

We probably don’t fit together so well if you

  • Frameworks such as SAFe prefer or think in classic project structures and specifications
  • has worked mainly in highly regulated environments and has little experience in fast, product-oriented organizations with large distributed systems
  • Feel more comfortable in role models where infrastructure, backend, frontend, testing and product thinking are strictly separated
  • do not have an infinite hunger for knowledge to constantly learn new things
  • More frustrated by imperfect systems than motivated - Spoiler: we are not there yet
  • Work and good mood strictly separated from each other

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.

"Together we shape the trade of tomorrow" Brack.Alltron employs around 1300 employees and generates over one billion turnover. The company exists because a person had the courage to find new ways and go them. Our success story shows that through each individual, something great is created together and we love our uniqueness. We set ourselves ambitious goals, but it is very important to us that we act responsibly, promote and challenge each other and enjoy what we do every day.
