Full description

Deputy Clinical Director in Rheumatology: a position where you combine professional responsibility, leadership and strategic participation. In the canton of Aargau, we are looking for a deputy head doctor or deputy head doctor for rheumatology and immunology in the range of 80 – 100 %. In a clinic in one of the largest center hospitals in Switzerland, the entire spectrum of inflammatory rheumatic and immunological diseases awaits you in mostly outpatient care. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They represent the clinical management in professional and organizational matters of rheumatology and immunology
  • They take care of patients with all diseases of the rheumatic type, mainly on an outpatient basis and in special situations in hospital
  • You bear cadreal responsibility for diagnostics, therapy planning and medical quality
  • They assume leadership responsibility as well as supervision, training and further education of assistant doctors
  • You work interdisciplinary with Physical Medicine, Internal Medicine and other departments of the House
  • You actively shape the challenges of modern rheumatology strategically and conceptually

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in rheumatology, ideally with additional qualification in clinical immunology
  • Several years of clinical experience in the detection and treatment of inflammatory rheumatic systemic diseases
  • Initial management experience as well as the willingness to assume management and further education responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Human and professionally committed leader with high quality standards
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and accompanying younger colleagues
  • Design will and interest in the strategic development of the department

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Deputy management function with room for manoeuvre in one of the largest center hospitals in Switzerland with more than 50 clinics, institutes and departments
  • Financial and time support for personal development and comprehensive internal training programme
  • Advanced working time models and a competence center for occupational health management
  • Furnished single rooms in small residential communities in the immediate vicinity of the hospital for an uncomplicated start
  • Own, qualikita-certified day care center with 74 full seats for children from 0 to 12 years old
  • Workplace in the middle of an award-winning park, ten minutes' walk from the train station and less than an hour's drive from Basel, Bern and Zurich

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
