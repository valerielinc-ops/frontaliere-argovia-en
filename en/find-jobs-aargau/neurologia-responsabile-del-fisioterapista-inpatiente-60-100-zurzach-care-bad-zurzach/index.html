Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic in Bad Zurzach offers rehabilitation at the highest medical level. The offer ranges from neurological, musculoskeletal, cardiological to rheumatological rehabilitation. In our modern infrastructure, we combine medical excellence with individual care. Our interdisciplinary team accompanies patients with a holistic approach on the way back to everyday life.

Are you a physiotherapist specialising in neurology and would like to work as a specialist? Then read on!

Your main tasks as a physiotherapist/physiotherapist

  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You carry out findings and treat our inpatients in the field of neurology. You are in a constant exchange with a team of doctors, nurses and other therapeutic professions.
  • Patient-oriented care: You carry out assessments and documentation, which are personally tailored to each patient and support the creation of an individual therapy plan
  • Therapies: You perform individual and group therapies. There are numerous possibilities for this, such as robotics, our modern MTT, the therapy garden and our therapy pools at your disposal.

Your main tasks as a professional responsibility

  • Implementation of specialist supervisions and internal training in physiotherapy
  • Collaboration in the team of specialists to develop quality standards in the field of physiotherapy neurology
  • Joint technical management of the physiotherapists at the site by issuing instructions and joint responsibility with the specialist management
  • Active exchange and participation in the cross-location specialist structure
  • Develop, implement and communicate interprofessional standards

Your profile

  • Training : Completed training as a physiotherapist or equivalent with at least one positive PreCheck
  • Experience : You bring already proven expertise in the field of neurology
  • Personality: You enjoy working with neurological patients, are committed to shaping the development of the department and initiate change processes. Furthermore, you like to take on responsibility and are open for new tasks.

Your advantages with us

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate. We offer you a varied activity with a wide range of treatment options (robotics, MTT, water, garden) in an interprofessional team
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care as well as comprehensive support and clinical supervisions by our specialist management.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits
  • Education and training : generous share of costs in education and training and opportunity for weekly internal training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to seeing you! Hospicing is possible at any time: Look forward to the team and everyday work when you first meet.
