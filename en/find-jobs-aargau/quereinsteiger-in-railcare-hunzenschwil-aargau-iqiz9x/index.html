Description

railCare — Hunzenschwil, Aargau, Svizzera

combined transport from a single source. As a young and innovative transport company, we connect the whole of Switzerland with our own trains and trucks and transport goods from door to door. railCare AG is a subsidiary of Coop and employs around 600 people at 14 locations.

Tasks

In the first phase, you would be employed as a mover and perform the following tasks:

  • Remove swap containers with swap containers between the court vehicle and railway wagons
  • Bridge-up and removal of swap boxes
  • Loading, unloading and provision of swap containers

In the second phase, you will be trained as a shunt (A40).

  • After passing the examination, functional activities (ranging) are added to the above-mentioned tasks.
  • In a further step, training as a locomotive driver is possible if suitable.

Requirements

You have completed an apprenticeship (EFZ/EBA) and would like to join the ranking cat. A40.

  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Physically fit and resilient
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Work in shift work (Monday to Sunday)
