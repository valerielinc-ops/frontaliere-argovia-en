Full description

For our special consultation hours with a focus on old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy at the attractive location Ambulatorium Dattwil (SIWF training centre), we are looking for a suitable reinforcement as

Tasks

  • Evidence-based integrated diagnostics and therapy as part of the standardised procedures for outpatient consultation hours under medical supervision
  • Carefully structured case documentation and reporting
  • Networking/communication with stakeholders (family doctor, hospital, relatives)
  • Cooperation in the multi-professional and interdisciplinary team
  • Participation in interdisciplinary case meetings

Requirements

  • Advanced training for the title of specialist psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Interest in the focus area of age psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Responsibility, reliability and teamwork
  • High social skills, independent and efficient working style and high resilience
  • Enjoyment of multi-professional and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
