Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Leadership, development, task and resource planning of the IT service BKS with its six employees.
  • Advises and supports the departments in IT projects and project management and is a member of control groups of IT projects. Responsible for procurement and financial planning for workplace infrastructure and licensing.
  • Responsible for the maintenance of the CMDB in the area of BKS, supports the creation of ISDS concepts or operating manuals and is responsible for compliance with applicable IT guidelines in the department of BKS.
  • Represents the departmental interests in all IT matters. Ensure the flow of information between departmental and cantonal IT centres and within the department. Active in the IT conference (coordination of ICT service provision in cantonal administration).
  • Supports the Secretary General in overarching IT issues and strategy decisions. Actively cooperates with the department’s CDO. Leads and assists the Departmental Information Security Officer.

What you bring

  • University of applied sciences degree computer science or vocational training in computer science with corresponding further education and project management or comparable training.
  • 5 to 7 years of professional experience in a comparable function as well as management experience.
  • Stylistic German and the competence for target group-oriented communication at all company levels.
  • Organizational and coordination talent with cooperative leadership style.
  • High social skills, very good communication, negotiation and conflict ability as well as a secure attitude towards different stakeholders.

Staff of the General Secretariat provide business and project services, legal, communication, human resources, finance, information technology, real estate and infrastructure for the Department of Education, Culture and Sport.
