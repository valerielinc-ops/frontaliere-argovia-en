Full description

Medical Day Station

Job: 1 February 2027 or by arrangement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • They look after outpatient and short-term inpatient patients of the intervention center from Monday to Friday and are ready to provide half-services.
  • In close cooperation with the intervention center, you mainly work with the disciplines of cardiology, angiology, interventional radiology and neuroradiology.
  • They ensure the nursing preparation of patients for interventions or diagnostic examinations and take over the professional monitoring after the procedure.
  • Your high level of technical, methodological and social competence will contribute to patient-oriented and professional care.
  • As a day care provider, you accompany our MPA learners and promote their learning success.
  • They are actively involved in the further development of processes within the framework of the upcoming new hospital building.

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a nurse HF, DN II or AKP and have at least two years of professional experience in an acute care hospital within the last five years.
  • They are accustomed to a motivated personality and independent work.
  • They value the close interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation, are capable of teamwork and are resilient and keep an overview even in complex, fast-changing situations.
  • They have pedagogical skills and enjoy accompanying young people.
  • Responsible, patient and empathetic action is a matter of course for you.

With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
