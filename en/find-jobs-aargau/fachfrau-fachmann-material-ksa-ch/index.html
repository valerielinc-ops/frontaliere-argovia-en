Full description

Women's Hospital and Children's Hospital

Job: 1 March 2027 or by arrangement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • Responsible for uniform, available and functioning consumables and for the proper functioning of the equipment.
  • Has the overview of the products of the different stations
  • Is responsible for the procurement of new equipment and consumables in compliance with budget
  • Ensure connections between stations and procurement and logistics
  • Transfer your knowledge, coordinate training
  • Ensures professional and cost-conscious procurement of the material
  • Ensure that special materials are specifically ordered and purchased
  • Delegates tasks of material and equipment management in accordance with hospital-internal competence regulation
  • Organizes training/instructions when needed
  • Creates guidelines on the use of the material together with the care experts

Requirements

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF, experience in an intensive care unit desirable or specialist medical engineering service. You have at least 5 years of professional experience.
  • Affinity and interest in materials and equipment as well as close cooperation between internal and external bodies
  • Engaged, open-minded personality who sees interdisciplinary cooperation as an interesting challenge
  • Transparent communication, independent working methods and organisation
  • Experience in the design of change processes
  • You are well versed in dealing with the common office and software applications and have a good digital affinity

With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
