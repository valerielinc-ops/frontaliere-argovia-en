Role overview

The opportunity As a global leader in power semiconductor technology, we offer one of the most diverse product portfolios in the industry.

Our products include thyristors, diodes, gate turn-off thyristors, integrated gate-commutated thyristors, insulated-gate bipolar transistors, and metal–oxide–semiconductor field-effect transistors.

These components are trusted across sectors such as power transmission and distribution, mobility, and renewable energy for their exceptional quality and reliability.
