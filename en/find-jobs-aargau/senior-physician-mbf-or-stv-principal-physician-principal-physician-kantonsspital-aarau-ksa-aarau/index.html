The Department of Rheumatology and Immunology is a category A training centre with an intensive, versatile training programme and a modern infrastructure. The focus is on outpatient care, the inpatients are treated together with the Medical University Hospital. We are strongly committed to the further education of specialists in both rheumatology and family medicine.

In this function, you conduct your office hours independently and are active on a consular level throughout the house. In addition, you take over the technical supervision of our assistant doctors and actively participate in the design of internal and external further and further training.

As a highly motivated team, we of course work interdisciplinary and interprofessionally and are therefore happy about a like-minded reinforcement by a human and professionally committed personality who wants to shape the challenges of modern rheumatology together with us.
