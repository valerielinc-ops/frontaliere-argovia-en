Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Cooperation in the preparation of task and financial plan and annual report
  • Support in litigation for the task and financial plan and annual report
  • Preparation of financial statistics and ad hoc evaluations
  • Support for the management and acquisition of insurance services
  • Participation in internal projects (e.g. digitalization projects) and in the project Management Office
  • Support in reporting on cantonal holdings

What you bring

  • University degree at bachelor or master level (preferably business or economics or public management)
  • Interest in public finances
  • Rapid comprehension and a networked and analytical mindset
  • Conceptual skills as well as an independent and precise working method
  • High service orientation and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

The Department of Finance and Resources, with around 650 employees, is responsible as a cross-sectional department for ensuring that the canton has the necessary resources to fulfil its tasks. The Finance Department is committed to a sustainable and balanced financial budget. It is responsible for the task and financial plan as well as the annual report with annual accounts, assesses financially effective government business and develops fiscal policy and financial law foundations. The department is also responsible for central investment management, cantonal accounting and computer systems used in accounting and reporting. In addition, it is responsible for the central payment transactions and liquidity management of the canton.
