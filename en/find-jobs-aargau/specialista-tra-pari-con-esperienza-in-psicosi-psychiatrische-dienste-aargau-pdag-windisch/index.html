Full description

We are looking for you immediately or by appointment as tasks:

  • Participation in the multi-professional treatment team of the acute ward
  • Introducing the peer perspective in case discussions, treatment planning and reflection processes
  • Conducting individual discussions with patients on a voluntary basis
  • Participation in recovery-oriented group offers
  • Supporting patients in acute crisis situations through hope, providing perspectives and experiential knowledge
  • Promoting self-determination, participation and personal responsibility in the treatment process
  • Participation in further development of peer work within the PDAG
  • Networking with external recovery-oriented offers and self-help organizations
  • Participation in internal ward projects for quality development Requirements:
  • Completed EX-IN further training
  • Own experience of psychosis
  • Ability to professionally reflect on one's own experience
  • Ability to work in a team and willingness to work interprofessionally
  • Resilience in dealing with acute psychiatric crisis situations
  • Willingness for continuous training
  • Professional experience in a psychiatric context is desired We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. That's exactly why we offer you a lot: we strongly encourage further training. We also invest a lot in team cohesion and offer all of our teams access to the “Moodtalk” software, which strengthens team communication. We also offer attractive discounts on well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport receive an impressive eco-bonus at the end of the year. We also offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy a holiday.
