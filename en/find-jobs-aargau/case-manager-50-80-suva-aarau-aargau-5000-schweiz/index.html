Full description

Do you want to accompany people with targeted case management on their way back to work? Then you are right with us. What you do:

  • UVG cases with reintegration potential are handled independently and proactively.
  • You assess performance claims and services.
  • To assess and control the cases, you will obtain medical, legal and other information.
  • You ensure the cost/benefit ratio for all interventions including situational setting of the strategy and intensity of case management.
  • In companies and/or with insured persons, you independently carry out clarifications, advise on site and submit solutions with appropriate decision-making responsibility.
  • You coordinate the services of all participating institutions and network partners. What you bring:
  • You have a commercial basic education or an equivalent education.
  • You are in possession of the diploma oath. Social Security Professional and ideally completed a Certificate of Advanced Studies (CAS) in Case Management.
  • You are expected to have in-depth knowledge of social and accident insurance as well as medical and legal issues.
  • You master German at native level and bring knowledge of a second national language.
  • A high level of consulting competence and self-responsible thinking and acting is a matter of course for you.
  • You are known for your coordination skills.
