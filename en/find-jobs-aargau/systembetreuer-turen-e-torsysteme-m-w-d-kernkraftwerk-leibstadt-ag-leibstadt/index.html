Full description

Your tasks

As a system engineer in doors and gates, you assume technical system responsibility for complex closing, door and gate systems at the Leibstadt nuclear power plant. This is not only about classic building or industrial doors, but also about technically demanding systems with different mechanical, electrical and safety functions and a wide range of interfaces within the plant.

  • Technical System Responsibility: You ensure a comprehensive understanding of the functions, tasks and design of the supervised systems. You are the central technical contact person for operation, maintenance, engineering and other specialist areas for questions about the function, design, availability and safety of the systems.
  • System monitoring and technical support: You accompany the systems throughout their entire life cycle and ensure their technical integrity and reliable function. This includes the analysis of disruptions and particular events, the assessment of their effects and the initiation and coordination of appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  • Maintenance: You create the maintenance planning and coordinate the corresponding execution.
  • Projects: You lead the projects for the replacement or new installation of doors and gates. site coordination and planning of objects, installation and transport
  • Support and coordination of assembly teams and acceptances
  • Documentation and verification: You are responsible for maintaining and updating the technical system documentation and the relevant plant databases. This includes, inter alia, design and configuration documentation, technical requirements, operational and maintenance information and system condition reports. You also create and coordinate the necessary technical evidence and ensure that the systems meet the plant-specific safety and functional requirements.
  • Regulatory and regulatory requirements: You regularly review the relevant legal and regulatory requirements for your area of responsibility and ensure their implementation and verification. In addition, you will process technical requirements and requirements of supervisory authorities and participate in appropriate tests and measures.
  • Collaboration and knowledge management: You work closely with the responsible component managers as well as with internal and external specialist departments and contribute your expertise to interdisciplinary projects and working groups. At the same time, you ensure the long-term preservation of technical know-how and a structured knowledge transfer within your area of responsibility.

Your profile

  • Completed studies as an engineer in the field of mechanical engineering (ETH, FH, BA or comparable training) or technician
  • Experience in the design, commissioning, maintenance or technical support of doors and gates
  • Ideally, experience in system engineering or technical system responsibility
  • Experience in dealing with technical documentation, databases and the systematic analysis of technical relationships
  • Interest in complex technical systems and the willingness to become deeply involved in the specific functions and requirements of the supervised facilities
  • Ideally, professional experience in a safety-critical, regulated or technically demanding environment
  • Nuclear experience is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods as well as strong communication and team skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
