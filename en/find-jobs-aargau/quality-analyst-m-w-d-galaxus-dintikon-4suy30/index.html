Full description

As a Quality Analyst, you procure and analyze quality data from after sales and retail, identify risks and fields of action and derive measures with team, managers and specialist areas. You test their impact, make quality work more efficient and scalable through automation and strengthen the data and analysis competence in the team.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG main camp Dintikon, parallel road, 5606 Dintikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You procure, connect and analyze relevant quality data, ensure its plausibility and identify patterns, causes and risks
  • With the further development of relevant quality indicators and measurement logics, you create transparency about quality, developments and the need for action
  • Together with the relevant areas, you derive concrete measures from the findings and check data-based whether they achieve the desired effect.
  • In audits, coaching and case reflections, you bring your analytical perspective and support you in classifying abnormalities and prioritizing fields of action.
  • Develop data-based quality solutions and automate quality inspections, analysis and monitoring to make our quality work more efficient and scalable

Requirements

  • First professional experience: In a data-driven profession within a comparable operational environment
  • Completed initial or continuing education: in a relevant area
  • In-depth experience in data analysis, quality management, service operations or in a comparable operational environment
  • BigQuery and SQL you use securely for the acquisition, structuring and analysis of data and visualize insights in an understandable way
  • You recognize complex relationships and derive pragmatic, measurable and scalable recommendations from them
  • You have experience with analytics and reporting solutions as well as workflow automation and use AI beyond classic prompting for analysis and automation.
  • Complex content you communicate understandably and question assumptions constructively
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (flowing)
