Full description

The recreational facilities of Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz offer many people variety, relaxation, and sports activities. With movement and relaxation offerings, they provide a balance to everyday life and quality time that both body and mind remember.

Responsibilities

  • Supervision and monitoring of swimmers in the pool and sauna areas to ensure safety
  • Providing first aid in emergencies
  • Cleaning and maintenance tasks throughout the area
  • Energetic sauna infusions
  • Conducting water aerobics classes
  • Supervision in the slide tower
  • Enforcement of pool regulations

Requirements

  • Professional experience: preferably in the service sector
  • Completed basic or advanced training: preferably in the service sector
  • Current BLS-AED-SRC certification
  • Valid Brevet plus Pool
  • Flexibility in scheduling, 7-day operation
  • Willingness to work weekends, holidays, and shifts
  • Friendly and customer-oriented demeanor
  • Resilience in stressful situations
  • Team-oriented working approach
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Occupational pension: attractive pension benefits with high employer contributions
  • Sports and fitness offers: discounts on subscriptions to recreational and fitness facilities
  • Insurance: advantageous conditions with various insurance companies
  • Cumulus points: multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
