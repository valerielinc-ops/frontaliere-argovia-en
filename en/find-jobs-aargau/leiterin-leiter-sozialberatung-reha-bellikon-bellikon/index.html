Affiliation, sensuality and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through heights and depths and help them find their way back to life or career after accident or illness. Would you like to introduce your leadership experience and expertise in an environment that stands for holistic rehabilitation, interdisciplinary cooperation and sustainable patient success? Then we look forward to meeting you. Tasks

  • You take over the professional and personal leadership of social counselling and actively shape their further development

  • You actively shape processes and bring your ideas into cross-disciplinary projects
  • With expertise and empathy, you accompany patients and their relatives in challenging situations of life
  • Insurance and financial questions you clarify solution-oriented and understandable
  • You organize sustainable connection solutions – from returning home to the appropriate living or care form
  • Through your strong networking, you create sustainable solutions with departments, authorities and external institutions
  • The interdisciplinary cooperation in the treatment team will help you actively and at eye level Profile

  • A further training at Master's level is a plus and underlines your technical claim
  • In social insurance and in particular in health care, you know well
  • Leadership experience and joy in developing and accompanying people, drawing you
  • With your high social competence and communication strength, you create trust and orientation
  • You will perform safely and professionally and convince by your structured, solution-oriented way of working
  • Even in dynamic situations, you remain flexible, resilient and keep an overview
  • Teamwork is self-evident – together you can achieve more
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
