Full description

Pensum: 80–100% | Place of work: Lupfig

As a senior project manager, you take responsibility for challenging projects in the field of telecommunications and ICT – in a team that lives diversity and personal responsibility.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You take overall responsibility for time, costs, quality and risk management – from offering to project acceptance
  • You analyze and concretize project ideas and specifications and create project proposals with goals, resources and project plan
  • You organize and lead project teams on the way to efficient goal achievement
  • Significantly support the bid phase and coordinate cooperation with engineering, operations and product and service management
  • You ensure communication with all stakeholders and are the central point of contact for customers in the implementation phase
  • You promote the further development of project management processes and pass on your know-how to the team through coaching and training.

Profile and Requirements

  • You bring several years of experience in project management, ideally including leading larger, complex, multidisciplinary projects
  • Telecommunications experience is an advantage
  • You have a university degree with a focus on ICT or Security or a comparable education
  • Project management certification (e.g. HERMES, PMI, IPMA, PRINCE2) is an advantage – or you are ready to purchase it
  • You communicate safely in German and English, knowledge of French is a plus
  • You communicate strongly and convincingly, work independently and keep an overview even in challenging situations

Even if you do not meet all points – we look forward to your application!

Your environment

You work at Axpo Systems, an independent subsidiary of the Axpo Group. We offer telecommunications, cyber security and data center solutions for operators of critical infrastructures in Switzerland.
