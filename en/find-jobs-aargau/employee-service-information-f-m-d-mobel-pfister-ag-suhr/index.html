Employee Service & Information (m/f/d)

50%

At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Answering questions from customers about purchasing at Pfister in direct customer contact at the information desk in the store
  • Answering customer questions by phone and email
  • Ensuring administrative tasks in the branch (correspondence, price labels, customer service cases, etc.)
  • Support for branch management in administrative tasks
  • Support of employees in sales in questions concerning sales documents, IT systems and administrative processes in the branch
  • Help out on the sales floor and at the checkout if necessary
  • Retail training
  • Contactful and communicative personality
  • Saturday is a working day
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Some years of professional experience in sales or administration
  • Foreign language skills in French and English are beneficial
