Full description

Who you are

As the Service Office People & Culture Manager, you are the strategic People & Culture partner for IKEA Switzerland's headquarters and a key contributor to the national People & Culture agenda. You ensure the effective delivery of People & Culture processes, enable leaders to grow their business through people, and strengthen collaboration across IKEA Switzerland.

By translating the IKEA People Strategy into action, you create a workplace where our culture, values and people are at the centre of everything we do.

To be successful in this role, you bring

  • Extensive experience as a People & Culture Business Partner, People & Culture Manager or in a comparable management role.
  • A passion for people and a proven ability to develop organisations, leaders and teams while creating an engaging co-worker experience.
  • The confidence and maturity to influence, coach and challenge leaders and stakeholders, even without direct reporting lines.
  • Strong leadership, communication and relationship-building skills, with the ability to navigate a complex stakeholder landscape and manage multiple priorities.
  • Experience in developing and implementing strategic people initiatives, ideally within a global matrix organisation.
  • A solid understanding of key People & Culture processes, including performance management, talent and succession planning, co-worker relations, and Swiss labour law.
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken. Knowledge of another Swiss national language is a plus.
  • Ability to work effectively in a hybrid environment, balancing collaboration in the office with independent work from home.

Your responsibilities

  • Act as a trusted business partner to leaders across the Service Office, driving business performance through people and taking responsibility for the Service Office staff budget, including effective planning and management.
  • Foster an engaging, inclusive and high-performing workplace built on IKEA's values and culture.
  • Strengthen collaboration between the Service Office and all other units, building strong partnerships to meet current and future business needs.
  • Lead the local implementation of IKEA's People Strategy and translate global priorities into meaningful local actions.
  • Drive talent and succession planning by coaching and supporting leaders to identify and develop future talent.
  • Secure the competence, leadership capabilities and organisational readiness needed to achieve our business ambitions.
  • Ensure People & Culture processes are delivered consistently while continuously enhancing the co-worker experience.

This position is open for applications until Monday 7th of September.
