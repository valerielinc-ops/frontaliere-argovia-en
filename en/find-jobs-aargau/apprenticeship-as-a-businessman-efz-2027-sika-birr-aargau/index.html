About the role Do you want to start after school, gain exciting insights into the professional world and become part of an international company? Then you are exactly right with us! Whether high-rise buildings, tunnels, wind farms or modern industrial plants: all over the world, Sika products ensure that buildings and vehicles become more durable, safe and sustainable. Behind these projects are not only technical professionals, but also dedicated merchants who hold the strings together in the background. For the apprenticeship start in August 2027, we are looking for motivated and curious talents for training as a merchant / merchant EFZ at our location in Birr. During your teaching, you will get to know different departments and gain exciting insights into the diverse world of an international company. You work actively from the beginning, take responsibility and are an important part of our teams.

With us you will

  • Managing administrative and organisational tasks independently
  • Get to know different areas of the company
  • Taking responsibility for your own tasks and projects
  • Working together with colleagues from different fields
  • Gaining insights into business processes
  • Develop your communication and team skills
  • Working in a global environment and experiencing international contacts

Of course, you will be accompanied by experienced vocational trainers who support and promote you.

Your skills and experience You bring that with you

You fit in with us when you

  • Are curious and want to learn something new
  • glad to take responsibility
  • Open, reliable and motivated
  • Enjoy teamwork and communication
  • Bringing interest in economic issues and figures
  • attend or have completed secondary school level A with good performances
  • Have fun with foreign languages, especially English
  • Want to find out where your strengths lie and how you can develop them

Why you should come to us You can look forward to that. At Sika, we focus on trust, personal responsibility and development opportunities. During your teaching you are not just there, but in the middle of it.

Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport.
