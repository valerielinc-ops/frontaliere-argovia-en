Under the 2024 Italy-Switzerland bilateral agreement (in force for cross-border workers hired after 17 July 2023) dual taxation applies: the Swiss employer withholds 80 % of the standard cantonal rate, the taxpayer declares the income in Italy with a 10,000 EUR allowance and tax credit for the Swiss withholding. Cross-border workers already classified before 17 July 2023 keep the old regime (exclusive Swiss taxation, 38.8 % refund to the Italian residence municipality).
