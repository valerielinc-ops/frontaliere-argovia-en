They support pharmaceutical manufacturing by carefully maintaining production data in the MES system, producing production documentation and preparing relevant production documents and labels. Their tasks

  • They take over the work preparation and follow-up of production orders in the MES system as well as the preparation and maintenance of the associated GMP documentation and paper documents.
  • They transfer production-relevant parameters from manufacturing specifications to Master Batch Records (MBRs) and ensure that all data, materials and documents are assigned to the correct batch.
  • They produce sample labels and production documents with the utmost care and thus make an important contribution to a smooth and error-free production process.
  • You ensure material availability in the MES system, work GMP-compliant and document your activities in a timely and comprehensible manner in accordance with the applicable requirements.
  • They work closely with production, quality and other interfaces, support deviations and cause analyses and ensure that production employees can start production in an optimally prepared manner.

What we are looking for

  • They have fluent knowledge of German, have basic knowledge of English and are confident in dealing with common PC applications such as Word and Excel.
  • They work extremely precisely, concentrated and carefully and keep track of a large number of figures, data and documents, as well as under time pressure.
  • You are characterized by an independent, structured working method and take responsibility for the quality of your results.
  • You are a team-oriented personality, communicate openly and appreciate the close cooperation in a collegial environment.
  • Ideally, you will have experience in the GMP-regulated environment as well as knowledge of SAP and/or MES systems.
