Full description

The Bachem Group is investing in a new planned location in Sisslerfeld in northwestern Switzerland. This step is part of the long-term strategy to expand production capacities by the end of the decade. The new production site will play a central role in achieving these goals. The planning phase of the project offers you unique opportunities to actively shape the organization of the future.

In order to drive our continuous growth, we are always looking for highly qualified specialists. To strengthen our Manufacturing and Process Technology (MPT) team within Technical Operations (TechOps), we are looking for a dedicated personality as an Expert Process Specialist Manufacturing & Process Technology - Sisslerfeld Site Development (a) 100% .

Their tasks

  • Support of Head MPT Sisslerfeld in building a local MPT team in Stein within the global MPT organization
  • Participation in the development of MPT business processes in the course of location development
  • Collaboration and team coordination in establishing and continuously improving (MPT) processes, standards and KPIs
  • Reporting to Head MPT Sisslerfeld and professional collaboration with Head of TechOps Sisslerfeld, Engineering, Quality and Manufacturing
  • Developing user requirements together with the future operator and ensuring their correct and GMP-compliant implementation in all design phases of the project with emphasis on downstream and dispensation
  • Creating, reviewing and/or revising POLs, SOPs (Standard Operation Procedures), and WOIs (Work Instructions)
  • Technical support in planning and implementing cleaning validations
  • Contact person / SME for Manufacturing and Materials Management in process engineering issues, deviations and implementation of CAPA measures
  • Support for audits on MPT-specific topics, such as qualification, validation and / or documentation
  • Participation in strengthening the GMP and HSE culture in the team and production environment

Your profile

  • Completed study of chemistry, pharmaceutical chemistry, biotechnology or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of experience in the GMP-regulated pharmaceutical environment, ideally peptide synthesis and API purification and isolation
  • Proven expertise in process engineering and plant planning as well as in-depth understanding of regulatory requirements (e.g. ICH Q7, FDA, EMA)
  • High motivation and initiative for working on a project for the establishment of a new location
  • Structured, precise and quality-conscious working methods with high standards of documentation, compliance and continuous improvement
  • Independent and solution-oriented working methods and the ability to develop pragmatic solutions in complex or unclear situations

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
