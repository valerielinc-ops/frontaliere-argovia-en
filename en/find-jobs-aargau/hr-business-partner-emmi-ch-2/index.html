Full description

Do you not only want to accompany HR topics, but also actively shape the future of a production location? With us, you are close to the business, work closely with managers and employees and bring your expertise where it has an impact. In addition to day-to-day operations, you will help shape changes, develop executives and accompany the location on its way into the future.

You can do that.

  • Support and advice of managers along the entire employee lifecycle.
  • Sparring partners: in site management for personnel, organizational and cultural issues.
  • Accompaniment of change processes as well as active participation in location and organizational development.
  • Moderation of workshops, leadership dialogues and team developments.
  • Develop sustainable solutions in the areas of talent management, employee retention and succession planning.
  • Professional handling of demanding personnel cases, labour law issues and absence management.
  • Close cooperation with our HR services for professional and efficient personnel support.

You bring that with you

  • You have an HR certificate or comparable further education as well as several years of experience as an HR Business Partner: in a manufacturing or industrial environment.
  • It is a pleasure to accompany managers both in everyday operations and in strategic issues.
  • Experience or further education in the areas of change management, organizational development, coaching or leadership development.
  • Independent, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method.
  • Strong consulting and moderation skills as well as a good feeling for people and organizations.
  • Confident knowledge of German and good knowledge of English.

This is what we offer you

  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Flexible working time models: We offer various working time models, such as flexibilities or trusted working hours, in order to meet the personal needs and wishes of our employees.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
