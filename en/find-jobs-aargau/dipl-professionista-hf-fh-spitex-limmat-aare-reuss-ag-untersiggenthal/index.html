Full description

Spitex LAR AG is not a 0-8-15 care service. With a catchment area of over 55,000 inhabitants in six municipalities, we offer future-oriented, hospital-external care – modern, flexible and always focused on the highest quality. What are we missing? You! Dipl. Care professional HF/FH Wanted: Engaged and empathetic Dipl. Care professional HF

Your mission

  • Professional care: comprehensive support and care of our customers
  • Care planning: implementation of needs assessments (interRAI) and preparation of care plans
  • Case management: Personal accompaniment and support as case leader
  • Daily responsibility: contact person for all employees on tour
  • Coordination: organisation of entries and exits as well as communication with external stakeholders
