Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Preparation and evaluation of educational data (e.g. career analyses) with the programming language R
  • Developing and producing statistical publications
  • Special analyses of inquiries from politics and administration
  • Exchange with specialist bodies at cantonal and national level

What you bring

  • University degree preferably at master level (e.g. educational science, educational sociology)
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss education system
  • Good skills in the preparation and analysis of data with a programming language (ideally R) and experience with complex data (ideally longitudinal data)
  • Independent, structured, precise working method
  • Pronounced, addressee-friendly communication capability and secure appearance

As a cantonal office for statistics, we provide statistical information for the population, administration and the federal government. With analytical methods and innovative technologies, we continuously develop our processes and products. We are a modern expert organization that likes to share its knowledge, learn from mistakes and maintain an active feedback culture. We are convinced that we provide an important public service with official statistics of high quality, presented in a timely, easily accessible and understandable manner.
