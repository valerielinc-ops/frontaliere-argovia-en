Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

The Business Development department deals with the strategic and operational development of Hirslanden Klinik Aarau. We are looking for you as an experienced project manager to support our dedicated team.

YOUR TASKS

  • You work at the forefront of the development of the clinic and support the strategy process
  • You plan and manage strategic and operational projects independently, from the preparation of the project application to the implementation
  • You lead interdisciplinary and interprofessional project teams with sensitivity for change management
  • You develop specialist strategies and identify expansion and cooperation options
  • You identify optimization measures and process improvements and implement them in a target-oriented manner

Your profile

  • You have completed business studies (Uni/FH) or a comparable further education with business knowledge
  • You have several years of experience in managing challenging projects that do not have a predefined standard path
  • You are well-versed in the Swiss healthcare system, professional experience in a similar function in an acute hospital is a great advantage
  • You have strong strategic and networked thinking and strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Your very good communication and moderation skills distinguish you
  • You have experience in accompanying change processes and dealing with different stakeholders

Maybe we have just the right thing for you! With us you will find a working environment that is characterized by appreciation, team spirit and humor. We offer you a varied job, interesting career and development opportunities, a family environment, at least 25 days of vacation and many other employment highlights.

Michael Küffer, Head of Clinical Staff and Quality, Head of Business Development and Member of Clinical Management, is happy to help you with questions under T.
