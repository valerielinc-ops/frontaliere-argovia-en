Specialist psychologist: in Lenzburg (f/m/d), 60-100% - clinic At Hasel AG The Clinic Im Hasel AG is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for the treatment of addiction and trauma disorders.

As a health insurance-approved institution and part of the cantonal hospital list, we offer high-quality, evidence-based and human-oriented treatment for our patients.
