Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • You manage our logistics clients and ensure smooth order processing.
  • As a direct contact, you coordinate the cooperation between customers, warehouses and transport.
  • You manage stocks in the AS400 and internal logistics systems and monitor incoming goods.
  • The organization of returns and complaint management are part of your task.
  • You create KPI evaluations and support with invoicing.

Profile

  • You have completed a commercial education and enjoy working in a dynamic environment.
  • Good knowledge of English as well as French support you in the daily exchange.
  • Ideally, you will bring your first knowledge in the import/export area.
  • A quick comprehension, networked thinking and a customer-oriented working method distinguish you.
  • You work reliably, accurately and keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • Flexibility, teamwork and good IT user knowledge round off your profile.

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Padel, football or unhockey? With us you can rent free seats over lunch and let off steam

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
