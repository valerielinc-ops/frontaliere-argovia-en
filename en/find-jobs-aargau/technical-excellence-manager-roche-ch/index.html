The Position The Technical Excellence Manager is responsible for overarching technical topics/projects in the building and supports the various departments within and outside the Manufacturing Unit (MU) with stabilization and continuous improvement. This requires very good knowledge of operational processes and plants, a high degree of technical understanding and very good organizational skills for project management.

The possibility

  • Matrix management of all trades and interfaces, support in personnel selection and training as well as promotion of team culture and preparation of huddles.
  • Central interface to assets, asset care and support functions as well as detailed planning of all activities and ensuring the flow of information.
  • Planning and implementation of interruption phases, high-level overview of planned downtimes and management and moderation of meetings and huddles.
  • Preparation and maintenance of GxP-relevant documents, data analyses and key figures evaluations as well as implementation of A3 problem solvings and RCAs.
  • Participation in Gemba Walks, ensuring GMP-compliant production and supporting inspections and audits, launches and transfers.
  • Technical representative as SME for compliance with safety and environmental regulations to promote operational safety and implement accident prevention measures.
  • Continuous improvement of productivity and costs by applying Lean Production System methods, cost-conscious work and process optimization.

Who you are

  • Completed studies, preferably in a technical field, or corresponding training with many years of professional experience.
  • Several years of professional experience within the Manufacturing Unit.
  • Knowledge of safety, quality systems and GMP.
  • Very good organizational skills and project management skills.
  • Ability to implement lean and agile ways of working.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.
  • Planning complex interruptions in a drug production.
