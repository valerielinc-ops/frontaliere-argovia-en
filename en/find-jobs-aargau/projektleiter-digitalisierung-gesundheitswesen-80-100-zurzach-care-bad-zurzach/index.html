Full description

Your tasks

  • Project management: You are responsible for or are working on the management and implementation of strategic and operational projects (project / subproject management).
  • Decision Basis: Together with the relevant departments, you develop concepts, analyses and well-founded decision basis for the management.
  • Optimization: You analyze existing processes, structures and organizational processes, identify optimization and digitalization potentials and develop suitable solutions together with the specialist areas.
  • Implementation: You accompany the introduction of new processes, systems and technologies.
  • Coordination: You coordinate the cooperation between departments, IT and external partners.
  • Workshops and moderation: You plan and moderate workshops, working groups and project meetings.

Your profile

  • Education: You have completed studies, for example in health economics, health management, business administration with a specialization in health care, medical informatics, or a comparable education
  • Experience: You bring 3–5 years of professional experience in the healthcare sector, especially in project work or consulting.
  • Interest: You are enthusiastic about organizational development, digitalization, project management and change processes and want to actively shape the further development of the healthcare sector.
  • Contexts: You have a good understanding of business contexts as well as of healthcare processes.
  • Personality: You have an analytical, structured and solution-oriented way of thinking, act independently, are reliable and work precisely. High initiative and enjoyment of cross-divisional cooperation distinguish you as well as a strong communication and teamwork ability.

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive, appreciative and humorous team that works together to provide the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: We offer you a generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach
