Your future team is strategically positioned within Manufacturing Supports and belongs to the Manufacturing Unit Assembly & Packaging. Cross-functional collaboration takes place here to ensure smooth operations and the high quality standards in production.

As Facility Excellence Lead, you are responsible for coordinating overarching and building-specific compliance activities. Your main goal is to ensure uninterrupted and stable production through continuous improvements while strictly adhering to GMP compliance. In addition, you provide professional and personnel leadership for your team and create an environment that promotes self-organization, personal responsibility, and peak performance.

The opportunity

In this position, you will take on responsible tasks and make a significant contribution to the success of our production:

  • People management of the team, including recruitment, feedback discussions, and targeted development of employees through active application of the VACC model (Visionary, Architect, Coach, Catalyst).
  • Responsibility for overarching, building-specific compliance activities to ensure seamless GMP compliance, as well as implementation of the hygiene and zoning concept.
  • Coordination, approval and training of SOPs as well as ensuring ownership for deviations, CAPAs, changes and multi-site assessments.
  • Review and operational release of qualification and validation plans/reports as well as active support and implementation of quality risk management activities (QRM).
  • Preparation, support and follow-up of official inspections, audits and customer visits on site.
  • Example of lean thinking and application of LPS methods to continuously improve quality, efficiency and cost structure.
  • Promoting an open, collaborative work environment with a strong culture of trust and a transparent speak-up and error culture.

Who you are

To succeed in this role, you will have the following qualifications and attributes:

  • A completed pharmaceutical, scientific or technical degree (or comparable training) coupled with at least 2 years of relevant professional experience in the pharmaceutical environment with appropriate compliance and cGMP knowledge.
  • Practical experience in dealing with CAPAs, changes and process descriptions/optimisation (SOP) as well as preparation and support of internal and external inspections
  • In-depth knowledge of quality systems and current cGMP regulations with the firm conviction: "Robustness and compliance before speed".
  • Strong leadership skills as Visionary, Architect, Coach and Catalyst with a strong local presence to inspire the team.
  • A solution-oriented, positive and self-reflective personality with strong communication skills to promote an open error culture.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken.
