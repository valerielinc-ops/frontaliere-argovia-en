Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics as an HR Business Partner.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG, Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You advise, coach and support the line managers and employees in all personnel matters of the assigned areas (including occupational health management)
  • You recruit new employees from a broad professional profile in the logistical, technical and commercial environment
  • You are the contact person in all employment law issues and advise the line and the employees
  • In the further development of our HR services (especially in the area of personnel development and health management), you actively participate
  • You are involved in various work and project groups

Requirements

  • 5-7 years: in a comparable function, experience in logistics is advantageous
  • Eidg. Professional ID Card: Human Resources, Personnel Development, Recruitment
  • You have a commercial basic education with further education in HR (at least federal). Professional card
  • You are an experienced person in the operational HR environment (as HR Business Partner or similar) and enjoy managing and accompanying HR processes
  • Experience in occupational health management and in change processes is desired
  • You are used to having challenging conversations at different hierarchical levels and act as a trusted person
  • Ideally, you have already worked with SuccessFactors
  • Readiness for cross-location work in Suhr (AG) and Neuendorf (SO)
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Flexible place of work / home office: We promote flexibility, agility and efficiency with our home office options
  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Cumulus Points: MVB AG employees can collect additional Cumulus Points for purchases in Migro’s stores and stores
  • Social and personnel consultancy services: We offer our employees contact persons for private and professional challenges
