The entity shall

As an intern in the field of Quality Assurance Packaging Vials at the Kaiseraugst production site, you support the team in a wide range of aspects. In this internship, you will:

  • Support the continuous improvement and harmonisation of processes and systems in cooperation with interfaces
  • Supporting the digitalization of workflows, including the creation and maintenance of QA gSites, huddle boards
  • Supporting the sample inspection of finished products manufactured in the packaging plant
  • Perform data analysis and reporting
  • Developing suggestions for improvement of existing processes

Who you are

You are matriculated or have Completed your studies at a university or university of applied sciences, preferably in the field of life sciences or a related discipline within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a 12-month internship in a modern working environment, as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry. In addition, you round off your profile with the following competencies:

  • Readiness to learn and initiative
  • Teamwork
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Responsibility
  • Reliability

Further information The preferred start of the internship is in February 2027 or by appointment. Please indicate your preferred start date and the desired internship duration in your application.

Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must accompany the application with a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training.

Part-time employment is not possible at this position. Writing a thesis is possible.
