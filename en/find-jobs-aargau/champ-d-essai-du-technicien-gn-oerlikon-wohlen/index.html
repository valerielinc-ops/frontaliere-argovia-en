Full description

Oerlikon Metco is a global leader in surface solutions. With innovative technologies and services we support our customers in demanding industries such as aerospace, medical technology and the automotive industry.

For our test site, we are looking for a reliable, motivated and responsible personality for the initial commissioning of our thermal coating plants, which are used by our international customers.

Technician (gn) test field

Their tasks

  • Initial commissioning of our thermal coating plants
  • Systematic error analysis and independent error correction
  • Performing calibrations, adjustments and leakage tests
  • Ensure internal quality standards incl. Final inspection before delivery to the customer
  • Installation, testing and testing of spare parts for current and older systems

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as an automatic technician (gn), ideally with further training as a technician (gn) HF
  • In-depth knowledge in measurement, control and regulation technology
  • Experience with PLC systems (Siemens, B&R) as well as software installations, practice in setting up IPCs
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English

We offer

  • An attractive compensation package with good social benefits, prospects for personal and professional development
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment
  • Varied activities in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and high-performance materials. With a unique portfolio that includes surface engineering, high performance materials, coating equipment and components, we improve products by increasing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medicine, luxury, semiconductor and tool technology. The headquarters are located in Pfäffikon, Switzerland. Oerlikon operates in 38 countries, employs around 10,000 people and achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2025.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential – guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
