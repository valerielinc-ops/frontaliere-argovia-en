Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Strategic, organisational, human, financial and professional management of the Cantonal Monuments
  • Fulfillment of the statutory mandate and future-oriented further development of the section with the specialist areas of construction consultancy, construction inventory, art monument inventory, cultural property protection, archive, subsidy and administration
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation and networking with authorities at cantonal and municipal level and institutions as well as representation in projects and specialist committees
  • Support for the implementation of cantonal cultural policy as a member of the management of the Culture Department

What you bring

  • Leadership and initiative personality with high management skills, organizational talent, strong communication skills, social skills and customer orientation
  • Completed university degree (min. M.A.) in architecture or art and architectural history, ideally supplemented with an MAS in heritage conservation or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in conservation practice
  • Solid knowledge of administrative processes, both in the political environment as well as in legal and financial matters
  • Convincing appearance, resilience, assertiveness

The Cantonal Monument Preservation of the Canton of Aargau is committed to the sustainable preservation of the building heritage – for the people of today and tomorrow. With a team of 17 employees, she accompanies developers, planners, authorities and executors in projects with protected objects, researches architectural monuments and offers advice, expertise and financial support. At the same time, it increases public awareness and appreciation of historical building culture.
