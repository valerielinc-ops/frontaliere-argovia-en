Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • They take on the important task of assessing the benefits of job seekers on the basis of the Unemployment Insurance Act (AVIG).
  • They check the capacity of job seekers and impose sanctions where necessary.
  • They carry out procedures independently, issue decisions and provide professional advice to the stakeholders involved.
  • They examine requests for recovery and assess notifications relating to short-time and bad weather compensation.
  • They work closely with the relevant partner offices, in particular with unemployment funds, regional employment centres (RAV) and disability insurance.

What you bring

  • They have a commercial vocational training EFZ.
  • You have a further education as a social security specialist with federal law. Professional card completed or ready to complete.
  • They bring expertise in the areas of AVIG/AVIV, social security law and employment law.
  • They communicate stylishly in German, verbally and in writing, and use the common IT tools safely.
  • They are characterized by a pronounced sense of responsibility and quality as well as a high service orientation.

Your working environment

In the Office for Economic Affairs and Labour, we enforce federal laws such as the Unemployment Insurance Act (AVIG), the Employment Services Act (AVG) and the Labour Law (ArG). The main tasks are to supervise industry and industry with regard to occupational safety and health protection, to advise and place job seekers and to pay unemployment allowances for beneficiaries.
