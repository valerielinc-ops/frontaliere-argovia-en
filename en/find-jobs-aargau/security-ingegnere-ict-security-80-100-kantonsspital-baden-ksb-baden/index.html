Full description

You are familiar with cybersecurity and want to actively shape the security of a complex IT landscape. In the area of digitalization and ICT at the Cantonal Hospital Baden, you will further develop our security architecture and protect cloud, server, network and workplace systems. You implement security measures, analyze risks and support security incidents. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You will continue to develop our security architecture according to modern cybersecurity and zero-trust principles.
  • You derive concrete measures from risk analyses, regulatory requirements and proven safety standards and implement them.
  • You help shape the security roadmap and support the digital development of the KSB.
  • You design, implement and operate security solutions for Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune and Azure.
  • You develop secure concepts for identities, accesses and permissions.
  • You harden end devices and server platforms and establish contemporary security standards.
  • You will continue to develop monitoring, logging and recognition solutions with Microsoft Defender XDR and Microsoft Sentinel.
  • You can assist with the analysis and management of security incidents as well as vulnerability and patch management processes.
  • You lead challenging ICT security projects and work closely with infrastructure, network, workplace and applications.
  • You advise internal departments on information security issues.
  • You provide pikettdienst and support in security-related incidents outside the regular working hours.

Your profile

  • You have completed training in computer science with further training in the field of IT security, or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring several years of experience in IT security, security engineering or the operation of complex ICT infrastructures.
  • You’ll be familiar with Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune and Azure and how to configure and operate these platforms securely.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of identity and access management, system hardening and security architecture.
  • You have experience with security solutions such as Microsoft Defender XDR and Microsoft Sentinel or similar SIEM and XDR solutions.
  • You assess security risks, prioritize vulnerabilities and derive concrete measures from them.
  • You work analytically, think networked and find pragmatic solutions even in complex technical challenges.
  • You communicate clearly and communicate technical security topics in an understandable manner to different specialist areas.
  • You take responsibility, work independently and develop your expertise continuously.
  • You live in a shuttle distance to the Canton Hospital Baden, so that you can be on site in good time if necessary.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
