Full description

Pensum: 80–100% Location: Baden and partly Bern Beginning: 16 February 2027 or by appointment Duration: 6 months

Energy meets politics – and you are in the middle of it. Axpo Public Affairs brings together political processes and the energy industry. You research, analyze, organize and bring your own ideas.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You plan, coordinate and implement public affairs measures such as events, campaigns or background discussions.
  • You support our team in preparations for the 2027 national elections.
  • You help us establish and develop relationships with actors in the political environment.
  • You will follow political developments and research topics that move Axpo and the energy industry.
  • You support the development of briefings, arguments and stakeholder analyses as a basis for political discussions and decisions.

Profile & Requirements

  • You are a “political native”, know the processes of Swiss politics and are interested in how energy policy decisions arise.
  • You are a maker and like to put something on your feet.
  • You capture complex issues quickly and reproduce them in an understandable way.
  • You formulate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English.
  • You use AI tools consciously and responsibly to make your work more efficient – this includes AxpoGPT, the Axpo Group’s internal AI platform with access to multiple AI models.
  • You have completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree or are about to do so.
  • Ideally, you already have initial experience in politics or public affairs.

Even if you do not fully meet all requirements, we look forward to your application. Diverse experiences and perspectives strengthen our team.

About the team

Axpo’s public affairs team analyses policy developments at cantonal, national and European levels with the aim of improving the framework for sustainable and innovative energy solutions. We work across specialist boundaries and are closely networked in Switzerland and internationally. We value different perspectives, open exchange and support each other in everything we do.
