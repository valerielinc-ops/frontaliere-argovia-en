Full description

The opportunity

Join Hitachi Energy and shape sustainable high-voltage technologies. You will join an international R&D team focused on gas insulation. The team develops eco-efficient solutions for high-voltage applications. You will work on advanced research and industrial implementation. You will contribute to cutting-edge projects in gas-insulated switchgear. You will collaborate across research centers and business units. The role offers exposure to both theory and practical testing. You will grow your expertise in high-voltage engineering and gas physics. You will strengthen your skills in simulations and experimental methods. You will work in a collaborative and diverse environment. This role offers strong development opportunities and technical ownership. You will contribute to future-ready, sustainable energy solutions.

How you'll make an impact

  • Actively contribute to research projects in gas insulation applications
  • Conduct investigations on dielectric strength, dielectric recovery, partial discharge
  • Plan and execute high-voltage experimental studies
  • Model, simulate, interpret gas discharge phenomena
  • Develop innovative concepts for gas-insulated technologies
  • Support design and optimization of HV switchgear
  • Collaborate with global teams and external research institutes
  • Document results, publish findings and present your work at international conferences

Your background

  • PhD in Electrical Engineering, Physics, or related field with application in high voltage engineering
  • Strong knowledge of gas insulation and discharge physics
  • Practical experience in HV testing
  • Expertise in simulation tools such as MATLAB, Python, or COMSOL
  • Ability to analyze complex datasets and interpret results using statistical methods, modern AI tools
  • Solid English communication skills and structured working style
  • Relevant experience in industrial R&D is very beneficial
