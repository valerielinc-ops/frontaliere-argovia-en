Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Clarification and assessment of legal issues in the field of road traffic law
  • Implementation of administrative measures procedures, including clarifications of the facts, correspondence with parties to proceedings and preparation of written decisions
  • Provision of general information to data subjects at the counter, by telephone and in writing
  • Provision of information and conduct of correspondence with criminal and administrative authorities

What you bring

  • Completed law degree (lic. iur. or MLaw) or shortly before graduation
  • Interest in state and administrative law and interdisciplinary issues
  • Stylish written expression
  • Precise and independent work

Your work environment

With its ten departments and more than 2,400 employees, the Department of Economics and Home Affairs performs important tasks in the police, law enforcement, penal system, migration and integration, community supervision and economics. The Secretariat-General provides the central services for the department and is the interface with the general administration.

At the Road Transport Office, we are committed to road safety and environmental protection. We provide a wide range of services for the testing and approval of persons, vehicles and ships.

The Administrative Measures Section provides a wide range of administrative measures for the enforcement of road traffic law and thus makes an important contribution to road safety. Depending on individual cases, warnings, temporary and permanent driver’s license revocations, driving fitness checks, traffic instruction and other measures relating to the issue and revocation of driver’s licenses are decreed. The administrative authority is in principle free to establish the facts and the legal assessment of fault and danger.
