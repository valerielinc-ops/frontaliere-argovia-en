Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Broad legal spectrum: clarification of legal issues in all areas of responsibility of the department (including constitutional, general administrative, contractual, personnel, procurement, data protection, IT, property and rental law); Processing state liability claims, supervisory complaints and requests for access to official documents
  • Benefit from mentoring: Gaining valuable practical knowledge through the close support of experienced lawyers
  • Preparation for bar examination: Due to the wide range of tasks, optimal training with regard to the bar examination (6 months credited)
  • Starting a career: Ideal springboard for a future in court, law firm or administration

What you bring

  • Completed Law Studies (MLaw)
  • Interest and ability to deal with complex legal issues in a solution-oriented manner
  • Willingness to delve into new areas of law
  • Precise and independent work
  • Teamwork, resilience and flexibility

The Department of Finance and Resources, with around 650 employees, is responsible as a cross-sectional department for ensuring that the canton has the necessary resources to fulfil its tasks.

The General Secretariat shall plan, steer and coordinate overall strategic and operational administrative activities. The employees provide the department head and the departments with cooperative and effective leadership support. Services include communication, human resources management, business planning and controlling, accounting, project coordination, legal service and quality assurance.
