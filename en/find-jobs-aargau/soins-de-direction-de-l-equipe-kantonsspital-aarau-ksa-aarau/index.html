Intensive care unit 121

Job: By immediate or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

  • Co-responsibility in the management, promotion and motivation of employees
  • Co-responsibility for the introduction and instruction of new employees
  • Collaboration in the management team of the Clinic for Intensive Care Medicine
  • Examining and monitoring uniform care policies
  • Active participation in the care and treatment of patients in need of intensive care
  • Participation in the establishment of the Clinic for Intensive Care Medicine and Process Optimization

Requirements

  • Completed training for Dipl. Nursing professional / dipl. Nursing specialist NDS HF Intensive care or comparable training
  • Completed leadership training or willingness to obtain it
  • At least two years of professional experience in an intensive care unit in a Swiss acute care hospital
  • Initial leadership experience as group leadership or deputy team leadership is advantageous
  • High level of social and managerial competence as well as coordination and organisational capacity
  • Enjoying the interdisciplinary cooperation with patients, doctors and employees
