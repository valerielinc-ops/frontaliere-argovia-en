Interdisciplinary Emergency Center (INZ)

Job: As of 1 January 2027 or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

  • Acting as a Dipl. Expert / Dipl. Expert Emergency Care NDS HF
  • Co-responsibility for the management and organisation of the care team
  • Promotion and support of the professional and personal development of employees
  • Ensuring structured training and support for new employees
  • Co-creation of framework conditions for a high level of care and treatment quality

Requirements

  • Completed postgraduate studies as a Dipl. Expert / Dipl. Expert Emergency Care NDS HF

  • Ideally first leadership experience
  • Completed management or leadership training or willingness to undertake such training
  • High social and self-competence, as well as organizational talent
  • Transparent and appreciative communication and leadership culture
  • Joy of actively shaping changes and developments
  • Motivation to actively shape the development and further development in the triad
