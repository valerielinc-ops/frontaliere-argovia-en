Full description

Workload: 80% - 100% Location: Baden

Join Axpo as a Platform Engineer and play a key role in evolving and operating the core of our data platform on Azure and Databricks. Help us build a reliable, scalable and observable platform that empowers teams across the organization to work efficiently.

What you will do

  • Enhance core platform capabilities, focusing on reliability, scalability and performance
  • Improve observability through centralized monitoring and alerting across the platform
  • Manage cloud networking and connectivity, including Azure VNet, private endpoints and hybrid network architectures
  • Design and optimize platform architecture, balancing performance, cost efficiency, security and long-term maintainability
  • Drive automation and Infrastructure as Code and strengthen platform resilience by improving operational processes, troubleshooting and incident management
  • Collaborate with software engineers, architects and security teams to deliver a secure, robust and user-friendly platform

What you bring & who you are

  • Experience in platform engineering and/or system engineering
  • Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud provider (Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud Platform), ideally combined with infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform
  • Solid understanding of distributed systems, cloud architecture and system design principles
  • Experience with observability and monitoring (metrics, logging, tracing) and a strong ownership mindset to independently investigate challenges and drive improvements
  • Ideally, an analytical and problem-solving mindset, with a curiosity for understanding how systems work and how to make them better
  • You use AI tools, including AxpoGPT - the Axpo Group’s internal AI platform with access to multiple AI models - thoughtfully and responsibly to boost your efficiency
  • Fluent in English

Even if you don't tick every box, we'd still love to hear from you – diverse experiences and perspectives make our team stronger. Eligibility to work in Switzerland is required. Please note that we do not accept applications submitted through recruitment agencies.

We are a collaborative, forward-thinking team passionate about using data to drive innovation. Our open culture values diverse perspectives, encourages mutual support and celebrates shared successes.
