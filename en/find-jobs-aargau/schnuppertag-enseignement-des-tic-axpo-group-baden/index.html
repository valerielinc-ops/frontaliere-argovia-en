Schnuppertag ICT teaching professions at Axpo in Baden

You are in the middle of the career selection process and want to find out which ICT profession suits you? Then take the opportunity and get an exciting insight into the ICT world at Axpo in Baden on our Try Day on October 302026.

Get to know the following professions

  • Mediatographer/EFZ
  • Developer/in digital business EFZ
  • Computer Scientist/in EFZ Application Development
  • ICT specialist EFZ

What awaits you?

In the morning you will get to know Axpo as a teaching company and receive information about the training and the vocational school. In a joint group task, you can also become active yourself.

In the afternoon, everything revolves around your wishing profession. You will get an in-depth insight into the chosen teaching profession, learn more about everyday training and have the opportunity to ask your questions. We also give you helpful tips about the application and show you what matters in your future apprenticeship search.

Curious? Then we look forward to introducing you to the ICT professions at Axpo!

Important to know

This trial day is aimed at pupils who are currently in the vocational exploration phase and want to get a first insight into the various ICT professions.

Please note: The trial day is not intended for pupils who would like to apply for an apprenticeship with the beginning of teaching in 2027. *
