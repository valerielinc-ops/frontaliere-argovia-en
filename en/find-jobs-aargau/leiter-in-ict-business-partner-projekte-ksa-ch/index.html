Full description

Job: 01.02.2027 or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

As head of ICT Business Partners & Projects, you position ICT as a reliable and solution-oriented partner in the departments. You lead a team of ICT Business Partners and ICT Project Leaders and ensure that digitization needs are identified at an early stage, evaluated in a structured manner and implemented effectively.

In this central management function, you combine the requirements of the departments with the possibilities of ICT. You create clear responsibilities, promote cooperation between business partners and project leaders and consistently align your team’s performance with benefits, quality, deadlines and available capacities.

Their tasks

  • They lead and develop the ICT Business Partners and ICT Project Leaders in terms of personnel, expertise and organisation.
  • They shape the ICT Business Partner model and establish a regular, trusting exchange with the leaders of the departments.
  • They ensure the development of joint business ICT roadmaps and ensure that digitization needs, optimization potential and cross-divisional synergies are identified at an early stage.
  • You control the qualification and evaluation of new demands and work with your team to develop comprehensible prioritization proposals and decision bases.
  • They are responsible for establishing, managing and continuously developing the ICT project portfolio and ensuring a complete and transparent overview of ongoing, planned and requested projects.
  • They are responsible for the deployment and capacity planning of the team and ensure a coordinated allocation of project management contracts.
  • They ensure the professional planning and implementation of the allocated ICT and digitization projects in terms of quality, costs, deadlines, risks and expected benefits.
  • They ensure cooperation with process and quality management, ICT architecture, information security, application management, service management and external service providers.
  • You advise clients and executives on challenging ICT and digitalization projects.

Requirements

You are an experienced leader who combines strategic thinking with a high implementation orientation. You will succeed in bringing together different interests, communicating complex relationships in an understandable manner and developing viable solutions together with the participants.

  • They have a completed degree or a higher education and training in computer science, business informatics, business administration or a comparable discipline.
  • They bring several years of leadership experience as well as sound practice in demand management and in the management of demanding ICT or digitalization projects.
  • They have experience in resource and project management and are familiar with established project management methods and ICT governance.
  • They have a good understanding of ICT architecture, infrastructure and application landscapes as well as the requirements for secure and stable ICT operations.
  • They impress with distinct leadership, communication and stakeholder management competence.
  • They think entrepreneurially and networked, work structured and act solution- and benefit-oriented.
  • They understand how to shape changes, provide orientation and promote employees in their professional and personal development.
  • Healthcare experience is beneficial.
